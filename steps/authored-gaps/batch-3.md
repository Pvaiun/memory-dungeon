# Batch 3

The requests on the director's page, as seeded. The page's store holds the live copy and the answers. Near lines are Greats, starred seats, Great formations and the director's own earlier answers only: no Fines.

## 1. The Hunt: where to start with the Mark (Road)

I don't know where to start with the Mark, and I'd like you to. Tell me what the Mark should be in the loot: where a party gets one, and what a Marked foe gives the party besides its aim. Write as much or as little as you like. I'll turn it into pieces and bring back what I'm unsure of.

The Mark is the player's one way to aim the whole party: basic attacks, and abilities aimed at a random foe, go to the Marked foe. One effect in the web names it, your opener, and nothing you marked Great does. In the kits only the Paladin Marks. So most parties choose who they hit through each ability's own aim and nothing else. With the two Greats that repeat the opening, your opener already moves the Mark on each kill. One rule stands: a piece or a seat Marks only while no foe is Marked.

- yours: At fight start: a Mark on the foe with the least max HP.
- Great: When a foe falls: every ally's start-of-fight effects happen again.
- Great: On a kill: its gear's and seat's start-of-fight effects happen again.

## 2. The Hunt: a fight with one foe (Road)

I need something for a party built on kills in a fight with one foe. A payoff.

Against a lone boss nothing falls until the fight is over. The two Greats that repeat the opening never fire, the Torch has no fire to pass on, and a Mark has nowhere to move. A party that took those pieces gets nothing from them in the fights where most runs end. If that's the price of building on kills, mark this wrong.

- Great: When a foe falls: every ally's start-of-fight effects happen again.
- Great: On a kill: its gear's and seat's start-of-fight effects happen again.

## 3. A way to hide (Supply)

I need a way for a unit that isn't an Assassin to become Hidden during a fight. A piece or a seat.

Two of your effects pay for being Hidden: hits deal ×2, and mana comes back. The Assassin hides itself, and your knockout survived at 1 HP hides a unit once a fight. Nothing else in the web hides anyone, so on the other fifteen classes both payoffs wait for a near-knockout. While a unit is Hidden, every blow goes to the allies still in the light.

- Great: While Hidden: hits deal ×2.
- Great: Once a fight: survives a knockout at 1 HP, Hidden.
- yours: While Hidden: regains MP every tick.

## 4. The wind-up (Function)

I need a second thing that happens while a unit is in a wind-up: something for the caster or its allies, not a status on the foes.

The long wind-ups hold the biggest things in the kits: the Pyromancer's spells, the Paladin's Smite, the Hoplite's shove. While the bar fills the unit does nothing else, and a Silence loses the cast. One effect in the web is about that wait, your Shock on every tick of it. Beyond that, a long wind-up is only a cost.

- yours: Every tick of a wind-up lays Shock on every foe.

## 5. The bench (Function)

I need a piece about the bench: something a unit brings into a fight for having sat the last one out, or does for the party from camp. Not a plain refill.

Who fights is one of the four things the player chooses, and the bench grows from two seats to four over a run. A unit at camp regains some MP after each won fight, and nothing else happens there. Nothing in the web is about the swap, so who fights is decided by HP bars and nothing more. The one effect about what happens between fights is yours.

- yours: When a fight is won: gives up half the HP it has left and gains that share of its max MP.

## 6. A count that grows (Function)

I need a third thing that counts up over a fight and gets stronger as it goes. Not casts and not dodges: Crescendo and Heat already count those.

Two of your Greats grow as a fight runs: Crescendo with every cast, Heat with every dodge. A unit that mostly swings, or mostly takes blows, has nothing that builds. Long fights are where most runs end, and a count is what makes a long fight play differently from a short one.

- Great: Each cast gives the wearer a Crescendo (+20% damage, healing and stacks each, up to 10).
- Great: On a dodge: 3 Heat (5% faster each, up to 10). When hit: loses 50% of its Heat.

## 7. The Monk and the Hoplite (Two classes)

I need a piece a Monk and a Hoplite would both want, each for its own reason. They share the spear and the cloak. I'd like it to be a Sunder piece: nothing in the web names Sunder.

One lands a blur of small blows and dodges, and every blow adds Sunder. The other swings half as often for double, and its shove Sunders every foe. Both open a foe up for someone else's hits, and nothing Great joins them. Sunder is a thread of two roads (Landslide's target opened before the blow, and Thousand Cuts' answer to Defense) and comes only from the kits. Nothing in the web is near it. The Knight, the Valkyrie and the Frostweaver hold spears too.

## 8. The Knight and the Ranger (Two classes)

I need a piece a Knight and a Ranger would both want, each for its own reason. They share the horn and the trophy. Not a Shield piece or a dodge piece: the web has plenty of both.

One starts behind a Shield, Taunts, and bashes from its max HP: nothing in its trunk scales from Attack. The other lives by its crits, and each crit readies a Blur. They're two of the three starters, so most runs hold both from the first floor, and a piece between them is one of the first real choices a run can offer. One Great joins a unit that crits to a unit that's struck. The Hoplite and the Valkyrie hold horns too; the Assassin and the Geomancer hold trophies.

- Great: Crits heal the ally with the lowest HP% for 10% of the damage.

## 9. The Bard and the Hexer (Two classes)

I need a piece a Bard and a Hexer would both want, each for its own reason. They share the wand, the dagger and the tome.

One deals no damage beyond its swings: each swing mends an ally and gives another mana, and its songs land on every ally. The other is a spellblade whose swing chills, and its curses land on every foe. One thing in the web suits both already: your Shock from abilities that land on more than one unit. The Cleric and the Pyromancer hold wands too; the Ranger and the Assassin hold daggers; the Frostweaver holds tomes.

- yours: Any ability that lands on more than one unit, allies included, lays Shock on every foe.

## 10. The Vampire and the Stormcaller (Two classes)

I need a piece a Vampire and a Stormcaller would both want, each for its own reason. They share the fist weapons and the rune sword. Not a piece about HP paid or held low: the web has plenty.

One feeds on what it hits, drinks twice as much below half its HP, and spends its own life to mend an ally. The other is quick and frail: every swing arcs to a second foe and leaves Shock on both. Both swing fast, and nothing Great joins the one who drinks to the one who spreads. The nearest thing is a swing that reads the charge. The Monk holds fist weapons too; the Valkyrie and the Frostweaver hold rune swords.

- Great: Basic attacks deal +5% per Shock on the target.

## 11. A formation about who acts often (Formation)

I need a formation about time: how often each seat acts is what the seats trade. It should change how the party fights, not add to it.

Your formations move stats, Shields, mana, blows and statuses between seats. None moves time. Two take a seat's actions away altogether: the Banner never acts, and in yours the third seat swings only when the other two do. Nothing sits between those and a normal party. Landslide wants a seat that acts rarely and lands big, and Thousand Cuts wants seats that act often and land small.

- Great formation: Bannerman: the Banner never acts, and the other two gain its Attack and Defense.
- yours: Two seats swing the third: it takes no action of its own, and basic attacks whenever either of the others does.

## 12. A formation about crits (Formation)

I need a formation about crits: whose crits they are, or what a crit in one seat does for the others. It should change how the party fights, not add to it.

Three classes live by their crits (the Ranger, the Assassin, the Geomancer), and three roads count on a few chosen hits landing big. In the gear a crit already heals, and with yours it sets off a hit's triggers 4 times. No formation you kept or wrote is about a crit, so a party built on them sits in a shape made for something else.

- yours: Crits set off a hit's triggers 4 times.
- Great: Crits heal the ally with the lowest HP% for 10% of the damage.
- Great: +50% Crit chance. Hits that don't crit deal −50%.

# Feel read, round 4: twelve rogue fights, cold

Read from `out/feel-r4/` only (glossary, index, packs 1 to 12 tick by tick, summary). Judgements are what a player watching would feel; no numbers proposed.

## Per pack

**1. Toll Gate (fight, won t46).** Three bandits chip 216 a hit; nobody drops under 1,175. Anthem goes to the Cleric three times (t8.7, t25.4, t42.1), stacking Might to 15, and the *healer* is the top damage: 1,915 light at t42, 47% of the total, all basics. Safe, flat; the Bard a passenger at 378 a swing.

**2. Archers' Nest (fight, lost t21).** Not a fight: the Bramble knight enters at 0 HP, the Ranger at 368 and dies to the first arrow (t5.8). The lone Bard is Taunted onto a 10,500-HP, Armor-5 Shieldman (t6.6) for 168 to 350 a hit while three archers Take aim (t14.5 to 19.1); crit 821 (t17.3), Shield slam 720 (t20.3). The wind-ups read well; the fight was lost in the previous room.

**3. Ogre Bridge (elite, won t63).** The Monk carries (69%): Burning fist every 17 ticks keeps Burn climbing 3 → 21.5 with pulses of 1,181 (t37.6); Pressure point crit + Stun (t30.9) holds the Ogre's swing. Flatten (t23.2) stuns the Paladin mid-cast: "Smite waits" 8 ticks. Blood gift heals a Paladin at 63% (t21.9), then at t50.3 *kills its caster* (827 HP pays 800) to heal one at 62%. Sanctuary lands at t41 on two elementless bandits after the elite is dead; 25 ticks of mop-up.

**4. The Packmaster (boss, lost t152).** The Stormcaller enters at 800 HP / 30 MP and casts once (t47.4). Crush is a real threat: 8-tick wind-up, 2,938 (t14.4), an Aegis block of 4,032 (t53.2, the best moment in the set), a miss (t100.9), a crit 5,807 on a 2,000-HP Hexer *while Weakened* (t151.5). But the Hexer's basics hold the boss at Chill 12 from t53.8 on, so it acts every ~25 ticks and Shock sits idle; Sunder caps at 20 (t44) and Enfeeble reapplies it seven more times. From t59 to t152 the Hexer plinks 498 a hit at 39,000 HP.

**5. Snow Wolves (fight, won t45).** Six status lines at t0 before anyone swings. Four 8,080-HP wolves bite twice a turn; three of the first seven bites pick the Pyromancer, who casts one Firebolt (Burn 13 → 3,362, a clean ramp) and dies at t16.4. The Assassin Vanishes twice, takes 0 damage, Backstabs for 5,059 (t20.1). Decided by t20; the last two wolves take 25 more ticks.

**6. Ghoul Pit (elite, lost t42).** Four 40,640-HP ghouls (162,560 total) against a party that deals 10,874. All four first draws pick the Vampire (Aggro 90) over a Knight at 120 and a Bramble knight at 140; it dies at t8.7, the tick the Knight's Taunt lands. Thorns + Bleed are 65% of the party's damage; the Bramble knight dies at t30.9. Raise never casts. Unwinnable from tick 0, and it looks it.

**7. The Giant's Wrath (boss, won t39).** The one fight with a JRPG shape. Cheer buffs the boss (t12.7); Inferno's 8-tick wind-up lands t19.8 for 2,037 on everyone (Cleric to 328, Burn on all); Renew+ strips the Burn, Cure+ catches, Shield bash stuns (t31.7). Then a Frostweaver at Magic 9.5 with ice on a −50 ice boss: Blizzard 3,532, Frost lance 8,361 (t34), crit 6,057: 21,000 in 12 ticks, 81% of the damage. The Knight's basics hit for 39.

**8. Whispers (fight, won t40).** Wraith B crits the Monk 1,935 at t4 (2,500 → 565); Lifesteal gear heals it back. Monk 54% (Burn 20.1, 1,105 pulses). Blood gift+ twice on near-full allies (t15.4: 608 of 1,540 used, for 1,100 HP). Mute interrupts the Hoplite: "Paired guard is lost" (t24.3). The Hoplite swings once in 40 ticks and casts nothing; 41% of its 13% is its coat's Bleed.

**9. Leech Den (elite, lost t73).** Five 13,829-HP leeches drain MP on hit: the Knight is at 0 by t18.5 and stays there (Fortify and Shield bash never cast; Stillness+ gives it Clarity twice, t29.7 and t61, drained on the next bite). Cleric at 30/2,500 (t7.4, Last Rites fires), full Cure+ at t8.9. Frost lance+ 5,934 on a Chill-12 leech (t34.7). Taunt lapses t27.4, three leeches kill the Cleric by t38.7; then 34 ticks of the Knight eating 497s against 360 Regen pulses.

**10. The Silent Court (boss, lost t355).** 150,480-HP boss, two priests whose Mend heals 9,100 (four on the boss, six on itself). Quiet silences everyone every ~39 ticks for 16 (×9) and costs three casts ("Earthen shell+ is lost" t26.2). Geomancer dies t39.6 with 3,969 MP. Monk's Burn peaks 23.1 (1,271 pulses), Pressure point+ stuns the boss (t55.3); dies t105.9. Then 250 ticks of one Vampire: Blood drain+ every 16 ticks, Lifesteal takes it from 102 to 6,500 (t280.8) and 181 to 6,500 (t319.7); its coat's Bleed does 45,763, more than its basics. Priest B stands until t275. Enrage at t201.6; a crit 5,675 (t343.6) ends it.

**11. Ogre Bridge (elite, won t66).** The best regular rhythm in the set. Barbed arrow every 17 ticks; Bleed pays when the Ogre is hit (1,248 at t18.7). Smite 3,187 + Burn (t26.3), Smite again for the kill (t59.6). Flatten stuns the Geomancer mid-cast ("Earthen shell waits", t24.7 → t32.7); Shield 2,000 on two soaks chip. Burn's tail pulses fifteen times under 270 from t40 to t58.

**12. Twin Ogres (elite, lost t80).** Valkyrie enters at 750, dead t15.7. Shock is the show: 4.2 → 25.2 in 12 ticks, 2,015 a pulse when the ogre swings (t15.7, t27.6), 30% of the party's damage. Flatten 1,533 kills the 1,354-HP Stormcaller (t27.6). Then the Paladin alone 52 ticks, stunned 16 of them. Ogre A dies to a Burn tick at t64.2.

## Across all packs

**5. Rhythm.** Openings build: eight ticks of basics, then slot-1 abilities at t8 to t13; gear fires at t0 as a status pile-up (pack 5). Wind-ups read and pay: Crush, Inferno and Quiet at 8 and 6 ticks, Flatten at 6, the Ogre's 6-tick "begins attack"; a Stun holding a cast and a Silence killing one are the most legible things in the logs. Regular fights land at 40 to 46 ticks (packs 1, 5, 8; summary act 1 mean 41); elites 42 to 73. Bosses have no shape: 39, 152 and 355 here; summary act 3 bosses 46 to 355, act 2 17 to 240. The dead stretches are the tails after the decision (see 11), plus thirty lines of sub-200 pulses after every single-cast Burn (packs 11, 12).

**6. Abilities.** Slot-1 and slot-2 abilities on short cooldowns fire often and matter (Blood drain ×14 in pack 10; Burning fist, Flow, Anthem, Thicket, Stand fast, Barbed arrow, Cure, Frost lance). Anything first-ready past t25 fires once or never in a 40-tick fight. Summary: Spear thrust 0.77 casts a fight (first t39), Aspis 0.82 (t30.6), Sanctuary 0.84 (t37.4), Pressure point 0.79, Fireball 0.39, Tremor 0.33, Pyre 0.13, Valhalla 0.14, Raise 0.05 (t69.8). Too big for their cost: Frost lance 8,361 (pack 7) and 5,934 (pack 9) for 300 MP; Blood drain+ 8,064 with a full heal attached for 100 MP (pack 10 t336). Too small: Enfeeble onto a capped Sunder (pack 4); Blood gift, a fifth of max HP for an overheal. Tempo: the Monk and the Vampire have one; the Hoplite's "a third as often" reads as standing still (3 swings in 110 ticks, pack 4); casters have one only while MP lasts.

**7. Statuses.** *Burn*: the best ramp when re-fed (Monk, 1,100 to 1,270 pulses at 20+ stacks); one burst and a long noisy tail when cast once (packs 11, 12). *Bleed*: legible because its trigger is a hit on the holder (pack 11); gear Bleed outran kits (pack 10: 45,763 from a coat); the Assassin's crit-Bleed arrives too late (pack 5, 3%). *Shock*: spectacular on foes that act (pack 12), idle on a Chilled boss (pack 4); never near its cap (summary peak 13). *Chill*: one Frost lance pins the cap (packs 7, 9); upkept, a lock, not a slow (pack 4); invisible except through Brittle. *Sunder*: "sundered 20" on every line, 0 damage over 123 fights. *Weaken*: ~12% (pack 8); did not stop a weakened crit one-shot (pack 4). *Might*: legible as bigger bracketed numbers; Anthem to 15 made a Cleric the DPS (pack 1). *Taunt*: matters (its lapse killed pack 9's Cleric); one tick late in pack 6. *Guard*: a 64% cut, clearly visible (pack 4, 685 → 247). *Aegis*: one perfect block (pack 4). *Ward*: cast on nothing (pack 3). *Stun*: holds wind-ups and decides moments (packs 3, 10, 11); a slog on a lone survivor (pack 12). *Silence*: Quiet ×9 covering 41% of pack 10 read as three "lost" lines and otherwise nothing, since basics continue. *Regen*: a trickle against 500 to 1,000 hits (pack 9); Renew+'s strip of Burn is what showed (pack 7). *Clarity*: small (pack 3); wasted on a drained Knight (pack 9). *Bloodlust*: flickers a dozen times (pack 10), then doubles a drain into a full heal. *Enrage*: 120 ticks after the decision, 150 more to finish (pack 10). *Last Rites*: fired at 30 HP and showed (pack 9).

**8. Mana.** Short, and it changed what they did: Stormcaller (one cast in pack 4; summary 56% left, the hungriest), Hoplite (0 MP at t99, pack 4), Monk (35 MP from t20, pack 10), Knight when drained (pack 9). Never spent to effect: Cleric left 4,970/5,000 (pack 1), Geomancer died with 3,969 (pack 10), Hexer regained more than it spent (pack 4), Valkyrie 95% left (summary). Eleven of sixteen classes end with ≥79% of their MP. Refrain fed +150 to a full Cleric (pack 1). The Leech drain is the one place MP felt like a stake (pack 9).

**9. Classes.** Best-feeling: the Monk (every ability fires, a tempo, a Burn ramp, a stun that holds a boss; 43% share, balanced sources), the Ranger (Barbed arrow's rhythm and Bleed-on-hit, pack 11; 47% share), the Stormcaller when fed (the Shock ramp, pack 12). Frostweaver: dominant rather than good; WEAK × Brittle × Berserker torc → 8,361 (pack 7), 72 to 81% shares. Vampire: unkillable-until-crit (pack 10) or dead at t8.7 (pack 6); Blood gift a trap (pack 3). Assassin and Valkyrie play their fantasy in pack 5; Einherjar never shows (Valhalla 0.14 casts). Paladin: Smite ×2 when alive long enough; Judgement never fired in three packs. Cleric: heals read (pack 9); its damage comes from a Shock amulet or Anthem. Knight: a wall (took 14,245, pack 9) with basics at 39 (pack 7). Geomancer: Earthen shell held by stun or lost to silence (packs 10, 11), Bedrock dead with it. Bramble knight: 46% share, 69% from being hit, down at t27.6 mean (summary): the tank is the DPS and the first to fall. Worst-feeling: the Hexer (pack 4: 100% basic 498s, nine Enfeebles onto a capped Sunder, 93 ticks alone; 45 KOs per 100 fights, the highest), the Hoplite (pack 8: one swing, no casts), the Bard (pack 1: 15%, Anthem on the same target thrice).

**10. Enemies and bosses.** Crush comes 4 times in 152 ticks and matters every time, but Chill stretched its 22-tick cadence to 40 to 50 (pack 4). Inferno is the best boss move in the set: telegraphed, hits all, leaves Burn, and the party answers it (pack 7). The Silent One: Quiet ×9 that mostly says "lost", 150k HP, priests healing 9,100 that nobody prioritised for 275 ticks (pack 10). Ogres feel like elites: slow telegraphed swings for 823 to 1,039 and a stunning Flatten (packs 3, 11, 12). Ghoul Pit and Leech Den are "elites" of four or five fat minions with no elite unit; Raise never showed. Minions act before dying (99 to 100%, summary). Regions read through one gimmick each. Felt like nothing: Bandits at 216 a hit (pack 1), Wolves at 35 a bite (pack 4), Fire Imp B, Hellhound (153 dealt an appearance, summary).

**11. Drama.** Close calls that paid off: Cleric at 30 HP then full (pack 9 t7.4 to 8.9), Geomancer at 35 (pack 10 t17.4), the party under half after Inferno then a 12-tick kill (pack 7). Close calls that cheated: 102 → 6,500 and 181 → 6,500 in a tick (pack 10). One-shots: 5,807 vs 2,000 (pack 4), 1,935 at t4 on a 2,500 Monk (pack 8), 1,533 vs 1,354 (pack 12). Decided in the first ten ticks: packs 2, 6 (and 12 by t15.7). Went on long after: packs 4 (93 ticks), 10 (250), 12 (52), 9 (34), 3 (25).

## 12. The ranked list

What looked wrong, worst first:

1. **Boss fights that run 100 to 250 ticks past their decision.** Pack 10 t106 to 355 (one Vampire vs a 150k boss healed 9,100 ×4); pack 4 t59 to 152 (a Hexer plinking 498). Summary: act 3 bosses mean 155 ticks, range 46 to 355; act 2 range 17 to 240. Enrage at t201 is far too late.
2. **The Vampire's full heals from the brink.** Blood drain+ Lifesteal under Bloodlust: 102 → 6,500 (pack 10 t280.8), 181 → 6,500 (t319.7). Summary: the Vampire lasts longest of any class (down at t71 mean).
3. **Minion-wall "elites".** Four ghouls at 162,560 HP vs 10,874 dealt (pack 6); five leeches at 69,145 vs 38,992 (pack 9). No elite unit, the gimmick unseen. Summary: act 2 elites 57% won, 1.4 KOs a fight.
4. **Fights lost before they start.** Pack 2 (Bramble knight at 0 HP, Ranger at 368, dead t5.8), pack 12 (Valkyrie at 750, dead t15.7), pack 4 (Stormcaller 800 HP / 30 MP). A quarter of the packs were formalities for one or two members.
5. **Chill as a lock.** Cap 12 from one Frost lance (packs 7, 9); held at 12 for 100 ticks by a Hexer's basics (pack 4 t53.8 to 152), stretching Crush to every 40 to 50 ticks and starving Shock. Summary: 71 fights, peak 8 mean.
6. **Boss crits one-shotting squishies through Weaken.** 5,807 vs 2,000 while weakened (pack 4 t151.5); Flatten 1,533 vs 1,354 (pack 12); Wraith 1,935 on a 2,500 Monk at t4 (pack 8); Leech crit 1,539 (pack 9 t3.8). Summary: Ogre Chief 8,914 dealt an appearance, 41% won.
7. **Blood gift.** Kills its caster (pack 3 t50.3: 827 HP pays 800) and heals allies at 62 to 76% (pack 3 t21.9; pack 8 t15.4, t35.6). Summary: 1.13 casts a fight over 106 fights, always at a fifth of max HP.
8. **Third slots and dead passives.** Hoplite: zero casts in 40 ticks (pack 8); Spear thrust 0.77 casts first at t39, Aspis 0.82, Sanctuary 0.84 (after the elite died, pack 3), Raise 0.05, Valhalla 0.14, Pyre 0.13, Tremor 0.33, Fireball 0.39 (summary). Judgement never fired in three Paladin packs; Bedrock off all fight (pack 10); Einherjar needs a revive that comes 0.14 times a fight.
9. **Sunder and Weaken read as nothing.** Sunder at cap 20 reapplied seven times (pack 4 t44 to 148), 0 damage in 123 fights; Weaken ~12% (pack 8), 72 fights. The Hexer's kit is these two: 100% basic damage (pack 4), 45 KOs per 100 fights (summary, the highest).
10. **Passengers: Hexer, Hoplite, Bard.** Pack 4 (498 a hit, 93 ticks alone), pack 8 (one swing, 13%), pack 1 (15%, and Anthem made the Cleric the DPS at Might 15: 1,915 a hit). Summary shares 23%, 21%, 20%; 0% ability damage for the Bard and Hexer.
11. **Aggro draws that pile on the squishy.** Four of four first draws on the lowest-Aggro Vampire, dead at t8.7 as the Knight's Taunt lands (pack 6); three of seven bites on the Pyromancer, dead t16.4 (pack 5). Summary: Pyromancer down at t24 mean, Bard t26.
12. **Absurd small numbers.** Knight basics for 39 on a boss (pack 7 ×4); Wolf bites for 35 (pack 4); the Knight's 575 vs 13,829-HP leeches (pack 9). Summary: Knight 13% share, 81% basic.
13. **Burn's tail.** Fifteen pulses under 270 over 18 ticks (pack 11 t40 to 58), fourteen under 300 (pack 12 t39 to 55): a single-cast Burn is one good pulse and thirty lines of noise.
14. **Frostweaver stacking.** WEAK × Brittle × Berserker torc: Frost lance 8,361 and a 39-tick boss fight (pack 7); Frost lance+ 5,934 (pack 9). Summary: 42% share, 56% from abilities, the highest ability share of any class.
15. **Silence ×9 and wasted Clarity.** Quiet covered 41% of pack 10 and read as three "lost" lines; Stillness+ spent 500 MP twice on a Knight drained to 0 on the next bite (pack 9 t29.7, t61); Refrain fed a full Cleric (pack 1).

What looked right:

- Wind-ups pay off and get played against: Crush and the Aegis block (pack 4 t53.2), Inferno answered by Renew+ and Cure+ (pack 7), "Smite waits" and "Earthen shell waits" under Flatten (packs 3, 11), "Paired guard is lost" under Mute (pack 8).
- The Monk: three abilities that all fire, a tempo, a Burn that ramps to 1,100+ pulses, a stun that holds a boss (packs 3, 8, 10).
- Shock and Bleed have triggers a player can see: 2,015 when the ogre swings (pack 12), 1,248 when the Ogre is hit (pack 11).
- Regular fights sit at 40 to 46 ticks and open as a build, not a volley (packs 1, 5, 8; summary act 1 mean 41).
- Ogres as elites: slow, telegraphed, heavy, with one stunning move (packs 3, 11, 12).
- Pack 7's shape: a boss move that hurts everyone, a healer that strips it, a burst that ends it.
- Guard's cut and Shield's soaks are legible (packs 4, 6, 7, 9); Last Rites showed at 30 HP (pack 9).
- Minions act before they die (99 to 100%, summary), and die to one big hit, as minions should.

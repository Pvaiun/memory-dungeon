# Step 5f tuning trail

One entry a round. The sheet is `npm run balance -- --trios 80 --seeds 2`, knobs frozen at 5e's values throughout the economy rounds; win rates are context. Three numbers are campaign / free play / rogue. The sheet after each round is saved beside this file (`sheet-a.md`, `sheet-b.md`, …).

1. **Phase A: pacing, mana, statuses, resistances** (design §2 to §5, as written; every number in `scale.ts` and `vocabulary.ts` with a comment naming this step; every ability on the cost ladder 200 / 350 / 600 / 900 with its tempo word and hit word in `classes.ts`; elites and bosses at 15 and 25 resistance; the HP 3s reverted). Result (`sheet-a.md`, 70 flags): **the statuses moved as meant** (Burn 9% → 21% of its appliers' damage, Shock 20% → 29%, Bleed 12% → 11%; the Pyromancer's statuses 23% → 44% of its damage, the Stormcaller's 20% → 28%, the Monk's 9% → 25%, the Valkyrie's 13% → 28%; Chill 5.2 stacks an application against a cap of 12; Burn 11.5 stacks an application, 2,040 damage each). **Fights didn't lengthen:** a regular fight 32 / 26 / 35 ticks (were 31 / 24 / 36), a boss fight 44 / 50 / 57 (were 32 / 45 / 54 at the new knobs): swings came half again as slowly but half again as big, and the shorter cooldowns added casts, so damage a tick was unchanged. Casts are 39% of actions (were 34%). Bosses' signature moves 0.5 to 2.6 a fight (the Ogre Chief's Crush 0.46 / 0.78 / 0.54: a 10-tick wind-up on a 34-tick cooldown in a 30- to 44-tick fight). **Mana went the wrong way:** the party regained 115% of what it spent (was 102%); the Knight is short of MP 8% of its time, the Vampire 27% (a 200 Blood drain every 16 ticks on a 2,000 pool), every caster 0% (they die at ticks 14 to 18, before they can run dry). The Vampire +24 / +37 / +13.5 with Lifesteal back at 30 (its Blood gift isn't in yet); the Monk +6 / +27 / +8.5 before its cut; the Bard −11, Hexer −7, Paladin −7 (0.8 casts a fight: a slow kit in a 32-tick fight), Hoplite −7 (Spear thrust on a medium cooldown with a long wind-up).

2. **Round 2: damage a tick cut by a quarter on both sides, the mana ladder a rung dearer at 2% regen, and the phase B kits** (design §6: the Knight's Vanguard and Stand fast, the Cleric's Last rites, the Bard's Anthem and Chorus, the Hexer's Malison, Unmake and Sunder, Absolve that heals, the Vampire's Blood gift, the Monk's cut, the Hoplite's Attack 4 and Aggro 5, the aggro words; engine 0.13 for Unmake's `casting` condition and the Clarity givers' `mp_share`). `SWING_REF` 10 → 14, the hit words three quarters (0.19 / 0.32 / 0.56 / 0.94), the foes' swings 1.1 × the old words (were 1.5 ×), the ladder 250 / 400 / 650 / 1,000. **Read on a quarter sample** (24 trios, one seed: ±5 points on a class) because the full sheet crashed on one fight in 200,000 (below): a regular fight **42 / 29 / 41 ticks** (target 45), a boss fight **60 / 71 / 80** (target 80), casts 41% of actions, the party spending 3,214 MP a fight and regaining 2,591. **Mana binds the wrong units:** the Knight is short 33% of its time, the Vampire 50%, the Bramble knight 27%, the Hoplite 28%, the Monk 26%, every caster 0% to 3% (a 2,000 pool against a 5,000 one on the same ladder; the casters die at ticks 15 to 18 besides). The kits: the Bard −12 → **−1 / −4 / −3** (3.8 casts a fight, statuses 34% of its damage); the Hexer's statuses **74%** of its damage (Malison) at −11 / −11 / 0; the Pyromancer's **58%**; the Monk +10 / +20 / +5 → −1 / +14 / +5; the Paladin −7 → −4 / +7 / −1.5 (Absolve heals); the Stormcaller +8 → −2; **the Vampire +38 / +15 / +6.5** (Lifesteal 30 back, Blood gift in, knocked out at tick 83 to 152: never); **the Knight +17 / +5 / +1** (a free 2,000 Shield every fight, in a mode where HP carries between fights); **the Assassin −26 / −45 / −15** (HP 2 again, dead at tick 20, when its first Vanish comes); the Valkyrie +15 / +17 / +6 with Gale spear's Shock 44% of its damage (three touches, each three times "a few" stacks).

   **The crash, and a rule that never held.** One fight in the formation probe crashed: a Last stand Martyr (max HP −1,500 at the 1.5 step) wearing Glass heart (−500) on the HP-2 Assassin reached **0 max HP**, a Raise brought it back at 0 HP, and Cure's "lowest share of HP" divided by zero. The registry floors max HP at 1 and Armor at 0, but `finalValue` applied a floor only when the stat also had a cap (the missing cap fell back to the unfloored value, which the min then kept): **Sunder has been taking Armor below zero all along** (the director's question in the list: "Is it eventually boosting damage past 0 armor? It shouldn't"; it was), and HP could reach zero. Fixed in the engine (0.13), max HP floored at half a point in the registry, and the HP-share value guarded.

3. **Round 3: the mana ladder in two tiers, the floor fixed, three kit fixes** (skills 150 / 250 / 400 / 600, spells 300 / 500 / 800 / 1,200, MP regen 2.5%; Vanguard 0.25; Vanish short with Hidden 14; Gale spear's Shock halved). Statuses: Burn **34%** of its appliers' damage, Shock **35%**, Bleed 17%; by class the Hexer 77%, the Pyromancer 58%, the Stormcaller 39%, the Monk 36%, the Valkyrie 30%, the Bramble knight 50% with its Thorns, the Ranger 24%. The Bard −2 / +2 / −2, the Hexer −5 / −3 / +1.5, the Paladin +4 / +7 / +1, the Stormcaller 0 / −5 / +0.5. **Still off:** the casters die at ticks 17 to 23, before the Knight's first Stand fast (tick 18 on a medium cooldown), so they never run short of MP (0% to 1%; the Vampire 51%); the Assassin, Hidden 85% of a fight on a short Vanish, leaves its two allies every hit (−20 in the campaign, −5 in the rogue); the Ogre Chief casts Crush 0.6 to 0.7 times a fight (its fight is the shortest); the formations' spread widened with the longer fights (Caravan +12.6, Tortoise +10.6, Champion +10.3, Escort +9.1, Last stand −10.3 against Neutral); Glass heart −2.7 against an empty slot, Mist claws −8 against the slot's mean, the Herald's horn +8 (Regen is 24 ticks again). 4. **Round 4: Stand fast short, Vanguard 0.2, Vanish medium with Hidden 14, Brittle 12% a stack, Blood drain on the skills tier, Crush short, damage a tick nine tenths again, the formation step 1.25 with the Martyr at −1 HP step and Caravan's Regen 8.** Result (`sheet-d.md`, 52 flags): a regular fight **41 / 29 / 40** ticks, a boss fight **56 / 63 / 85**; casts 44% of actions. **The Knight taunts from tick 6:** Taunt applications 0.93 a fight (were 0.6), the Knight takes 2.26× an average unit's damage per tick, and the squishy classes die half as often as in 5e (the Pyromancer knocked out in 25 of 100 campaign fights, was 47; the Hexer 29, was 60; the Bard 22, was 51; the Assassin 31, was 41), a few ticks later each (the Cleric at tick 31, was 27). **The Ogre Chief casts Crush about once a fight** (0.99 / 1.22 / 1.15, was 0.6); the other bosses 1.0 to 2.4 in the two played modes and 1.0 to 1.5 in free play. Statuses: Burn **37%**, Shock **38%**, Bleed 18% of their appliers' damage; the Pyromancer's statuses 62% of its damage, the Hexer's 80%, the Stormcaller's 44%, the Monk's 39%, the Valkyrie's 32%, the Ranger's 28%. **The rogue holds:** every class within ±6.2 (the Vampire +6.2, the Assassin −4.2, the Frostweaver −3.4, the Cleric −3.7, the rest within ±3). **The campaign spreads toward sustain:** the Vampire **+33.5** (knocked out 9 times in 100 fights; the next fewest 16), the Knight +14, the Valkyrie +13.5, the Bramble knight +13, the Monk +8; the Assassin −23, the Frostweaver −19, the Geomancer −10, the Hoplite −9, the Pyromancer −9, the Ranger −8. HP carries across its six fights and a unit that dies once stays dead to the boss unless the campfire raises it. **Mana doesn't bind the casters yet:** Stand fast's Clarity every 16 ticks (0.86 applications a fight, were 0.02) and Refrain made the party regain 100% of what it spends again; every caster is short 0% to 1% of its time, the Vampire 34%, and the Knight casts 7 times a regular fight. Formations: Caravan **+12** (its Quartermaster's MP regen slot is worth a whole unit's regen now that mana binds), Tortoise +8.5, Champion +8, Blitz +7.8, Escort +7.2, Last stand −4.3. Pyre 0.03 casts a regular fight (a long cooldown with a 10-tick wind-up on a caster that lives 19 ticks), Static charge 0.06.

5. **Round 5: MP regen 1.5%, Clarity 3% a pulse, Fortify medium, Pyre medium, Static charge medium and quick, the bosses' blows and spells on short cooldowns, Caravan's Quartermaster +1 and its Regen 6.** Pushed; its sheet was stopped half-way when the feel read below arrived, since round 6 changes what it would have measured.

   **The feel read** (`reads-round4.md`, an agent that watched round 4's fights tick by tick: campaign nodes 1 to 3, a rogue act, free play, two custom parties). What the sheet's averages hid: **Burn is a front-loaded nuke, not a ramp.** A Firebolt lands 88 stacks (three times "several" times the Pyromancer's 2.5, from Magic 5 and a +2 staff), 5,265 damage on its first pulse, a quarter gone each pulse after; the Pyromancer killed the campaign's Ogre Chief in 14 ticks, before Crush landed, and Fireball's Burn dropped four Wolves on one tick. The sheet's "12 stacks an application" averaged it with Kindling's and the Snakes'. **Anthem's Might at the cap (20 stacks, +200%) refreshed every 14 ticks tripled an ally's basic attacks for the whole fight** (a Hexer's swing 665 → 1,995), and the random target was the Bard itself four times of eight. **Fights have two waves, not a pulse:** every party ability fires once between ticks 5 and 9, then nothing from the party until about 18 while the foes poke the Knight's Shield; the third slots land at 22 to 27; Pyre never fired. Cast times read now (Fire arrow and Fireball six ticks, Raise and Crush ten). **Bosses barely act:** the campaign's Ogre Chief landed Crush once in three fights (dead mid-wind-up twice, to the Burn), and the Hexer's Unmake, waiting for a cast, interrupted every signature move it reached (three of three); only the untuned free-play Chief (four Crushes) and the Reaper's Doom felt like something to play against. **Mana never binds** in a party with the Knight and the Bard (a Hexer at 5,000 of 5,000 after four Enfeebles). Vanish on a medium cooldown arrives at tick 22, after the Assassin died at 20 in three of eight fights. Never perceptible in a log: Chill (a Hexer's two or three stacks thaw before its next swing), Haste, Sunder, Clarity, and Caravan's Regen on full-HP units ("healed for 0" ten times a fight). Two tool bugs: campaign-play's campfire text and a debug line in the dungeon tool's player output.

**1. Classes**

**Staples**  
- **Warrior**: The front-line sword-and-board fighter.  
  Swings hard physical attacks, absorbs hits with high armor, and occasionally taunts or shields allies. Passive: gains temporary armor or damage reduction after taking hits. Needs: Taunt, Guard, Armor Up.  
- **Rogue**: The quick dual-wielder or dagger specialist.  
  Attacks fast, lands many small hits or backstabs, and stacks critical chance or bleed. Passive: higher crit or extra damage on targets already hurt. Needs: Bleed, Crit Up, Stealth.  
- **Mage**: The pure elemental caster.  
  Channels long-cast high-damage spells of one or two elements, spends MP freely. Passive: MP regen or spell power rises as the fight goes on. Needs: Burn/Freeze/Shock, Spell Power.  
- **Cleric / Healer**: The support priest.  
  Keeps the party alive with targeted or group heals, cleanses, and occasional shields. Passive: heals for more when allies are low or after they take damage. Needs: Regen, Cleanse, Barrier.  
- **Paladin**: The holy tank-healer hybrid.  
  Mixes solid physical attacks with self-heals or party-wide protection. Passive: heals or gains armor when allies fall below half health. Needs: Holy Light, Aegis, Smite.  
- **Archer / Ranger**: The steady ranged physical attacker.  
  Fires rapid single-target or multi-hit shots, sometimes marks a target for extra damage. Passive: attack speed or damage rises against the same foe. Needs: Mark, Focus, Pierce.  
- **Berserker**: The reckless high-damage brute.  
  Hits harder the lower its own HP drops, ignores defense in exchange for raw power. Passive: Attack and Attack Speed scale with missing HP. Needs: Rage, Bloodlust, Reckless.  
- **Knight**: The disciplined shield specialist.  
  Intercepts damage, raises party defense, and counters when struck. Passive: allies near it take less damage or it gains Armor when blocking. Needs: Cover, Counter, Fortify.  
- **Sorcerer**: The multi-element or status-focused caster.  
  Applies lingering elemental effects and debuffs while dealing moderate damage. Passive: statuses last longer or spread. Needs: Poison, Slow, Elemental Affinity.  
- **Monk**: The unarmed or staff martial artist.  
  Chains rapid strikes that build combo or chi, then expends it for big hits or self-buffs. Passive: every few hits grants a free action or temporary Attack Speed. Needs: Combo, Chi, Focus Strike.  

---

- **Necromancer**: Raises temporary undead helpers or drains life from foes.  
  Passive: gains MP or a short-lived minion when something dies. Needs: Drain, Summon, Decay.  
- **Bard**: Sings party-wide buffs or enemy debuffs on a timer.  
  Passive: songs refresh or grow stronger the longer they play. Needs: Song of Power, Discord, Harmony.  
- **Assassin**: High single-target burst that vanishes after a kill.  
  Passive: next attack is guaranteed crit after a knockout or from stealth. Needs: Ambush, Execute, Vanish.  
- **Druid**: Shifts forms or calls nature effects that heal and harm.  
  Passive: regenerates HP/MP in nature form or gains resistance after shifting. Needs: Shapeshift, Thorns, Bloom.  
- **Warlock**: Sacrifices own HP or allies for powerful dark spells.  
  Passive: damage rises as its own HP falls or when it spends life. Needs: Life Tap, Curse, Shadow.  
- **Samurai**: Precise high-crit physical attacker that builds focus.  
  Passive: next ability is free or stronger after a perfect hit streak. Needs: Focus, Iaido, Resolve.  
- **Alchemist**: Throws potions that apply unique status mixes.  
  Passive: potions have a chance to trigger twice or leave residue. Needs: Toxin, Catalyst, Volatile.  
- **Summoner**: Calls a persistent pet that fights alongside the party.  
  Passive: pet inherits some of the summoner's stats or grows stronger over time. Needs: Bond, Command, Familiar.  
- **Gunslinger**: Rapid-fire piercing or explosive shots with reload windows.  
  Passive: Attack Speed spikes after reloading or landing consecutive hits. Needs: Reload, Overheat, Bullet Time.  
- **Geomancer**: Manipulates terrain-like elemental fields that linger.  
  Passive: fields persist and empower later casts of the same element. Needs: Quake, Avalanche, Resonance.  
- **Time Mage**: Speeds allies or slows enemies with temporal effects.  
  Passive: cooldown reduction or haste stacks on the whole party. Needs: Haste, Slow, Chrono.  
- **Vampire**: Drains life on hit and grows stronger while fed.  
  Passive: basic attacks restore HP; excess healing becomes temporary HP. Needs: Bloodlust, Drain, Night.  
- **Engineer**: Deploys short-lived turrets or gadgets that attack or shield.  
  Passive: gadgets last longer or gain extra charges as the fight continues. Needs: Deploy, Overclock, Shield Generator.  
- **Illusionist**: Creates decoys that draw fire or confuse targeting.  
  Passive: chance to redirect an attack to a false image. Needs: Mirror Image, Confuse, Mirage.  
- **Reaper**: Harvests stacks of doom that explode for massive damage.  
  Passive: gains a stack whenever anything dies; spends them for big hits. Needs: Soul Harvest, Doom, Scythe.  

**2. Statuses a player would see**

**Staples**  
- **Poison** (debuff): Deals damage over time, ignores armor.  
- **Burn** (debuff): Deals fire damage over time, may lower Armor.  
- **Stun** (debuff): Stops all actions for a short time and cancels wind-ups.  
- **Regen** (buff): Restores HP steadily over time.  
- **Shield / Barrier** (buff): Absorbs a set amount of damage before HP is touched.  

---

- **Bleed** (debuff): Physical damage over time that stacks with each hit.  
- **Freeze** (debuff): Greatly slows Attack Speed and may stop movement of actions.  
- **Haste** (buff): Raises Attack Speed and reduces cooldowns.  
- **Slow** (debuff): Lowers Attack Speed and lengthens cast times.  
- **Taunt** (debuff on foe / effect): Forces enemies to target this unit.  
- **Silence** (debuff): Prevents ability use while basic attacks still work.  
- **Blind** (debuff): Greatly raises chance to miss basic attacks.  
- **Fortify** (buff): Raises Armor or reduces all incoming physical damage.  
- **Empower** (buff): Increases damage of the next few attacks or abilities.  
- **Doom / Mark** (debuff): Timed effect that explodes for heavy damage if not cleansed.  
- **Thorns** (buff): Reflects a portion of physical damage back to the attacker.  
- **Stealth** (buff): Lowers chance of being targeted until the unit acts.  
- **Curse** (debuff): Lowers one or more resistances or max HP for the duration.  
- **Berserk** (buff/debuff mix): Raises Attack and Attack Speed but also damage taken.  
- **Cleanse-ready / Immunity** (buff): Next few debuffs are ignored or removed instantly.  

**3. Special stats**

**Staples**  
- **Threat / Aggro**: How likely enemies are to choose this unit as a target.  
- **Lifesteal**: Percentage of damage dealt restored as HP.  
- **Block Chance**: Chance to negate or heavily reduce an incoming physical hit.  
- **Dodge / Evasion**: Already core, but special variants can raise it temporarily or against specific types.  
- **Accuracy**: Chance to land hits against high-Evasion targets.  

---

- **Combo / Chi**: Builds with successful hits; spent to empower the next ability.  
- **Focus / Resolve**: Accumulates over time or on crits; spent for guaranteed big hits.  
- **Soul / Essence**: Gained on enemy death; used to fuel unique abilities or summons.  
- **Overheat**: Builds with rapid fire or spells; too much applies a self-debuff.  
- **Blood**: Gained by dealing or taking damage; spent for vampiric or rage effects.  
- **Charge**: Fills while idle or on timers; empties into a powerful once-per-fight action.  
- **Bond**: Shared with a pet or ally; raises both units' power while both live.  
- **Resonance**: Amplifies the next elemental spell of a matching type.  
- **Mark Count**: Number of times a target has been marked; multiplies damage taken.  
- **Stacks of Rage**: Temporary Attack and Attack Speed that decay if not refreshed by hits.  
- **Shield Charges**: Number of times a barrier can refresh before vanishing.  
- **Echo**: Chance that an ability or attack repeats at reduced power.  
- **Decay**: Accelerates the remaining duration of all statuses on the target.  
- **Presence**: Passive aura radius that grants small bonuses to nearby allies (still non-positional numerically).  
- **Execution Threshold**: Extra damage multiplier against targets below a set HP percentage.  

**4. Elements**

- **Fire**: Aggressive, lingering burn, strong against nature and ice.  
- **Ice / Frost**: Slowing, freezing, strong against fire and water.  
- **Lightning / Thunder**: Fast, chain-prone, strong against water and metal.  
- **Earth / Stone**: Heavy, armor-piercing or defensive, strong against lightning.  
- **Wind / Air**: Mobile, multi-hit or knockback feel, strong against earth.  
- **Holy / Light**: Cleansing, extra effective against dark or undead.  
- **Dark / Shadow**: Draining, curse-linked, strong against holy and light.  
- **Nature / Poison**: Regenerative or toxic, strong against earth and water.

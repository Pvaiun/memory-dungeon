### 1. Classes

### Staples

1. Warrior — The dependable frontline damage dealer.

Uses powerful physical attacks and can weaken an enemy before the party focuses it. Passive: gains Might the first time its HP falls below half.

Statuses: Might, Vulnerable. Special stats: None.

2. Knight — The armoured protector.

Draws attacks away from fragile allies and shields the party with Barrier. Passive: begins every fight with Barrier.

Statuses: Barrier, Taunted. Special stats: None.

3. Mage — The elemental spellcaster.

Deals heavy magical damage to individual enemies or the entire opposing party. Passive: its first spell each fight hits every enemy.

Statuses: Burn, Stun. Special stats: None.

4. Cleric — The dedicated healer.

Restores HP, removes debuffs and grants regeneration. Passive: its healing is stronger than that of other classes.

Statuses: Regeneration, Barrier. Special stats: Healing Power.

5. Thief — The evasive opportunist.

Makes rapid physical attacks, steals MP and exploits distracted enemies. Passive: gains Might briefly after dodging an attack.

Statuses: Might, Bleeding. Special stats: Evasion, Mana Leech.

6. Archer — The focused single-target hunter.

Marks priority targets and excels at finishing enemies rather than spreading damage around. Passive: deals bonus critical damage to Marked enemies.

Statuses: Marked. Special stats: Accuracy, Critical Damage.

7. Paladin — The holy warrior who protects through strength.

Combines physical damage with healing and defensive abilities. Passive: grants Barrier to the first ally whose HP falls below half.

Statuses: Barrier, Might. Special stats: Healing Power.

8. Berserker — The reckless bruiser who gets stronger when wounded.

Trades protection for powerful attacks and can inflict Bleeding. Passive: gains Enraged when its HP falls below half.

Statuses: Enraged, Bleeding. Special stats: Lifesteal.

9. Monk — The disciplined fighter who thrives on repeated attacks.

Uses rapid strikes and self-healing to sustain a prolonged fight. Passive: every few successful hits restore some HP.

Statuses: Might, Regeneration. Special stats: Evasion.

10. Bard — The support who controls the party's tempo.

Improves allies' attack speed and weakens enemies, trading personal damage for team-wide advantages. Passive: grants Haste to the party at the start of combat.

Statuses: Haste, Weakened. Special stats: Cast Speed.

### Less common classes

11. Assassin — The executioner who specialises in vulnerable targets.

Combines precise physical hits with poison and bleeding effects. Passive: gains bonus critical damage against enemies below half HP.

Statuses: Poison, Bleeding, Marked. Special stats: Accuracy, Critical Damage, Status Chance.

12. Necromancer — The dark caster who turns attrition into an advantage.

Curses enemies with persistent damage and can weaken their defences. Passive: restores MP whenever an enemy is knocked out.

Statuses: Poison, Doomed. Special stats: Mana Leech, Debuff Duration.

13. Druid — The nature healer who sustains the party over time.

Combines regeneration, healing and nature-themed damage. Passive: grants Regeneration to the ally with the lowest HP at the start of combat.

Statuses: Regeneration, Poison. Special stats: Healing Power.

14. Alchemist — The experimental support who weaponises concoctions.

Uses bombs for magical area damage and potions to heal, shield or inflict status effects. Passive: healing potions also remove a debuff.

Statuses: Barrier, Burn, Poison. Special stats: Status Chance.

15. Spellblade — The enchanted swordsman who combines physical and magical attacks.

Adds elemental damage to weapon strikes and exploits enemy elemental weaknesses. Passive: basic attacks deal an additional hit of its chosen element.

Statuses: Burn, Vulnerable. Special stats: None.

16. Duelist — The counterattacker who punishes aggression.

Focuses on one opponent, using counters and critical hits rather than broad attacks. Passive: counterattacks after being hit by an enemy it has Taunted.

Statuses: Taunted, Might. Special stats: Counter Chance, Critical Damage.

17. Time Mage — The temporal specialist who manipulates action timing.

Accelerates allies and interrupts enemies with Stun. Passive: gains additional Cast Speed after using an ability.

Statuses: Haste, Stun. Special stats: Cast Speed, Debuff Duration.

18. Witch — The hexer who compounds pressure through debuffs.

Applies lasting curses that weaken enemies and threaten them with delayed damage. Passive: debuffs it applies last longer.

Statuses: Doomed, Weakened. Special stats: Status Chance, Debuff Duration.

19. Lancer — The armour-piercing physical specialist.

Delivers heavy, focused strikes that bypass some of the target's Armor. Passive: basic attacks benefit from increased Armor Penetration.

Statuses: Vulnerable. Special stats: Armor Penetration.

20. Templar — The warder trained to resist hostile effects.

Protects allies from debuffs, cleanses harmful statuses and uses defensive abilities to prolong fights. Passive: has increased resistance to debuffs.

Statuses: Barrier, Regeneration. Special stats: Status Resistance.

21. Executioner — The finisher who specialises in wounded enemies.

Targets the weakest foe and deals increasingly dangerous attacks as its target loses HP. Passive: gains bonus damage against enemies below half HP.

Statuses: Marked. Special stats: Finisher, Accuracy.

22. Plague Doctor — The battlefield physician who makes illness a weapon.

Inflicts persistent poison and bleeding while providing limited healing. Passive: basic attacks apply Poison.

Statuses: Poison, Bleeding, Regeneration. Special stats: Poison Coating, Status Chance.

23. Oracle — The seer who identifies threats before they become dangerous.

Marks priority enemies and protects allies likely to come under attack. Passive: marks the enemy with the highest Attack at the start of combat.

Statuses: Marked, Barrier, Vulnerable. Special stats: None.

24. Reaper — The soul-harvester who grows stronger as enemies fall.

Drains life from wounded targets and converts successful takedowns into momentum. Passive: restores HP when it knocks out an enemy.

Statuses: Doomed, Enraged. Special stats: Lifesteal, Finisher.

25. Beastmaster — The hunter who fights alongside a bonded beast.

Basic attacks include an additional small physical strike, while abilities exploit enemies suffering from Bleeding or Marked. Passive: the beast's extra strike deals bonus damage against those targets.

Statuses: Marked, Bleeding. Special stats: None.

### 2. Statuses a player would see

### Staples

| Status       | Type   | Effect                                            |
| ------------ | ------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Haste        | Buff   | Increases Attack Speed.                           |
| Might        | Buff   | Increases Attack.                                 |
| Regeneration | Buff   | Restores HP periodically.                         |
| Barrier      | Buff   | Absorbs incoming damage before it reaches HP.     |
| Poison       | Debuff | Deals damage periodically and can stack.          |
| Stun         | Debuff | Prevents actions and cancels the current wind-up. |

### Less common statuses

| Status     | Type   | Effect                                                                                  |
| ---------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Burn       | Debuff | Deals periodic damage and reduces healing received.                                     |
| Bleeding   | Debuff | Deals damage whenever the affected unit starts an action.                               |
| Vulnerable | Debuff | Increases damage taken from all sources.                                                |
| Weakened   | Debuff | Reduces damage dealt.                                                                   |
| Taunted    | Debuff | Forces the affected unit to target the unit that applied Taunted, when possible.        |
| Silenced   | Debuff | Prevents ability use but allows basic attacks.                                          |
| Marked     | Debuff | Increases the affected unit's targeting priority, making it more likely to be attacked. |
| Enraged    | Buff   | Increases Attack but reduces Armor.                                                     |
| Doomed     | Debuff | Deals a large burst of damage when its duration expires.                                |

### 3. Special stats

### Staples

| Special stat    | Effect                                                                                   |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Accuracy        | Reduces the chance that physical attacks miss due to Evasion.                            |
| Evasion         | Gives physical attacks a chance to miss the unit.                                        |
| Critical Damage | Increases the damage dealt by critical hits.                                             |
| Lifesteal       | Restores HP based on damage dealt by the unit.                                           |
| Status Chance   | Increases the chance of successfully applying statuses when an effect can fail to apply. |

### Less common special stats

| Special stat      | Effect                                                                                           |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Counter Chance    | Gives the unit a chance to retaliate with an immediate counterattack when hit.                   |
| Status Resistance | Gives the unit a chance to resist incoming debuffs.                                              |
| Healing Power     | Increases healing the unit provides.                                                             |
| Mana Leech        | Restores MP based on damage dealt.                                                               |
| Thorns            | Deals damage to attackers when the unit is hit.                                                  |
| Cast Speed        | Reduces the time required to wind up actions.                                                    |
| Armor Penetration | Allows physical attacks to bypass some of the target's Armor.                                    |
| Finisher          | Increases damage dealt to enemies below a specified HP threshold.                                |
| Poison Coating    | While positive, adds Poison to the unit's basic attacks; its value determines the Poison damage. |
| Debuff Duration   | Increases the duration of debuffs applied by the unit.                                           |

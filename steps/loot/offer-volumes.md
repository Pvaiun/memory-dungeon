# Offer volumes, counted

From 40 runs of the simulated player on today's content at heat 0: all 40 reached act I, and 37 reached acts II and III. Each figure is the average number of offers per run, in that act.

| Act | Common | Uncommon | Rare | Legendary | Mythic |

| I | 10.0 (fights 8.3, shop 1.7) | 9.2 (fights 8.0, shop 1.2) | 8.2 (fights 6.6, chest 0.5, shop 1.2) | 7.0 (elites 4.7, fights 1.5, shop 0.6, chest 0.2) | 0.9 (boss) |

About 34 to 37 pieces are offered an act. Legendaries come close to rares, mostly because each elite offers three. "Fights" means regular fights; the elites' pick is its own source. The boss's mythic is counted in the act whose boss gives it.

## Roster and training

- **Roster size when a piece was offered** (party plus bench): 3.65 in act I, 5.18 in act II, 6.27 in act III.
- **Trainings** (a separate count over 30 runs): about 9 a run in act I, 12 in act II and 15 in act III, across the whole roster. A unit in a fight knows 1.5 abilities on average in act I and 2.9 in act II.

## How far to trust it

The volumes depend on the simulated player's path (how often it takes elites and shops) and its roster. A person's runs will differ by a few offers.

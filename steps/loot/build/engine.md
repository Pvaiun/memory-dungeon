# Building a card: what the engine gives you

For whoever writes a share's content. The engine support the cards need is built (schema 0.12, engine 0.21); `PRIMITIVES.md` is the full list of what content can say, generated from the schema, and it's the authority. This page is the map from the catalog's *Code* hints to those building blocks, and the patterns every share should write the same way. Scratch: it goes when the step closes.

## Where things are

- `content/core-set/pieces/kit.ts`: the helpers every share writes with. Read it whole before writing anything: `piece`, `pieceStatus`, `shelf`, `st`, `sp`, `critStep`, `own`, `swingStacks`, `state`, `marker`, `used`, `oneUse`, `count`, `countAt`, `give`, `lift`, `on`, `react`, `reactIf`, `atStart`, `atFirstStart`, `EV`, `TO`, `hits`, `against`, `whileIt`, `share`, `ofAmount`, `stacksOf`, `whileHp`, `whileHolding`, `onceBelow`, `TAUNTS_ALL_FIGHT`, `swingNow`, `NO_MARK`, `SO_OFTEN`, `FEW_TICKS`, `weaponHit`.
- `content/core-set/pieces/shared.ts`: the six counters (`cuts`, `heat`, `spite`, `wrath`, `crescendo`, `cascade`), `COUNTER` (their numbers), `CRESCENDO`, `OVERHEAL`, `marked`.
- `content/core-set/scale.ts`: every number's word. Use the constant (`STACKS`, `FLAT_STACKS`, `HIT`, `SPECIAL`, `COOLDOWN`, `STATUS`, `KIT`), never a copied value, where the catalog's *Numbers* line names one.
- `steps/loot/old/*.txt`: today's content, for a card that says "as today's X". Reuse the shape, in this page's patterns; the old helpers and several old rules are gone (below).
- `src/engine/combat/loot-h.test.ts`, `loot-w.test.ts`: each new building block in a small fight. The quickest way to see exactly what one does.
- `npx tsx steps/loot/check.ts <share>`: your share against its catalog: validator, cards and stats word for word, and the piece check's play. `--no-play` skips the play; `--only id,id` plays a few.

## Rules that changed under today's content

- **A status's `strike` field** makes an extra strike (kind `extra`: Double strike's, the only "extra strike") unless it says `kind: "bonus"`: a hit with its own name (a leap, a volley's arrow, Arc's second hit). A bonus strike carries nothing unless it says `carry: true` ("carrying what the swing carries": the weapon's attached statuses and the riders). It may `apply` statuses of its own (`swingStacks(...)` for stacks: the swinging unit is `self` there), wait on a test of its holder (`when`), and name a `tag`.
- **The `strike` effect** (a reaction's hit out of turn) is kind `bonus` by default, and takes the shape its striker's basic attacks have (Millstone's, Phalanx's boosts reach it). "Strikes with its weapon" is `{ op: "strike", carry: true, to }`. It also does: `of: "dealt"` (a second landing), `of: "over"` (a left-over), `amount` with `apply` (a blow of its own size, not from the weapon), `by` (another unit strikes), `split`, `seek`, `after`, `tag`.
- **A weapon's own `apply`** rides its basic attacks and extra strikes ("its basic attacks and extra strikes add..."), and a bonus hit only with `carry`.
- **A rider** is an `apply` (or `one`) to `other` in a reaction to the holder's own `hit_lands`. That is how "its basic attacks add a few X" is written (`react(EV.basic, give("burn", "other", own("few", "attack")))`), and "every hit its wearer lands adds" (`EV.hit`). The engine knows riders: a hit that carries (a `carry` strike, an extra strike under `echo`) is answered by the `kind: basic` riders alone; a bare hit (a second landing, a left-over) skips every rider; `borrow` and the `riders` effect read them. Mark an application `rider: false` when it's not something the swing carries (a share of a status put back, a held fire delivered).
- **`echo: true`** (Echo charm) now means: the holder's extra strikes carry what the swing carries (riders only).
- **A killing blow** is `EV.kill` (`ko`, `whose: foe`, `other: holder`, `tags: ["physical"]`): a hit that fells a foe. Thorns, damage sent back and a status's own damage aren't hits. `EV.fall` is any foe's fall.
- **`hp_lost` isn't how a card watches HP any more.** Use the HP lines: `hp_low` / `hp_high` with `line` (percent of max HP), which fire whatever moved the HP (a price included), as a fight starts and on a revive, and again only after the unit has been back on the other side. `whileHp(stateId, 50, true)` holds a state below half; `onceBelow(50, usedId, ...effects)` is "the first time each fight"; "whenever an ally drops below a third" is `react(on("hp_low", { whose: "team", line: 33 }), give("aegis", "owner"))`. `team` is the holder's side, the holder included ("an ally" on a card usually includes the wearer); `ally` is the others.
- **Counters** are `shared.ts`'s. A stack by itself does what the rules page's table says (Cuts, Spite and Cascade: nothing; Heat: speed; Wrath: the next basic attack, then spent; Crescendo: kept by the engine per ability). The card's own worth is written on the card's status: `boosts: hits(stacksOf("cuts", 2))` (2% a Cut), a live stat (`stats: [{ stat: "lifesteal", flat: stacksOf("cuts", 2), live: true }]`), `{ ratio, event: "raw", times: { stacks: "spite", of: "holder" } }`.
- **Marked** steers basic attacks and abilities aimed at a random foe (the engine's). A piece or a seat that Marks does it under `NO_MARK` ("while no foe is Marked"): `reactIf(EV.basic, NO_MARK, give("marked", "other"))`.
- **Hidden** never lands where it would leave the side with nobody in sight, and the last in slot order steps out if the one in sight falls (the engine's). Just apply `hidden`.
- **A chain of shatters** is damped by Chill's own `damp`: mark Chill that a shatter passes on to *another* foe with `passed: true` (`give("chill", to, stacks, { passed: true })`). Cold put back on the foe that burst isn't passed.

## The code-support numbers, as building blocks

1. **Marked steers abilities.** The engine's; nothing in content but `NO_MARK` on gear and seat Marks.
2. **A count of different afflictions.** A term for each: `{ ratio, when: { has: "burn" }, of: "target", times: { stat: "attack", of: "self" } }`; a count of debuffs is `count: "debuffs"`.
3. **A foe's next basic attack called at once.** `swingNow("other")`, or `swingNow(TO.everyFoe)`. It doesn't stack by nature.
4. **A blow before mitigation, and what the cover stopped.** In a reaction to `hit_taken`: `{ ratio: 0.5, event: "raw" }` (the blow as it was struck, before Defense), `{ ratio: 1, event: "stopped" }` (what the Shield soaked and the Aegis blocked). Sent back as `{ op: "damage", type: "none", amount, to: "other" }`.
5. **Damage carried past a kill.** A reaction to `EV.kill` (its `amount` is the left-over) with `{ op: "strike", of: "over", to }`. The left-over is tagged `bare`: "carries once" is `notTags: ["bare"]` on the kill; "and on again each time it kills" leaves that out. Two cards that carry the same left-over share it: give each reaction `split: "over"`. Split among several foes: `split: true` on the strike with `to: TO.everyFoe` (the fallen one is no longer among them).
6. **Damage from MP first; MP past full.** `soak: "mp"` on the status. MP past full is the `mp_over` event (its `amount`); MP an ally gives is `mp_gained` with `other: "ally"`. MP given in answer to `mp_over` is never sent on again.
7. **A cast called by a count; a swing that cuts a cooldown.** `count(...)` and `countAt(...)` for "every fifth"; `{ op: "recast", which: "first", paid: true }` casts the first-slotted ability whatever its cooldown; `{ op: "cooldown", which: "first", set: { times: 80 }, to: "holder" }` leaves four fifths of the wait.
8. **The opening, again.** `{ op: "restart", to }`: the `fight_start` reactions of the target's gear and seat statuses happen again, once an event. So write every "when each fight starts" as `atStart(...)` on the piece's or seat's own status, and a price or drawback of the opening as `atFirstStart(...)`.
9. **A status moved or laid elsewhere.** A fallen foe's Burn: `react(on("status_ended", { status: "burn", cause: ["ko"], whose: "foe" }), give("burn", to, ofAmount()))`, with `split: "fire"` on every reaction that moves a fallen foe's fire. A shatter's cold: `EV.shatter` carries the stacks held as `amount`; pass it on with `passed: true`. The wearer's own afflictions onto what it hits: `stacksOf` / `when: { has }` of the holder. A whole debuff handed on: `{ op: "pass", label: "debuff", except, to: "other" }`. Chill added to one foe added to all: a reaction to `status_gained` (`whose: "foe"`, `other: "holder"`, `notTags: ["area"]`, `repeat: false`) that gives `ofAmount()` to `TO.otherFoes("owner")` with `copy: true`.
10. **A cooldown cut by the foes it hit.** `react(EV.cast, { op: "cooldown", which: "event", set: { lessPct: { scale: [{ ratio: 10, count: "hit" }] } }, to: "holder" })`.
11. **Crescendo.** An ability change that counts the ability's own casts: `mods: [{ damage: 20, heal: 20, stacks: { pct: 20 }, times: CRESCENDO }]`, and for the price `mp: 40` in the same change (an HP price follows the cost by itself).
12. **Riders copied between units.** `borrow: { from: rule }` (the holder's basic attacks also carry that unit's riders); `{ op: "riders", plain: true, to: "other" }` (lay what the actor's swing carries on a unit); `carry: true` on a strike; `{ op: "one", of: [...], pick: "most", to: "other" }` (the status its target holds most of).
13. **A status a unit gives itself, copied.** `react(on("status_gained", { other: "holder", repeat: false }), { op: "share", to })`. The engine shares only a status laid at a moment (an icon with a duration, stacks or a block), and marks the copy.
14. **Healing capped, and healing past full.** `healCap: 50` on the status. Healing past full is the `overhealed` event (`whose`, `other` the healer, `status` when a status healed: `status: "lifesteal"`, the tag `pulse` for a Regen's). The order among claimants is by `OVERHEAL`: a piece that takes its wearer's own overhealing also lists `OVERHEAL.own` in the piece's `statuses`; one that takes what its holder gives lists `OVERHEAL.given` and tests `{ unit: "owner", where: { lacks: OVERHEAL.own } }`; the formation tests both (`{ unit: "other", where: { lacks: OVERHEAL.given }, absent: true }`). A pick that passes by an ally that can't be healed: `where: { canHeal: true }`. A heal that isn't passed on again: `final: true`.
15. **A counter one unit fills and another reads.** Give the stack to the other unit (`give("wrath", TO.seat("hammer"), 1)`); read another seat's stacks with `seat` on the scale (`{ ratio: 4, stacks: "spite", seat: "wall" }`). A count the party shares: each ally's own copy, fed by `whose: "team"` events.
16. **A blow landed a second time; a cast made twice.** `react(on("hit_lands", { notTags: ["bare"], ... }), { op: "strike", of: "dealt", to: "other" })`, with `share` for a part of it and `after` for a tick later. A cast twice: `{ op: "recast" }` in a reaction to `on("action_resolve", { kind: "ability", repeat: false })`; `paid: true` makes the second a cast of its own.
17. **Strikes on new triggers.** A foe that holds Shock acts: `on("status_triggered", { status: "shock", whose: "foe" })`, strike `to: "owner"`. Foes that have hit the wearer since its last swing: a per-source marker laid on `EV.struck`, a `strike` field aimed at its holders, lifted on `EV.swing`. A foe starts a wind-up: `on("action_start", { whose: "foe", kind: "ability" })` with `{ unit: "owner", where: { casting: true } }`. By an ally: `by` on the strike. Through wind-ups and recoveries: `tireless: true`.
18. **The party's whole Shield, the field's whole Shock.** `{ ratio, count: { side: "allies", stacks: "shield" } }`, `{ ratio, count: { side: "foes", stacks: "shock" } }`.
19. **Marks from new events.** On a crit: `reactIf(EV.crit, NO_MARK, give("marked", "other"))`. On a heal: `to: "assailant"` (the foe that last hit the healed ally, if it stands). On the lowest foe: a reaction to the fight's start and to `status_ended` of `marked` (`whose: "foe"`), under `NO_MARK`, giving `marked` to `TO.lowestHpFoe`.
20. **Costs in HP.** `mods: [{ hp: { pct: 50, share: true, floor: 10, instead: true } }]` is "costs HP in place of MP: half the share, never below a tenth" (`of: { does: [...] }` narrows it; no `floor` where the card only says the price never takes it below 1 HP). Paid by another: `payer: "source"` on a per-source status its giver lays on the others. A basic attack's price: `mods: [{ of: { basic: true }, pay: num }]`, read by the swing as `{ ratio: 4, cost: "hp" }` in the weapon's amount. An ally pays HP: the `hp_paid` event (`whose: "team"`, its `amount`). Damage from the MP a cast cost: `adds: [{ on: "ability", amount: { scale: [{ ratio: 1, cost: "mp" }] } }]`. "Pays for" (MP or HP): `on("action_resolve", { kind: "ability", paid: true })`. A heal as a fight is won: a reaction to `fight_won`.
21. **How an ability is cast.** No cooldown: `mods: [{ of: { first: true }, cooldown: -100 }]`. Every other cast with no wind-up: a one-use state with `mods: [{ cast: 0 }]`. A random order: `order: "random"`. Aim at a casting foe: `mods: [{ of: { single: true }, target: rule }]` (a change's rule that finds nobody leaves the ability its own). A longer wind-up: `wind: 50`. More damage: `damage: 50`. A longer cooldown: `cooldown: 100`.
22. **Small ones.** Two stats swapped: `swap: ["attack", "defense"]`. Every Shield another ally would gain sent to one unit: a per-source status laid on the others with `divert: { what: "shield", to: "source" }`. A status the wearer lays on itself: a pulse.
23. **Chains that end.** The shatter chain: `passed: true` (above). A repeat that's never repeated: `notTags: ["bare"]`, `repeat: false`, `copy: true`. A call that doesn't stack: `swingNow` by nature.

## Beyond the numbered list

- **Crit rules:** `crits: [{ when, set: 80 }]` (a set chance nothing raises), `{ when, chance: 50, cap: 80 }`, `{ times: 2, cap: 80 }`.
- **Extra damage on a hit:** `adds: [{ on: "basic" | "ability" | "physical", amount }]`, added before boosts; `target` is the unit hit (`stacksOf("burn", 25, "target")`). An Intensity status adds it for each stack (a heal's size carried to the next hit: a tally with `adds: [{ on: "physical", amount: 1 }]`, spent by `trigger: { on: EV.hit }, drop: "all"`). A weapon's own basic attack can take the term straight in its `weapon.amount`.
- **A boost by the stacks on the target:** `boosts: [{ tag: "basic", pct: stacksOf("shock", 5, "target") }]`.
- **Tests:** `topHp` (the most current HP on its side), `top: { stats: ["aggro"] }`, `top: { stats: ["attack"], others: true }` ("the other ally with the highest Attack", as a test of an event's unit), `canHeal`, `casting`, `acted`.
- **Event filters:** `wasBelow: 50` (the unit hit was below half before the hit), `ability: ["backstab"]` (on action events and ko), `does: ["damage"]`, `paid`, `counted` (not a free repeat), `line`, `tags` / `notTags` on hits, ko, heals and status_gained.
- **A count shown on the unit** ("every third basic attack"): `count(id, name, text, 3)`; the reaction that pays tests `countAt(id, 2)` and is listed before the one that adds a stack, so the third event itself pays and the count then empties itself. See `loot-w.test.ts` (the volley) and the engine's notes on `count` in `kit.ts`.
- **A beat:** a `pulse` on the piece's status (`every: SO_OFTEN`, or the card's own number), with `if` for "while any foe burns". "The first at once" or "the first beat halfway": the card's *Numbers* line says which.
- **Effects that spend:** `{ op: "spend", status, share | count, to }`. A knockout that isn't damage: `{ op: "fell", to }`.
- **One of several statuses:** `{ op: "one", of, count: 2, same: true, to }` (two different ones, the same for every target).
- **A rule a party holds once:** `party: true` on a formation's own status (one of its holders answers each event, the first standing in slot order).

## How to write an entry

- One piece: `S.add(piece({ name, kind, slot, rarity, effect, stats, statuses, weapon?, trade? }), [its statuses], "its Serves line, copied from the catalog")`. `effect` is the catalog's **Card**, word for word (a common has none; a trade piece's "A heavier swing." comes from `trade`, not from `effect`). `stats` are the catalog's **Stats**, through `st`, `sp`, `critStep`; a shape stat that counts no step (Attack speed −100 on a half-speed swing) is written raw beside them.
- A piece's status takes the piece's id; more statuses take the id with a suffix: `_on` a state, `_used` a once-a-fight marker, `_mark` a marker left on a foe, `_count` a count.
- A status's `text` is one plain sentence of what it does (the card's, shortened if long). Statuses a player sees as icons have short upper-case names.
- Statuses are universal: never give a duration on an application. Stacks from a piece read the stat the card names through `own()`; a seat's are flat (`FLAT_STACKS`).
- Where a card is unclear or can't be built as written, make the natural call, keep the card's words, and say so in your report: the item, what you built, why.

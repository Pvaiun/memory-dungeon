# Step 5d intent: a rogue mode

From the director's message in the session of Oct 26, 2026, after playing the campaign a lot. Not an interview: the items are their words, lightly cleaned, tagged with how firmly each was held. They asked for it to be built overnight (12 hours), so there was no read-back; correct anything here and the design follows.

## The ask

- [firm] "I've got a hunch that it wants to be a roguelite." "Build a third option in the menu, where you have journey and free play, and add a rogue mode where each run is a bespoke individual thing that you go through." On its own branch, branched from the campaign branch.
- [lean] "This is extremely simple, and you might find through simulating and referencing top roguelites that you find a more interesting way to make the design more interesting. And I encourage you to do so."
- [lean] "You've got 12 hours to simulate all you need to simulate and try to find the best version of this possible."

## What they imagine

- [lean] **Start:** "Start by selecting a class out of the ones that you own. You start with Cleric, Ranger and Knight, as always. And you begin your run."
- [lean] **The run:** "You face increasingly challenging dungeons that are generated from remixes of the different enemies and bosses that we have already created, trying to be thematically appropriate to the location that you are."
- [lean] **Rewards:** "I'll actually let you decide when you get the rewards, but basically the rewards are loot, new classes and formations."
- [lean] **Swaps:** "We're given the opportunity to swap classes at specific campfire points. But gear, we can swap whenever we want."
- [lean] **Gear:** "I suspect we're going to need to create a semi-procedural gear system that basically takes what we have and expands on it." "Right now gear is kind of meaningless, because without progression in the regular journey mode you just get all the gear and it's all flat upgrades or sidegrades. Having gear really matter, like during your run you'll unlock a legendary item that you're like, oh, I need this class for, I think would be a really good system." Art: "just use recolors of the art for the gear or whatever."
- [firm] **Meta:** "When you beat a run, you unlock the classes that you beat it with as starting classes."
- [lean] **Why it fits:** "There's a lot of [riding] classes, which can lead to interesting builds. There's a lot of gear." "Formations play exceptionally well into that, as things that you get during the game." "The whole thing is balanced around attrition. And I think attrition is kind of the nature of a roguelite."
- [lean] **Structure:** "If you want to have the ability to choose which of the next encounters you're going to do, I would choose to follow Slay the Spire's structure over anyone else's."
- [lean] **Shop:** "A Slay the Spire style store and a currency system for loot, classes, formations, whatever have you, would be one of the best fits for our game, because there's a lot of variance, and we want to present a random buffet to the players, but we need to give them the opportunity to pick what feels right for them."
- [idea] **Levels:** "Don't hesitate to include some kind of character level up system if you think it's important, though I doubt it is, with gear being modified."
- [idea] **Abilities:** "Don't hesitate to unlock abilities for the units. I think there's a lot of potential in that too: not all abilities are available from the start and you need to unlock them."

## Left to the designer

The run's shape and length, when rewards come and what they are, the gear system, the shop and its prices, the campfire, levels or not, ability unlocks, difficulty, and every number.

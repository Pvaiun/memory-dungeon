# Defining the web: the director's intent

What the director wants from this step, in short. An idea they asked to have challenged or applied, in a back and forth; nothing here is ruled.

- **The question:** what sits at the edges of the web, and whether that stays the nine builds.
- **Abstract mechanics are the wrong edges.** The three facts about a party were "more correct, more objective, but it makes an abstraction of any sort of emotional or thematic experience the player might have". They sort nothing: things at the same spot don't work together, and a payoff for Shield or Evasion wants that one stat stacked. The four lines (`four-lines.md`) were preferred, for "thematic families that I could understand", though "less correct and more arbitrary". Preferring the less correct one says the method was wrong.
- **What excites a player is the words:** "a player isn't going to get excited because a fight ran long. A player is going to get excited because a fight ran long while they were doing something": shocking the whole team, burning it, killing it with thorns.
- **Colours and ways to win are two things,** as in Magic. A colour holds themes, kinds of effect it's often seen with. A deck wins by a style (aggro, control, go wide). Each colour suits each style more or less, decks are rarely one colour, and two decks can win the same way by different means.
- **"I think the builds are our colors. But the problem is that the way we had described the build was mixing the color with the winning strategy."** Each build is a theme with one way to win. "So basically our builds are too narrow."
- **Burn and Chill show it:** two poles that are "a single win condition tied to a theme, rather than a set of themes that the player can use to build win conditions".
- **The work, as they see it:** design the ways to win ("we've kind of already done that in a lot of ways"), then which theme families suit which, and where they overlap.
- **Maybe not a web:** any colours could mix, if the party finds the right way to win for them. They aren't sure the game is complex enough for every colour to match every other.
- **The classes** are "bundles that are way more tailored and controlled on our end than something like a Magic card". A class can stand for one or a few families, so the combinations are curated.
- **The scope:** "more about the game as a whole and the classes at their identities than the current web". The 108 effects may need restarting.

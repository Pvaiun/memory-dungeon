# Round 4 feel read (5h, the roster)

A fresh reader's judgement of ten packs from hand-built rosters, each fielding its best three, checked against the summary's 336 fights. "t" is a tick. I read the packs, the glossary and the summary, plus two short looks at the code to explain the pack headers (item 14).

## Per pack

**Pack 1, Toll Gate (fight, won t33).** Three bandits, no threat: the lowest anyone fell was the Pyromancer at 67% (t10.2). The Pyromancer did 62% with two Firebolts; the second's Burn 13 finished Bandit C. The Cleric's staff basics (781) out-hit the Knight's sword (484). Stand fast landed at t30.4, three ticks from the end, giving Clarity to a Cleric with 1,750 MP.

**Pack 2, Ogre Bridge (elite, won t49).** The best fight of the ten:
- The Hexer's basics built Chill on the Ogre (3.6, 6.3, 9.9, 12) while Enfeeble kept Weaken and Sunder on it.
- The Monk's double strikes pinned Sunder at 20 from t34.7 and hit 1,184 to 1,620 a swing (71% of the damage).
- The shatter at t48 (1,989) was the killing blow.

An Ogre swing took the Hexer to 525/2,000: a real moment in an elite.

**Pack 3, Ogre Chief (boss, lost t52).** Crush, begun at t10.8, landed at t18.8 for 3,900 on the Bramble knight (4,643 down to 743); basics finished it at t20.8. Three ticks earlier, the Cleric had topped up the Assassin by 700. Thicket was ready at about t28. The second Crush landed at t51.1, 0.6 ticks before the Assassin's Vanish: 4,442 on 1,875 max HP. Earlier, Backstab had crit for 6,159 on a Bandit with 266 left. Briar's Bleed (420 to 1,248 a hit) read clearly.

**Pack 4, The Wailing (fight, won t47).** Two Ghosts (75% magic resist), each hitting for 658 about every ten ticks. The Hoplite's swings (1,726) did the work. Its Spear thrust at t40 hit for 6,359 into a ghost on 33%. Paired guard landed at t45, two ticks before the end. Gale spear's Shock pulsed 294, 147 and 73, because ghosts act rarely. Judgement never fired. The lowest HP was 62%. Flat but fair.

**Pack 5, Grave Watch (elite, lost t158).** Two Trolls (23,360 HP each) and a Bone Priest:
- Two Troll hits of 1,415 killed the Hexer at t15.7, after one Enfeeble.
- The Bramble knight was down to 65 MP at t45 and cast nothing for 112 ticks. The Geomancer's last cast came at t65.7.
- Then two units swung for 380 to 817 against two Trolls, with Bleed doing half the work.

The knight fell at t158 with the Trolls on 14% and 30%. Decided at t15.7, over 142 ticks later.

**Pack 6, Giant's Forge (boss, lost t69).**
- At t0, Might 7 appeared on all three, credited to the Valkyrie, though nothing had been cast. A Hellhound died unacted at t2.4.
- Inferno (begun t16.5, landed t24.5) dealt 1,427 to each member plus Burn 4. Burn killed the Valkyrie at t28.5, 1.7 ticks before her Rally would have landed.
- At t39.1, Blood gift cost the Vampire 875 of its 1,093 HP; it died 1.4 ticks later.
- The Bard fought alone for 28 ticks, buffing itself, until the second Inferno hit it for 2,058.

**Pack 7, Storm Front (fight, won t19).** Six minions, gone in one volley. At t15.2 to t15.3, Chain lightning+ killed both Drones, and Firebolt+'s Burn 32.8 (a 1,621 pulse) killed a Leech. The Leeches' MP drains (25 to 75) and the Drones' Taunts showed, but didn't matter. The Knight cast nothing; Thorns was 47% of its damage.

**Pack 8, Chapel Watch (elite, lost t124).** Two Bone Priests and a Troll:
- The Paladin (tank) took four hits of 590, then Flatten for 1,935 plus Stun, then an 885 crit. It was down at t22.2 without casting, with 2,765 MP unspent.
- Four of the Cleric's five Cure+ went on itself.
- Eight Mends healed 36,211, including 9,100 twice on the Troll, against the party's 45,774 dealt. Nothing died in 124 ticks.
- The Cleric reached 26 MP at t61.8, never near Raise.

Decided by t22, then 100 ticks of treadmill.

**Pack 9, Silent One (boss, won t70).** A stun-lock. The Monk's double strikes added Chill 5 and Sunder a swing; the Frostweaver's basics added more. Chill shattered six times (t8.3 to t61.8), each for 3,092 to 4,765 plus a Stun. The boss was stunned about 48 of the 70 ticks and swung twice; two members took no damage at all. Quiet silenced the party from t38.8 to t56.3, but the Monk (120 MP from t31) and the Frostweaver (390 from t32.5) had nothing to cast anyway. The Monk's single Burning fist+ left Burn that did 6,310.

**Pack 10, Storm Queen (boss, lost t133).** The Queen had 87,422 HP. Two of her basics (945 each) and a Tempest bolt killed the Ranger at t20.8; its 6,325 crit had gone into a Drone on 13%. She summoned Drone pairs at t30.6, t82.2 and t124.9, and two of the Knight's three Shield bash+ went into Drones. Tempest's bolts did 550 to 790, each with one stack of Shock. After t48 the Geomancer never again had the 1,000 MP for Tremor. The Queen stood on 70% at the end. Decided at t21, over 112 ticks later.

## 5. Rhythm

- **The opening is a round of basics, then a volley.** Everyone swings in t0 to t5. After a quiet stretch, the first abilities land together at t14 to t18 (pack 7: three kills at t15.2 to t15.3; packs 2, 3 and 9 likewise). The summary agrees: 21% of casts begin on an ally's tick, and 25% chain with no basic between.
- **Won fights breathe in the middle.** In pack 2, Enfeeble, fist, ballad and basics take turns.
- **Wind-ups are the most legible thing in the logs.** Crush gives 8 ticks of warning, Inferno 8, Flatten and Tempest 6, Troll swings 6 to 7. All of them pay off.
- **The dead stretches are lone survivors after a lost fight is decided:** 22 to 29 ticks in packs 5, 6, 8 and 10.
- **Tier lengths differ on average** (regular fights 37 to 41 ticks, elites 49 to 66, bosses 76 to 87). The spreads are enormous: act 2 elites run 16 to 132 ticks and act 2 bosses 33 to 145 (10th to 90th percentile).

## 6. Abilities

- **Often enough:** Blood drain+ 3.03 casts a fight, War ballad+ 2.94, Enfeeble+ and Gale spear+ 2.81, Cure+ 2.46.
- **Once or never:** Valhalla 0.03 (first cast t90), Raise 0.05 (t84), Pyre 0.16 (t55), Stillness 0.21, Static charge 0.28, Flow 0.32, and Thunderstorm, Fireball and Aspis 0.33.
  - Third slots start uncharged on cooldowns of 33 to 63, so in a 40-tick fight they hardly exist. Tremor first lands at t50, Unmake at t46.
  - The Hoplite casts about 1.3 abilities a fight in all.
- **Value for MP:**

  | Ability | Dealt a cast | MP |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Fireball | 845 | 1,000 |
  | Thunderstorm | 1,340 | 1,000 |
  | Spear thrust | 4,032 | 325 |
  | Garrote | 5,533 | 325 |

  Pyre (10,122) is the biggest number in the game, about once every six fights.
- **Overkill swallows the big hits:** Spear thrust into 33% (pack 4), Backstab 6,159 into 266 HP (pack 3), Barbed arrow+ into 13% (pack 10).
- **Each unit has a tempo you can read:** the Hoplite is slow and heavy, the Monk and the Stormcaller strike twice, the Bard swings small and often.

## 7. Statuses

- **Bleed** is the clearest ramp: Briar stacks on whatever hits the Bramble knight, and every later hit pays. It did 10,107 in pack 5, the largest share in the summary (24%).
- **Burn** is a burst with a visible fade. Firebolt+ puts 32.8 of a possible 40 stacks in one cast (pack 7). The Monk's fist pulsed 1,007, 856, 719 and on down to 62 (pack 9). A good shape.
- **Chill** makes the best event in the game when it shatters (pack 2's finisher), and a lock in pack 9. The thaw is invisible: "chilled 7.3" stays on Troll A's lines from t9 to t111 (pack 5), and "chilled 5.6" on the Queen's for 70 ticks (pack 10).
- **Sunder** is wallpaper. One shatter lifts it from 12.2 to 20 (pack 9, t8.3), Iron palm keeps it there, and "sundered 20" is on every line of packs 2 and 9.
- **Might** sat at its cap of 15 for most of pack 6; Frenzy alone fills it.
- **Shock** scales with how often its holder acts: 1,146 to 1,699 a pulse on the Silent One, 73 on a ghost. One stack from Tempest is nothing.
- **Readable:** Stun, Shield ("soaked"), Regen, Taunt (Drones pulling the Knight), Clarity.
- **Invisible:** Weaken (a tag, never a number), Ward, Haste, and Silence on units with no MP.

## 8. Mana

See section A: MP runs out in long fights, and gear decides who notices.

## 9. Classes

- **Knight (packs 1, 7, 10):** a working wall. Thorns is half its damage, and Stand fast's Clarity often lands on itself.
- **Bramble knight (3, 5):** loud and on-fantasy; a tank making 51% of the party's damage. It regains only 15% of the MP it spends.
- **Hoplite (4):** heavy basics. Its kit arrives after the fight.
- **Paladin (4, 8):** down at t22 with no casts in pack 8; 25.9 KOs per 100 fights, the most of any front-liner.
- **Valkyrie (4, 6, 9):** plays its description. Valhalla is a dead slot.
- **Cleric (1, 3, 8):** heals the wrong person at the wrong time. 99% of its damage is basics.
- **Monk (2, 9):** the best swings, but a 650 MP pool.
- **Bard (2, 6):** its Might is everywhere, and it is never short of MP.
- **Hexer (2, 5):** set up pack 2's shatter; died at t15.7 in pack 5. The most KO'd class (33 per 100 fights).
- **Geomancer (5, 8, 10):** carried two lost fights with Bedrock crits (Rockfall CRIT 5,880) and shells. Tremor's cost makes it rare.
- **Vampire (6):** its lifesteal shows, and its own Blood gift killed it.
- **Pyromancer and Stormcaller (1, 7):** decide regular fights in one volley (56% and 64% of the party's damage).
- **Assassin (3):** crits and crit Bleed, but Backstab only 0.4 to 0.7 times a fight.
- **Frostweaver (9):** the shatter engine.
- **Ranger (10):** 58% of the party's damage in the summary; dead at t21 in pack 10.

**Worst-feeling three:** the Cleric (its heals miss the moment), the Hoplite (its kit arrives late) and the Hexer (dies before its kit plays).

**Best-feeling three:** the Monk, the Geomancer and the Bramble knight, each a loop you can watch.

**Dominant:** the Stormcaller, Ranger and Pyromancer, and the Bramble knight, a tank out-damaging its party.

## 10. Enemies and bosses

- **Boss moves come one to three times a fight** (Crush 2.50, Tempest 2.59, Tolling 2.92, Inferno 1.08), and when they land they decide fights: Crush one-shot the Assassin, and Inferno killed the Valkyrie and the Bard. Tempest is middling. Tolling and Quiet did nothing in pack 9.
- **Bosses' basics kill too:** the Queen's basics did more to the Ranger than her Tempest did.
- **Elites:** the Ogre feels like one. Trolls hit casters for about 1,450, so two hits kill a Hexer. The Bone Priests' Mend is the strongest thing in the game.
- **Minions** act before dying 86% to 100% of the time. Hellhounds are nothing: 135 dealt an appearance.
- **The regions differ:** Old Road bruisers, the Crypt's magic-proof Ghosts and healers, Burn on both sides in the Fire Mine, Taunting Drones and draining Leeches in the Storm Tower.

## 11. Drama

- **Near misses read well:** Rally 1.7 ticks too late (pack 6), Vanish 0.6 ticks too late (pack 3).
- **One-shots:** Crush, Inferno, and a Troll's two swings.
- **No comebacks.** Every lost fight lost its first member by t29 (t15.7, t20.8, t21.3, t22.2, t28.5), and none recovered.
- **Only pack 2's win was close.**

## 12. Ranked list

1. **Lost fights are decided by the first death, then drag on.** Packs 5 (decided t15.7, over t158), 8 (t22, t124) and 10 (t21, t133), as well as 3 and 6. In the summary, the act 2 elite 90th percentile is 132 ticks and the act 2 boss 145.
2. **Bone Priests undo the fight.** In pack 8, Mends healed 36,211 against 45,774 dealt, with no kills in 124 ticks. 26 appearances in the summary, 1.27 Mends each.
3. **A Chill engine stun-locks an act 3 boss** (pack 9): stunned about 69% of the fight, two swings.
4. **Casters go dry halfway through the long fights** (packs 5, 8, 9 and 10; section A).
5. **Third slots and revives are dead:** Valhalla 0.03 casts a fight, Raise 0.05, Pyre 0.16.
6. **Regular fights never threaten:** all 168 won, 0 to 0.1 KOs a fight.
7. **The boss cliff:** act 3 elites are won 94% of the time, the act 3 boss 39%.
8. **Fragile members die to ordinary basics:** the Hexer (pack 5) and the Ranger (pack 10). The Hexer has 33 KOs per 100 fights, the Frostweaver 27.
9. **The healer misses its moment:** pack 3 at t15.4; pack 8, four of five Cure+ on itself.
10. **Blood gift kills its caster** (pack 6, t39.1 to t40.5).
11. **Statuses sit at their ceilings:** Sunder at 20 (packs 2 and 9), Might at 15 (pack 6), Burn 32.8 of 40 from one cast (pack 7).
12. **Siphon pays on overkill:** a Backstab that counted 266 refunded 615 MP (pack 3); a crit into a dying Drone refilled the Ranger (+632, pack 10).
13. **Boss moves wasted by timing.** A shatter Stun followed each Tolling within 1.1 ticks. Quiet silenced units with no MP, and it landed at t38.8 while the log showed the boss stunned from t32.5 to t40.5.
14. **The pack tool misleads.**
    - **The header gear doesn't match the fight.** Robes sit in weapon slots. Pack 2's Monk is listed with an MP-regen charm but shows MP regen 0. Pack 3's Cleric siphons with no siphon piece listed. Packs 2 and 9 share run seed 9101, and rogue item ids are built from the seed, so I suspect the ids collide.
    - **Shatter damage is counted as the foe's own "dealt".** The Silent One "dealt 25,886" while the party took 1,553; the difference is exactly the Chill line. Foe damage in Chill fights is inflated, and the Frostweaver's status share reads 0%.
    - **Pack 6's Might 7 at t0 has no source in the log.**
15. **Minions that are nothing:** Hellhounds (135 an appearance), Snakes (326).

**What looked right:**
- Wind-ups, and the slow swings of big foes.
- Briar's Bleed, Burn's fade, and the shatter as an event.
- Pack 2's elite: a plan you can watch work.
- A tempo for each class, and near misses.
- Signature moves one to three times a fight.
- Each tier's length near its target on average.

## A. MP across fights

**Who runs out.** Someone visibly runs dry in four packs, all long elites or bosses:

| Pack | Who | Ran out | What followed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | Bramble knight | 65 MP at t45 | No casts for 112 ticks |
| 5 | Geomancer | t65.7 | No casts for 64 ticks |
| 8 | Cleric | 26 MP at t61.8 | Never near Raise's 1,500 |
| 9 | Monk | 120 MP from t31 | Two casts in 70 ticks |
| 9 | Frostweaver | 390 MP from t32.5 | Two casts in 70 ticks |
| 10 | Geomancer | after t48 | One Tremor in 133 ticks |

The summary shows less of it (the Monk is short of MP 14% of its time, the Bramble knight 11%, everyone else 1% to 8%), because most fights last about 40 ticks and a pool at 70% covers them.

**It reads as a class switching off, not a choice.** Nobody saves MP or picks a cheaper spell. A unit casts whatever is ready until it can't, then basic-attacks for 30 to 110 ticks. It bites where it matters least: in pack 9 the casters were dry by t32 and the fight was won on basics and shatters. In the lost fights it only deepens the slog.

**Who never notices: gear decides more than class.**
- In pack 6 the Bard regained 3,713 of the 3,750 MP it spent: Refrain 1,229, one Blood gift's Clarity 1,056, and about 1,400 from gear regen that no log line shows.
- The Geomancer regained 94% with siphon (pack 8), yet went dry in packs 5 and 10.
- The Assassin and the Ranger are refilled by siphon on overkill crits.
- The Knight and the Hoplite cast too rarely to care (the Hoplite leaves fights on 67%).

**Who feels it, by the summary:** the Bramble knight (regains 15% of its spending and leaves fights on 36%), the Vampire (30%), the Pyromancer (36%), the Assassin (40%), and the Monk, whose 650 pool is one or two casts. Most other classes regain 70% to 90% of their spending in-fight, so the two-or-three-fight drain lands mainly on these few.

## B. Difficulty

- **The tiers differ in danger, not in shape.** Regular fights are a formality: all won, 0 to 0.1 KOs, and the lowest HP in packs 1, 4 and 7 was 45% to 67%.
- **Elites** are won 94% to 100% of the time. When they go wrong they become slogs of 120 to 160 ticks (packs 5 and 8, both healer-and-Troll fights).
- **Bosses** are the only real danger (39% to 69% won, 1.3 to 1.9 KOs a fight), and the act 3 drop from elite to boss is a cliff.
- **Boss fights turn on their first 20 to 30 ticks**, on whether a big blow or two basics land on a fragile member. Every boss I saw either killed someone by t29 or never got to act (pack 9).
- **Slogs:** packs 5, 8 and 10, each running more than 100 ticks after the decisive death.
- **Decided at once:** pack 7, three kills at t15.

## C. Puzzle fights

**No pack shows snakes or the Bomb Nest**, so I can't say how they read. The summary's numbers:
- **Snakes:** 19 appearances, always acting, 326 dealt an appearance, among the lowest of any foe.
- **Bomb Bugs:** 26 appearances (about nine nests), always acting, 764 dealt an appearance. A whole nest comes to about 2,300, less than two Troll swings.

Neither looks threatening. Whether the bombs' death blasts are visible, or make the order of kills matter, needs a pack.

The fights that played as puzzles in practice were the Bone Priest pairs (packs 5 and 8). The party, unable to focus its attacks, lost them every time.

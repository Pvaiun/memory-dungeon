# Feel check: answers

All seven packs use the same dungeon, the Hollow of the Grey Mother, at levels 1 to 6. It always has the same three rooms and the same boss. Where I give enemy HP, I worked it out by adding up the damage that went in before something died. The game itself never shows it.

## Pack 1

Level 6. The party is a Vanguard, an Archer and a Duelist.

1. **Retell.** The three trash fights took 2 or 3 rounds each and cost almost nothing, so the party reached the boss on 117, 68 and 71 HP. Against the Grey Mother every hit shrank to 2 or 3 damage. Each round she bit one Ranger for 8 or the Vanguard for 5. The Duelist fell in round 22 and the Archer in round 25. The Vanguard then stood alone for 18 more rounds, losing exactly 5 HP a round, and went down in round 43.

2. **Enemies.**
   - Ash Wolf: hits Rangers for 4 and the Vanguard for 1. It took three hits (more than 28 damage) to kill, and it often acts before the Archer and Vanguard. Nothing showed it being "strong in numbers": a lone wolf hit just as hard as a pair.
   - Bog Wight: hits for 1, even at level 6, and dies to two hits (about 27), so it's softer than the wolves. Nothing "festering" happened.
   - The Grey Mother: has a very thick hide, turning our 13s and 14s into 2s and 3s. She attacks once a round, for 8 on a Ranger or 5 on the Vanguard. She never struck more than once a turn and never called for help, though her description says she does both. She took 207 damage and was still standing.

3. **Why it was lost.** Attrition. Her hide stretched the fight into a 40-round race, and nobody in the party healed, so the Rangers wore down and the Vanguard couldn't finish her alone. The other packs suggest this was lost by about one hit. She dies at roughly 78, 130 and 156 damage at levels 1, 3 and 4, about 26 more per level, which would put her near 208 here. She also acts before the Vanguard in each round, so he died with his last swing unthrown. Next I'd swap the Duelist for a Cleric, to stretch the fight with healing. I'm trusting the class blurb there, not anything I saw: the only Cleric in these logs (pack 4) never healed once. If Clerics don't really heal, the same party with a little more luck would probably win.
   Colour: Warrior, Ranger, Cleric

4. **Drama.** On paper this is the best moment in all seven packs: a lone Vanguard's last stand that ends one hit short. The player can't see any of that, though. Nothing hints that the Mother is nearly dead, so it reads as a slow, certain loss. You could also see each Ranger's death coming about ten rounds ahead, since they lost the same 8 HP every time.

5. **Flat or unclear.**
   - The boss fight is 43 rounds of nearly identical lines. Damage numbers never vary and there are no critical hits.
   - None of the class blurbs showed up:
     - The Vanguard never cleaved, and never stepped in while his friends died.
     - The Archer never caused bleeding or covered anyone.
     - The Duelist got hit every time the Mother went for him ("slips every blow"), and never made a big lunge.
   - None of the perks had any visible effect:
     - Swift Feet: the turn order didn't change.
     - Keen Eyes: still no critical hits.
     - Red Riposte: no counterattack or bleeding ever appeared.

## Pack 2

Level 3. A Vanguard alone.

1. **Retell.** The Vanguard killed each trash enemy in two swings while wolves and wights pecked him for 1 a hit. He lost 41 HP across the three fights and reached the boss on 79 of 120. The Grey Mother fight took 79 rounds. She took 1 HP off him almost every round, and he took 2 off her most rounds. She fell in round 79 with him on 5 HP.

2. **Enemies.**
   - Ash Wolf: hits for 1, misses now and then, and takes two hits (26 damage) to kill. He never missed a wolf, so "agile" didn't show. "Strong in numbers" didn't either.
   - Bog Wight: hits for 1 and takes two hits to kill. He actually missed the wights three times. No festering.
   - The Grey Mother: hits him for 1, the same as a wolf, and took about 130 damage to kill. She attacked once a round and nobody came to help her.

3. Won.
   Colour: -

4. **Drama.** Technically this is the closest win in the set: a lone hero finishing on 5 HP. Because her HP is hidden, there's some real "who drops first" tension. But at 1 damage a round for 79 rounds, it feels like watching two timers run down, not a fight. There's no comeback, and the lines are the same from start to finish.

5. **Flat or unclear.**
   - The 79-round boss fight is the low point of all seven packs.
   - Trash that does 1 damage to a 120-HP fighter never feels like a threat.
   - None of the perks had any visible effect:
     - Battle Rhythm: no "Rhythm" and no critical hits ever appeared.
     - Second Breath: he began the third fight on exactly the 100 HP he'd ended the second one with.
     - Red Riposte: it's a Ranger perk, and there was no Ranger in the party. I don't know why it was offered, or why it was picked.
   - The loot included an Ash Staff for a lone Warrior. I can't tell whether he can use it.

## Pack 3

Level 3. A Vanguard and an Archer.

1. **Retell.** The pair swept the trash in 2, 4 and 3 rounds. One 14 plus one 13 kills anything, so they mostly cleared an enemy a round. They reached the boss on 114 and 70 HP. The Mother switched between them, biting the Archer for 4 or the Vanguard for 1, while they chipped her for 3 and 2 a round. She fell in round 27, with the Archer on 26 and the Vanguard on 99.

2. **Enemies.**
   - Ash Wolf: hits the Archer for 2 and the Vanguard for 1. It dies to 27 damage, one hit from each.
   - Bog Wight: hits anyone for 1 and dies to 27 damage.
   - The Grey Mother: hits the Archer for 4 and the Vanguard for 1, and died after about 131 damage. She attacked once a round and nobody came to help her.

3. Won.
   Colour: -

4. **Drama.** None. The run was comfortable from the first round. The Archer lost half her health but was never in real danger.

5. **Flat or unclear.**
   - This was the cleanest and most readable run, but the boss was still 27 rounds of the same two numbers.
   - Wolf's Blood says a unit that lands a kill strikes again. Both heroes got kills in the next fight, and neither struck again.
   - Second Breath says everyone recovers a little health as each fight begins. No health came back: the Vanguard went from 117 to 117.
   - If the perks do anything, the log doesn't show it.

## Pack 4

Level 2. A Mender alone.

1. **Retell.** The Mender fought alone and hit for 3. The two wolves took 10 rounds, and each of the next two fights took 16, with every enemy pecking her for 1. She never healed herself, and her HP fell from 90 to 73 to 36 to 2. She won the third fight on 2 HP, and the Grey Mother's first bite, for 3, killed her. The log ends "Lost after 1 rounds."

2. **Enemies.**
   - Ash Wolf: hits for 1 and has about 15 HP (five of her hits).
   - Bog Wight: hits for 1 and also has about 15 HP.
   - The Grey Mother: hit for 3. That's all I saw of her.

3. **Why it was lost.** A Cleric alone does almost no damage. At 3 a hit, every trash fight dragged on for 10 to 16 rounds of free hits on her. She also never used any healing, so the trash wore her down to nothing before the boss. Next I'd put a Warrior in front of her. A lone Warrior won level 1 easily and level 3 narrowly, so one should carry level 2. Having a friend to mend would also show whether she can heal at all.
   Colour: Warrior, Cleric

4. **Drama.** Finishing the third fight on 2 HP was a real close call, and the tensest trash fight in the whole set. Then the boss killed her in one hit, which felt like an execution, not a climax. The run's big moment happened in the wrong fight.

5. **Flat or unclear.**
   - Her blurb says she mends wounds and keeps everyone standing. She sat at 2 HP and never healed.
   - Second Breath promises a little health as each fight begins. It was picked right before the boss, the one moment it really mattered, and she still went in on 2 of 90.
   - The other two picks, Serrated Heads and Haemorrhage, are Ranger perks offered to a party with no Ranger. "Barbed Arrows" (in Serrated Heads) appears nowhere else in any log.
   - Before the one-line boss fight came 42 rounds of "hits for 1" and "hits for 3".

## Pack 7

Level 5. A Vanguard and a Duelist.

1. **Retell.** The trash took 2, 4 and 3 rounds. The wolves hit the Duelist for 3 at a time, so he reached the boss on 58 of 80. The Mother then hit the Duelist for 7 in nine of the first ten rounds, and he fell in round 10. The Vanguard fought on alone for 29 rounds, losing 4 a round from 110, and fell in round 39 with her still standing.

2. **Enemies.**
   - Ash Wolf: hits the Duelist for 3 and the Vanguard for 1. It has about 27 HP.
   - Bog Wight: hits for 1 and dies to 27 or 28 damage.
   - The Grey Mother: hits the Duelist for 7 and the Vanguard for 4. She took 91 damage and wasn't close to done. If she follows the pattern of about 26 more HP per level, she has around 182 here, so they got about halfway.

3. **Why it was lost.** There were only two of them, and the Duelist was the Mother's favourite target. Once he was gone, the Vanguard's 2 damage a round couldn't keep pace with her 4 a round, and nothing healed him. A third member is the obvious fix. Pack 1 shows a Warrior and two Rangers can push her to the brink a level higher, so even another Ranger would probably win this. Because this loss was also attrition, I'd add a Cleric for the healing. Again, I'm trusting the blurb, not anything a Cleric did in these logs.
   Colour: Warrior, Ranger, Cleric

4. **Drama.** The Duelist's collapse was the one moment, and it was a steady countdown of 7 a round. The Vanguard's last stand was a foregone conclusion: at 4 a round, you can read the ending 25 rounds out. It was never close.

5. **Flat or unclear.**
   - The Duelist's blurb says he "slips every blow". The Mother hit him 9 times out of 9, the wolves hit him most of the time, and he never made his "decisive lunge".
   - Iron Skin ("your party shrugs off blows") was picked after the first fight. The wolves then hit for exactly the same 3 and 1 as before.
   - Serrated Heads was picked, but it refers to Barbed Arrows, which nothing in the log shows the party having.

## Across the packs

**Within a dungeon, did it build up to its boss?** No, not really. Every run has the same four fights:
1. Den mouth: two wolves.
2. Drowned path: two wolves and a wight.
3. Wight pool: a wolf and two wights.
4. The den: the Grey Mother alone.

The trash fights are quick and nearly free, at 2 to 8 rounds and a few HP each. The only escalation is going from two enemies to three, and fights 2 and 3 are interchangeable. Then the boss is a different kind of fight: 27 to 79 rounds against a hide that cuts our damage by about 80%. That's a cliff, not a build-up. Nothing earlier hints that armour or a thick hide will matter. The wights, which the "wight pool" seems to be building to, are the weakest thing in the game.

The room names and "something big sleeps at the bottom" do give a nice sense of going down into the den, and that's the most build-up there is. The only run where the earlier fights mattered was the lone Mender's (pack 4), and there they were the whole story.

**Across dungeons, did they feel different?** No. It's one dungeon, numbered 1 to 6, with the same rooms, enemies and boss each time. Only the numbers go up:

| Level | Mother's HP (by my sums) | Her bite on the Vanguard | Her bite on a Ranger |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | about 78 | 1 | not seen |
| 3 | about 130 | 1 | 4 |
| 4 | about 156 | 2 | 5 |
| 5 | about 182 (estimate) | 4 | 7 |
| 6 | about 208 (estimate) | 5 | 8 |

The wolves' bite on a Ranger rises from 2 to 4 over the same levels. As a difficulty curve it makes sense:
- A lone Warrior wins levels 1 and 3.
- A Warrior and a Ranger win levels 3 and 4, then lose at 5.
- A Warrior and two Rangers only just lose at 6.

But the levels don't feel different, and the loot doesn't change much either: Studded Jerkin and Notched Blade at levels 1, 3 and 4.

**The biggest feel problems, most important first:**
1. **Boss fights are far too long and samey to read as text.** They run 27 to 79 rounds of "X hits Y for N", and the numbers never vary.
2. **Nothing in the descriptions happens.**
   - Enemies never strike several times, call for help, fester or gang up.
   - Heroes never cleave, step in, cause bleeding, cover a friend, dodge, lunge or heal.
   - Every unit just attacks once a round for a fixed number. Picking a party comes down to HP and one damage figure, and watching a class fight doesn't teach me what it's for.
   - The only traits that come through are the Grey Mother's thick hide, the Warrior's toughness (he takes about half what a Ranger takes), the Rangers' speed (they act first) and the Cleric's weakness.
3. **Perks never show any effect,** and the game offers (and picks) perks for classes the party doesn't have. So the perk pick after each fight feels pointless.
4. **Close calls are invisible.** Hiding enemy HP is fine, but nothing like "she's staggering" tells you when a boss is nearly dead. Pack 1's loss by about one hit reads exactly like pack 7's loss at about half.
5. **Smaller things.**
   - "Open Order" appears next to every party and is never explained.
   - Swift Feet's "moves a little quicker" doesn't say where or what moves.
   - HP carries over between fights with no rest, which is fine, but nothing tells you so.
   - "Lost after 1 rounds" should say "1 round".

What does work: the log is easy to follow, showing HP left after each hit makes the attrition readable, and the boss's hide comes across clearly.

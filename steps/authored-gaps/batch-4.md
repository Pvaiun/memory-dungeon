# Batch 4

The requests on the director's page, as seeded. The page's store holds the live copy and the answers. Near lines are Greats, starred seats, Great formations and the director's own earlier answers only: no Fines.

## 1. Bonfire: a payoff for a high fire (Road)

I need a payoff for a high fire: something that happens because a foe's Burn stands high, besides the Burn's own damage. Not an applier, and not upkeep. If the damage is payoff enough, mark this wrong.

Burn drains fast, so its height on the hottest foe is how hard the party is feeding it right now. Nothing in the web cares about that height: a fire twice as high deals twice the Burn damage and does nothing else. Shock has two Greats that read the charge, and the fire has none. I'm not asking for appliers (the plain ones are mine to write) or for upkeep (your pile and your mana for the foes' debuffs answered that in batch 1).

- yours: Its first-slotted ability adds Burn to every target, allies included.
- starred seat: Torch (Pyre line): when a burning foe falls, its Burn passes to the foe with the most HP.
- yours: Damage statuses it lays go to the foe holding the most of that status, unless that foe is at its cap.

## 2. Landslide: the blow in a pack (Road)

I need something for a party that loads one huge blow, in a fight against a pack. A payoff or a rule.

The blow takes a long time to come round. In a pack it lands on a foe that needed a tenth of it, and the rest is lost: a pack is this road's worst fight. Two of yours help. The wide seat lands a single blow on every foe at a share, and a Stunned foe is Marked, which aims it. Your opening Mark works against it: it sits on the foe with the least max HP, so it calls the blow onto the smallest thing in the fight. If a wasted blow is the price of loading one, mark this wrong.

- Great: Basic attacks come 50% as often, deal ×3 and Stun their target.
- yours: Wide and narrow: in the first seat, every single-target action lands on every foe or every ally at a share.
- yours: A Stunned foe is Marked, while no foe is.
- yours: At fight start: a Mark on the foe with the least max HP.

## 3. Silence from more hands (Supply)

I need Silence from somewhere other than the Assassin and the Hexer: a piece or a seat that Silences a foe. An applier.

Your piece that counts a Silenced foe as a foe falling has two classes for partners, the only two whose kits Silence. A run that finds it without either gets nothing from it, and it was written for the fight with one foe, where most runs end. Nothing in the web Silences. It's bounded already: a Silence doesn't land on a foe that's Silenced, and a boss comes to resist it.

- yours: When a foe is Silenced: the party's "when a foe falls" effects happen.
- Great: When a foe falls: every ally's start-of-fight effects happen again.

## 4. An ability slot that isn't the first (Function)

I need a piece or a seat that does something with the ability slotted second or last, the way three effects already do with the one slotted first.

A unit uses its first ready ability in the order the player slotted them, so the first slot is cast most and the last least. Three effects name a slot, and all three name the first. A unit holding any of them has one answer, its best ability first, and what goes second or last is never a thought. If the first slot is the only one worth naming, mark this wrong.

- starred seat: Soloist (The cathedral): its first-slotted ability has no cooldown.
- starred seat: Breaker (Icefall): any shatter readies its first-slotted ability.
- yours: Its first-slotted ability adds Burn to every target, allies included.

## 5. The Paladin and the Geomancer (Two classes)

I need a piece a Paladin and a Geomancer would both want, each for its own reason. They share the great weapon and the kite shield. Not a Shield piece or a crit piece: the web has plenty of both.

One judges: it Marks a foe and the party's swings land on it at once, its hits land half again as hard on a foe holding any buff or debuff, and its Smite is a heavy blow behind a long wind-up. The other hits hardest behind stone: its rocks read its Defense, and its Tremor Stuns. Both are slow, heavy bodies that put one big thing on one foe, and they ride Landslide together. Two of yours join them already: a Stunned foe is Marked, so the Geomancer's Tremor does the Paladin's calling, and a foe Stunned gives its wearer Might. The Bramble knight and the Hoplite hold great weapons too; the Knight and the Frostweaver hold kite shields.

- yours: A Stunned foe is Marked, while no foe is.
- yours: When a foe is Stunned: gains Might.

## 6. The Valkyrie and the Frostweaver (Two classes)

I need a piece a Valkyrie and a Frostweaver would both want, each for its own reason. They share the spear and the rune sword. Not a Shield piece: the web has plenty.

Both also hold Shields, which is why I've set those aside. One of yours suits both already: the pile. The Knight, the Hoplite and the Monk hold spears too; the Vampire and the Stormcaller hold rune swords.

## 7. The Bramble knight and the Hoplite (Two classes)

I need a piece a Bramble knight and a Hoplite would both want, each for its own reason. They share the great weapon and the round shield.

One draws every foe onto its thorns: each attacker is hurt and left bleeding, it Taunts and is Guarded, and its lash hits from its Thorns. Both are heavy bodies at the front, one the foes come to and one they walk past, and both look after whoever is lowest (Regen from one, the Aegis from the other). Nothing in the web is near the two together, and no effect names Thorns or an Aegis. The Paladin and the Geomancer hold great weapons too; the Valkyrie holds round shields.

## 8. A formation with no front (Formation)

I need a formation for a party with no front: the foes' blows fall on all three, and the formation is about that. It should change how the party fights, not add to it. Not a Shield or a Blur on everyone: the Peak and your opening Blur are those already.

Every shape you kept or wrote that says who is struck puts the blows on one seat: the Shepherd takes them, the Stones draw them, your one Shield draws them, the Lifeline halves the Anchor's. Your ruling is that a wall isn't every build's start, and neither is a front seat. A party that follows it sits in a shape with an anchor seat nobody suits, or in Neutral.

- Great formation: The shepherd: takes the hits meant for the ally with the lowest HP%.
- yours: One Shield: one seat holds triple the Shield and draws more; the others can't hold any.
- starred seat: Peak (The mountain): while every ally holds a Shield, each hit lands 2 times.
- yours: At fight start: Blur on every ally.

## 9. A formation about what a class does (Formation)

I need a formation where part of one seat's kit works for another seat: its passive, or one of its abilities. It should change how the party fights, not add to it.

A class's passive is only its own, and so are its abilities. Your seats already pass along what a unit swings with (the Standard), what it gives itself (Twins), its stats (the Bannerman) and its amulet (yours). None passes along what the class itself does. One that did would make every class a partner of every other, and a run that shows a fifth of the loot needs partners most.

- Great formation: The standard: the Followers' basic attacks add what the Lead's basic attacks add, sized by their own stats.
- Great formation: Twins: statuses a Twin gives itself, the other Twin gains too.
- yours: One amulet for three: the third seat's amulet counts as worn by every member.

## 10. A formation about the statuses on the foes (Formation)

I need a formation about the statuses the party lays on the foes, whichever they are: who lays them and who uses them is what the seats trade. It should change how the party fights, not add to it.

Five statuses are built up on the foes: Burn, Chill, Shock, Bleed and Sunder. Icefall is the one Great shape for any of them, and it's Chill's alone; the Torch is Burn's. Your gear already treats them as one family: the pile gathers four of them, mana comes back for any debuff, and one Great hits harder for each different one. No shape does, so a party built on Shock, Bleed or Sunder sits in a shape made for something else.

- Great formation: Icefall: one seat's abilities add +50% Chill, one's basic attacks add a Chill, and any shatter readies the Breaker's first-slotted ability.
- yours: Damage statuses it lays go to the foe holding the most of that status, unless that foe is at its cap.
- Great: Hits deal +10% per different debuff on the target.

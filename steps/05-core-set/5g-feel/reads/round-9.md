# Round 9 feel read

A fresh reader's judgement of ten packs, checked against the summary's 385 fights. Only the packs, glossary and summary were read. "t" is a tick.

## 5. Rhythm

Fights have two openings and no build. Where abilities carry a charge the first ten ticks are a volley: pack 9 (seven casts t3 to t10.7, three begun within 0.4 ticks at t6.3 to t6.7), pack 5 (three by t3), pack 7 (five by t14). Where nothing carries (pack 1, floor 1) it is sixteen ticks of basics then three casts landing together at t17 to t18.7. Mid-fight, units do alternate: the pack 7 Paladin's line is Smite t6, basic, basic, Smite t41.4, basic, Absolve t53.4, basic, basic, Smite t77.2; the Vampire's is drain, basic, basic, drain. The stall is at the open: the pack 7 Vampire cast at t3, t6, t9 with no basic between; the pack 5 Stormcaller at t1 then t6; the pack 7 Knight at t32.5 then t37.9. Summary: 18% of casts chain on the same unit, 16% begin on an ally's tick. Allies' casts do pile onto one tick often enough to notice (pack 7 t0, t8, t128; pack 5 t24.5/t25.1; pack 9 t6.3 to t6.7), at the open and wherever kits share a cooldown word (28/29, 14/15).

Wind-ups pay off, usually as a kill: Crush 8t, 4,434 (pack 4 t17.4); Soulfire 8t, 6,804 (pack 9 t23.3); Pyre 8t, 9,610 (pack 8 t13); Smite 6t, 3,375 + Burn (pack 7 t6); Flatten 6t, 2,431 + Stun (pack 6 t22.8). The exception is Rockfall, begun twice, never landed (pack 6). Ogres and trolls telegraph plain basics for six ticks ("begins attack, lands t11.7", pack 3), which reads like an ability each time.

Dead stretches are the tails: pack 4 t24.5 to 49.8, pack 6 t42.3 to 70, pack 8 t17 to 39.4, pack 10 t77.6 to 95.7, pack 7 t130 to 171. Regular fights (20 to 40 ticks) and bosses (50 to 171) feel different; elites have no shape of their own: pack 3 at 33 ticks was a regular fight, pack 6 at 70 was worse than a boss. Summary: act 2 elites 69.8 ticks, 1.8 KOs, 42% won, against the act 2 boss's 73.5 ticks and 55%.

## 7. Statuses

- Burn (19% of party damage where applied): the biggest number and a one-cast nuke. Firebolt+ 29.1 stacks, 1,441 a pulse (pack 8 t5); Burning fist+ to 35.4, 2,376 a pulse (pack 9 t19.5); it killed Bandit B alone (pack 2) and kept killing 28 ticks after its caster died (pack 4). Its tail is noise: thirty pulses of 47 to 200 (pack 7 t26 to t40). Legible and very chatty.
- Shock (15%): a stored charge whose shape the foe sets. Against trolls acting every eight ticks it caps at 40 and lands 3,040 (pack 6 t32.4, t51.2), and 2,759 killed an ice spirit on its own swing (pack 5 t29.1); against the hasted Silent One it halves every three ticks, 1,197 to 74 (pack 10 t41.8 to t57.4).
- Bleed (18%): a hit after a hit. 15.6 stacks gave 1,872 (pack 6 t20.6); the Briar coat's 4 stacks gave 480, 319, 213 behind every Titan hit (pack 7). Snake Bleed 1 is 120 on the party (pack 2). Felt right.
- Chill (4%): reached its shatter once in ten packs, pack 5 t21.1, and that shatter healed the Ice Spirit 1,718. Elsewhere a label: "chilled 4.2" on Troll B for 56 ticks (pack 6 t7.5 to t63.5), "chilled 1" on the party after every Hailstorm (pack 7). The Frostweaver applied none (pack 1). Summary peak 8.5 of 12. The shatter did not feel reachable except for a staff Hexer on a slow foe.
- Sunder reads on every hit ("sundered 20", pack 9) and caps in one fight from the Monk's basics; for anyone else it decays first (pack 2 t13.6). Weaken is legible and large: boss hits -33% (pack 7, 1,262 to 849), near-permanent beside a Vampire. Might has fractional stacks (4.2, 5.4, 10.4); the Bard's 4.2 is +25% for four actions (pack 1), Frenzy's 15 lasts a fight; the label is stale ("might 15" while hits fall 822 to 341, pack 10). Haste doubles visibly (Monk, Silent One). Taunt works on both sides (pack 7: 7 of 12 hits on the Knight; pack 9: the Mummy covering its Liches). Guard reads against clubs (887, pack 3), not against Soulfire (pack 9 t24.8). Stun is 8 silent ticks, legible, and the window a Soulfire wound up in (pack 9 t13 to t21). Silence is a lock, 48 of 96 ticks (pack 10). Regen's 320 a pulse was fair (pack 6), Renew's 200 was not (pack 10). Clarity showed as +120/+240 pulses and kept a Vampire casting (pack 7). Shield soaks the first hit (Vanguard 1,300, packs 7, 9; Earthen shell 1,750, pack 6). Ward: one Hailstorm RESIST 777 (pack 7 t40.5); on Liches nothing. Bulwark 6 on the Titan: unchanged for 171 ticks, invisible. Bloodlust flickers each hit (pack 8 t9 to t35). Aegis, Hidden, Rot, Doom, Slow, Crust, Broken: never seen.

## 8. Mana

Mana reads as absent for most and punishing for one. The Vampire is MP-dead by t15 to t23 in all three fights it fought (pack 7: 37 at t22.6, short 74% of its time; pack 10: 48 at t14.8, 81%; pack 8: 0 from t29, drained): two 450 abilities on a 2,000 pool, then a 125 drain on Clarity's drip. The Paladin runs dry in long fights (pack 7: 38 at t134, 68% short, the last Smite waited for t170). The Stormcaller is hungry (pack 5: 3,000 to 847 after three casts; summary 15% short). For everyone else MP never bound: Knight 1,136 left after nine casts (pack 7), Monk and Valkyrie pinned at full by siphon ("MP +147 → 3,000" ten times, pack 6; pack 9), Hexer 5,124 of 6,000 (pack 5), Cleric 3,378 when it died (pack 10), Assassin 84% left and never short, Bramble knight 73%. Mana changed what a class did only for the Vampire (drain every 34 ticks instead of 14, pack 10) and the Paladin's last Smite. Clarity showed and mattered (pack 7). Refrain showed as 87 to 149 (pack 1). Siphon showed as a refund (Pyre returned 961 of 1,500, pack 8 t13). The Leeches made MP dramatic once (pack 8).

## 9. Classes

- Vampire: plays its text; drain heals 2,015 to 2,760, Weaken keeps bosses honest; half drain, half basics; the sustain engine (healing 16,954, pack 7). Spoiled by Blood gift and a dry bar.
- Knight: the wall and nothing else; 205 a swing, 4% to 6% of damage (packs 7, 9); Taunt works, Clarity feeds others. Flat.
- Paladin: Smite + Burn, Absolve, Sanctuary, basics 501 to 666, 33% to 54%; Judgement never fired in its packs. Best all-rounder.
- Bramble knight: Thorns, Bleed, Taunt; 14% to 21% in its packs (the summary's 46% did not show here); Lash+ 2,752 its one big hit.
- Monk: dominant (84%, 73%): Burn ramp, Pressure point + Stun, Haste, Sunder to cap; 567 taken in a fight that killed both tanks.
- Stormcaller: 52% to 64%, dead at t13.3 and t29.1; summary down at t22.3. The glass cannon exactly.
- Pyromancer: 51% to 56%, half Burn, dead at t17 and t21.7; Burn from the grave.
- Ranger: dead at t13.6 and t17.1; its Bleed did 5,120 posthumously (pack 6). A passenger by death.
- Hoplite: one swing in 30 ticks (pack 5); a statue with a 4,163 thrust.
- Hexer: 12% of damage but the fight's multiplier (Sunder 11, Weaken, the one shatter); summary down at t14.4, earliest of all.
- Cleric: a staff that out-hits the Bard (781 to 293, pack 1); Cure+ on itself three times, two deaths (pack 10); 100% basics.
- Bard: Might visible in numbers, 0% ability damage, safest class (11.3 KO per 100). Passenger.
- Frostweaver: 48% of a fight on staff basics, no Chill, no shatter (pack 1).
- Valkyrie: Shock cannon with Rally and Valhalla, 45% to 76% when alone; both Valhallas raised a unit that died in 7 and 22 ticks (packs 6, 10).
- Geomancer: 7%, Rockfall never landed, Shell once (pack 6).
- Assassin: 263 a poke on Armor 3, 729 crits with Bleed 7.9 (pack 4); never spends MP.

Worst-feeling three: Knight (pack 7), Geomancer (pack 6), Hoplite (pack 5). Best-feeling three: Monk (pack 9), Paladin (pack 7), Stormcaller (pack 5), the last with the caveat that it dies.

## 10. Enemies and bosses

Boss moves come often enough to be a story: Crush every 32 ticks (pack 4), Hailstorm every 29 (pack 7), Tolling every 20 and Quiet every 32 (pack 10). When they land they are a one-shot (Crush 4,434 and 4,632 above two classes' max HP; Soulfire 6,804 above a Knight's 6,500) or a third of everyone's HP that heals back (Hailstorm 1,296). The Silent One's ramp (1,893 to 3,208) is the best-read boss; the Ice Titan is a wall of HP (90,145 at Armor 6, 171 ticks). Elites: pack 9 is a real puzzle (a Taunt covering two Soulfires), pack 3 at its knob is a regular fight, pack 6 is harder than any boss. Minions act before dying (summary 87% to 100%), though Bandit A's one action was a miss (pack 4). The Bone Priest cannot die against a lone attacker (pack 6). Regions read: Chill labels through the Ice Pass, MP drain in the Storm Tower, dark and heals in the Crypt. Foes that felt like nothing: bandits at 216 a hit (pack 1), Dark ward (pack 9), the Titan's Bulwark (pack 7).

## 11. Drama

The best beat: Ice Spirit A dying to its own Shock 0.2 ticks after killing the Stormcaller (pack 5 t29.1). The cruelest: the Ranger's arrow landing 0.1 tick after its death (pack 2 t17.2); Rally ready 0.3 ticks after the Valkyrie fell (pack 8 t12.3). One-shots: Crush twice (pack 4), Soulfire twice (pack 9), a 1,462 crit on a half-HP Stormcaller (pack 3 t13.3), 2,654 on a 2,500 Cleric (pack 10 t65.3). A close call without doubt: the Knight at 570 with the Titan at 4% (pack 7 t160.7). No comebacks: both Valhallas bought 7 and 22 ticks. Decided early: pack 2 before t0, pack 8 by t17, pack 3 by t6.2. Long after decided: packs 4, 6, 8 and 10 each ran 22 to 30 ticks past their last real chance; pack 7 ran 40 past the point its result was clear.

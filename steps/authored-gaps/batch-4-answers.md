# Batch 4: the director's answers and the agent's read

Copied from the page's store (Nov 19). Each card: what was asked, the director's answer, and the agent's read. Then the director's reply and the agent's response, in order, as the page shows them. The open card wasn't answered. Cards 1 and 5 wait on the director's reply.

## Bonfire: a payoff for a high fire

**The director (answered):**

> When a unit's burn reaches X, remove all burn from it, dealing all the damage instantly and healing the party for that amount

**The agent's read (Question, waiting on the director):** Not placed yet. It breaks two of your own rulings, and I'd like your call.

Your vocabulary rulings say a status runs its own course: nothing spends it or sets it off early, and a payoff reads the stacks and leaves them. This removes the Burn and deals it at once. They also say a status's damage belongs to its holder, with no lifesteal from it. This heals the party by it.

It also changes what Bonfire is to watch. A fire that bursts at a number is Chill's shatter under another name: the party stops holding a fire high and starts counting to X. And it empties the foe, so the Torch has nothing to pass on, your mana for the foes' debuffs drops, and the pile starts again from nothing.

What I like: it's one clear moment, it would be the first heal that comes from a status, and with the pile a whole party's fire reaches X on one foe.

Two ways on. Keep it as written, and I rewrite the two rulings so a piece may do this. I'd then read "all the damage" as what those stacks would have dealt while they drained, and the heal as shared out across the party. Or give me one that reads the fire and leaves it burning. Which?

**The director's reply:**

> Ok you've convinced me, this isn't good.
>
> What about something simpler, is it enough of a payoff? I figure the pyromancer is squishy:
>
> Whenever burn ticks, heal for 30% (no idea what the right number is) of its damage.
>
> Or
>
> Take damage equal to 1.5% of max health every time burn ticks, gain a might stack for every 10 burn on the foe

**The agent's response (Question, waiting on the director):** The burst is dropped. Of the two new ones, the second is taken and the first left: a heal for a share of each Burn tick is lifesteal from a status, the ruling the burst broke, and an eleventh effect on a heal. The second reads how high the fire stands and leaves it burning. Read as: each time a foe's Burn ticks, the wearer loses HP and gains 1 Might for every 10 Burn on that foe. Asked, the price: Burn ticks every 2 ticks on every burning foe, so 1.5% of max HP a tick takes its wearer from full to nothing in about 130 ticks against one burning foe and about 35 against four, and it would be the ninth effect on HP paid or held low. The HP price sized small, no price, or another price?

## Silence from more hands

**The director (answered):**

> If above 30% health, when a unit start a cast, lose 30% health and silence them

**The agent's read (Placed):** Placed. I'm reading it as: while its wearer is above 30% HP, when a foe starts a wind-up, the wearer pays 30% of its max HP and Silences that foe, which loses the cast. Say if "a unit" meant something else.

It does the job. Silence now comes from any class, so your piece that counts a Silenced foe as a foe falling has every class for a partner. Together, a foe's cast becomes the party's opening again, at 30% HP a time.

What I've assumed. It pays only when the Silence lands: not on a foe that's already Silenced, and not on a boss that has come to shrug it off. The HP is a price, not damage, so it keeps Heat and leaves a Shield alone. It doesn't choose: it fires on the first foe to cast, up to three times from full HP.

Beside the rest: your Great that heals another ally when one pays HP hears every Silence, and Attack for HP missing reads the wearer afterward.

Two things to know. It does nothing against foes that only swing. The Hexer's Unmake has the same limit, so I've let it stand. And it names HP paid, which is on watch: eight effects now, one under the line. I've placed it because it needs HP to spend, which the pieces that hold a unit low don't leave it.

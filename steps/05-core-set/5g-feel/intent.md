## How this step reads the intent

- **The loop is the method:** a feel read (an agent reading rogue fights tick by tick) after every round of changes, with short simulations beside it; the read's findings drive the next round. Rogue first; the campaign only where a rogue finding points there.
- **Numbers, not behaviour.** Fix a problem by the amount applied, the cost, the cooldown, the duration, never by a new rule that changes what a thing does (a cap that binds on one hit, a target rule that excludes the caster). A behaviour change that could read as esoteric goes to the director first, one question at a time, as it comes up. 5f's Burn cap is such a sledgehammer and comes out.
- **The enemy side of 5f's intent** (buffs, taunts, multi-hits on more foes) is in scope and may be built without asking, as a secondary objective after the fights read right.
- **Win rates and class win-rate spreads are not the measure** in this step; subjective combat experience is. The knobs get re-tuned at the end, as before.
- The three standing rules hold: a status scales by its stacks, MP costs by hand, Aggro by draw.

# Step 5c tuning trail

One line a round. The audit (`src/campaign-audit.ts`) and the campfire oracle (`src/campaign-campfire.ts`) read each node's best builds (up to 9) on a few seeds at the knobs then shipped. Passes continue 5b's numbering.

1. **Baseline, 5b as shipped** (pass 14's builds, 6 seeds), with the audit's new MP section: time with an ability ready and no MP for it, the Vampire 32%, the Knight 27%, the Bramble knight and the Hoplite 23%; every caster 0% (the Pyromancer, Cleric, Frostweaver, Hexer, Stormcaller start every fight with 75% to 99% of their MP). The director's "the mana is inverted", in numbers. In regular fights the Paladin casts nothing in 60% of them, the Assassin 36%, the Stormcaller 24%.
2. **Built** (engine 0.11): the PoE armor at k = 1, Bleed (60 a stack, a half) on the Ranger, Briar, the Assassin and snakes, the slotted opening (25, 15, 10), ability costs × Magic ÷ 2.5, the three-way campfire (40% each), Burn and Shock stacks × 1.5 for the Pyromancer and Stormcaller, Shock as before, Thunder chord, Smite with Burn on a medium cooldown. On pass 14's builds and knobs: wins 95%, regular fights 46 ticks (were 37): Armor at k = 1 slows every physical blow. The casters still barely starve: their second and third abilities come off in a third of regular fights or fewer (the Pyromancer's Pyre never), so they don't spend.
3. **Armor's k**, same builds and knobs: wins 94% at k = 1, 89% at 0.5, 78% at 0.25, 61% at 0.15, 80% with the old flat cut and floor. k = 1 takes 92% off a minion's swing into a plated Knight (the floor took 75%); k = 0.25 takes 74% and keeps the old balance. **k = 0.25.**
4. **Cooldowns 24, 38 and 56** for the party (foes keep 30, 48, 70), **class MP regen halved**, costs × Magic ÷ 2.5: the casters starve now (the Geomancer 45%, the Cleric 34%, the Pyromancer 24%), but so do the Bramble knight (36%) and the Vampire (57%): halving regen hit the walls too. Basic attacks 61% of party actions (were 67%).
5. **Costs × (Magic − 1) ÷ 2, at least a quarter, class regen in full**: Magic 1 pays a quarter, 2 half, 3 the word, 4 one and a half, 5 double.
6. **Bleed's X and Y** (pass 14's builds): per application 460 at (60, ½), 699 at (60, ¼), 765 at (100, ½), 952 at (100, ⅓), 1,127 at (120, ⅓), 1,462 at (200, ½). At (120, ⅓) Bleed is 16% of the Ranger's damage, 20% of the Bramble knight's, 13% of the Assassin's. **120 and a third.**
7. **Burn and Shock multiplier**: the Pyromancer's Burn 20% of its damage at × 1.5, 24% at × 2, 27% at × 2.5; the Stormcaller's Shock 16%, 18%, 19%: Shock's cap of 25 binds. With the cap at 40 and 80 a stack, Shock is 26% of the Stormcaller's damage, 31% of the Bard's, 19% of the Valkyrie's. **× 2, Shock 80 capped at 40.**
8. **The campfire, first look** (pass 14's builds and knobs): every option wins in 88% of visits; REST is the only winner in 64 of 989, RAISE in 11 (of 79 offered), FOCUS in 5. Too easy a setting to judge; re-read after the re-tune, at the margin.
9. **Pass 15** (the whole road re-tuned on rounds 2 to 7, with two ability orders in the build space): the knobs rose (the Crypt from 69/11 to 107/106, the Storm Tower from 94/−2 to 187/31): the party got stronger. Basic attacks 58% of party actions (were 66%); party damage 42% basic, 47% abilities, 10% statuses in regular fights (14% in boss fights). Bleed 15% of the Ranger's damage, 27% of the Bramble knight's, 19% of the Assassin's; Burn 23% of the Pyromancer's; Shock 27% of the Stormcaller's, 32% of the Bard's. MP: the Magic-1 and Magic-2 classes never starve, the Pyromancer 16% (75% in its fifth fight), the Geomancer 15%, the Cleric 12%, the rest of the casters 0% to 3%; casters wear MP gear in their best builds (the Hexer and Bard always, the Cleric 47%, the Paladin 38%, the Stormcaller, Frostweaver and Geomancer about a quarter; under 5b, 0% to 8%). Shares while owned: the Vampire 65%, the Knight 57%, **the Stormcaller 57%** (was 23%); the Pyromancer 12%, the Frostweaver 13%, the Hexer 11%. The Paladin casts nothing in 73% of regular fights, the Hexer 55% (fights end before their slow abilities come; the search kept the documents' order for them).
10. **The campfire at pass 15's knobs** (8 seeds, every option played out): the choice decides the run in 29% of visits. REST is the only winning pick in 270, RAISE in 76 (of 288 visits with someone down; there RAISE wins 53% of runs, REST 27%), FOCUS in 30: the party reaches the fire with 74% to 86% of its MP. RAISE earns its place; FOCUS doesn't.
11. **Shock back to 60 a stack** (cap 40 kept): the Stormcaller overshot.
12. **Caster costs and FOCUS** (pass 15's builds and knobs): with Magic 4 paying double and 5 triple (were 1.5 and 2), the casters starve 15% to 36% of the time (the Cleric 33%, the Geomancer 36%, the Pyromancer 27%, the Stormcaller 16%, the Frostweaver 15%) and FOCUS-only wins rise from 30 to 47; with FOCUS at 60% of max MP, 68; at 100%, 77 to 87. REST at 30% only trades its wins to nobody. By the party's casters (Magic 4 or more), FOCUS is the only winning pick in 2 of 99 decided visits with none, 40 of 183 with one, 18 of 75 with two, 8 of 21 with three. **Magic 4 doubles, 5 triples; FOCUS 60%, REST and RAISE 40%.** The same builds win 68% at the old knobs (were 77%): the casters pay for it, and the re-tune follows.
13. **Pass 16** (round 12 re-tuned): the casters starve 10% to 37% of the time (the Geomancer 37%, the Cleric and Pyromancer 26%, the Frostweaver 11%, the Stormcaller 10%), the walls 0%; casters wear MP gear in their best builds (the Cleric 57%, the Paladin 55%, the Geomancer 47%, the Stormcaller 35%, the Frostweaver 33%). The Stormcaller still in 52% of top builds; the Pyromancer 10%. **A regression:** at the director's 25, 15 and 10, every short ability in a first slot (cooldowns 20 to 28) fires on the first tick, the whole party at once, and 19% of minions die before acting (35% in boss fights); 5b had it at 6%. The director's own complaint from 5b, back.
14. **Head starts** (pass 16's builds and knobs): minions killed before acting 19% at 25/15/10, 11% at 20/12/6, 9% at 18/10/5, 5% with the old roll; party actions sharing a tick 5%, 1%, 0%, 0%. Wins at the same knobs 78%, 60%, 46%, 56%: the opening volley is a large part of the party's power, which the re-tune hands back. **20, 12 and 6.** The Pyromancer's costs 2.5 times (it was the weakest class before tripling) and its Burn stacks 2.5 times (the Stormcaller's Shock stays doubled).
15. **Pass 17**: the Stormcaller 40% of top builds, the Pyromancer 15%; the Vampire 75%, the Knight 59%; the Hexer 9%, the Frostweaver 12%. Basic attacks 62% of party actions, minions killed before acting 15%, shared ticks 1%, abilities chained 17%. Counted only for members who start a fight standing (the audit counted the knocked out before), the Paladin casts nothing in 65% of regular fights, the Assassin 62%, the Hexer 50%, the Bard 34%: kits of long cooldowns, which come off after a 41-tick fight is over.
16. **Cooldowns** (pass 17's builds and knobs): at 24, 34 and 46, the Assassin's no-cast share 45%, the Paladin's 56%, the Hexer's 37%, the Bard's 23%; at 24, 32 and 42, 38%, 52%, 30%, 24%, with basic attacks 57% of party actions and the casters starving more (the Cleric 34%, the Geomancer 41%, the Pyromancer 22%, the Paladin 14%, the Frostweaver 13%). **24, 32 and 42.**
17. **Pass 18** (round 16 re-tuned; shipped): basic attacks 57% of party actions (5b 66%); party damage 44% basic, 47% abilities, 8% statuses in regular fights (12% in boss fights); shared ticks 0%; abilities chained 16% (5b 22%); minions killed before acting 11% (5b 6%: the price of the slotted opening). Regular fights a member starts standing with no cast: the Paladin 42% (5b 59%), the Hexer 24% (19%), the Assassin 21% (33%), the Bard 17% (13%), everyone else 11% or less; slot 1's first cast comes at tick 3 to 17 (5b 14 to 33). MP starved: the Geomancer 45%, the Cleric 36%, the Pyromancer 27%, the Paladin and Frostweaver 20%, the Stormcaller 14%, the Valkyrie 5%, the Hexer 3%; the walls 0% to 2%. Casters wear MP gear in their best builds (the Bard and Hexer always, the Cleric and Paladin half, the Geomancer, Frostweaver and Stormcaller a third; the Pyromancer never). Bleed 29% of the Bramble knight's damage, 17% of the Assassin's, 11% of the Ranger's; Burn 22% of the Pyromancer's; Shock 21% of the Stormcaller's, 33% of the Bard's. Shares while owned: the Vampire 78%, the Knight 67%, the Valkyrie 41%, the Stormcaller 31%; the Hexer 9%, the Pyromancer and Frostweaver 11%. What each class adds to a build's wins (with − without, mean over nodes): the Vampire +26 (Blood Moon forces it), the Knight +18 (5b +17), the Valkyrie +10, the Stormcaller +7; the Cleric +5 (5b +17: gated by MP now); the Hexer −9, the Pyromancer −7, the Frostweaver −5.
18. **The campfire at pass 18's knobs**: the choice decides the run in 31% of visits; REST is the only winning pick in 287, FOCUS in 49, RAISE in 44 (with someone down RAISE wins 57% of runs, REST 43%). By casters in the party: none, REST 111, FOCUS 0, RAISE 8; one, 137, 31, 24; two, 36, 17, 12. The simulations' picker chose a winning option in 97% of the visits where one existed.
19. **Prices, at pass 18's knobs** (the same builds, 8 seeds): shipped 79%; Armor at k = 1, 93% (the Tower Top 42%: its foes hit with magic, which Armor never meets, while the party's blows into the Soldiers' plate shrink); the old flat cut, 80%; head starts of 25, 15 and 10, 89% (the opening volley; 19% of minions dead before acting); the old roll, 71%; class MP regen at zero (gear only), 21%: at these prices every class, the walls too, lives on its regen, so "zero across the board" would need costs cut about fourfold and makes the pool the run's whole budget.
20. **The Pyromancer at double costs** (as Magic 4; pass 18's builds that use it, 12 seeds): 71% against 67%. Its trouble is surviving (23 knockouts per 100 fights it's in; the Knight 10), not its price.
21. **The sixth AI playtest of the road, the first of 5c** (pass 18; a fresh agent, the game as a command line, player-visible information only): 20 of 20 nodes in 27 attempts (5b's took 51), never more than two at a node. It found Bleed a side dish on the Ranger and the Assassin, the Bramble knight dying in both its runs; the Paladin unable to afford its kit (two spells of 2,000 MP on a 3,000 pool), the Cleric never casting Raise (3,200 MP, starved by Cure and Renew in front of it), the Hexer at 351 MP after one fight (Hush 2,000); the Bard a passenger (Thunder chord at 10 stacks); the Monk the carry from node 10 (2 to 3 times anyone's damage); the game's FOCUS pick costing it node 10, after which it wanted "REST, or RAISE if someone's down"; Fireball first but Firebolt still first (the head start is in ticks). Favourite moments: the Vampire soloing node 17's boss, Valhalla turning node 11.
22. **Campfire pickers** (pass 18, the 407 visits where the pick decides the run): the simulations' picker (FOCUS when the party is shorter of MP than HP) wins 363, "REST, or RAISE if someone's down" 343, always REST 305. FOCUS is worth 20 runs in 400 to a picker that takes it at the right time.

24. **The seventh AI playtest, the second of 5c** (pass 19, the same brief): 19 of 20 nodes in 56 attempts, stuck on node 18 (Judgement: the Imp Rally's cheered ogres killed its Paladin wall eight times; the Fire Giant left at 14% and 35%), node 19 on its eighth try. Two playtests, 27 and 56 attempts: the road's difficulty depends on whether a player finds the narrow answers. Bleed read well on foes and the Ranger felt good (10 to 26 stacks); the Bramble knight did nothing for either playtester and died early in all four runs it was given; the Assassin's crit Bleed stayed small. The campfire after the first of two regular fights (nodes 19 and 20) is a non-choice.

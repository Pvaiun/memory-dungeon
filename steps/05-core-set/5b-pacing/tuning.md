# Step 5b tuning trail

One line a round. "Top builds" are the tuner's top 10 per node (175 across the road); the audit reads each node's best builds (up to 9) on 6 seeds at the shipped knobs.

## Part 1: the director's three changes

1. **Built:** the Cleric (Renew, Cure, Raise; Encore), the Bard (Refrain), Stun pauses for 5 ticks, a campfire before each campaign boss (40% HP or 40% MP). Engine 0.8.
2. **Probe at pass 9's knobs, same builds:** wins 80% (was 78%). Cure heals about 32,000 a run where Mercy healed 24,000, and the Cleric still starts fights with 73% to 93% of its MP: a small heal on a 6-tick cooldown fired almost all the time. Crush now lands 95% of the time (was 82%; 64% against a Knight in Prayer beads), Inferno 67%: only Silence (Garrote, Hush) interrupts now.
3. **Re-tuned on the nine Cleric-heavy nodes:** the Cleric still in 77 of 90 top builds; the Knight 30 (was 40). The knobs rose by 10 to 33 points: the party got stronger (the campfire). Stun and the campfire work; the Cleric's new kit doesn't yet.
4. **Two Cure fixes** on five of those nodes (old pacing): Cure at 0.12 of Magic, the Cleric in 39 of 50 top builds (was 46); with its MP regen halved as well, 29 of 50. MP is what limits a healer. Superseded by Part 2, which slows every ability.

## Part 2: pacing and value

5. **The audit, as shipped (pass 9):** see `design.md`. The party acts twice for each foe action and casts two thirds of the time; a swing is the size of most abilities; a Poison is a fifth of its arrow; 21% of party actions share a tick and 58% of abilities chain.
6. **First cut** (cadence per weapon kind, cooldowns 30/48/70 with a per-ability jitter, opening charge, abilities about twice as big, area spells a light hit a foe, stacks from abilities doubled, buff and debuff durations doubled, MP costs up, crit 4% a point), at pass 9's knobs: basic attacks 67% of party actions (was 32%), party to foe 1.3 to 1 (was 2), actions sharing a tick 0% (was 21%), abilities chained 23% (was 58%). The party wins 16% at the old knobs: foes act as often as it does now, and most knockouts are minions' swings.
7. **Poison scaled from its applier's Attack** for one round. The director's rule: a status never scales from its applier; a stackable status scales by the stacks applied, an unstackable one by nothing. Poison is flat again, 400 a pulse for 10 ticks (was 160 for 12), and Venom arrow seeks a foe that isn't Poisoned.
8. **The audit attributes statuses to their applier** (the log now names who applied a status, and a pulse's source), and shows each source's size per use against the class's own swing: the eyeball table the director asked for.
9. **Pass 10** (the whole road re-tuned on the first cut): the knobs fell (the Crypt from +249 to +82, the Ossuary from +190 to +46): foes don't need padding to threaten a party that swings. Every node's best build 88% to 100%. The Cleric in 71 of 175 top builds (was 101 in pass 9), builds with no healer 56 (was 35); the Monk 64 (was 27: fast weapons with on-hit effects gain most when swings are most of a fight), the Knight 88 (was 80). Audit: 67% basic, 1.4 to 1 in regular fights and 1.3 to 1 in boss fights, 0% shared ticks, 20% chained; party damage 57% basic, 37% abilities, 5% statuses in regular fights; a regular fight costs the party 35% of its max HP and lasts 37 ticks, minions act about three times; a boss fight lasts 123 ticks, 3.3 times a regular one, and 179 of 248 losses are there.
10. **Boss round:** bosses at 55% of their document HP; their signature moves on a medium cooldown (Crush, Inferno, Death bell, Tempest, Call drones, Rally); Shield bash a light hit (it was 7.7 of the Knight's swings); Venom arrow a 0.15 tap so its Poison is the damage. Re-tune (pass 11).
11. **Pass 11** (the boss round re-tuned): a boss fight lasts 82 ticks (was 123), 2.2 times a regular one (was 3.3); fights reaching the enrage 9 (was 69); the median biggest boss hit is still 57% of a member's max HP. The action and pacing numbers held (67% basic, 1.4 to 1, 0% shared ticks, 21% chained). The Ranger's Poison pulse is a third of its arrow's tap (was a ninth). Shares of top builds while owned: the Vampire 58% (Blood Moon forces it), the Knight 50%, the Bramble knight 46%, the Cleric 39% (was 58% in pass 9), the Monk 39%; the Pyromancer 9%, the Frostweaver 10%, the Geomancer 14%, the Ranger 15%. Knockouts per 100 fights a class is in: the Bard 52, the Stormcaller 31, the Pyromancer 26, the Assassin 25; the Knight 9, the Vampire 3.
12. **Class round:** Armor can't take a hit below a quarter of its size (`ruleset.armorFloor`, engine 0.10): plate on a Knight blanked every weak and normal swing, so minions couldn't touch it. Haste 14 ticks (the Monk's own Flow kept it Hasted two thirds of a fight). Bramble lash 6 × Thorns (was 9). Re-tune (pass 12).
13. **Pass 12** (the class round re-tuned): the class shares barely moved (the Knight 49%, the Vampire 60%, the Bramble knight 46%, the Cleric 43%; the Pyromancer 10%, the Frostweaver 9%), so the Knight's lead isn't its armor alone. A boss fight 80 ticks, 2.2 times a regular one; the enrage reached in 4 fights. Squishy classes die most: knockouts per 100 fights the Bard 46, the Pyromancer 27, the Stormcaller 18, the Assassin 18; the Vampire 2.
14. **Reading the Old Road by eye** (its best build, seed 3): bandits at 1,600 HP dying to two swings, wolves to one, minions hitting for 37 to 70, a snake's Poison at 400 a pulse the biggest threat. The cause is the tuner, not the content: one knob for every foe, set so the Ogre Chief can be beaten (−60 at node 1), leaves the regular fights soft: the director's "the bandits at the start do 2 damage to you".
15. **Two knobs per node:** the tuner sets the regular fights first, alone (the boss left out): the hardest at which two and a half times the node's share of builds win half their runs of them and the best win 95%; then the boss, on full runs, at the node's share and 85%. A dungeon's `bossFoes` applies to its boss fight in place of `foes`. On nodes 1 and 4: −40 / −47 and +64 / +86.
16. **Poison 250 a pulse.** At 400 the Old Road's snakes, which Poison with every bite and aren't weakened by the knob, stopped the starters in 35 runs of 40 at the snake pit and the fight after it, with the regular fights at −30; at 200, in 10.
17. **The campfire in the middle of the run** (after half the regular fights, rounded up), as the director asked, not before the boss: it splits the run's attrition in two, so each regular fight can hit harder. Node 1's regular-fight knob rose from −60 to −40 with it. Re-tune (pass 13).
18. **Pass 13** (two knobs a node, the campfire in the middle): the regular fights' knob now sits above the boss's on most nodes (the Fire Mine +20 and −57, Knives Out +28 and −62, the Royal Court +103 and −56). Losses split 141 in regular fights and 151 in boss fights (was 73 and 154); a regular fight costs the party about half its max HP (minions 39%, elites 8%), minions live 26 ticks and 6% die before acting (was 11%); a boss fight is 1.7 times a regular one. But a boss eased to −55 dealt 55% less damage too: its biggest hit's median fell to 38% of a member's max HP (was 57%), the director's "they do so little damage". Shares while owned: the Vampire 70%, the Knight 54%, the Cleric 49%, the Paladin 43%; the Hexer, the Pyromancer 9%, the Frostweaver 10%. Squishy classes fall to the regular fights' chip: knockouts per 100 fights the Pyromancer 32, the Bard 31, the Stormcaller 28.
19. **The knob's easy side mirrors its hard side:** a negative k takes k% off foes' HP and k/2 % off their damage (it took k% off both), so an eased boss is a shorter fight, not a softer one. Re-tune (pass 14).
20. **Pass 14** (the mirrored knob): losses 151 in regular fights and 127 in boss fights; a boss fight 64 ticks, 1.5 times a regular one; most knockouts are swings' chip (1,048 of 1,576), then a boss move's spike (240); the party still has 61% of its MP when it loses, and 4 fights in 990 runs reach the enrage. The pacing numbers held (66% basic, 1.4 to 1, 0% shared ticks, 22% chained). Six bosses sit at or near the knob's floor (the Fire Giant on three nodes, the Iron Gate, the Hive Queen, the Royal Court): their designed moves are too much for their nodes after harder regular fights, even at a fraction of their HP. Shares while owned: the Vampire 68%, the Knight 55%, the Cleric 48%, the Paladin 43%; the Pyromancer 10%, the Hexer and the Frostweaver 9%. Knockouts per 100 fights: the Pyromancer 30, the Bard 30, the Stormcaller 27; the Knight 11, the Vampire 4.
21. **Sixth AI playtest** (pass 14): 20 of 20 cleared in 51 attempts, none stuck (the fifth took 34 on the old pacing); node 10 on the last allowed try. Fights play out over time; statuses carry the classes built on them (Sunder 50 on the Ice Titan, Burn 30 to 40 stacks, Shock 10,000 to 17,000 a long fight); 18 of 31 losses in regular fights. Wrong: the campfire is always REST (casters keep 70% to 90% of their MP; tanks run low); supports often don't act in 15 to 25 tick fights (first casts at tick 10 to 15); bosses uneven (the Fire Giants fold, the Long Road's Reaper and the Tower Top's mid-run Storm Queen wall); Bulwark's chips, Doom, the Storm King's anger don't show. Fixed at once: the campfire default rests if anyone standing is below a third of their HP; Sunstaff, Heartwood staff and the Long Road's texts no longer name things the player can't see.

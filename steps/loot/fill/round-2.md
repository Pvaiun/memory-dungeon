# The fill, round 2: the lead's rulings

Work in progress: what the agents raised while applying round 1, and what the fresh reader finds. Sent once the fresh read is in.

## For every share

- **A hit that lands a second time lands at once, unless its card says otherwise.** Twinfall's lands a moment later (one tick), as its line says.
- **A Mark steers blows, not what a piece sends.** A phrase's fallback to a random foe follows the Mark for a basic attack or an ability. Where a piece sends a status or left-over damage to a random foe (Winter's chain with nothing chilled, Overrun's carry), it goes by draw and a Mark doesn't take it.
- **A counter's bracket may be worded to fit the card** ("each acts 5% faster…" where every ally gains the Heat), as long as the numbers are the counter's.

- **Healing received is all of it:** a heal, a Regen's pulse, a drink. Round 1's ruling that a Regen isn't a heal is about the heals a unit *gives* ("its wearer's heals"). A card that reads healing its wearer *receives* (Red stone mail, Sanguine mail, Last bastion, Halved heart, Shared cup) counts Regen.
- **Firing as a fight starts isn't a start-of-fight effect** when it's a crossing: a piece that fires because its wearer began under the line (Holdfast, Warded mail, Aegis mail) doesn't happen again under Second dawn or Overture coat.

- **A killing blow is a hit.** A foe felled by damage sent back, by Thorns or by a status's own damage isn't anyone's killing blow. Cards that wait for a foe to fall, whoever felled it, still fire.
- **The crossing ruling is for HP.** A watcher of MP keeps its own card's words.

- **Where left-over damage lands is a target after the first,** so a Taunt doesn't decide it, as it doesn't decide a chain's later foes. Round 1's ruling stands otherwise: it's a hit on the foe it reaches, and that foe can dodge it.
- **A free cast costs nothing, in MP or in HP.** "Costs no MP" on a card means the cast is free, so under an HP price it costs no HP either. New cards say "is free"; a kept card may keep today's words.

## Weapons, the first six kinds

- **Rimed blade adds a few Chill, read off its wearer's max HP.** At several from Attack it laid a shatter every second swing on a Hexer, beside Cold hex, and held a lone foe still all fight. From max HP it's seen on a Knight, which is the wearer its line names first, and a Hexer's stays a few. The spine's line changes.
- **Bellows wraps adds a twentieth of the fire a hit,** not a tenth. At a tenth, about seven hits in eight ticks on one foe made the fire grow with nobody feeding it, and Burn has no cap: a loop whose only brake was the foe's death. At a twentieth no build reaches it, Everflame lantern beside it included. The spine's line changes.
- **Overkill axe's left-over** isn't turned by a Taunt (the ruling above).
- **Lamprey claws at a tenth, Victor's spear's Aggro, the four spears' new steps, Forked knuckles counting Arc's second foe, Reaver's axe's Might from Attack, the dagger's commons:** as written.

## Versions and mythics

- **Malison's power step grows the damage and every status the cast adds** by two fifths: the Bleed, and on Plague its Burn and Shock too.
- **Stormspear's blow has Seek.** Gale spear charges the back row past a Taunting front, and the blow that reads that charge shouldn't land on the guard. The spine's line changes.
- **Frost fist's "a few"** is the scale's few, not today's smaller size: it's Cold Snap's first step from a kit, and it has to be seen.
- **Reprise keeps today's "the longest cooldown left".** The spine's line gains the word. Reveille means the slowest ability, so the two differ by more than their moment.
- **Drinking palm loses its red stone tag:** nothing a Monk can wear or sit in reads its heal as a Shield.
- **Huntmaster's quiver:** the spine's line now reads "ready again", as the card does.
- **Writ of judgement answering Condemn in any trained version, Stormspear with no Shock on the field, Splinters' sums:** as written.

## Offhands, the other seven kinds

## Formations

- **Toll gate's two added sentences, Twins passing a debuff a unit lays on itself, Decoy's card, Spur's Opener at 20 points with no ceiling:** as written.
- **The covenant:** "Lifesteal, high" is the fill's size for the spine's "every ally has Lifesteal". No mismatch.

## Amulets

- **Clear-headed charm:** the first card comes back. While its wearer is below half its MP it gains Clarity every so often, the first at once. Fired once a crossing, it's worth least to the driest caster, which is the one it's for.
- **Halved heart under the crossing ruling:** yes. Its wearer starts every fight under the line, so the pieces that watch for a drop fire on it as the fight starts, and a "whenever" one doesn't fire again. It's the brink doing what it's for; nothing goes on the card.
- **Gambler's coin** beside a card that raises Crit chance "to four in five at most": as written. The raise adds to the coin's half and stops at four in five.
- **Burning heart, Downhill charm, Shatterstone charm:** as written.

## Offhands, the first six kinds

- **Phylactery names Regen:** the part of its holder's heals and of its Regen's pulses that would go past full becomes a Shield on the healed ally. With nothing to mend a healer casts only Regen, so without it the piece's own sight never happens. It's what today's piece does.
- **Charnel icon, Bloodstone reliquary** heal the lowest share, capped or not: healing past a cap counts as healing past full, which the red stone's pieces read. Stands.
- **The changed commons, Hoplon's steps, Shuttered lantern's steps, Everflame lantern's "every 3 ticks":** as written.

## Armor

- **Mail's commons** stand as written: Attack, Lifesteal and HP.
- **Red stone mail** naming Regen stands (the ruling above).
- **Aegis mail set off by a price** goes to the director's sample: a payer that drinks back over a third gets an Aegis every cast or two. Its brakes are that an Aegis fizzles while one is held, and the crossing back.

## Weapons, the other six kinds

- **Frostward blade adds several Chill,** not a few. Read off the mage's Attack, a few is a shatter on a foe's fifth or sixth blow, and the frozen gate's link asks for "within a few". The spine's line changes.
- **Twinfall, Overrun, Winter's chain:** as written, by the rulings above. Winter's chain's fallback doesn't follow a Mark.

# Intent: classes and gear without Magic

The step after the one-kind-of-damage prototype (`steps/no-magic/`): what the classes and their gear become now that Attack is the only offensive stat. The weapons are settled (`weapons.md`). What's left: the class stat lines, the offhands, and the ability upgrades.

The director's direction, as recorded from the weapons conversation (`weapons.md` has all of it):

- **A class's identity is its kit first,** then its gear kinds, then its stats. The stat line is a template, and gear can stretch a class far from it. A few roles are off-limits by identity: the Hoplite is never a healer, the Pyromancer never a tank.
- **Toughness is designed class by class.** Frailty is an identity for some classes; the Assassin is never really tough.
- **No "caster" tier.** The elemental classes all feel like different-coloured mages. Pick a couple to be frail spellcasters and let others be sturdier. Renames, new art and changed abilities are allowed.
- **The stat lines should follow how each class stays alive.**
- **Everything scales from the user,** never from the target, including heals.
- **Special stats are not reserved:** widespread where the classes want them; "odd" is about theme.
- **Upgrades keep their format:** a plain power step and two versions, the same for every class and ability. The versions can be redesigned. Upgrades are the strongest commitment lever. Scaling can be part of a version's identity, never the whole of it.
- **The upgrade audit:** check that every class's roles can be reached through its versions. The Knight reads the sword's Attack only through its swing; fix it with a version that scales from Attack.
- **All gear has stats, and higher rarity is generally better.** Many pieces carry both stats and effects. Gear must be balanced on its own.
- **Every class has a one-hander,** so it can always use an offhand.

**Offhands: no intent recorded yet.** The one word so far is a rejection: no soft job per slot ("armor for staying up, offhands for what you add"), and the weapon method is not to be pattern-matched onto armor and offhands. The questions are in `report.md`.

# Step 5e findings: what the balance sheet found, before any fix

Ranked worst first. Every number is from `npm run balance` on the numbers as they stood on Oct 27, 2026 (`sheet-before.md` is the whole sheet): 160 parties (80 trios, each once wearing a rotation of every piece its classes can wear and once in the campaign tuning's sensible kit, in all ten formations) played every campaign node at its frozen knobs, the seven free-play runs at the documents' numbers, and all 100 rogue fights at their calibrated knobs, twice each; plus 186,000 probe runs that put every piece of gear alone on every class that can wear it beside the same companions, and the same forty parties in every formation. Win rates below are context, never the measure: a class's "win ±pts" is the win rate of parties with it minus parties without it, each class in about the same number of parties.

Where three numbers are given they are campaign / free play / rogue.

## The principle, and what the sheet judged against

Every option is a real choice: nothing useless, dominated or required, and nothing wins on its own. The bands come from that and from what the documents say each thing is for; each is written down with its reason at the end of the sheet. 61 flags came up. They cluster into the findings below.

## 1. The Vampire is required

Parties with the Vampire win **+37 / +45 / +18 points** more than parties without it (the band is ±8; the next class is the Valkyrie at +17). It deals **1.9×** an average unit's damage in the campaign, heals itself **3,850 a fight** through Lifesteal (the Cleric heals the whole party 1,430), and is knocked out **13 times in 100 fights**, the least by far (the Valkyrie 23, the walls 32 to 36). It also draws 1.3× an average unit's hits because it's the one left standing.

Why: Blood drain is a normal hit from **max HP** (4,000 to 5,500 with gear), on a 24-tick cooldown for 200 MP, cast twice a regular fight and three to four times a boss fight: 60% of its damage at 4 swings a cast, with Weaken on top. Lifesteal high (30%) heals a third of all that, doubled under half HP. Lifesteal is 52% of all the healing on the party in the campaign. The class's sustain is its own damage, so every damage number feeds it twice.

## 2. Caravan is the formation, because Regen is too big

The same forty parties win **+20.5 points** in Caravan against Neutral (the band is ±7). Escort is **+11.2**, Blitz **+8.1**; every other formation sits within ±5. Caravan's effect is Regen on every ally at every fight's start: 12 pulses of 3% of max HP over 24 ticks, **36% of each unit's max HP a fight, for the whole party, for nothing**, in fights that last 23 to 35 ticks. That is more healing than a Cleric casting Cure and Renew. The same status drives the Herald's horn, the one piece of gear outside its band (**+8.9 points** against the amulets' mean, +16 on a Paladin), and makes Renew and Regrowth most of what the Cleric and Bramble knight give.

Escort's edge is its two Armor-and-Aggro slots plus a Shield for the survivors when one falls: moderate slots with nothing taken away, as the document says, and the document already asked whether Escort's effect was too much (it was trimmed once in 5a).

## 3. The Bard is a passenger

**−16 / −30 / −21 points.** It deals 0.37× an average unit's damage (two thirds plain dagger swings, a third Thunder chord's Shock), it's knocked out **51 times in 100 fights** (second worst) at tick 20, and its songs come late and seldom: Allegro 0.8 casts a regular fight, War ballad 0.37, Thunder chord 0.4, each first landing around tick 26 of a 31-tick fight. War ballad does put 19 Might on the hardest hitter when it lands, and Thunder chord 21 Shock on every foe; the trouble is a 2-HP, Armor-2 unit on 34- to 37-tick cooldowns that dies before its second song. Refrain hands MP to allies who are at 98%.

## 4. The Hexer is a passenger

**−10 / −16 / −14 points.** Knocked out **60 times in 100 fights**, the worst of all (HP 2, Armor 1), at tick 19. All of its damage is staff swings (0.38× an average unit). Hush 0.46 casts a regular fight (1,600 MP), Enfeeble 0.65, Unravel 0.22 (it waits for a buffed foe; most foes never buff). Its Siphon works (it regains twice the MP a Frostweaver does), and Chill from Cold hex is 0.41 applications a fight. One curse on one foe is thin against fights of four to eight.

## 5. The Assassin is a passenger

**−9 / −26 / −12 points.** Knocked out **41 times in 100 fights** at tick 17, the earliest death after the Pyromancer, despite drawing only 0.6× an average unit's damage per tick (Aggro 1, Evasion medium). It casts 1.2 abilities a regular fight, the fewest with the Paladin: Vanish 0.18 (a 47-tick cooldown; its first cast comes at tick 29, when the fight is nearly over) and Garrote 0.18 (45 ticks). Backstab is fine (5.5 swings a cast, 45% of its damage, Lacerate's Bleed on its crits).

## 6. The Hoplite is a wall that doesn't draw, with a thrust its own swing beats

**−8 / −20 / −7 points.** It takes **0.98×** an average unit's damage per tick (a wall's band starts at 1.1): Aggro 3 is an ordinary unit's, so the bodyguard is hit like anyone. Spear thrust deals **0.9 of its own basic attack** (Phalanx triples the swing, and the thrust is a normal hit that isn't tripled), so the ability is dominated by not casting it, yet it's 42% of the Hoplite's damage because it's cast 1.2 times a fight. Aspis's Aegis comes 0.87 times a regular fight.

## 7. The Paladin barely casts, draws like anyone, and is never short of MP

**−7 / −6 / +2 points.** 1.2 casts a regular fight, the fewest of any class: Smite has an 8-tick wind-up on a 31-tick cooldown (0.57 casts a fight, 58% of its damage at 7.5 swings), Sanctuary a 4-tick wind-up on 38 ticks (0.53), and Absolve waits for a debuff that seldom comes (0.05). It takes 0.99× an average unit's damage (Aggro 3) and spends **0%** of its time short of MP: 5c's "casters hungry" doesn't reach it (nor the Bard 0%, the Valkyrie 1%, the Pyromancer 1%).

## 8. Bosses die before their signature move

In free play, at the documents' numbers, a boss fight lasts **46 ticks**, and the signature moves the enemies document wants "two or three times a fight" come once or less: the Ogre Chief's Crush **0.5** times a fight, the Silent One's Quiet 0.83, the Storm Queen's Tempest 0.86, the Reaper's Death bell 0.87, the Storm King's Rally 0.89, the Ice Titan's Hailstorm 0.95; only the Fire Giant's Inferno (1.45) and Brand clear the band. Crush has an 8-tick wind-up on a 48-tick cooldown that starts a third to two thirds charged, against a boss at 55% of its written HP (5b) that a geared party kills in 40 ticks. In the campaign the Ogre Chief casts Crush 0.24 times a fight: its node's boss knob is −88.

## 9. Minions die before they act

In free play **42% of Wisps, 36% of Drones, 33% of Wolves and Snow Wolves, 29% of Skeletons and 25% of Hellhounds** are killed before they act (the band stops at 25%; 5c had it at 12%). The first-slot abilities fire at ticks 2 to 6 (Firebolt 2.5, Blood drain 4.6, Flow 4.9, Chain lightning 5.3, Gale spear 5.7, Barbed arrow 5.9) and regular fights at the documents' numbers last 23 ticks. The campaign's knobs (+70% to +148% HP on most nodes) hide it: there it's 12% to 24%.

## 10. Three abilities never fire, and three more barely

Casts per regular fight in which a standing member had it slotted (the band starts at 0.45), and per boss fight (0.9):

| Ability | regular | boss | why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pyromancer's Pyre | 0.01 | 0.05 | 47-tick cooldown, 8-tick wind-up, 2,000 MP, in the third slot (6 ticks of head start) |
| Stormcaller's Static charge | 0.02 | 0.05 | 44 ticks, 4-tick wind-up, 1,200 MP, for a Haste on itself |
| Frostweaver's Stillness | 0.04 | 0.14 | 46 ticks, 1,600 MP, for Clarity on a random ally (Clarity: 0.01 applications a fight) |
| Assassin's Vanish | 0.18 | 0.4 | 47 ticks; first cast at tick 29 |
| Assassin's Garrote | 0.18 | 0.34 | 45 ticks |
| Paladin's Absolve | 0.05 | 0.09 | waits for a debuff |

The "long" cooldown (42, moved up to 48 by the jitter) plus a third-slot head start of 6 ticks puts an ability's first cast at tick 36 to 44, in fights of 31 ticks (campaign) to 52 (rogue bosses).

## 11. Third-slot abilities seldom fire in boss fights

14 abilities come less than 0.9 times a boss fight: Fire arrow 0.66, Rending arrow 0.87, Fireball 0.52, Blizzard 0.71, Thunderstorm 0.65, Thunder chord 0.65, War ballad 0.65, Tremor 0.57, Sanctuary 0.72, Smite 0.81, Fireball 0.52. A boss fight is 37 ticks in the campaign, 46 in free play and 52 in the rogue; a medium ability (32 ticks) in the third slot first fires at tick 26 plus its wind-up. The documents' "a boss fight is about one and a half regular fights long" and "every slotted ability should come" don't both hold at these lengths.

## 12. Casters aren't hungry

Over whole campaign runs the party regains **110% of the MP it spends**. Time with a spell ready and no MP for it: the Geomancer 14% (the only one in 5c's intended band), the Cleric 4%, the Stormcaller 3%, the Frostweaver 3%, the Hexer 3%, the Pyromancer 1%, the Valkyrie 1%, the Paladin and Bard 0%. The casters end a campaign run with 46% to 73% of their MP, the walls with 96% to 98%. Hand-set costs are the director's rule; the costs as set don't gate the casters' casting, except the Geomancer's.

## 13. Three more classes carry their parties

The Valkyrie **+17 / +32 / +11** (Gale spear 1.5 casts a regular fight: three Seek hits with Shock at 2.7 swings each, 47% of its damage; Rally heals 1,735 a fight, more than the Cleric; knocked out 23 in 100). The Stormcaller **+12 / +15 / +9**, the top dealer at **1.83×** an average unit (Chain lightning on four foes is 43% to 49% of its damage; Thunderstorm fires 0.27 times a regular fight on 1,200 MP). The Bramble knight **+10 / +15 / +11**: a wall that deals 0.89× an average unit (Thorns 26%, Bleed 18%) while taking 1.66×, doing the Knight's job with twice the Knight's damage. The Monk **+6 / +20 / +8**.

## 14. The Knight is a weak wall

**−3 / −9 / +1 points.** Deals 0.41× an average unit (the lowest with the Cleric, Bard and Hexer), draws 1.35× an average unit's hits (Aggro 5 is 44% of three foes' picks against two ordinary units), is knocked out **32 times in 100 fights**, as often as a Ranger, and casts Protect 0.46 times a regular fight (a 40-tick cooldown, the director's pick to try). The Bramble knight does its job better on every line.

## 15. Two enemies outside their rank

The **Bomb Bug** deals **4.85×** the median minion's damage per appearance in free play (Volatile's blast, 2,000 to 2,800 to the whole party each) and knocks out 1.8 party members a fight: the Bomb Room is the deadliest regular encounter in the set. The **Ghoul** lives **2.7×** the median minion's ticks (Raise 1 to 1.4 casts a fight, 60% to 70% of them lost for want of a corpse).

## 16. Gear is a sidegrade to the point of indifference

Probed alone on every class that can wear it, beside the same companions on the same fights, **every piece but one sits within ±3.5 points of its slot's mean**, and an empty armor or amulet slot costs about 1 to 3 points. Nothing is dominated and nothing is required, which the document asks for; but a piece worth one point of win rate is not a choice either. The exceptions: the Herald's horn +8.9 (Regen again), the War banner and Soul jar +3.4, Brigandine +11.5 on the Vampire alone, Mist claws −6 (−13 on a Vampire, as the document intends). The sensible kits do beat the rotation (the kit-against-rotation measure is in the next run). Resistances take 4% to 6% off the magical damage the party takes, so the three elemental charms do nothing anyone can feel; Evasion dodges 5% of hits on the party; Accuracy has one foe to answer (the Bat).

## 17. Control is rare

Applications a fight on foes: Stun 0.19, Silence 0.07, Slow 0.08, Weaken 0.35, Chill 0.41, Rot 0.26. Stuns hold a wind-up 0.12 times per application, Silence interrupts 0.07. The damage statuses are where 5c put them: Bleed 13% / 12% / 16% of its appliers' damage, Burn 11% to 15%, Shock 17% to 21%.

## What's within the bands

The Ranger (−2 / 0 / +1), the Cleric (−3 / −3 / −7), the Frostweaver (−8 / −16 / −5: fragile, 41 knockouts in 100), the Geomancer (−6 / −10 / +1), the Pyromancer (−10 / +8 / +5: knocked out 44 times in 100 at tick 13, the earliest of all, so it loses the campaign's long runs and wins the short fights), the Monk. Every regular enemy but the two above sits in its rank's bands. Bleed, Burn and Shock. Eight of the ten formations. Every status is applied somewhere except Clarity (Stillness never fires).

## The fix order

Upstream first, as asked: (1) how often units act and what they can afford: the opening volley, the long cooldown and the wind-ups that stop six abilities, fight length against bosses' moves, the casters' MP; (2) what actions do: Regen's size, Blood drain and Lifesteal, Spear thrust, the Bard's and Hexer's abilities, the Bomb Bug's blast; (3) who's worth bringing: the Vampire, the Bard, Hexer and Assassin's survival, the Hoplite's and Paladin's Aggro, the Knight. Knobs stay frozen; each round re-runs the sheet.

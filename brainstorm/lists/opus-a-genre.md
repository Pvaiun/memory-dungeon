# Genre Brainstorm

## 1. Classes

**Knight**: the wall between the party and harm. Heavy armor and a high targeting stat, so most hits come its way. Abilities taunt all foes, or raise a Guard that cuts damage taken. Passive: when an ally drops below a third of its HP, the Knight taunts all foes for a few seconds. *Needs: Taunt, Guard (damage taken down).*

**Fighter**: a big blade and bigger swings. Slow, very heavy slashes that armor barely dents. Abilities: a cleave on all foes, a crushing blow on the highest-Armor foe. Passive: its crits leave Sunder on the target. *Needs: Sunder; Crit Damage, Penetration.*

**Thief**: quick hands, quicker feet. Fast, light hits and high Evasion. Abilities strip a buff from a foe or steal its MP. Passive: every dodge gives it a stack of Haste. *Needs: Haste; Evasion, Mana Burn.*

**Ranger**: patient, precise, picks its mark. Piercing shots. Abilities: Mark the highest-HP foe, a volley at all foes. Passive: its basic attacks always go to a Marked foe if there is one. *Needs: Marked (takes more damage, draws its shots); Accuracy, Execute.*

**Black Mage**: fire, ice and lightning, late but hard. Long wind-ups, big elemental hits, fragile. Abilities: a spell on all foes, a nuke on the foe least resistant to its element. Passive: when it knocks a foe out, its next spell has no wind-up. *Needs: a buff that skips the next wind-up; Cast Speed, Penetration.*

**White Mage**: keeps everyone standing. Heals whoever is lowest and cleanses debuffs. Abilities: a big single heal, Regen on the party. Passive: healing past full HP becomes Barrier. *Needs: Regen, Barrier.*

**Monk**: fists, discipline, and a body that answers back. Many quick blunt hits. Ability: Chakra heals itself and clears its own debuffs. Passive: when struck by a basic attack, it may strike back at once. *Needs: Counter, Double Strike.*

**Paladin**: holy steel and a steady hand. A Light-element weapon, a small heal and a party Barrier. Passive: whenever the Paladin is healed, it clears one debuff from a random ally. *Needs: Barrier; Tenacity.*

**Berserker**: most dangerous near death. Fast heavy hits, little defense. Ability: puts Berserk on itself. Passive: its Attack and Attack Speed rise as its HP falls. *Needs: Berserk; Lifesteal.*

**Bard**: songs that carry the party. Weak attacks, party-wide buffs. Abilities: a Haste song, an Attack song. Passive: when one of its songs ends, it plays once more for free. *Needs: Haste, an Attack buff.*

---

**Samurai**: one draw, one cut. Long wind-ups, huge slashes. Ability: a stance that counters the next hit. Passive: gains crit chance for every second it goes unhit, and loses all of it when struck. *Needs: a stacking crit buff that clears on being hit; Crit Damage, Counter.*

**Ninja**: never where the blade lands. Two light slashes per swing. Abilities: poisoned shuriken at random foes, a smoke bomb that makes it untargetable briefly. Passive: starts every fight with three Blink. *Needs: Blink, Poison, untargetable; Stealth.*

**Dragoon**: leaps out of reach, lands on someone. Ability: Jump. It can't be targeted during a long wind-up, then lands a heavy piercing hit. Passive: its hits cancel whatever the target is winding up. *Needs: untargetable while jumping, a very short Stun that cancels wind-ups.*

**Time Mage**: speeds friends, stalls foes. Abilities: Haste on the slowest ally, Slow on all foes, Stop (a long Stun) on the highest-Magic foe. Passive: every Slow it lands gives it a stack of Haste. *Needs: Haste, Slow, Stun.*

**Necromancer**: feeds on the dying. Abilities: Drain on the highest-HP foe, Doom on a random foe. Passive: every time any unit is knocked out, it gains a stack of Magic for the rest of the fight. *Needs: Doom, a stacking Magic buff; Lifesteal.*

**Alchemist**: bombs for them, bottles for us. Abilities: a fire bomb on all foes, a potion on the lowest-health ally. Passive: each bomb also leaves a random one of Poison, Blind or Slow. *Needs: Poison, Blind, Slow.*

**Dancer**: dodges and dazzles. Light hits, high Evasion. Abilities: a dance that lowers all foes' Attack, a charm that turns one foe against its own side briefly. Passive: each dodge gives a random ally a short Attack buff. *Needs: Charm, Attack up/down; Evasion.*

**Spellblade**: sword in one hand, spell in the other. Abilities wrap its weapon in an element, adding magical damage of that element to its basic attacks. Passive: those hits stack a resistance-down on the target for that element. *Needs: a weapon-element buff, a stacking resistance-down.*

**Valkyrie**: chooser of the slain. Piercing spear hits. Abilities: Reraise on the lowest-health ally, a thrust at the highest-Attack foe. Passive: comes back once per fight at half HP. *Needs: Reraise.*

**Oracle**: has already seen how this ends. Little damage; protects through foresight. Abilities: Blink on an ally, Blind on the highest-Attack foe. Passive: at fight start, every ally gets one Blink. *Needs: Blink, Blind.*

**Onmyoji**: paper talismans and bound spirits. Abilities: Silence on the highest-Magic foe, a ward raising an ally's resistance to one element. Passive: its wards can push resistance past full, so that element heals the ally instead. *Needs: Silence, element resistance up.*

**Hexer**: death by a hundred small curses. Little direct damage, many debuffs. Abilities: Curse on the lowest-health foe, Sunder on the highest-Armor foe. Passive: every debuff it applies also adds a stack of Hex. *Needs: Curse, Sunder, Hex (stacking damage-taken-up).*

**Blood Mage**: pays for power in its own blood. Abilities wound itself to hit hard: a blood nova on all foes, a Drain on the highest-HP foe. Passive: losing HP restores MP. *Needs: Lifesteal.*

**Plague Doctor**: spreads what it touches. Stacks Poison and Bleed, light direct damage. Ability: a tonic that cures an ally's debuffs. Passive: when a Poisoned foe is knocked out, its Poison jumps to a random foe. *Needs: Poison (survives knockout), Bleed.*

**Druid**: slow green growth and sharp brambles. Weak hits, strong over time. Abilities: Regen on the lowest-health ally, Thorns on the ally most likely to be targeted. Passive: its Regens stack instead of refreshing. *Needs: Regen, Thorns.*

## 2. Statuses

- **Poison** (debuff): damage on a timer; stacks add up and wear down.
- **Regen** (buff): heals a little on a timer.
- **Haste** (buff): faster attacks and shorter wind-ups.
- **Slow** (debuff): slower attacks and longer wind-ups.
- **Stun** (debuff): can't act, and loses what it was winding up.
- **Silence** (debuff): no abilities, basic attacks only.

---

- **Taunt** (buff): foes are forced to attack this unit.
- **Blind** (debuff): basic attacks often miss.
- **Barrier** (buff): soaks a pool of damage before HP.
- **Bleed** (debuff): takes damage every time it starts an action.
- **Berserk** (mixed): hits harder and faster, but can't use abilities.
- **Sunder** (debuff): Armor lowered, stacking.
- **Thorns** (buff): attackers take damage back when they hit it.
- **Blink** (buff): each stack makes one physical hit miss outright.
- **Doom** (debuff): when the timer runs out, takes damage equal to its current HP.
- **Reraise** (buff): revives once with some HP when knocked out.
- **Curse** (debuff): healing received is cut sharply.

## 3. Special stats

- **Evasion**: chance to dodge physical hits.
- **Accuracy**: cancels out the target's Evasion.
- **Crit Damage**: how much harder crits hit.
- **Lifesteal**: heals for part of the damage dealt.
- **Penetration**: ignores part of the target's Armor and resistance.
- **Tenacity**: shortens debuffs on this unit.

---

- **Counter**: chance to strike back when hit by a basic attack.
- **Double Strike**: chance a basic attack swings twice.
- **Echo**: chance an ability goes off a second time for free.
- **Splash**: part of each hit also lands on another random foe.
- **Execute**: more damage against low-HP targets.
- **Cast Speed**: shorter wind-ups on abilities.
- **Poise**: chance to keep a wind-up when something would cancel it.
- **Mana Burn**: hits drain the target's MP.
- **Thorns**: while above zero, gives the Thorns status at that strength.
- **Stealth**: starts the fight untargetable for that many seconds.

## 4. Elements

- **Fire**: fast, hungry, all-out damage.
- **Ice**: cold that stalls and grinds down over time.
- **Lightning**: the thunderbolt of Zeus and Thor, sudden and sharp.
- **Water**: tides and drowning, a crushing weight.
- **Earth**: slow, heavy, the mountain falling on you.
- **Wind**: quick, light cuts from nowhere.
- **Light**: holy fire of the gods; sears the wicked and the dead.
- **Dark**: rot, shadow and the grave; what necromancers drink.
- **Aether**: raw stuff of the heavens; almost nothing resists it, and almost nothing is weak to it.

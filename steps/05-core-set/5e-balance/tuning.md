# Step 5e tuning trail

One entry a round. The sheet is `npm run balance -- --trios 80 --seeds 2` (about four minutes on four cores; 160 parties × 20 campaign runs, 7 free-play runs and 100 rogue fights × 2 seeds, plus the gear and formation probes). Knobs are frozen at the values of Oct 27, 2026 throughout the fix rounds; win rates are context. A class's "win" is the win rate of parties with it minus parties without it, campaign / free play / rogue. Every number changed is in `content/core-set/scale.ts` or `classes.ts` with a comment naming this step.

0. **Baseline** (`sheet-before.md`, `findings.md`): 61 flags. The Vampire +37 / +45 / +18, the Bard −16 / −30 / −21, the Hexer −10 / −16 / −14, the Assassin −9 / −26 / −12; Caravan +20.5 against Neutral on the same parties, Escort +11.2, Blitz +8.1; bosses casting their signature move 0.5 to 0.9 times a fight in free play; a third of Wolves, Wisps and Drones killed before acting; Pyre, Static charge and Stillness never cast; MP regained 110% of MP spent over a campaign run.

1. **How often units act, and what they can afford** (all in `scale.ts`): the long cooldown 42 → 36; foes' medium and long cooldowns 48 → 40 and 70 → 60; the very long wind-up 8 → 6; the slotted head starts 20/12/6 → 16/12/8; MP regen 0.05 → 0.04 of the stat every 4 ticks; Static charge and Stillness 1,200 and 1,600 MP → 400 (`classes.ts`). Result: 53 flags. Every boss now casts its move at least 0.85 times a fight in free play (the Ogre Chief 0.85, the Fire Giant 1.35, the Ice Titan 1.32; were 0.5 to 0.95); Wolves, Skeletons and Hellhounds act before they die (Wisps 33%, Drones 27% and Mini Slimes 28% still don't: the Wisp has 1 HP by design, and the other two enter mid-fight on cooldown, which the sheet now excludes). MP regained fell to 97% of spent; the Cleric is short of MP 6% / 12% of its time, the Hexer 7% / 12%, the Stormcaller 4% / 7%, the Geomancer 19% / 21%, the Vampire 8% in the campaign (its 200 to 330 costs on a 2,000 pool at regen 2); the Bard and Paladin still 0%. Vanish and Garrote 0.28 and 0.37 casts a regular fight (were 0.18); Stillness 0.21 (0.04); Pyre and Static charge still 0.04 and 0.09, because the Pyromancer and Stormcaller are knocked out at ticks 13 and 14 on average, before a third slot comes round. The campaign's spread win rate fell from 23% to 18% at the frozen knobs (the casters cast less).

2. **What actions do:** Regen 12 pulses of 3% → 10 of 2.5% (36% → 25% of max HP); Blood drain 0.75 → 0.5 of max HP; Spear thrust a normal hit (0.75 of Attack) → 1.5 of Attack (Phalanx triples the swing, not the thrust); Volatile 0.25 → 0.18 of Magic. Result: 50 flags. Caravan +17 → +14.7 (Regen is still the whole of it: Vanguard, Champion, Tortoise and Scatter sit within +2.4 of Neutral); the Herald's horn left the flags (+8 → +5). The Vampire +32 / +45 / +18 → +25 / +40 / +16: Blood drain fell from 60% to 46% of its damage, but it now swings more (54% basic) and heals itself 4,350 a fight, up from 3,850: Lifesteal high at 30% (60% under Bloodlust) on 1.6× an average unit's damage is the sustain, not the one ability. The Hoplite −5.5 / −15 / −6 → −3.7 / −12 / −2.4 (Spear thrust 61% of its damage at about 2 swings). The Bomb Bug 4.2× → 3.4× the median minion's damage. The Bard −13 / −25 / −22, the Hexer −9 / −17 / −15, the Assassin −8 / −20 / −11: unmoved, as expected (nothing in this round touched them).

   **The sheet itself:** the pooled flags were weighted by runs, so the rogue's 32,000 single fights outvoted the campaign's 6,400 runs five to one. The pooled sheet now averages the three sets' rates (each mode counts once) and sums their counts; the flags above are re-read from it in round 3.

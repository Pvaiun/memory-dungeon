# Gate 1: the loot spine

The spine is `spine.md` in this folder. Part 1 is its rules (the stock, new words and counters, the rules bent, the code support); Part 4 is the web. This page is what you need to judge it.

## Decisions for you

**1. The counts.** The spine's stock is 314 pieces against today's 272, plus the 12 plain weapons and 16 mythics as now:

| Slot | A kind holds (common · uncommon · rare · legendary) | Today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weapon, 12 kinds | 2 · 3 · 3 · 2 | 2 · 2 · 2 · 2 |
| Armor, 4 types | 3 · 4 · 4 · 3 | 3 · 3 · 3 · 3 |
| Offhand, 13 kinds | 2 · 2 · 2 or 3 · 2 (three rares on the kite shield, totem, tome) | 2 · 2 · 2 · 2 |
| Amulet | 6 · 8 · 8 · 9 | 6 · 6 · 6 · 6 |

Formations: 12 common, 14 rare, 11 legendary, and Neutral (today 11, 12, 9). Seven versions change, five mythics are rewritten, and no kit changes beyond the two you approved.

*Why:* an offer picks a slot, a class, a kind, then a piece, so the number of pieces in a kind doesn't change how often a road is offered its pieces; only the share of the kind's pieces that serve it does. More pieces buy variety (two runs reach a road by different pieces) at the price of each piece coming round less: a weapon piece a third less often than today, an armor rare a quarter less. The extra go where the roads are many (eight of the twelve weapon kinds have three wearers, and every armor type is worn on six or seven roads), and on the amulet, the one kind everyone wears, which is where engines that cross classes put their keystones.

*The other way:* keep weapons at 2 · 2 · 2 · 2 and armor at 3 · 3 · 3 · 3 (about 260 pieces). Every piece is met a third more often and each kind serves fewer of its wearers' roads with a piece of their own. I'd keep the spine's: you asked for too much over too little. The fresh reader pointed out the offhands could take the weapons' count by the same reasoning (six offhand kinds have three wearers); the spine doesn't, and that's yours to call.

**2. What `builds.md` says that the spine shows doesn't hold, or holds only by an assumption.**
- **Malison.** The approved change didn't say whether its few Bleed stay; the spine keeps them, which keeps Thousand Cuts' "four base kits open wounds" true. And it reads "each different affliction" as **every debuff** (Weaken, Sunder, Marked, Slow, Rot and Silence count, as well as Burn, Chill, Shock and Bleed); the narrower reading, the stacking statuses only, makes the Hexer's own Weaken count for nothing. Confirm both.
- **"Five engines can start in act I."** Four can (red rain, held charge, reaping, brink). The lead's colour needs a rare formation or a legendary amulet, which act I offers about one run in eight.
- **The emptied quiver has one keystone, Quick draw.** Every second home I tried read as Quick draw's card on another item, which the rules for writing pieces forbid. Accept one home (it's the Ranger's loop, as `builds.md` writes it), or ask for a seat that does it its own way.
- **Some keystones are rarely offered.** On their own roads' parties, by the end of act II: the frozen gate 18% from pieces (42% with the Frostweaver's version), the riposte 29%, the goad 30%, mana as armour 25%; every colour 16% from pieces and the quiver from its version alone, though most runs with the Hexer or the Ranger have them from their kits and versions. None of these is a fault of the design: each is an anchor's, a dancer's, a caster's or one class's engine, as `builds.md` writes them. Say if any should be met more; the lever is a common seat or an amulet.

**3. Two facts about the game that the counts rest on, both outside the loot.**
- **The offer rarity doesn't rise by act.** `loot.md` says later acts roll more rares and legendaries; the config rolls every act's regular spoils on one table (35 / 32 / 27 / 6), and only the shop's legendaries and the formations' rarity rise. So act I offers as many rares as act II, and the spine puts first steps on rares as well as uncommons. Either `loot.md`'s line is rewritten to say so, or the offer roll gets a table by act (the balance pass's call). If it does, the first steps on rares become act II's.
- **Training offers every option, not three.** `docs/rules.md` says three choices a training; the code offers everything. The spine counts a version as met once its class knows the ability. If training becomes three choices, the keystones held in versions (the judge's drum, the frozen gate, the reaping, the emptied quiver, every colour) are met a third to half as often, and each would want a second piece or seat.

**4. New words and rules.** Heat becomes speed that builds while the field burns (Bonfire's far end is "feeds faster"); Charge is renamed Wrath, because "charge" is the Storm's word for Shock everywhere; Beat, Crescendo and Cascade are new; Surge, Fervor and Tempo are retired. Seven counters, as today. Twelve rules are new or bent, each on the items named in the spine: Marked on random picks and Malison's breadth (approved), a foe's action called early, a blow read as it was struck, damage carried past a kill, damage taken from MP, an ability cast by a count of swings, start-of-fight effects happening again, a status passed on by its holder, an ability brought back by the foes it hit, a gift copied, and the party's whole stone read by one blow.

## What the spine is

- **Every rare and legendary piece (160), every formation (37), the seven changed versions, the sixteen mythics and the two kit changes,** each as one line: what it does, its kind and rarity, the roads and rungs it serves and how much to each, the engines it's part of, and what the player sees for a first step or an engine's part.
- **The 86 uncommons by kind, named, with a rule for the commons** and each kind's stat leans by road.
- **The parts that recur written first,** then everything else built round them; **each engine's link** (what one part makes, what the other reads); and **the web checked:** every rung reached by more than one route, every keystone but the quiver's in two to four places, every door with pieces both sides want, seven exotics, nothing fenced off, every road's sign in a kit in act I.
- **Kept from today where it serves a road:** about half the rares and legendaries keep a name from today, many with a changed card or rarity; among those kept as they are: Hearth (now with the fuse as well), Hoarfrost plate, Rubble plate, Thousandth cut, Flurry wraps, Quietus, Executioner's axe. Every item that served only Phoenix, Lockdown, Harvest, Overflow or Ambush is gone or rebuilt, apart from a small net (Lamp of vigil, Swan's grace, Oathbound shield and the revive versions), which `builds.md` keeps as protection for every build.

## What changed after the fresh read

A fresh reader read the spine against `builds.md`, the brief and the rules, and found real problems. Every one was ruled on:
- **The same card in two places**, the biggest. To give keystones a second home I had copied cards: about twenty pairs read alike for a class that wears both kinds, or a version or mythic repeated a piece. Each home now has its own trigger, target or cost (Heat by time, by casting and by swinging; the avalanche chained or split; the riposte answered by the dancer or by the ally beside it), or the copy went: three of my version changes (Gutting arrow's reset, Goading thorns, Flashfire), and Fletcher's bow, Winter's chain, Crescendo staff, Twin shield, Orb of the second sun and Dancer's bell were replaced.
- **Enemy-only statuses** could be passed on (Doom) or taken (Bulwark); the cards now exclude them.
- **Three loops** got their brakes: Chains of light's strikes don't strike again, The choir counts only casts paid for, and the arrow-chaining bow is gone.
- **Dragon's tooth** no longer strikes "past Defense", which only a status's damage does.
- **Lines of `builds.md`** the spine had dropped: stone-only pieces no longer carry a Landslide tag (its pieces promise a blow); Leather has a Shield from its swings; a fast kind's uncommon lays Chill; the goad has a home in Mail.
- **A wrong wearer:** the Mark-on-a-spell uncommon moved from the lantern (no Hunt class wears it) to the relic, which gives the Paladin's judge a Mark piece of its own.
- **Uncommons that were a smaller rare of their kind,** and rares a smaller legendary, were moved to other threads (Hailbow, Notched knife, Pavise of the keeper, Annotated score, Shield of the sister).
- **Unclear words:** what "start-of-fight effects happen again" covers (never a price), Hidden's own length, which stacks a carried status lands at, and that Marked steers only an ability's first pick. Three code-support lines were added (C15–C17).
- **Claims corrected:** a reason for the weapon count that wasn't true (act I's full weapon slot is the plain weapon every class arrives with), armor's legendaries "falling" (they stay at three), and the third offhand rare's reason.

## What the numbers say, and how sure

From the offer roll and the offer volumes of 40 simulated runs (about 34 pieces offered an act, and four of them for a given party member). The count is `web-odds.ts` in this folder, and can be rerun.
- **Every road's parties are offered a build piece by the end of act II** most of the time: 69% to 97% by route, before versions. Cold Snap is lowest (72% to 77%), and leans on its versions and the Chill counts.
- **Act I offers a road's first step** to 37% to 81% of its parties, on top of the sign the kits already show. Cold Snap is lowest.
- **A run is offered about nine engines' keystones by act II** from pieces and seats, ten with its kits and versions. It meets several and not all, which is what found synergies should be.
- *How sure:* the volumes are the simulated player's, whose path and roster differ from a person's, so the figures are right to within a few points, not exact. It's a check that the stock is met, not a measure of strength.

## Least sure

- **Marked on random picks** may make the Hunt too easy to fall into: with one Mark source (Hunting party is a common formation), every random bolt, arrow and bash in the party follows it.
- **Foes called to act early** (the goad) speed them up, which a player may never choose.
- **Heat as speed** rewrites a counter and a kept mythic (Ember palms).
- **Generic strength:** Ready stance, Second dawn, The last drink, Lamp of vigil and First light help every build; each gives something up or is narrow in who wears it, and the fill should look again.

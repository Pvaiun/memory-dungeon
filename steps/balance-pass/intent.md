# The balance pass: the director's intent

A balance pass on today's game (the creative-builds loot), because "the last one was way too easy". The director played the version from just after the last balance pass, before creative builds, at heat 0.

- **What was soft:** regular fights and elites, with everything soft rather than a few strong things carrying.
- **The evidence:** two runs (Nov 13), the director's own records. In both, the choices after act I were "basically random. Just clicked on random things", and the second run still reached act II's boss (the Ice Titan), where it lost twice, the second time after spending a Destiny Shard. The first lost act I's floor-8 elite.

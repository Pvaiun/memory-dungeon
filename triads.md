# Triads

Every triad and the director's answer, in their words. The question is the same every time: **which two feel connected?** (The brief's wording, "which two want the same thing?", was changed by the director before any answer.)

## The pilot

Ten triads, each inside one way to win, the three cards on different mechanics and of different kinds where the way allowed. Three Burst, two Build up, three Debuff or disable, two Sustain; each card used once, 30 of the 40. The page shows them in this order, the cards in the order given.

### pilot-01 · Burst

- **A (P10, seat):** The unit in this seat keeps swinging its basic attack through its own wind-ups and recoveries.
- **B (P05, ability):** Pays half this unit's current HP, then hits a foe for a great deal more than the HP it paid.
- **C (P02, version):** This ability hits a lot harder against a foe that hasn't acted yet this fight.

**Answer:** not yet given.

### pilot-02 · Build up

- **A (P17, seat):** Whenever the unit in this seat gains Might, every other ally gains as many stacks.
- **B (P13, ability):** Lays a lot of Shock on the foe with the fastest basic attack and on the foe with the slowest.
- **C (P16, piece):** This unit's extra strikes, such as Double strike's second hit, count as basic attacks for everything its basic attacks lay on foes.

**Answer:** not yet given.

### pilot-03 · Debuff or disable

- **A (P24, version):** This ability also strips a buff from its target and gives it to this unit.
- **B (P27, seat):** Whenever a foe is healed, the unit in this seat gives it Rot.
- **C (P21, ability):** Silences a foe that is winding up an ability, so it loses the wind-up; it waits until some foe is winding up.

**Answer:** not yet given.

### pilot-04 · Sustain

- **A (P36, seat):** Foes pick among the party by each ally's max HP instead of by its Aggro.
- **B (P34, ability):** Trades HP shares with the other ally with the lowest share of max HP, leaving each at the other's share.
- **C (P35, piece):** Hits on this unit don't hurt at once: their damage adds up and lands all together a while after the first.

**Answer:** not yet given.

### pilot-05 · Burst

- **A (P01, seat):** The unit in this seat always crits a foe that is winding up an ability.
- **B (P09, ability):** Marks the foe with the lowest current HP and hits it, harder the more HP it is missing.
- **C (P06, piece):** This unit's damaging abilities wait for one another: once all are ready, they go off together and only one is paid for.

**Answer:** not yet given.

### pilot-06 · Debuff or disable

- **A (P30, piece):** Whenever an ally falls below half HP, the foe that last hit it is Stunned.
- **B (P22, ability):** Bewilders the foe with the highest Attack, so its next basic attack strikes one of its own side.
- **C (P28, version):** This ability also sends its target's next ability back to a full cooldown.

**Answer:** not yet given.

### pilot-07 · Build up

- **A (P20, piece):** Once the fight has gone on long enough to enrage, this unit's abilities wind up in a moment.
- **B (P12, version):** This ability adds more Burn the more Burn its target already holds.
- **C (P18, seat):** Each time a foe hits another ally, that foe gains whatever the basic attacks of the unit in this seat lay on a foe.

**Answer:** not yet given.

### pilot-08 · Sustain

- **A (P39, piece):** MP this unit gains past full goes to the ally with the lowest share of max MP.
- **B (P40, ability):** Taunts, and makes every other ally Hidden for a while.
- **C (P37, version):** This revive brings its ally back with every ability ready, not on a full cooldown.

**Answer:** not yet given.

### pilot-09 · Burst

- **A (P03, piece):** When a fight is won, the ability this unit has first in its order comes off cooldown, ready to open the next fight.
- **B (P07, version):** After its hits, this ability knocks out every foe left with only a small share of its HP.
- **C (P08, piece):** While this unit holds Haste, each of its abilities that deals damage goes off a second time at once, free.

**Answer:** not yet given.

### pilot-10 · Debuff or disable

- **A (P26, seat):** When an Aegis on the unit in this seat blocks a hit, the attacker is Weakened.
- **B (P29, ability):** Makes a foe that holds Guard Brittle, or a random foe if none does.
- **C (P25, piece):** Whenever this unit gains a debuff, all its debuffs move onto a random foe.

**Answer:** not yet given.

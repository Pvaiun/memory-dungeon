Flags are judged on the pooled numbers (each mode counting once: the three sets' rates averaged), except where a band names a set (the enemies in free play, MP in the campaign's runs, gear and formations in their probes).

## The bands, and why

| Band | lo | hi | unit | why |

| class.win | -8 | 8 | pts | Every class is in about the same number of parties (the spread). A class whose parties win 8 points more than the parties without it carries them (required); 8 points less, and it's a passenger (useless). The documents: each class 'should be worth getting on its own', and every class is in at least one winning trio and has fights where it's the wrong pick. |
| class.dmgDealer | 0.8 | 1.7 | × an average unit | A dealer's damage per unit-fight against the average unit's. The classes document names eight dealers (the three fighters, the four mages and the Ranger); one at 0.8 is outdamaged by the walls, one at 1.7 is the party's whole offence. |
| class.dmgOther | 0.25 | 1.2 | × an average unit | A wall or support still swings (nothing is useless), but one that outdamages an average dealer (1.2) makes the dealers pointless. |
| class.ko | 0 | 2.2 | × the median class | Knockouts per fight against the median class. The casters are fragile by design (HP 2), so up to about twice the median is their shape; past 2.2 a class is a liability nobody brings. |
| class.takenWall | 1.1 | 2.4 | × an average unit, per tick alive | A wall's job is to be hit (Aggro 4–5, plate). Below 1.1 it isn't drawing; above 2.4 it takes everything and the draw rule's 'between a third and three times' is doing nothing for the others. |
| class.takenOther | 0.35 | 1.25 | × an average unit, per tick alive | A dealer or support that takes more than 1.25 average units' damage per tick is the one being hit while the wall stands by; below 0.35 it can't be reached (the draw floor is a third). |

Read on the campaign's runs, where MP carries from fight to fight. |

| ability.stacks | 1.5 | ∞ | stacks an application | The scale: a few is about 2, several 4, many 7 (doubled on abilities). An application under 1.5 stacks is noise.

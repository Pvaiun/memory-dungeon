# Intent: one kind of damage (prototype)

The director, Nov 5:

- Magic resist is a weird stat, and magic is weird as well. The itemization loop is complex enough without the player gearing for Magic resist, and on enemies it's an unreadable stat that's hard to prepare for.
- **Remove magic damage and Magic resist completely.** The stats are Attack, Defense and Evasion; Defense is the new name for Armor. Everything acts as physical damage does today. The only thing that bypasses defenses is status effects.
- **Identity comes from ratio and scaling.** A sword, against an equally fast staff, has a higher base damage and a higher Attack scaling; the staff has a lower base and scaling but a higher flat Attack on it. Heavy gear has less Attack than medium, medium less than light. Defense and Thorns turn up more on heavy gear, MP regen more on robes.
- Anything can still scale off any stat. Convert the game as it stands into this system: no enemy has Magic resist, everyone just has Defense.
- A prototype, with a first pass of balancing if there's time.

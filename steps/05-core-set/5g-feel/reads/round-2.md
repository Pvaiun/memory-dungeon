# Feel read, round 2: twelve packs from 375 rogue fights at heat 0

A cold read of `out/feel-r2`: glossary, index, every pack tick by tick, then the summary. Nothing else was read.

## Per pack

### Pack 1: Toll Gate (fight, act 1 floor 1), won t69

## Across all packs

### 5. Rhythm
Length is set by the knob, not the tier: regular fights ran 15, 44, 61, 69 and 116 ticks (packs 8, 11, 12, 1, 5); elites 28, 35, 44 (packs 9, 3, 6), all shorter than pack 1; bosses 92, 114, 237. The summary agrees: elites average 52–57, the same as regular fights (49–67); bosses' 10–90 ranges are 16–177 and 6–237. Dead stretches: pack 5 t8–t66, pack 10 t79–t101 (all silenced, Burn shrinking). Pile-ups: pack 8 t9.2 (three kills and eleven refund lines in a tick). Wind-ups read when the number is big (Crush, Rockfall, Smite, Inferno, Flatten); 1-tick ones are invisible. Openings are a build (first casts t8–t13) unless a nuke sits in slot 1 (pack 8). Third slots land at t27–t56 and fire once or never.

### 6. Abilities
The game is slot 1: Blood drain 4.2 casts a fight, Cure 3.5, Stand fast 3.1, Anthem 3.0, Burning fist 2.7, Gale spear 2.6, Barbed arrow 2.4, Thicket 2.3. The long slots barely exist: Raise 0.10 (first t74), Valhalla 0.08, Unmake 0.11, Stillness 0.38, Pyre 0.40, Tremor 0.55 (first t56), Fireball 0.58, Aspis 0.64, Sanctuary 0.68, Spear thrust 0.75, Frenzy 0.77. Too small for the cost: Tremor (800 MP, 8 ticks) at 2,100 a cast against Rockfall (300, 6) at 2,694; Fireball 1,441 against Firebolt 1,264; Sanctuary; Frenzy; Blood gift; Chorus 450; Regen. Too big: Chain lightning+ 6,127 a cast, Cure+ 6,370, Crush 5,040. Units with a tempo: the Hoplite (one swing in twenty ticks), the Assassin (Vanish, Backstab, crit, Bleed), the Vampire (drain, heal, Bloodlust), the Knight (Stand fast as a chant). Everyone else swings at weapon cadence and casts slot 1 on cooldown.

### 7. Statuses
Burn: a ramp-down, right at the top (26.7 pulsing 1,333, pack 11) and noise at the bottom (twenty lines of 904 to 54, pack 10); 20% of party damage where applied. Shock: a pulse when the holder acts, so a bomb on slow bosses (3,330, pack 10 t16.2) and nothing on wisps that die first; 21%. Bleed: a delayed nuke (15.6 stacks = 1,872, pack 12); on heroes, 2 stacks from a 67 bite = 240 a hit (pack 2); 16%. Sunder: 140 fights, no visible effect. Weaken: real but unlabelled. Chill: Brittle shows, the slow never. Haste: never visible. Might on one ally: hits doubled (pack 1). Taunt: legible, deadly to its holder at high knobs (pack 9). Stun: good when it holds a cast, invisible on one wolf in six. Silence: brutal and legible. Regen: nothing against 940–1,925 hits. Shield: the best-reading defence ("260 physical, 260 soaked [898/6,500, 3,095 shield]"); Aegis the best beat. Ward: nothing. Hidden: expires on the last hero standing and the boss swings (pack 4 t82). Blood Price: 800 of own HP for 800 healed.

### 8. Mana
Only the Vampire's mana changes what it does: pack 7 at 20 MP for 87% of the fight (Blood drain every 20 ticks instead of 16, Frenzy and Blood gift never); summary short 21% of its time. The Stormcaller's 500-MP spell drains a 3,000 pool (pack 3: 125 left; pack 9: never cast). The Hoplite at 180 (pack 6) and the Geomancer at 90 (pack 10 t157) ran dry. Everyone else never spends: the Cleric left 4,700 and 4,786 (packs 6, 9) at regen 8; the Paladin sat full through pack 8 and, by Siphon's +220 a basic, pack 10; the summary has 76–96% left for Bard, Ranger, Hexer, Valkyrie, Assassin. Refrain and Soul jar poured MP into full pools (packs 1, 8).

### 9. Classes
Stormcaller: dominant, 64% of its party's damage over 61 fights (next best 47%); 67%, 64% on one cast, 71% with none (packs 3, 8, 9). Monk, Ranger, Pyromancer, Assassin (42–47%): damage from the right place. Vampire (30%, in 146 fights): the loop reads, the hunger is real, Blood gift's price is odd. Paladin (32%): Smite pays off; Judgement found no buff in three packs (foes carried Might in 9 fights of 375); Sanctuary is dead weight on physical bosses. Bramble knight (38%, 70% Thorns and Bleed): a tank whose damage is being hit, KO'd in a third of fights at mean t24. Knight (14%): a wall of upkeep. Hoplite (22%): Phalanx reads; Aspis 0.64, thrust 0.75. Geomancer (24%, 73% basic): Rockfall reads, Tremor is Rockfall at 2.7 times the cost. Cleric (16%, 100% basic): heals overkill or nothing. Bard (22%, 0% abilities): one buff that lands, then noise. Hexer (28%, 0% abilities, KO'd in 40%): Malison was pack 6's best play, Enfeeble shows nothing. Valkyrie: gone by t19 in both boss packs; Valhalla 0.08.
Worst three: Hexer, Cleric, Bard. Best three: Assassin (pack 4's solo to 5%), Vampire (pack 7's 74-tick solo with a rhythm), Stormcaller (every cast an event; the caveat is that it is the whole party).

### 10. Enemies and bosses
Boss moves come: Crush 2.07 an appearance, Inferno 1.4 and Brand 1.6, Quiet 6. When they land they one-shot (Crush x3; Inferno and Brand on both casters) or lock (Quiet); between them a boss's basics are taps (274–617, pack 4) or, at +170%, two-shots themselves (2,371, pack 10). Punching bag or wall, nothing between. Elites are "more HP", not a different fight: Ogres acted three times in 35 ticks, the Golem three in 44 while its Spirits did the killing, Leeches are five minions at 11,880; the summary has elites as long as regular fights. Minions act before dying 79–100% (summary), though clean packs show them as speed bumps (hounds dealt 40 and 183 in pack 7) and at high knobs they are the whole fight (packs 5, 9, 12). Regions read through the status thrown at you: Bleed on the Road, 1,925 ice hits in the Pass, Burn and a fire-absorbing boss in the Mine, Shock and mana-drinkers on the Tower, Silence in the Crypt. Felt like nothing: the Snake as a tally line (82 an appearance while its wounds kill), Wisps, the Ogre's basic, the Golem's Crust.

### 11. Drama
Close calls: 34/6,000 (pack 3); the Knight at 898 under shields for fifty ticks (pack 5); a boss at 5% (pack 4); a Monk solo won at 1,370 (pack 11). Comebacks: packs 7 and 11. One-shots: Crush x3, Inferno and Brand, 1,925 on 2,000 (pack 6), two hits on the Valkyrie (pack 10). Slogs: packs 5, 10, and 1 (69 ticks, nobody in danger). Decided in ten ticks: packs 8 (won t15), 9, 6 (Hexer at 75 by t3.8), 2 (a dead Vampire at t0). Long after decided: pack 10 from about t100; pack 12 after t28.

### 12. The ranked list

What looked wrong, worst first:

1. **Unwinnable knobs on elites and bosses.** Three to five times the HP the party can deal, with one- or two-shot hits: pack 6 (107,760 HP, lost t44, foes at 53–90%), pack 9 (59,400, lost t28), pack 10 (217,800, lost t237 at 36%). Summary: act 2 elites won 45%, act 2 bosses 38%, act 3 bosses 40%.
2. **Boss moves one-shot or do nothing.** Crush 6,577 / 2,986 / 4,385 (pack 4 t19.4, t81.7, t113.9) against basics of 274–617; Inferno and Brand killed both casters by t18 (pack 7); the Silent One's basic two-shot a 3,375 Valkyrie (pack 10 t4, t16). Summary: bosses 1.5–1.6 KOs a fight.
3. **Fight length is ungoverned and tiers don't differ.** Regular fights 15 to 116 ticks (packs 8, 5); elites 28–44, shorter than regular fights; bosses 92–237. Summary: elite mean 52–57 equals fight mean 49–67; 10–90 ranges 31–116 and 6–237.
4. **Third-slot abilities don't exist.** Tremor 0.55 casts a fight (first t56), Sanctuary 0.68, Aspis 0.64, Pyre 0.40, Stillness 0.38, Unmake 0.11, Raise 0.10, Valhalla 0.08. Pack 5's second Tremor outlived the fight; pack 11's Stillness came ready the tick its owner died; pack 8's Paladin had nothing ready at t15.
5. **Sunder reads as nothing.** 140 fights, no visible effect anywhere: eleven on Armor-0 wolves (pack 12), eight on Armor-0 hounds (pack 11), 15.6 on a Golem nobody hit (pack 6 t10.7).
6. **Debuffs and speed have no line.** Weaken (96 fights) exists only in arithmetic (260 to 202, pack 5; 197 to 121, pack 12); Chill's slow never shows (78 fights); Haste never shows (packs 1, 10, 11, 12); Guard only as 260 to 201.
7. **The Stormcaller is a different game.** 64% of its party's damage over 61 fights against 47% for the next; 67% (pack 3), 64% on one cast (pack 8), 71% with none (pack 9).
8. **Heals mismatched to the hits.** Cure+ 6,370 on 2,000-HP allies (pack 9 t8.9); Regen 60 against 940 hits (pack 9 t16.9) and 150 against 1,925 (pack 6); Chorus 450 x3 against 6,826 incoming (pack 12 t17.3); Absolve 234 on a full Paladin (pack 3 t32.2). Summary: the Cleric's damage 100% basic, 16% share.
9. **Fights lost before t1 to the run's attrition.** A dead Vampire at the start (pack 2), a Bard at 1,129/4,000 (pack 12), a Valkyrie at 1,428 and a Hoplite at 1,705 (pack 4). Three of the packs' five losses started this way; the summary cannot show it.
10. **The Vampire's expensive buttons.** Blood gift: 500 MP and 800 of its own HP for an 800–1,200 heal (pack 5 t23, t52.7, t94.8); Frenzy: 500 MP for Might 9 at 365 MP (pack 5 t27.8); in pack 7 neither was ever affordable. Summary: short of MP 21% of its time, the only class whose mana binds.
11. **Snake Bleed.** A 67-damage bite leaves a wound that does 240–506 a hit (pack 2 t5–t29), killed two heroes, and the tally credits the snake 268 (the summary 82). 120 a stack is a nuke at hero scale.
12. **Burn's long tail.** Twenty-plus lines of shrinking numbers after each big cast (pack 10 t103–t147, 904 to 54; pack 6 t19–t43, 420 to 90). The first five pulses are the status; the last thirty ticks are noise.
13. **Ward against physical bosses.** Sanctuary x3 for 2,400 MP in pack 10 (t38, t128, t190): 2,221 with and without. Summary: 0.68 casts a fight.
14. **The leech drain is illegible.** "Leech A MP +108 → 0" (pack 9), and the hero's loss never written on its side (Knight 370 to 34 after a 150 cast).
15. **An elite that doesn't swing.** The Ogre's 6-tick basic wind-up (pack 3): two elites acted three times in 35 ticks for 2,366. Summary: Ogre 2,318 an appearance, Flatten 1.08.

What looked right:

- Shield lines ("260 physical, 260 soaked [898/6,500, 3,095 shield]", pack 5) and Aegis eating Crush (pack 4 t49.7).
- Wind-ups that pay off: Crush, Rockfall 3,375–4,725, Smite finishing Ogre B to the point (pack 3 t27.2), Inferno; "begins X (lands tY)" is a readable threat.
- Stun holding a boss cast ("Quiet waits", pack 10 t67.5, t157.4; "Thicket waits", pack 3); Silence as a boss mechanic with Stun as its counter.
- Bleed as a delayed nuke on foes (pack 12 t27.4, t37.4: 1,872 kills); Lacerate's Bleed on a boss (pack 4, 26%).
- Elemental lines: "2,273 ice WEAK" (pack 11), "ABSORBS it, healing 1,522" (pack 7).
- The Vampire's loop (pack 7 t51–t61: Bloodlust on, 2,340 Lifesteal), the Assassin's (pack 4), the Hoplite's one big swing, the Knight's Taunt wall and Vanguard shield (pack 5).
- A Pyromancer's Burn that burns (1,333 a pulse, pack 11 t11.7); Might on one ally doubling its hits (pack 1).
- Boss patterns a player can learn (Inferno then Brand two ticks later, three times, pack 7).
- Regions that feel different by the status they throw; minions that act before dying (79–100%, summary).

# Feel read, round 8

Twelve packs from 322 rogue fights at heat 0, read tick by tick and cold: no source, no earlier reads. Ticks are cited as t-numbers.

## Per pack

**1. Toll Gate (fight, act 1 f1), won t57.** Three 6,520-HP bandits, never a threat (lowest point: Frostweaver 1,323 at t47); a flat grind for floor 1. The Cleric did 32% of the damage with its staff and Cured every 12 ticks (clipped to 590–921); War ballad walked Might 4.2 → 9.8; Chill peaked at 8.4, no shatter in 57 ticks. Chill's slow read once (Bandit C swung 3 times to the others' 5).

**2. Wolf Pack (fight, act 1 f7), lost t11.** The Vampire and Paladin arrived at 0 HP: one Stormcaller against seven 7,220-HP wolves at +261%. Thunderstorm t3, Chain lightning t6, Shock 21 taking 1,511 off a wolf at t9.4 (33% of the damage), dead at t10.2. Over before it started, for a reason the fight can't show.

**3. Ogre Bridge (elite, act 1 f4), won t65.** The Knight Taunted at t1, took Flatten 1,456 + Stun at t23.1, stood stunned to t31.1 and died at t31.9 having dealt 922. Bleed (26%) and Shock (17%) ate the Ogre; Flatten CRIT 2,944 one-shot the Valkyrie from 791 at t64.1 in the tick Shock finished the Ogre: the best ending in the set. Three Gale spear hits at t28.9 popped Bleed 1,617 + 1,078 + 718 at once.

**4. The Packmaster (boss, act 1 f10), lost t82.** The Stormcaller arrived with 84 MP: one Chain lightning at t49, and Crush killed it that tick (3,276 through Weaken and Guard). Aegis blocking Crush's 4,080 at t13.2 was the great beat; six Enfeebles pinned Sunder at 20 from t34 and Crush still one-shot the Hoplite (2,983 from 2,922, Armor 6) at t79.6. Chill shattered at t52.4 after 50 ticks; boss at 46%, 4 basics in 82 ticks.

**5. Bat Roost (fight, act 2 f1), won t57.** Four 10,400-HP bats hitting 299 into a 6,500-HP Bramble knight re-Taunting every 11 ticks: never in doubt. Thorns → Bleed 14,241 (34%); the wall dealt 58% with three basics; Tremor CRIT 5,000 (t28.2) and Rockfall CRIT 6,000 (t36.2); the Geomancer ended at 96 MP. Blood gift cost 800 HP to heal an ally at 83% (t15.1).

**6. Golem Gate (elite, act 2 f8), lost t68.** A t0 volley (Might 7, Regen, 3,300 Shield, Taunt, Backstab 6,759 at t3); the Knight took 9,183 in 19 ticks (1,491 ice a hit, CRIT 2,236 through Guard) and died t19.4. Assassin crits 16,318 (t24), 10,630 one-shotting a 21,700-HP spirit from 43% (t30), 13,428 on the guarded Golem (t45); Pyre 4,729 for 1,200 MP after 8 ticks (t33.5). Crust invisible; the Assassin alone t35–68 while Hidden kept the Golem idle.

**7. The Giant's Wrath (boss, act 2 f10), won t54.** Knob −35%. Backstab+ CRIT 16,413 on a 1,950-HP imp at t5.1; Garrote at t11.1 interrupted Brand and Silenced the Giant 16 ticks, Tremor Stunned it t29–37, Garrote again t32–48. One boss ability landed, 3,524 damage in 54 ticks, Inferno never; Assassin 81% and untouched; the Cleric Renewed a full-HP Assassin at t3.6 (healed 5).

**8. Barracks (fight, act 3 f3), lost t127.** Three 48,600-HP Soldiers (145,800 for a regular fight), 866 lightning every 8 ticks past Armor. The Assassin arrived with 962 MP, dry at t31; Geomancer dead t63.6, Raised t79, dead t84.5. Cleric alone t79–127 on a Cure+ loop (Last rites at t94.8): 48 ticks of a loss playing itself out; Shield up 21 times, nothing visible.

**9. Chapel Watch (elite, act 3 f6), won t80.** Troll 58,720, Mended 9,100 three times (it took 86,184 to die), Flatten zero times in 80 ticks, 4 swings into a Bramble knight never under 4,253. Frenzy+ Might 15 → Blood drain+ 5,255 for 100 MP (39%); Siphon gave +362 ×3 a cast on a full bar (regained 10,657 on a 3,000 pool). Shock hit the 40 cap at t72.3 and 3,040 killed the Troll; Vampire at 6 MP, Bramble knight at 1.

**10. The Silent One (boss, act 3 f10), lost t58.** 105,435 HP; the Geomancer arrived with 231 MP, the Paladin 556: one Rockfall, two Smites, Sanctuary and Absolve never. Firebolt + Smite+ put Burn at 49 by t5.2 (2,260 a pulse, 29,391 total, 40%). Quiet Silenced everyone for 32 of 58 ticks; Tolling ×3 took swings 1,621 → 2,599, killing the 1,375-HP Pyromancer with a basic at t19.7. The one boss that felt like one and still read.

**11. The Ogre Chief (boss, act 1 f10), won t48.** Bandit A dead at t1 before acting (Bleed 13 popped by three Burning fist hits: 1,560 + 1,039 + 693). Crush 4,051 on the guarded Bramble knight at t12.2 (6,269 → 2,218) and t42.3 (4,878 → 827), Regen 520 a pulse between. Bleed 33%, Burn 20% (Fire arrow 33 stacks → 1,271 pulses); Sunder 3.3 → 8.5 with nothing to show. Tense and quick.

**12. Lava Flow (elite, act 2 f4), lost t226.** Two 70,280-HP slimes splitting into 35,140 minis: 210,840 HP at act 2 floor 4. The Ranger did 105,128 by t47 (Rending arrow CRIT 17,900 at t31.8) and died at t47.4 unhealed; then 147 ticks of Bard and Hoplite: 22 War ballads with Might pinned at 15 from t33, 8 Aspis each blocking a 1,680 swing, basics. Enrage t200.6, Bard alone t194–225. The worst slog in the set, and the act-2 elite tail (26–205 ticks) says it isn't rare.

## Across all packs

**5. Rhythm.** Fights open as a volley, not a build: three abilities land at t1 in packs 3, 8, 11 and 12 (head starts plus carried-over cooldowns), and pack 10's Burn is at 49 by t5. A regular act-1 fight breathes (pack 1: a Cure, a ballad, a lance every 12–14 ticks with basics between). Elites and bosses breathe through the foe's wind-ups: "Ogre begins attack (lands t9.8)", Crush's 8 ticks, Flatten's 6 pay off every time (4,051; 4,080 blocked; CRIT 2,944) and are the moments a player would watch. Pyre's and Tremor's 8 ticks pay off less (4,729 at 1,200 MP; 600 on a Warded boss). Dead stretches are the tails after a fight is decided: pack 8 t79–127, pack 12 t47–226, pack 6 t35–68, pack 4 t52–82. Lengths: act-1 fights 43 ticks, elites 58, bosses 73 feel like three shapes; act-2 elites at 86 (26–205, 33% won) run longer than act-2 bosses (48) and are where the game stops breathing.

**6. Abilities.** A tempo of their own: Thicket (4–5 a fight, first t2), Stand fast, War ballad (4.4), Blood drain (5.3 at 100 MP), Gale spear, Burning fist, Barbed arrow, Cure, Vanish. Once or never: Raise 0.25 a fight (first t50), Valhalla 0.23, Pyre 0.61 (first t20; Pyre+ and Fireball+ never), Unmake 0.75, Thunderstorm 0.86, Fireball 1.04, Tremor 1.27 (t22.6): a third slot with no head start, a 6–8 wind-up and 800–1,200 MP comes once in a 45-tick fight if its caster is alive and has mana. Too big for their cost: Backstab and Garrote crits (10,125–16,413 for 250 MP, packs 6, 7), Rending arrow 6,905 a cast (pack 12), Blood drain+ 5,255 for 100 MP every 12 (pack 9). Too small: Pyre (pack 6), Fireball (939 on the Golem for 800 MP), Bramble lash (3 misses in 4, pack 5), Renew and Cure on allies who don't need them (packs 7, 1). Tempos that read: the Hoplite's rare double swings, the Assassin's Backstab–Vanish–Garrote, the Bramble knight's Taunt every 8–11, the Pyromancer's Firebolt–Fireball–Pyre ramp. Bard and Hexer have an upkeep tempo with nothing to show for each cast.

**7. Statuses.** Bleed (19% of damage where applied) is the clearest thing in the game: a charge released on every hit, bursting on multi-hits (pack 3 t28.9, pack 11 t1), 2,520 a pop at 21 stacks (pack 5 t41.4). Burn (19%) ramps right on a weak target (pack 6: 1,000, 850, 723…), vanishes on a resisting one (Golem: 163 → 48), and from a geared Pyromancer plus Paladin is a nuke from two casts (pack 10: 49 stacks, 40% of the damage) with a 50-tick tail. Shock (11%) punishes action, so it sleeps on slow bosses and lands huge when they move (pack 9: 3,040 at the 40 cap, which binds). Chill is a tag: 2% of damage, one shatter in twelve packs (pack 4 t52.4, after 50 ticks). Sunder pins at 20 from one or two applications (pack 4 from t34, pack 10 from t15) and no number beside it visibly changes; Weaken shows as 299 → 235 (pack 5) and as a boss one-shotting through it (pack 4). Might reads in tag and numbers, pins at 15 from upkeep (pack 12); the boss's own ramp (Tolling, pack 10) is its best version. Taunt works as a wall and gets the wall killed (Knight, packs 3, 6); Guard is Armor, so ice and dark ignore it (pack 6). Stun is 8 ticks and a death when a basic follows (pack 3). Silence on a boss is a 16-tick lock chained to 32 (pack 7); on the party it is the Silent One's whole threat. Aegis is the best defensive beat in the game (pack 4 t13.2). Regen, Clarity and Shield are legible trickles; Crust (pack 6) and Siphon on a full bar (pack 9) showed nothing a player could use.

**8. Mana.** Healthy in-fight, with a bind that lives between fights. "Short of MP" is 0–9% of every class's time, and the walls hardly spend (Knight 85% left, Ranger 84%, Hexer 78%). Clarity showed and mattered (pack 3: the Knight to full; pack 6: the Assassin +180 a pulse), Refrain carried a 226-tick fight (pack 12: 14,354 regained), Siphon was absurd (pack 9). What punished was arrival: pack 4's Stormcaller at 84 MP (one cast, at t49), pack 10's Geomancer at 231 and Paladin at 556 on the act-3 boss, pack 8's Assassin at 962 (dry at t31). Spenders run out in a long fight (pack 5 Geomancer 96; pack 9 Vampire 6, Bramble knight 1) and drop to basics. Nobody was absent; the hungry classes arrive hungry.

**9. Classes.** Bramble knight: Taunt plus Thorns plus Bleed to the letter, and the top damage dealer as a tank (44% share, 73% statuses). Knight: a Taunt that dies (9% share, 33 KOs per 100). Hoplite: rare big swings and Aegis read; a passenger without them (pack 12). Paladin: Smite and its Burn carry (pack 10). Vampire: Frenzy then Blood drain is a real engine (pack 9); Blood gift hurts it for little (pack 5). Valkyrie: triple-hit spear, Shock stacking, brisk. Ranger: Bleed and Burn from arrows, 48% share, never hit, the cleanest DoT fantasy. Assassin: a cycle you can see and crits that end fights (49% share, 13.5 KOs per 100); dominant. Monk: triple Burn and a Sunder ramp nobody can see. Geomancer: a party Shield plus crits off Bedrock (pack 5); MP-hungry (pack 10). Pyromancer: the game's biggest status (pack 10) and the first to die (t27.6 on average). Stormcaller: 65% share for ten ticks, then dead (packs 2, 4). Frostweaver: a Chill that never shatters (pack 1). Hexer: six debuffs that stop nothing (pack 4). Cleric: heals, 100% basic damage, 16% share. Bard: upkeep against a cap, damage from basics (pack 12). Worst three: Knight (dies doing its job), Hexer (an invisible job), Cleric (a passenger outside the heal loop). Best three: Assassin (tempo and payoff, though too much of it), Ranger (every arrow visibly does something), Bramble knight (being hit is the plan and you can watch it work).

**10. Enemies and bosses.** The Ogre Chief is a boss: Crush every 18 with an 8-tick telegraph, 4,051–4,080 raw, a wall at two thirds and a caster dead (packs 4, 11); played against by Aegis and Regen. The Silent One is a boss: a visible ramp and a lock (pack 10). The Fire Giant in pack 7 was nothing: Silenced 32 of 54 ticks, one ability landed, Inferno never. Elites: the Ogre's Flatten and six-tick swings read (pack 3); the Troll cast Flatten zero times in 80 ticks and was a healed punching bag (pack 9); the Golem has Brace and an invisible Crust (pack 6); the Lava Slimes have nothing but 210,840 HP (pack 12). Minions act before dying 97–100% except Fire Imps (78%); pack 11's Bandit A died at t1. Regions read by element more than by behaviour: the Old Road is physical and the walls work; the Crypt's dark, the Ice Pass's 1,491 ice hits and the Tower's 866 lightning ignore Armor and Guard, and the Knight's kit goes blank.

**11. Drama.** Two real close calls: pack 3's double death at t64.1 and pack 11's Bramble knight at 827 after the second Crush, winning six ticks later. The one-shots are the boss blows (packs 3, 4, 10) and the Assassin's crits (packs 6, 7). Decided in ten ticks: packs 2 and 7. Long after decided: packs 12 (179 ticks), 8 (48), 6 (33), 4 (30).

## 12. The ranked list

What looked wrong, worst first:

1. **Fights that run long after they are decided.** Pack 12: Ranger down t47.4, loss at t226. Pack 8: decided by t64, lost t127. Pack 6: t35.5 → t68. Summary: act-2 elites mean 86 ticks, 26–205, 33% won; act-3 fights mean 88.
2. **Arriving dead or dry.** Pack 2: two of three at 0 HP into seven wolves. Pack 4: Stormcaller at 84 MP, one cast at t49. Pack 10: Geomancer 231 and Paladin 556 MP on the act-3 boss. Summary: arrival MP 72–94% on average, so a tail, but the tail lands on bosses.
3. **Crits that end things.** Backstab/Garrote 16,318, 13,428, 10,630 (pack 6), 16,413 on a 1,950-HP imp (pack 7); Rending arrow CRIT 17,900 (pack 12 t31.8). Summary: Backstab+ 5,665 a cast, Rending arrow 6,905, Assassin 49% share and never hit.
4. **Act-2 elite and act-3 regular HP.** 210,840 effective (pack 12), 145,800 for a regular fight (pack 8), 41,600 of bats that hit for 299 (pack 5). Summary: act-2 elites the worst tier in the game.
5. **The Knight dies doing its job.** Pack 6 t19.4 (9,183 in 19 ticks, Guard useless against ice); pack 3 Stunned t23–31 then dead at t31.9 without acting. Summary: 9% share, 33.3 KOs per 100, 85% MP left.
6. **Bosses locked out.** Pack 7: Silence 32 of 54 ticks plus an 8-tick Stun; one ability landed, Inferno never. The summary says the Giant usually casts Inferno once, so this is the Assassin's doing: a 16-tick lock on a 19-tick cooldown is a lock.
7. **Boss one-shots the party can't answer.** Crush 3,276 through Weaken and Guard (pack 4 t49.1), 2,983 on a near-full Armor-6 Hoplite (t79.6), a basic 2,665 on a 1,375-HP Pyromancer (pack 10). Summary: 1.3–3 party KOs a boss fight. Only Aegis answers, and one class has it.
8. **Chill is a tag.** 70 fights applied, 900 damage a fight, 2%; one shatter in twelve packs (pack 4 t52.4, after 50 ticks); pack 1's Frostweaver peaked at 8.4 in 57 ticks.
9. **Sunder and Weaken show nothing.** Sunder pinned at 20 from t34 (pack 4) and t15 (pack 10) with no visible change in the numbers; six Weakens and a one-shot anyway (pack 4). Summary: 117 and 63 fights, nothing a player could see.
10. **Casts into a ceiling or a full bar.** 22 War ballads with Might at 15 (pack 12); Siphon regaining 10,657 on a 3,000 pool (pack 9); Renew on a full ally (pack 7 t3.6); Blood gift −800 for +800 at 83% (pack 5 t15.1).
11. **Third slots once or never.** Raise 0.25 a fight (first t50), Valhalla 0.23, Pyre 0.61 (first t20), Unmake 0.75, Fireball 1.04, Tremor 1.27; Pyre 4,729 for 1,200 MP after 8 ticks (pack 6 t33.5); Tremor 600 on a Warded boss (pack 7).
12. **Elites that aren't.** The Troll's Flatten zero in 80 ticks, healed 27,300 (pack 9); the Golem's Brace and an invisible Crust (pack 6); slimes that are only HP (pack 12). Summary: Troll Flatten 1.23 per appearance, so pack 9 is a bad draw; the Golem's is typical.
13. **The t1 volley.** Three abilities land at t1 in packs 3, 8, 11, 12; Burn 49 by t5 (pack 10); a bandit dead at t1 (pack 11), an imp at t5 (pack 7). Summary: Fire Imps act before dying 78%.
14. **Passengers.** Hexer 17% from a staff after six Enfeebles (pack 4); Cleric 4% damage and 430 healed (pack 7); Bard basics only (pack 12). Summary: Cleric 100% basic, Hexer 0% abilities, Bard 18% share.
15. **Guard and Armor against elements.** 1,491 ice through Armor 7 and CRIT 2,236 through Guard (pack 6), 866 lightning ×3 every 8 ticks (pack 8), 929 dark guarded (pack 9): whole regions where the walls' kit does nothing.

What looked right: Bleed's shape (pack 3 t28.9, pack 11 t1); Aegis against a telegraphed Crush (pack 4 t13.2); Crush and Flatten wind-ups that pay off and can be survived (pack 11 t12.2, t42.3); the Silent One's ramp and Quiet (pack 10); Burn as a ramp on a weak target (pack 6) and a slow burn on a boss (pack 11); the Bramble knight's Taunt–Thorns–Bleed engine; the Assassin's cycle and its interrupt (pack 7 t11.1); Refrain and Clarity as mana that shows (packs 12, 3); Shock waking at the cap (pack 9 t79.4); the Hoplite's rare heavy swings; act-1 regular fights at 43 ticks and 95% won; pack 3's ending.

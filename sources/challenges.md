# Challenges

The puzzles a fight can set, from the enemies' design. Each enemy is built on one; a party answers it with the strengths in its kit.

## Challenges

The full list: 58 challenges, plus two run types, Echo and Lock. Fundamental answers come first in each list, and a semicolon separates them from the rest. *Built as* names this set's enemy, boss or region for the challenge, or "–" for the next wave. Gear and formations never count as answers: a party's answers come from its classes' kits.

The original design gave Poison as the Fundamental answer to Armored shell, Dodger, Duelist, Incorporeal, Hardening hide, Mixed defenses, Mixed gauntlet, Spiked, Splitter, Purifier, Counter stance, Hit counter and Burrower. Bleed replaced Poison. Its damage is untyped too, but it comes only when its holder is hit, so it answers these only in part. The answers below leave Poison out.

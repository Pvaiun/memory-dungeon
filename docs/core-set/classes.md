### Conventions in class descriptions

- **Stats** run from 1 to 5: 1 is very low, 3 average, 5 very high. Special stats and resistances are zero unless a class lists them.
- **Armor weight** is the heaviest armor the class can wear; the weight rules are under Gear in *Core set: rules and vocabulary*.
- **Timing.** Cooldowns are short, medium or long. Wind-ups (cast speed, in other docs) are quick, normal, long or very long. MP cost follows the cooldown unless an ability says it's costly. Basic attacks have no wind-up by default.
- **Damage and stacks.** Every ability that deals damage says what the damage scales from. Every ability that adds a stacking status says how many stacks (a few, several or many) and what sets that number. Statuses that don't stack have one fixed size.
- **Firing, Fizzle statuses and target phrases** are defined in *Core set: rules and vocabulary*, under Combat system: Time and actions, and Targeting.
- **Tests.** Each class ends with a test: one fight against a challenge it should answer, traced through its kit.

## Starting classes

The starters cover every Fundamental challenge between them: a wall, a healer and an archer. They have no Might, Haste, Silence, Dispel or Area, so most Specialist bosses need an unlock.

### Knight

**Role:** protector. **Plan:** stands in front in heavy plate, Guards and Shields whoever is lowest, bashes a foe now and then, and when an ally falls, calls every foe onto itself.

**Weapons:** sword, axe. **Armor weight:** heavy. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Sworn.** When an ally is knocked out, the Knight gains Taunt.
- **Fortify.** Applies Guard to the ally with the lowest share of max HP. Medium cooldown, quick wind-up.
- **Protect.** Applies Shield to the ally with the lowest share of max HP; the amount scales from max HP. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **Shield bash.** Deals physical damage that scales from Armor to a random foe, and applies Stun. Long cooldown, quick wind-up.

*Test, Tank buster:* with Aggro 5, the boss picks the Knight most of the time, so the Knight is usually the lowest and gets its own Guard and Shield. Early on, with everyone at full HP, they go to any ally. If the boss knocks out the Cleric, Sworn Taunts and the next swings come to the Knight. Shield bash lands at a random moment, often during the long wind-up.

### Cleric

**Role:** healer. **Plan:** keeps the party standing through a whole dungeon: its staff hits mend the lowest ally, Prayer keeps everyone regenerating, curses are lifted and the fallen raised.

**Weapons:** Light staff. **Armor weight:** light. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Mercy.** Each of its Light hits heals the ally with the lowest share of max HP for half the damage the hit dealt.
- **Prayer.** Applies Regen to every ally. Long cooldown, long wind-up.
- **Purify.** Cleanses a random ally with a debuff. Waits until an ally has a debuff. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **Raise.** Revives a random knocked-out ally with half of its max HP. Waits until an ally is knocked out. Long cooldown, very long wind-up, costly.

*Test, Raidwide:* every boss hit lands on all three. Prayer's Regen ticks on everyone, and between Prayers each staff hit heals the lowest ally for no MP. Under Anti-heal, Rot cuts both, so there the Cleric is the wrong pick.

### Ranger

**Role:** wears down tough targets. **Plan:** looses poison, fire and armor-rending arrows at whatever stands in front, while its plain arrows fly past any guard.

**Weapons:** bow, dagger. **Armor weight:** medium. **Special stats:** Accuracy medium. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Hunter's eye.** Its basic attacks ignore Taunt: they go to a random foe, Taunting or not.
- **Venom arrow.** Deals physical damage that scales from Attack to a random foe, and applies Poison. Short cooldown, quick wind-up.
- **Fire arrow.** Deals Fire damage that scales from Attack to the foe with the most Burn, and adds many Burn; stacks scale from Attack. Medium cooldown, long wind-up.
- **Rending arrow.** Deals physical damage that scales from Attack to the foe with the most Sunder, and adds several Sunder; stacks scale from Attack. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up.

*Test, Shield wall:* its arrows have no Seek, so they go to the Taunting, Guarded foe, which gets Poisoned, Burned and Sundered, and the Sunder offsets its Guard. Its basic attacks ignore Taunt and land on the fragile dealers behind. Against a lone boss, everything stacks on one target.

## Defenders and support

Six classes that win through the party: a tank that punishes, a bodyguard, a shelter against magic, a storm spear who heals and revives, a tempo-setter and a curser.

### Bramble knight

**Role:** thorns tank. **Plan:** draws every foe onto its thorns, where each attacker bleeds and is poisoned, regrows whoever is lowest, and lashes out with the thorns themselves.

**Weapons:** sword, Earth staff. **Armor weight:** heavy. **Special stats:** Thorns high. **Resistances:** weak to Fire.

- **Passive – Briar.** Each time its Thorns damage a foe, that foe gets Poison.
- **Thicket.** Applies Taunt and Guard to itself. Medium cooldown, quick wind-up.
- **Regrowth.** Applies Regen to the ally with the lowest share of max HP. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **Bramble lash.** Deals physical damage that scales from Thorns to a random foe. Short cooldown, quick wind-up.

*Test, Swarm:* Thicket pulls all eight foes onto the knight. Each basic attack costs its attacker Thorns and Poisons it, Thicket's Guard blunts the many small hits, and Regrowth mends the knight. Against a Brute it does worse: one big swing triggers Thorns once.

### Hoplite

**Role:** bodyguard who breaks armor. **Plan:** plants its great shield beside the most vulnerable ally to catch the killing blow, locks shields with it, and cracks armor with slow, crushing blows.

**Weapons:** spear, sword. **Armor weight:** heavy. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Phalanx.** Its basic attacks come a third as often and deal triple damage.
- **Aspis.** Applies Aegis to the ally with the lowest share of max HP. Medium cooldown, quick wind-up.
- **Paired guard.** Applies Guard to itself and to the other ally with the lowest share of max HP. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **Spear thrust.** Deals physical damage that scales from Attack to a random foe, and adds a few Sunder; stacks scale from Attack. Short cooldown, quick wind-up.

*Test, Tank buster:* the huge swing lands on whoever the boss picked. Aspis puts Aegis on the lowest ally, which isn't always the target; when it is, the blow is blocked outright. Paired with the Knight it's reliable: the boss hits the Knight, the Knight becomes the lowest, and the Knight holds the Aegis. Between swings, its slow, triple-damage basic attacks get through Armor that stops smaller hits, and Spear thrust's Sunder opens it further.

### Paladin

**Role:** shelter against magic. **Plan:** shelters the party from spells and curses, and brings down slow, heavy judgement, twice as hard on any foe that holds a buff.

**Weapons:** sword, Light staff. **Armor weight:** heavy. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** resists Light.

- **Passive – Judgement.** Its hits deal double damage to foes with a buff.
- **Sanctuary.** Applies Ward to every ally. Long cooldown, long wind-up.
- **Absolve.** Cleanses itself and one random other ally with a debuff. Waits until it or an ally has a debuff. Long cooldown, quick wind-up.
- **Smite.** Deals heavy Light damage that scales from Magic to the foe with the highest Attack or Magic. Long cooldown, very long wind-up, costly.

*Test, Nuker:* Sanctuary's Ward cuts the heavy spell across the party, and Absolve lifts whatever it leaves behind. Against a Buffer's allies, every hit lands double while their buffs last; beside a Hexer that strips those buffs, it loses that edge. Smite's very long wind-up is its weakness: a fast fight may end before it lands.

### Valkyrie

**Role:** storm spear who heals and revives. **Plan:** a storm-borne spear that reaches past any guard and Shocks what it hits, mends the wounded, and snatches the fallen back ready to fight.

**Weapons:** spear, sword, Light staff. **Armor weight:** medium. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Einherjar.** Allies it revives come back with all their actions ready, instead of on cooldown.
- **Gale spear.** Has Seek. Three quick hits on a random foe that isn't Taunting; each deals Wind damage that scales from Attack and adds a few Shock, with stacks that scale from Attack. Short cooldown, quick wind-up.
- **Rally.** Heals the ally with the lowest share of max HP; the amount scales from Magic. Waits until an ally is below max HP. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **Valhalla.** Revives a random knocked-out ally with a tenth of its max HP. Waits until an ally is knocked out. Long cooldown, quick wind-up, costly.

*Test, Executioner:* Doom knocks out the Ranger. Valhalla brings it back almost at once, fragile but with every arrow ready, so the party loses little damage. With no Cleanse or Aegis beside her, Doom lands every time: the Valkyrie undoes it rather than stopping it. Next to the Cleric's Raise, Valhalla is the fast, fragile return; Raise is the slow, sturdy one.

### Bard

**Role:** party tempo. **Plan:** plays the fight's tempo: allies faster and stronger, foes slower, and nothing stops the song.

**Weapons:** dagger, bow. **Armor weight:** medium. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Encore.** It can't be Silenced.
- **Allegro.** Applies Haste to a random ally. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **War ballad.** Adds several Might to the ally with the highest Attack or Magic; stacks scale from Magic. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **Lullaby.** Applies Slow to a random foe. Medium cooldown, long wind-up.

*Test, Curser:* the Curser Weakens and Slows the party. Allegro's Haste offsets the Slow on one ally at a time, and War ballad's Might outweighs the Weaken on the hardest hitter. It lifts neither debuff, so a Cleric's Purify still helps. Against a Binder, Encore makes the Bard the one ally it can't shut down.

### Hexer

**Role:** curser. **Plan:** curses the most dangerous foe into a crawl, silenced, weakened, slowed and chilled, and strips foes' buffs one at a time.

**Weapons:** Ice staff, Dark staff. **Armor weight:** light. **Special stats:** Siphon medium. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Cold hex.** Its basic attacks add a few Chill, whatever staff it carries; stacks scale from Magic.
- **Hush.** Applies Silence to the foe with the highest Attack or Magic. Long cooldown, quick wind-up.
- **Unravel.** Removes one random buff from a random foe with a buff. Waits until a foe has a buff. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **Enfeeble.** Applies Weaken and Slow to the foe with the highest Attack or Magic. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up.

*Test, Buffer:* Unravel strips one buff per cast, so the fight is a visible race against the Buffer's casts. Hush and Enfeeble go to the foe with the highest Attack or Magic, which may be the Buffer's guard rather than the Buffer: the Hexer slows the race but doesn't end it alone.

## Fighters

Three damage dealers, each beating armor its own way: the Monk with Sunder, the Assassin with crits, the Vampire by outlasting it.

### Monk

**Role:** breaks armor with a blur of blows. **Plan:** every blow cracks armor, Flow keeps it fast, burning fists pile onto the hottest foe, and a pressure point drops the most dangerous one for a moment.

**Weapons:** fist weapons, spear. **Armor weight:** medium. **Special stats:** Evasion high, Double strike medium. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Iron palm.** Its basic attacks, and the second strike of its Double strike, add a few Sunder; stacks scale from Attack.
- **Flow.** Applies Haste to itself. Short cooldown, quick wind-up.
- **Burning fist.** Three quick hits on the foe with the most Burn; each deals Fire damage that scales from Attack and adds a few Burn, with stacks that scale from Attack. Short cooldown, quick wind-up.
- **Pressure point.** Has Seek. Deals physical damage that scales from Attack to the foe with the highest Attack or Magic, and applies Stun. Long cooldown, normal wind-up.

*Test, Armored shell:* its first double strikes barely scratch the shell, but each adds Sunder and Flow speeds them up; within seconds every hit gets through. With a spear, its double strikes also break Bulwark.

### Assassin

**Role:** burst killer. **Plan:** slips out of sight, picks off the weakest foe past any guard with crits that punch through armor, and silences the back line.

**Weapons:** dagger, sword. **Armor weight:** medium. **Special stats:** Crit damage high, Evasion medium. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Shadow strike.** Its hits always crit while it's Hidden.
- **Vanish.** Applies Hidden to itself. Waits until it isn't Hidden. Long cooldown, quick wind-up.
- **Backstab.** Has Seek. Deals heavy physical damage that scales from Crit chance to the foe with the lowest current HP. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **Garrote.** Has Seek. Deals physical damage that scales from Attack to a random foe that isn't Taunting, and applies Silence. Long cooldown, quick wind-up.

*Test, Stalker:* the Stalker hunts the lowest-HP unit, usually the Assassin. Once Vanish is up, the Assassin is out of reach and every hit it lands crits. Until then it's exposed, so it wants a Shield or an Area ally beside it.

### Vampire

**Role:** drains to outlast. **Plan:** feeds on everything it hits: the harder it hits, the more it heals, and its prey is left weakened and unable to mend.

**Weapons:** sword, fist weapons (claws), Dark staff. **Armor weight:** medium. **Special stats:** Lifesteal high. **Resistances:** weak to Light, resists Dark.

- **Passive – Bloodlust.** While it's below half of its max HP, its Lifesteal is doubled.
- **Blood drain.** Deals Dark damage that scales from max HP to a random foe, and applies Weaken. Short cooldown, quick wind-up.
- **Wither.** Applies Rot to the foe with the lowest current HP. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **Frenzy.** Adds several Might to itself; stacks scale from Attack. Medium cooldown, quick wind-up.

*Test, Regenerator:* alone, the boss is the foe with the lowest current HP, so Wither Rots it and its Regen and Lifesteal shrink. Frenzy raises the Vampire's damage and with it its Lifesteal. In a group, the lowest foe is usually the one an enemy healer turns to next. Against Anti-heal it falters: Rot cuts its own Lifesteal.

## Mages

Four mages, each on its element's job: Fire ramps, Ice controls, Lightning spreads, Earth crits from behind its Shields. Burn, Chill and Shock each have a source outside their mage, so the mage is the strongest source, not the only one.

### Pyromancer

**Role:** ramping Burn. **Plan:** every strike adds Burn, bolts pile more onto the hottest foe, and a pyre lands on it.

**Weapons:** Fire staff, Lightning staff. **Armor weight:** light. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Kindling.** Its basic attacks add a few Burn, whatever staff it carries; stacks scale from Magic.
- **Firebolt.** Deals Fire damage that scales from Magic to the foe with the most Burn, and adds several Burn; stacks scale from Magic. Short cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **Fireball.** Deals Fire damage that scales from Magic to every foe, and adds a few Burn to each; stacks scale from Magic. Medium cooldown, long wind-up.
- **Pyre.** Deals heavy Fire damage to the foe with the most Burn; the damage scales from Magic and rises with each Burn stack on that foe. The Burn stays. Long cooldown, very long wind-up.

*Test, Two states:* in the Armor phase its fire lands in full. In the resistance phase its hits do little, but Burn still lands and ticks as untyped damage. With a Ranger or Monk adding Burn too, it stays high through both phases.

### Frostweaver

**Role:** locks foes down with Chill. **Plan:** buries foes in Chill and keeps them crawling, its hits landing harder the colder they are, and spends the time it buys keeping an ally's mana flowing.

**Weapons:** Ice staff, Wind staff. **Armor weight:** light. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** resists Ice.

- **Passive – Brittle.** Its hits deal more damage for each Chill stack on the target. The Chill stays.
- **Frost lance.** Deals Ice damage that scales from Magic to the foe with the most Chill, and adds several Chill; stacks scale from Magic. Short cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **Blizzard.** Deals Ice damage that scales from Magic to every foe, and adds a few Chill to each; stacks scale from Magic. Medium cooldown, long wind-up.
- **Stillness.** Applies Clarity to a random ally. Long cooldown, quick wind-up.

*Test, Skirmisher:* Blizzard and Frost lance build Chill on the fast foes until they crawl. Its own basic attacks add no Chill, so between casts the Chill drains. With a Hexer beside it, Chill stays high and every hit lands harder.

### Stormcaller

**Role:** Shocks every foe. **Plan:** rains quick strikes across every foe so the fast and the many hurt themselves by acting, and charges itself up to strike faster.

**Weapons:** Lightning staff, Wind staff. **Armor weight:** light. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Arc.** Each of its basic attacks also hits a second, random foe, and adds a few Shock to both; stacks scale from Magic.
- **Chain lightning.** Deals Lightning damage that scales from Magic to a random foe, then to up to three other random foes, and adds a few Shock to each; stacks scale from Magic. Short cooldown, quick wind-up.
- **Thunderstorm.** Deals Lightning damage that scales from Magic to every foe, and adds a few Shock to each; stacks scale from Magic. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **Static charge.** Applies Haste to itself. Long cooldown, long wind-up.

*Test, Swarm:* Arc, Chain lightning and Thunderstorm Shock all eight foes, and every time one acts, Shock hurts it. Against a lone Nuker it's weak: one slow caster barely sets off Shock, and Arc has no second foe.

### Geomancer

**Role:** hits hardest behind stone. **Plan:** wraps itself and an ally in stone; while its own Shield holds, its rocks crit far more often and far harder, and a tremor drops a foe now and then.

**Weapons:** Earth staff, Fire staff, axe. **Armor weight:** heavy. **Special stats:** Crit damage high. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Bedrock.** While its Shield pool is above zero, its crit chance is doubled.
- **Earthen shell.** Applies Shield to itself and to the other ally with the lowest share of max HP; the amount scales from Armor. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **Rockfall.** Deals Earth damage that scales from Armor to a random foe. Short cooldown, normal wind-up.
- **Tremor.** Deals Earth damage that scales from Magic to a random foe, and applies Stun. Long cooldown, very long wind-up.

*Test, Hit counter:* the boss strikes back every few hits it takes. The Geomancer lands few hits, but while its Shield holds they crit often and hard, so the counter rarely fires. A Shield breaks once hits use up its pool, so the Geomancer crits most while a Knight keeps foes off it, and the Knight's Protect also turns its crits back on. Area damage or a Stalker turns them off.

## New mechanics and things to watch

The classes need four mechanics beyond the base combat system:

- a basic attack that also hits a second foe (Arc);
- damage that scales from a condition instead of a stat, resolved with the base amount: stacks on the target (Brittle, Pyre) or a buff on it (Judgement);
- reviving with actions ready (Einherjar);
- basic attacks at a third of the speed for triple damage (Phalanx).

To watch in simulation:

- **Stun.** A long-cooldown Stun with no timing condition may rarely catch a wind-up. The fallback is a shorter Stun on a shorter cooldown.
- **Pyre** may fire on a foe with very little Burn.
- **Unravel** may strip a buff the moment it lands, which could feel like constant reversals.
- **Sworn.** The Knight Taunts only after a knockout, so check it holds bosses on Aggro alone.
- **Aspis** goes to the lowest ally, who isn't always the one about to take the big hit. Check how often Aegis catches anything without a Knight drawing the boss.
- **Bedrock** depends on how long the Geomancer's Shield lasts. Check its crit uptime with and without a protector beside it.

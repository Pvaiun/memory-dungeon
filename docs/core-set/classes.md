### Conventions in class descriptions

- **Stats** run from 1 to 5: 1 is very low, 3 average, 5 very high. Special stats and resistances are zero unless a class lists them.
- **Armor weight** is the heaviest armor the class can wear; the weight rules are under Gear in *Core set: rules and vocabulary*.
- **Timing.** Cooldowns are quick, short, medium, long or very long (14, 22, 30, 40 and 56 ticks since step 5g). Wind-ups (cast speed, in other docs) are quick, normal, long or very long (1, 3, 6 and 8 ticks). Basic attacks have no wind-up by default. The word follows the ability's job (step 5g, the director's): abilities meant to repeat, such as stack applicators and top-up heals, are quick; a buff a unit puts on itself has a cooldown longer than the buff lasts, so it lapses before it comes back; the big nukes are long and the revives very long.
- **MP costs** are set by hand on each ability (Oct 26, 2026; the director's: no rule from a stat to a cost), on one ladder in two tiers (step 5f), a third dearer since step 5g: a skill (the physical classes' and the Valkyrie's spear) costs 200, 325, 500 or 750 MP, and a spell (the healers', the Bard's, Hexer's and Paladin's, the mages', the Vampire's dark arts) 450, 650, 1,000 or 1,500. Blood drain, at 125, is the one cost off the ladder. The casters' spells cost several times the walls' and fighters' skills, so casters run short of MP and wear MP gear, and walls rarely do. The revives (Raise, Valhalla) and Pyre cost the most.
- **Damage and stacks.** Every ability that deals damage says what the damage scales from. Every ability that adds a stacking status says how many stacks (a few, several or many) and what sets that number. Statuses that don't stack have one fixed size.
- **Firing, Fizzle statuses and target phrases** are defined in *Core set: rules and vocabulary*, under Combat system: Time and actions, and Targeting.
- **Tests.** Each class ends with a test: one fight against a challenge it should answer, traced through its kit.

## Starting classes

The starters cover every Fundamental challenge between them: a wall, a healer and an archer. They have no Might, Haste, Silence, Dispel or Area, so most Specialist bosses need an unlock.

### Knight

**Role:** protector. **Plan:** starts every fight behind its own Shield, Guards whoever is lowest, calls every foe onto itself while an ally regains its mana, and bashes a foe with the weight of its own body.

**Weapons:** sword, axe. **Armor weight:** heavy. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Vanguard.** It starts every fight behind a Shield that scales from its max HP. (Sworn, Taunt when an ally fell, until Oct 27, 2026: it rarely fired, and too late.)
- **Fortify.** Applies Guard to the ally with the lowest share of max HP. Long cooldown, quick wind-up, 325 MP.
- **Stand fast.** Applies Taunt to itself, and Clarity to the ally with the least MP among those under half their max MP, or, with nobody under half, the ally with the least. Medium cooldown, quick wind-up, 200 MP. (Protect, a Shield on the lowest ally, until Oct 27, 2026: it overlapped the Hoplite. On a short cooldown until Oct 28: it Taunted again before the last Taunt ran out.)
- **Shield bash.** Deals physical damage that scales from max HP to a random foe, and applies Stun. Medium cooldown, quick wind-up, 325 MP. (From Armor until Oct 27, 2026.)

*Test, Tank buster:* the Knight starts behind its own Shield and Taunts as soon as Stand fast is ready, so the boss's single-target blows go to it and its Guard goes to itself as the lowest. The Cleric behind it is left alone while the Taunt holds (about two thirds of a fight), and gets Clarity when its MP runs low. Shield bash lands at a random moment, often during the long wind-up. (Until Oct 27, 2026, the test ran on Sworn and Protect.)

### Cleric

**Role:** healer. **Plan:** mends whoever is lowest with quick, small heals, lifts a curse and wraps the ally in Regen, raises the fallen, and prays twice as fast when its own life is in danger. An active healer that spends MP: it has no free heal.

**Weapons:** Light staff. **Armor weight:** light. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Last rites.** Below 30% of its max HP, its cooldowns are halved; healed back to 30% or more, they return to normal. (Encore, immunity to Silence, until Oct 27, 2026: two enemies in the roster silence. Until Oct 28, a bug kept the halving on for the rest of the fight.)
- **Renew.** Cleanses an ally with a debuff and applies Regen to it; with no ally debuffed, Regen on the ally with the lowest share of max HP. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up, 650 MP.
- **Cure.** Heals the ally with the lowest share of max HP; the amount scales from Magic. A quick, small top-up. Waits until an ally is below 80% of its max HP. Quick cooldown, quick wind-up, 450 MP. (Until Oct 28, 2026, a heal almost twice the size on a short cooldown, waiting for any ally below max HP: it healed to full every time, a heal nuke rather than a top-up.)
- **Raise.** Revives a random knocked-out ally with half of its max HP. Waits until an ally is knocked out. Very long cooldown, very long wind-up, 1,500 MP.

Until Oct 25, 2026 it had Mercy (each Light hit healed the lowest ally for half its damage), Prayer (Regen on every ally) and Purify (a cleanse). Mercy's free healing was what broke the campaign's balance (5a's probe), so the director replaced the three.

*Test, Raidwide:* every boss hit lands on all three. Cure keeps topping up whoever is lowest and Renew's Regen ticks on one ally at a time, both paid in MP, so a long fight can run it dry. Under Anti-heal, Rot cuts both, so there the Cleric is the wrong pick.

### Ranger

**Role:** wears down tough targets. **Plan:** looses barbed, cold and rending arrows at whatever stands in front, while its plain arrows fly past any guard and open the wounds.

**Weapons:** bow, dagger. **Armor weight:** medium. **Special stats:** Accuracy medium. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Hunter's eye.** Its basic attacks ignore Taunt: they go to a random foe, Taunting or not.
- **Barbed arrow.** Deals light physical damage that scales from Attack to a random foe, and adds several Bleed; stacks scale from Attack. Quick cooldown, quick wind-up, 200 MP. (Venom arrow, with Poison, until Oct 26, 2026.)
- **Frost arrow.** Deals light Ice damage that scales from Attack to the foe with the most Chill, and adds a few Chill; stacks scale from Attack. Quick cooldown, quick wind-up, 200 MP. A second source of Chill, so the Hexer and Frostweaver reach a shatter sooner. (Fire arrow, a heavy hit with many Burn on a medium cooldown, until Oct 28, 2026: it out-burned the Pyromancer, and made the Ranger a unit of three nukes.)
- **Rending arrow.** Deals physical damage that scales from Attack to the foe with the most Sunder, and adds a few Sunder; stacks scale from Attack. Short cooldown, normal wind-up, 325 MP. (A heavy hit adding several Sunder until Oct 28, 2026.)

*Test, Shield wall:* its arrows have no Seek, so they go to the Taunting, Guarded foe, which gets Bleed, Chill and Sunder, and the Sunder raises the damage it takes, offsetting some of its Guard. Its basic attacks ignore Taunt and land on the fragile dealers behind. Against a lone boss, everything stacks on one target.

## Defenders and support

Six classes that win through the party: a tank that punishes, a bodyguard, a shelter against magic, a storm spear who heals and revives, a tempo-setter and a curser.

### Bramble knight

**Role:** thorns tank. **Plan:** draws every foe onto its thorns, where each attacker is left bleeding for the party's blows, regrows whoever is lowest, and lashes out with the thorns themselves.

**Weapons:** sword, Earth staff. **Armor weight:** heavy. **Special stats:** Thorns high. **Resistances:** weak to Fire.

- **Passive – Briar.** Each time its Thorns damage a foe, that foe gets a few Bleed; stacks scale from Armor.
- **Thicket.** Applies Taunt and Guard to itself. Medium cooldown, quick wind-up, 200 MP.
- **Regrowth.** Applies Regen to the ally with the lowest share of max HP. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up, 325 MP.
- **Bramble lash.** Deals physical damage that scales from Thorns to a random foe. Quick cooldown, quick wind-up, 200 MP.

*Test, Swarm:* Thicket pulls all eight foes onto the knight. Each basic attack costs its attacker Thorns and leaves it bleeding for the party's next hits, Thicket's Guard blunts the many small hits, and Regrowth mends the knight. Against a Brute it does worse: one big swing triggers Thorns once.

### Hoplite

**Role:** bodyguard who opens foes up. **Plan:** plants its great shield beside the most vulnerable ally to catch the killing blow, locks shields with it, and leaves foes sundered with slow, crushing blows.

**Weapons:** spear, sword. **Armor weight:** heavy. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Phalanx.** Its basic attacks come half as often and deal double damage. (A third as often for triple damage until Oct 28, 2026: one swing a fight.)
- **Aspis.** Applies Aegis to the ally with the lowest share of max HP. Long cooldown, quick wind-up, 500 MP.
- **Paired guard.** Applies Guard to itself and to the other ally with the lowest share of max HP. Long cooldown, normal wind-up, 325 MP.
- **Spear thrust.** Deals physical damage that scales from Attack to a random foe, and adds a few Sunder; stacks scale from Attack. Medium cooldown, long wind-up, 325 MP.

*Test, Tank buster:* the huge swing lands on whoever the boss picked. Aspis puts Aegis on the lowest ally, which isn't always the target; when it is, the blow is blocked outright. Paired with the Knight it's reliable: the boss hits the Knight, the Knight becomes the lowest, and the Knight holds the Aegis. Between swings, its slow, double-damage basic attacks get through Armor that stops smaller hits, and Spear thrust's Sunder makes the boss take more from every hit.

### Paladin

**Role:** shelter against magic. **Plan:** shelters the party from spells and curses, and brings down slow, heavy, burning judgement, half again as hard on any foe that holds a buff.

**Weapons:** sword, Light staff. **Armor weight:** heavy. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** resists Light.

- **Passive – Judgement.** Its hits deal half again as much damage to foes with a buff. (Double until Oct 27, 2026.)
- **Sanctuary.** Applies Ward to every ally. Long cooldown, long wind-up, 1,000 MP.
- **Absolve.** Cleanses and heals itself and the other ally with the lowest share of max HP; the heal scales from Magic. Waits until an ally is below max HP. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up, 650 MP. (A cleanse alone, waiting for a debuff, until Oct 27, 2026.)
- **Smite.** Deals heavy Light damage that scales from Magic to the foe with the highest Attack or Magic, and adds several Burn; stacks scale from Magic. Medium cooldown, long wind-up, 650 MP. (Long cooldown and no Burn until Oct 26, 2026; a very long wind-up until Oct 28: the Paladin died in it.)

*Test, Nuker:* Sanctuary's Ward cuts the heavy spell across the party, and Absolve lifts whatever it leaves behind. Against a Buffer's allies, every hit lands half again as hard while their buffs last; beside a Hexer that strips those buffs, it loses that edge. Smite's long wind-up is its weakness: a fast fight may end before it lands.

### Valkyrie

**Role:** storm spear who heals and revives. **Plan:** a storm-borne spear that reaches past any guard and Shocks what it hits, mends the wounded, and snatches the fallen back ready to fight.

**Weapons:** spear, sword, Light staff. **Armor weight:** medium. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Einherjar.** Allies it revives come back with all their actions ready, instead of on cooldown.
- **Gale spear.** Has Seek. Three quick hits on a random foe that isn't Taunting; each deals Wind damage that scales from Attack and adds a few Shock, with stacks that scale from Attack. Quick cooldown, quick wind-up, 200 MP.
- **Rally.** Heals the ally with the lowest share of max HP; the amount scales from Magic. Waits until an ally is below 80% of its max HP. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up, 650 MP.
- **Valhalla.** Revives a random knocked-out ally with a tenth of its max HP. Waits until an ally is knocked out. Very long cooldown, quick wind-up, 1,500 MP.

*Test, Executioner:* Doom knocks out the Ranger. Valhalla brings it back almost at once, fragile but with every arrow ready, so the party loses little damage. With no Cleanse or Aegis beside her, Doom lands every time: the Valkyrie undoes it rather than stopping it. Next to the Cleric's Raise, Valhalla is the fast, fragile return; Raise is the slow, sturdy one.

### Bard

**Role:** party tempo. **Plan:** a caster at the back whose every note mends an ally a little and gives another mana, with a ballad that steadies the whole party, an allegro that quickens it, and thunder that shocks every foe.

**Weapons:** dagger, bow, Light staff. **Armor weight:** medium. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Refrain.** Each of its basic attacks that lands heals a random ally and gives a random ally MP, each 30% of the damage the attack dealt, so its weapon is a choice against a foe's Armor and resistance. (Encore, which went to the Cleric, until Oct 25, 2026; MP alone, a flat amount and then one that grew with Attack, until Oct 28.)
- **War ballad.** Adds a few Might to every ally; stacks scale from Magic. Quick cooldown, quick wind-up, 450 MP.
- **Allegro.** Applies Haste to every ally. Long cooldown, quick wind-up, 650 MP.
- **Thunder chord.** Adds several Shock to every foe; stacks scale from Magic. Short cooldown, normal wind-up, 650 MP. (Lullaby, Slow on a random foe, until Oct 26, 2026; many Shock until Oct 28: one geared chord reached Shock's cap.)

Step 5f merged War ballad and Allegro into Anthem (Haste and Might on a random ally) and gave the freed slot to Chorus (a small heal on every ally); in step 5g the director called the merge a mistake. The two songs came back on the whole party, and the Bard's healing moved into Refrain.

*Test, Curser:* the Curser Weakens and Slows the party. War ballad's Might, every few ticks on every ally, and Allegro's Haste outweigh both, and Refrain mends the chip damage. It lifts neither debuff, so a Cleric's Renew still helps. Thunder chord makes every foe pay for each action it takes.

### Hexer

**Role:** curser. **Plan:** the debuff bot, the Bard's counterpart: burns and shocks every foe a little so the party's statuses pile up, weakens and sunders the most dangerous one, chills whatever it strikes, and silences a foe mid-cast. (Oct 27, 2026; before: single-target curses that rarely mattered.)

**Weapons:** Ice staff, Dark staff. **Armor weight:** light. **Special stats:** Siphon medium. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Cold hex.** Its basic attacks add a few Chill, whatever staff it carries; stacks scale from Magic. (A third fewer until Oct 28, 2026: alone, it could never reach a shatter.)
- **Malison.** Adds a few Burn and a few Shock to every foe; stacks scale from Magic. Short cooldown, normal wind-up, 450 MP. (Hush, Silence on one foe, until Oct 27, 2026: it interrupted a cast 0.06 times an application.)
- **Unmake.** Applies Silence to the casting foe with the highest Attack or Magic and strips its buffs. Waits until a foe is in a wind-up. Medium cooldown, quick wind-up, 650 MP. (Unravel, one buff off one foe, until Oct 27, 2026; the director's rework also named a buffed foe as a trigger, which the engine's conditions can't join with "or": casting alone, for now.)
- **Enfeeble.** Applies Weaken and adds a few Sunder to the foe with the highest Attack or Magic; stacks scale from Magic. Quick cooldown, normal wind-up, 450 MP. (Slow until Oct 27, 2026: Chill covers it. Several Sunder until Oct 28: half of Sunder's cap in one cast.)

*Test, Buffer:* Unmake waits for the Buffer's wind-up, silences it and strips what it has already given; Malison's Burn and Shock on every foe punish the buffed allies for acting; Enfeeble's Weaken and Sunder go to the foe with the highest Attack or Magic, which may be the Buffer's guard rather than the Buffer. (Until Oct 27, 2026, Unravel stripped one buff per cast and Hush silenced one foe.)

## Fighters

Three damage dealers, each beating armor its own way: the Monk with Sunder, the Assassin with crits, the Vampire by outlasting it.

### Monk

**Role:** sunders with a blur of blows. **Plan:** every blow leaves the foe a little more open, Flow keeps it fast, burning fists pile onto the hottest foe, and a pressure point drops the most dangerous one for a moment.

**Weapons:** fist weapons, spear. **Armor weight:** medium. **Special stats:** Evasion high, Double strike medium. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Iron palm.** Its basic attacks, and the second strike of its Double strike, add a few Sunder; stacks scale from Attack.
- **Flow.** Applies Haste to itself. Long cooldown, quick wind-up, 200 MP.
- **Burning fist.** Three quick hits on the foe with the most Burn; each deals Fire damage that scales from Attack and adds a few Burn (three fifths of the word since step 5g: one cast made the Monk a bigger Burn engine than the Pyromancer), with stacks that scale from Attack. Quick cooldown, quick wind-up, 200 MP.
- **Pressure point.** Has Seek. Deals physical damage that scales from Attack to the foe with the highest Attack or Magic, and applies Stun. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up, 325 MP.

*Test, Armored shell:* its first double strikes barely scratch the shell, but each adds Sunder, so every hit after lands harder, and Flow speeds them up. With a spear, its double strikes also break Bulwark.

### Assassin

**Role:** burst killer. **Plan:** slips out of sight, picks off the weakest foe past any guard, leaves every crit a bleeding wound, and silences the back line.

**Weapons:** dagger, sword. **Armor weight:** medium. **Special stats:** Crit damage high, Evasion medium. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Lacerate.** Each of its crits adds several Bleed to the foe; stacks scale from Attack. (Shadow strike, every hit a crit while Hidden, until Oct 26, 2026.)
- **Vanish.** Applies Hidden to itself. Waits until it isn't Hidden. Short cooldown, quick wind-up, 200 MP.
- **Backstab.** Has Seek. Deals physical damage that scales from Crit chance to the foe with the lowest current HP. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up, 325 MP. (Heavy until Oct 28, 2026: too strong in the first fights, before gear adds crit.)
- **Garrote.** Has Seek. Deals physical damage that scales from Attack to a random foe that isn't Taunting, and applies Silence. Medium cooldown, quick wind-up, 325 MP.

*Test, Stalker:* the Stalker hunts the lowest-HP unit, usually the Assassin. Once Vanish is up, the Assassin is out of reach, and its crits leave Bleed for the party's hits to open. Until then it's exposed, so it wants a Shield or an Area ally beside it.

### Vampire

**Role:** drains to outlast. **Plan:** feeds on everything it hits: the harder it hits, the more it heals, its prey is left weakened, and it bleeds its own life into an ally's wounds and mana. (Oct 27, 2026: an exotic class, at the director's word, rather than a Lifesteal cut.)

**Weapons:** sword, fist weapons (claws), Dark staff. **Armor weight:** medium. **Special stats:** Lifesteal high. **Resistances:** weak to Light, resists Dark.

- **Passive – Bloodlust.** While it's below half of its max HP, its Lifesteal is doubled. (Step 5g, the director's: healing back from the brink is the identity, not sustain all fight, so Lifesteal is lower and the doubling stays.)
- **Blood drain.** Deals Dark damage that scales from max HP to a random foe, and applies Weaken. Quick cooldown, quick wind-up, 125 MP.
- **Blood gift.** Spends a fifth of its max HP to heal the other ally with the lowest share of max HP for as much, and gives it Clarity. Waits until another ally is below 80% of its max HP and the Vampire itself is at 35% of its max HP or more, so the price never kills it. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up, 450 MP. (Wither, Rot on the lowest foe, until Oct 27, 2026: one enemy in the roster heals.)
- **Frenzy.** Adds several Might to itself; stacks scale from Attack. Medium cooldown, quick wind-up, 450 MP.

*Test, Regenerator:* the Vampire out-heals the boss by hitting it: Blood drain's share of max HP and every basic attack feed its Lifesteal, Frenzy raises both, and Blood gift turns the surplus into an ally's HP and mana. Against Anti-heal it falters: Rot cuts its own Lifesteal, and Blood gift's price is still paid. (Until Oct 27, 2026, Wither Rotted the boss.)

## Mages

Four mages, each on its element's job: Fire ramps, Ice freezes and shatters, Lightning spreads, Earth crits from behind its Shields. Burn, Chill and Shock each have a source outside their mage, so the mage is the strongest source, not the only one.

### Pyromancer

**Role:** ramping Burn. **Plan:** every strike adds Burn, bolts pile more onto the hottest foe, and a pyre lands on it.

**Weapons:** Fire staff, Lightning staff. **Armor weight:** light. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Kindling.** Its basic attacks add a few Burn, whatever staff it carries; stacks scale from Magic.
- **Firebolt.** Deals Fire damage that scales from Magic to the foe with the most Burn, and adds several Burn; stacks scale from Magic. Quick cooldown, normal wind-up, 450 MP.
- **Fireball.** Deals Fire damage that scales from Magic to every foe, and adds a few Burn to each; stacks scale from Magic. Medium cooldown, long wind-up, 1,000 MP.
- **Pyre.** Deals heavy Fire damage to the foe with the most Burn; the damage scales from Magic and rises by 200 with each Burn stack on that foe (300 until Oct 28, 2026, when Burn lost its cap). The Burn stays. Long cooldown, very long wind-up, 1,500 MP.

*Test, Two states:* in the Armor phase its fire lands in full. In the resistance phase its hits do little, but Burn still lands and ticks. With a Monk or Hexer adding Burn too, it stays high through both phases.

### Frostweaver

**Role:** freezes and shatters. **Plan:** buries foes in Chill until it shatters into damage and a Stun, leaves the shattered foe sundered for the party, and spends the time it buys keeping an ally's mana flowing.

**Weapons:** Ice staff, Wind staff. **Armor weight:** light. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** resists Ice.

- **Passive – Shatterpoint.** When a foe's Chill shatters (at its cap, for Ice damage and a Stun), that foe takes several Sunder; stacks scale from Magic. (Brittle, 12% more damage for each Chill stack on the target, until Oct 28, 2026, when Chill became a shatter.)
- **Frost lance.** Deals Ice damage that scales from Magic to the foe with the most Chill, and adds several Chill (about half of a shatter at base Magic, so one lance never shatters a foe alone); stacks scale from Magic. Quick cooldown, normal wind-up, 450 MP.
- **Blizzard.** Deals Ice damage that scales from Magic to every foe, and adds a few Chill to each (a little under half the word); stacks scale from Magic. Medium cooldown, long wind-up, 1,000 MP.
- **Stillness.** Applies Clarity to the ally with the least MP among those under half their max MP, or, with nobody under half, the ally with the least, and adds a few Chill to every foe; stacks scale from Magic. Medium cooldown, quick wind-up, 650 MP. (Clarity alone until Oct 28, 2026; a random ally on a long cooldown until Oct 27.)

*Test, Skirmisher:* Blizzard, Frost lance and Stillness build Chill on the fast foes, slowing them, until it shatters for Ice damage and a Stun, and Shatterpoint leaves the foe sundered for the party. Its own basic attacks add no Chill, but Chill thaws slowly, so it builds between casts. With a Hexer or Ranger adding Chill too, the shatters come sooner.

### Stormcaller

**Role:** Shocks every foe. **Plan:** rains quick strikes across every foe so the fast and the many hurt themselves by acting, and charges itself up to strike faster.

**Weapons:** Lightning staff, Wind staff. **Armor weight:** light. **Special stats:** none. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Arc.** Each of its basic attacks also hits a second, random foe, and adds a few Shock to both; stacks scale from Magic.
- **Chain lightning.** Deals Lightning damage that scales from Magic to a random foe, then to up to three other random foes, and adds a few Shock to each; stacks scale from Magic. Quick cooldown, quick wind-up, 650 MP.
- **Thunderstorm.** Deals Lightning damage that scales from Magic to every foe, and adds a few Shock to each; stacks scale from Magic. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up, 1,000 MP.
- **Static charge.** Applies Haste to itself. Long cooldown, quick wind-up, 650 MP.

*Test, Swarm:* Arc, Chain lightning and Thunderstorm Shock all eight foes, and every time one acts, Shock hurts it. Against a lone Nuker it's weak: one slow caster barely sets off Shock, and Arc has no second foe.

### Geomancer

**Role:** hits hardest behind stone. **Plan:** wraps itself and an ally in stone; while its own Shield holds, its rocks crit far more often and far harder, and a tremor drops a foe now and then.

**Weapons:** Earth staff, Fire staff, axe. **Armor weight:** heavy. **Special stats:** Crit damage high. **Resistances:** none.

- **Passive – Bedrock.** While its Shield pool is above zero, its crit chance is doubled.
- **Earthen shell.** Applies Shield to itself and to the other ally with the lowest share of max HP; the amount scales from Armor. Medium cooldown, normal wind-up, 650 MP.
- **Rockfall.** Deals Earth damage that scales from Armor to a random foe. Short cooldown, long wind-up, 450 MP.
- **Tremor.** Deals Earth damage that scales from Magic to a random foe, and applies Stun. Long cooldown, very long wind-up, 1,000 MP.

*Test, Hit counter:* the boss strikes back every few hits it takes. The Geomancer lands few hits, but while its Shield holds they crit often and hard, so the counter rarely fires. A Shield breaks once hits use up its pool, so the Geomancer crits most while a Knight keeps foes off it. Area damage or a Stalker turns them off. (Until Oct 27, 2026, the Knight's Protect also turned its crits back on.)

## New mechanics and things to watch

The classes need five mechanics beyond the base combat system:

- a basic attack that also hits a second foe (Arc);
- damage that scales from a condition instead of a stat, resolved with the base amount: stacks on the target (Pyre) or a buff on it (Judgement);
- reviving with actions ready (Einherjar);
- basic attacks at half the speed for double damage (Phalanx);
- a reaction to a foe's Chill shattering (Shatterpoint).

To watch in simulation:

- **Stun.** A long-cooldown Stun with no timing condition may rarely catch a wind-up. The fallback is a shorter Stun on a shorter cooldown.
- **Pyre** may fire on a foe with very little Burn.
- **Unmake** may strip buffs the moment they land, which could feel like constant reversals.
- **Stand fast.** The Knight's Taunt lasts less than its cooldown, so check it holds bosses on Aggro alone in the gaps. (Sworn, the Taunt after a knockout, is gone since Oct 27, 2026.)
- **Aspis** goes to the lowest ally, who isn't always the one about to take the big hit. Check how often Aegis catches anything without a Knight drawing the boss.
- **Bedrock** depends on how long the Geomancer's Shield lasts. Check its crit uptime with and without a protector beside it.

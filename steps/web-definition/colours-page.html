Defining the web · a proposal

The Four Colours

What sits at the corners of the web, in place of the nine builds. This is the agent's proposal, for the director to argue with. The names are placeholders.

One blow, four moments

A foe's blow can be answered at four moments. Each moment is a colour, and a colour kills with the same thing it lives by. That is why what sits inside one colour works together.

a foe decides to strike
Before it's thrownSorcerystops itStun, Slow, Chill, Silence, Weaken
As it arrivesWindisn't thereEvasion, Blur, Hidden
As it landsIrontakes itShield, Defense, Guard, Aegis
After it landsBloodundoes itheals, Regen, Lifesteal
the blow is over
  • Killing first isn't a colour. It's Rush, one of the four ways to win.
  • A colour isn't an element. Sorcery holds fire, frost and lightning. One element to a colour is what made the first cut narrow.
  • A colour isn't a role. Each has to make a whole party: a front of its own kind, something that kills, and a way to last.

The colours

Each holds four or five themes: the words a player collects. Each has one hole, which the rules already make real, and which is the reason to take a second colour.

Iron

Let them come.

A fight is won by whoever is still standing, and what protects it is what it strikes with.

  • The anvilTaunt, the draw, blows taken, Thorns, Fixated
  • The shella Shield made, moved, broken and read; Aegis
  • The weightDefense, Guard and max HP, as armour and as what its blows scale from
  • The hammerslow heavy swings, long wind-ups, Sunder to open the target
Its hole: it can't hurry and can't choose.Defense never meets a status's damage. Nothing in its kits has Seek. Its killing waits on the foes' attacks or on long cooldowns.
Classes
Knight, Bramble knight, Hoplite, Geomancer
Wears
Plate, shields, hammers, great weapons

Wind

Strike first, strike often, be elsewhere.

A fight is won by whoever acts first, most often and where it chooses.

  • The flurryAttack speed, Haste, Double strike, extra strikes, what a hit carries
  • The dodgeEvasion, Blur, and what answers a dodge
  • The ambushHidden, crits, Crit damage, Brittle
  • The huntthe Mark, Seek, the weakest foe, a kill, the fight's start
  • The woundBleed, shared with Blood
Its hole: it has no body.Its classes have the least HP and Defense. Evasion never meets a status's damage or an Area. Defense cuts many small hits most: the same damage in swings half the size took 45% longer.
Classes
Ranger, Monk, Assassin, Valkyrie
Wears
Leather, cloaks, bows, daggers

Sorcery

Let the field do the work.

A fight is won by what's laid on the foes, which no armour stops and no dodge slips.

  • FireBurn. All killing, no safety. It dies unless it's fed, so it leans to Wind's tempo and Blood's well
  • FrostChill, Slow, the shatter and its Stun. The most safety, on a beat. It leans to Iron, who can wait for the break
  • LightningShock. Fast foes hurt themselves, slow foes hit softly. It leans to Iron, whose wall the foes have to strike
  • The curseSilence, Weaken, Bewildered, Dispel
  • The great castwind-ups, Cast speed, cooldowns, Area
Its hole: it starts late and runs dry.A built-up status pays only with upkeep. Its spells cost several times anyone else's, and nobody regains MP unaided. Bosses resist repeated Stun, Silence and Slow. A fight ended fast took Chill's killing down to a fifth.
Classes
Pyromancer, Frostweaver, Stormcaller, Hexer
Wears
Robes, orbs, totems, tomes, staves, wands

Blood

Everything has a price, and everything can be paid back.

HP and MP are currency: spent for power, drunk back, and given.

  • Mendingheals, Regen, Cleanse, Revive, healing past full
  • The priceHP paid, power when low
  • The drainLifesteal, Siphon, Rot
  • The wellMP and Clarity, Might, what one unit gives another
Its hole: it prevents nothing, and kills through others.Mending comes after the blow: Regen kept up saved 14 points of HP in a fast fight and 49 in a slow one. Under Rot only a Shield still works.
Classes
Cleric, Paladin, Bard, Vampire
Wears
Mail, relics, vials, maces

Six pairs, six doors

Any two colours can be combined, and each fills the other's hole. Each pair shares one word, which is the door between them.

  • Iron and Winddoor: SunderThe flurry opens what the hammer strikes. The wall gives the blades a body.Pulls: on one unit, Evasion 60 halved what Thorns killed.
  • Iron and Sorcerydoor: StunThe wall buys the pile its time. Foes that strike the wall are charged and chilled.Pulls: a Stunned or slowed foe strikes less, so Thorns earns less. Not measured.
  • Iron and Blooddoor: MightThe anchor and its keeper. Might given to the slow hitter. Healing past full becomes a Shield.Pulls: the slowest pair.
  • Wind and Sorcerydoor: HasteA faster unit lays more stacks.Pulls: both are thin, and a fight ended fast starves the pile.
  • Wind and Blooddoor: BleedCrits and kills feed. A wound is cashed by many hits.Pulls: neither prevents a big blow.
  • Sorcery and Blooddoor: MPThe well and the caster: two serve one.Pulls: slow to start.

  • The starters are three colours, and each points at the others. The Ranger's three arrows are three doors: Bleed to Blood, Chill to Sorcery, Sunder to Iron. Sorcery is the first colour a run finds.
  • The passive carries the signpost in 14 of 16. It is the one thing a new recruit always shows, whichever abilities it knows. The two that don't: the Paladin's reads anything laid on a foe, and the Valkyrie's is Iron's word on a Wind and Sorcery class.
  • The Vampire and the Bard are the softest of the six. The Vampire's only Wind word is Bleed, and the Bard hands out three doors at once (MP, Haste, Might).
  • Rush has no pair's signpost, only the Assassin and the Paladin's Mark. Retaliation has one.
  • The Valkyrie holds all four colours, so she has no hole. She is one of the two classes that lead whole runs.
  • A class's three identities are its smaller signposts, each leaning to another colour. By the agent's count of them, Sorcery with Blood has the fewest (three classes), and it is also the pair the loot is thinnest on.

What was checked

The kits fall four to a colour

Every class's passive, abilities and versions were read from the game's data. The sixteen classes sort four to a colour with none redesigned. The starters are three colours (Knight, Cleric, Ranger), so Sorcery is the first colour a run finds.

The armour already agrees

All four Iron classes wear Plate, all four Wind classes Leather, all four Sorcery classes Robes, and three of the four Blood classes Mail. Nobody designed armour as colours. It is how a colour's pieces can reach its classes with nothing written on a card.

The loot crosses every pair

Of the 268 pieces with an effect, by the words on their cards: 173 sit in one colour (Iron 39, Wind 42, Sorcery 40, Blood 52), 48 cross two or more, 47 have none. Every pair is crossed. The thinnest are Sorcery with Blood (3 pieces) and Iron with Sorcery (6). Of the 108 kept effects, 57 sit in one colour and 40 cross.

Three independent reads, and the fork

Three agents were set the same question apart from each other and from this cut: one starting from the rules, one from the class kits, one from the player.

  • All three found Iron as it is here. Two kept fire, frost and the curses in one colour, as here.
  • The one that started from the rules found the same root by itself: four moments at which a blow can be answered.
  • They split on the other half of the game: speed, the dodge, aim and kills, blood, and mending. It can be cut three ways, and that choice is yours.

Least sure

  • Blood may be a role and not a colour. Its bare trios get the least far. Its killing comes through gear (the price, the drain) and two classes, the Vampire and the Paladin.
  • Four reads like warrior, rogue, mage and priest. What has to hold is that a colour is a whole party. Bare, only Iron's front works. That is the wall problem from a new side: each colour would need a front of its own kind.
  • Retaliation and Rush may not be ways to win. Each is strong in one colour only. If that stays so, Retaliation is Iron's theme and Rush is Wind's. They hold only if Sorcery's charged field and Blood's feast become real.
  • Shock has no settled home. The three reads put it in three colours: it is laid by speed, paid on the foes' actions, and looks like an element.
  • The holes are soft. Of 40 pairings of a way to live with a way to kill, 29 changed nothing. A hole is mostly what a colour's payoffs read, and little in the rules forces it.
  • Two classes signpost three or four colours: the Paladin and the Valkyrie. A signpost should have its colours' holes, and the Valkyrie has none.

What I need from you

  1. The root. Four colours, one for each moment a blow can be answered. Is that the game?
  2. The fork. This cut, the predator, or five.
  3. Retaliation and Rush. Keep them as ways to win and give the other colours a real version of each, or let them be Iron's and Wind's themes.
  4. The names. Iron, Wind, Sorcery and Blood are placeholders.

# The wheel, version B: the director's intent

What the director wants from this work. Where it's wrong, they correct it.

- **Problems first, patterns after.** The director designs best by solving constrained problems one at a time, each from a specific part of the game, a tension or a missing connection, and then sorting the solutions into a shape. A blank page or a list to react to is harder ground for new ideas. This is the way of working they hold to be right.
- **The agent poses the problems.** Posing good problems (where the game is thin, what doesn't connect, what nobody does) is what the agent has done well. Finding the shape top-down from the rules is what it has done badly: it strips the game down to numbers going up and down.
- **The colours come out of the answers,** the way a set of concepts comes out of a hundred ideas: grouped by attitude, by the director, the agents or both.
- **Agnostic of the classes and the builds.** Both are tailored to synergies and combinations already made, and would bring them back in. That's why this workspace holds neither.
- **Run beside version A,** which finds the colours from a complete list of plays the director marks. The two are compared at the end.

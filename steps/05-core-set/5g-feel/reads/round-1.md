# Feel read, round 1: 12 packs from 291 rogue fights at heat 0

A cold read of `out/feel-r1/`, tick by tick, checked against the summary. Ticks are cited as `t`.

## Per pack

### Pack 1: Toll Gate (fight, act 1 floor 1), won t62
Eight ticks of basics, then Anthem, Cure and Frost lance land t8.7–t12.9, and fifty ticks of the same loop follow; nobody dropped under 983/2,000 and the first bandit fell at t42.7. The Cleric did 48% of the party's damage with a staff: Anthem went on it every time (Might 7.8 → 15) and its swings grew 1,008 → 2,217. The Bard swung for 378 and took the most hits. Chill read through Brittle (598 → 903 → 1,207 on Bandit C) and Bandit C swung three times to the others' five. A floor-one fight of 62 ticks with nothing at stake.

### Pack 2: Night Wolves (fight, act 1 floor 8), lost t10
The party arrived at 449/4,000, 997/4,500 and a dead Hexer against seven 5,600-HP wolves. Valkyrie dead to a 168 hit (t5.5), Stormcaller to a crit (t9.4), two wolves never acted. Only Arc showed: two 1,113 hits, Shock on both, 629 pulses when the wolves moved. Lost before tick 0.

### Pack 3: Twin Ogres (elite, act 1 floor 4), won t63
Ogres whose basics take six ticks to land (t3.6 → t9.6). The Knight Taunted at t8.7 and ate both Flattens (1,326 and Stun, t19.7, t25.5) and every swing, then died at t59.6 to a 422 basic, 2.6 ticks before Smite finished Ogre B. The Assassin carried it (56%): Backstab crits of 2,964 (t20.1, t48.2) and a Bleed cascade of 780 / 519 / 346 / 231 as allies hit. Paladin and Assassin took 0; Judgement never triggered; Stand fast's Clarity went to a Paladin at 2,920–3,000 MP (t8.7, t28.7). Legible, controlled, and the tank's death in a won fight felt wrong.

### Pack 4: The Packmaster (boss, act 1 floor 10), lost t90
Wolves dead by t19.7. Then Crush: 3,931 on the Bramble knight at 2,124 (t21.5), 5,410 raw on the Geomancer behind a 1,324 shield (t53.7), 4,635 on the Vampire (t85.9). Three casts, three kills, each telegraphed eight ticks with nothing to do about it. Blood gift cost the Vampire 900 HP to give the knight 900 (t20.4) a tick before Crush deleted it. From t53.7 the Vampire soloed the boss from 75% to 64% for 36 ticks.

### Pack 5: Fuse Room (fight, act 2 floor 1), won t36
Hellhound A: basic Burn 4.2 (t5), Burning fist 9.8 → 23 (t9.6), Firebolt → 35.8 (t11.7), pulses of 1,149 and 1,791, dead at t13. A 50%-fire-resistant dog burned to death by untyped Burn; Hellhound B the same at t29.7. The Hoplite swung once (3,029, t16.8) and its coat's Bleed did 6,805, 69% of its damage; Spear thrust began t31.8 and never landed; Aspis never ready. Sunder 12.7 on the bug showed nothing. Volatile never exploded (t35.7).

### Pack 6: Ghoul Pit (elite, act 2 floor 8), lost t51
The Monk arrived at 85/3,000 HP and 40 MP and at t13.8 put Burning fist+ on Ghoul D: 13.1 → 39.4, Malison topped it to the 40.3 cap (t17.3), pulses of 1,969 / 2,013 / 1,771 killed an 18,880-HP ghoul by t29.8. A near-dead Monk did 39% of the party's damage from one cast. Weaken read if compared (825 → 636 → 474); Sunder 20 did not. Three ghouls began Raise on one corpse (t29.8–t31.1); two failed. Ghoul basics at 635–825, four at once, decided it.

### Pack 7: The Giant's Wrath (boss, act 2 floor 10), won t18
Knob −77%: a 12,017-HP boss. An imp died before acting (t4.1). The Hoplite's single swing did 4,124 (t10.3); the Monk's 1,699 × 2 finished it (t17.9). Inferno began t9.4 to land t18.2; the giant died t17.9, having cast nothing and dealt 477. Vanish (t15.1) hid an Assassin nobody attacked. Sunder capped at 20 and meant nothing. A mugging, not a boss.

### Pack 8: Drone Wall (fight, act 3 floor 6), lost t31
Two members against seven zappers at 483 each. The Knight's 1,200 Shield was gone at t3.9, its 6,000 HP by t20.3; Guard (t16.3) did nothing against lightning; Thorns were 87% of its damage; it swung once. Six drone Taunts had zero effect (the Ranger ignores Taunt, the Knight never attacked). Rending arrow one-shot a 5,939-HP drone (6,800, t27.3); Barbed arrow's 20.8 Bleed never ticked (nobody hit Drone A again); Fire arrow never came.

### Pack 9: Sniper Ridge (elite, act 3 floor 4), won t59
Four archers at 1,049 a shot into a 3,000-HP Cleric (t3, t5.1, t10...), which healed itself all fight; Regen pulsed 60. The Shieldman Taunted and Guarded (t6.2, t10.6) against casters: Guard meant nothing to dark and earth, Taunt pulled the Vampire's basics into Armor 5 for 176–318, and it dealt 1,555 in 36 ticks. Blood drain hit 3,060 while Hidden (t12.7); Blood gift+ cost 1,000 HP for 1,399 (t20.4). Tremor+ (800 MP, 8 ticks, Stun) killed a 3,160-HP archer (t56.1). The Cleric died at t43.1; the win came healer-less.

### Pack 10: The Hive Queen (boss, act 3 floor 10), lost t329
Tempest (2,197 to all) killed the Hoplite at 1,642 and the Geomancer at 2,026 at t23.5. The Vampire then soloed 305 ticks on one loop: Call drones → Blood drain+ a drone (4,300–5,500, lifesteal 1,385, Lantern 800) → Tempest (1,690–2,197) → repeat. 17 Blood drain+, the queen at 22% by t318, Enrage t302.1, dead t328.6. Frenzy+ and Blood gift+ never cast (MP 170–320 all fight). Thirteen drone Taunts; two drones never acted. At t23.5 the Vampire got 600 + 1,200 Shield from no visible caster.

### Pack 11: Hounds (fight, act 2 floor 1), won t30
Firebolt and Chain lightning landed t9 and t9.2: Hellhound A at 49% with Burn 24.6 and Shock 12.6, dead at t13.1 to pulses of 1,228 / 1,081 / 1,195. Fire-resistant dogs burned again. Shock paid when hounds acted (1,259, 1,469, 1,889), 24% of damage. The hounds' Burn ramped on the Paladin to 4.9 stacks (247 a pulse), which read as "its bite burns". Smite wound up eight ticks for 2,700 (t27.8); Judgement and Sanctuary never. Pyromancer ended at 5,625/6,000 MP. Decided at t9.2, done at t30: a good shape.

### Pack 12: The Glacier King (boss, act 2 floor 10), lost t144
A 99,825-HP Titan with Bulwark 6, broken by wind basics and Gale spear at t41, t59.7, t84.8, t103.4, t128: five-tick windows where 1,307 became 2,614 and the Frostweaver critted 5,777 (t103.4, Broken + Chill 12 + torc). Hailstorm every ~40 ticks hit all three for 1,340 / 383 / 1,915. The Bramble knight (120 MP at start) cast Thicket five times and Bramble lash once (t94), reflected 750 Thorns + Bleed per swing taken, died t64.7, was raised at 650 HP (t71.6), Rallied to 2,000, Hailstormed to 85 (t94.1), killed t96. Blizzard: 800 MP, CRIT 255 (t34). After t96 two members took the Titan from 46% to 17%. The best boss here, and still a 144-tick loss.

## Across all packs

### 5. Rhythm
Every fight opens the same: eight ticks of basics, then every slot-one ability lands at t8–t10 (pack 1 t8.7/t9.5; pack 11 t9/t9.2; summary first casts 8.7–10.1 across ten slot-one abilities). A volley, not a build; in pack 11 it decided the fight. Wind-ups read when they pay off (ogre swings, pack 3; Crush, pack 4; Bulwark breaks, pack 12) and not when the fight ends first (Inferno, pack 7; Spear thrust, pack 5). Dead stretches: one survivor with no chance (pack 4 t53–t90; pack 10 t23.5–t329) and pack 1 end to end. Regular fights 30–62 ticks (summary mean 51–60), elites 51–63 (60–70), bosses 18 to 329 (act 2 boss 10%–90%: 18–144). Elites don't feel longer than fights; bosses have no length of their own.

### 6. Abilities
Slot one fires 2.5–4 times a fight and is each kit (Gale spear, Burning fist, Firebolt, Chain lightning, Blood drain, Cure, Anthem, Enfeeble, Thicket). Slot three barely exists: Raise 0.04 casts a fight (first t96.9), Valhalla 0.15, Unmake 0.22, Sanctuary 0.32, Tremor 0.5, Fireball and Pyre 0.57, Aspis 0.72 (first t39), Fire arrow 0 of 5. The 800-MP casts are the worst value seen (Blizzard 1,084 a cast against Frost lance's 1,286 at 300 MP; the packs' cases are in item 9 below); Blood drain+ at 150 MP is the opposite: 4,600 and a 1,385 heal every 16 ticks (pack 10). Smite is eight ticks for one 2,550–2,700 hit a fight (packs 3, 11; 1.07 casts). The Valkyrie and Stormcaller have a tempo; the Hoplite (one swing a fight), Knight and Bard don't.

### 7. Statuses
- **Burn** is the nuke: one cast reaches 25–40 stacks (packs 5, 6, 11) and at the cap pulses ~2,000 every two ticks, 44% of a party's damage (pack 6), 27% over 137 fights. Untyped, so fire-resistant Hellhounds die to it. From a bite, Burn 1 is 50 a pulse (pack 5); four bites on one tank ramp to 247 (pack 11), which read.
- **Shock** is legible: 100 a stack when the holder acts, then halved (packs 11, 12). Tempest's Shock 1 is nothing.
- **Bleed** reads best: a cascade as allies hit (pack 3), Thorns + Briar on every incoming swing (pack 12). It needs follow-up hits: 20.8 stacks never ticked (pack 8).
- **Sunder**: 109 fights, 0 damage, pinned at 20 after two or three sigil hits (packs 7, 12), visible only as physical hits creeping up. **Chill** shows through Brittle and a slower target (packs 1, 12); a boss's Chill 1 shows nothing. **Weaken** shows on boss basics (910 → 700, pack 10). **Might** doubled the Cleric's swings (pack 1).
- **Taunt** works for the party (every Flatten and Crush on the tank) and is a dud for foes (packs 8, 9, 10). **Guard** vs magic is nothing (packs 8, 9). **Regen** and **Clarity** show nothing (item 8). **Shield**: Vanguard's lasts 20 ticks against two ogres, 4 against seven zappers (packs 3, 8). **Bulwark/Broken** is the one boss status with a shape (pack 12). **Haste**, **Aegis**, **Ward**, **Silence**: never seen to do anything (Flow's Haste invisible, pack 5; Aspis, Sanctuary, Garrote never cast).

### 8. Mana
Casters never run short inside a fight: Pyromancer left 4,870 and 5,625 of 6,000 (packs 5, 11), the Cleric ended pack 1 at 5,000 having regained more than it spent, Hexer 95% left, Ranger 96%. Mana changed what a unit did only when it arrived empty: the Bramble knight at 120 MP cast Thicket five times and its damage move once (pack 12), the Vampire at 380 never reached Frenzy+ in 329 ticks (pack 10). Clarity, Refrain and Soul jar mostly refilled full units (packs 1, 3, 9); Soul jar did take the Vampire 350 → 1,910 (pack 9). Siphon never appeared.

### 9. Classes
Sources (summary): Cleric 99% basics; Bard and Hexer 0% abilities; Knight 78% basics at a 12% share; Hoplite 70% basics from one swing a fight; Bramble knight 71% statuses; Pyromancer and Monk 50% statuses (Burn); Stormcaller 61% share; Vampire 34%, 92% alone; Assassin 41% abilities; Valkyrie a spread; Geomancer and Frostweaver mostly staff basics. Fantasy fits: Assassin, Stormcaller, Bramble knight, Pyromancer. Misses: the Cleric as top damage with a staff (pack 1), the Hoplite whose spear isn't its damage (pack 5), the Knight whose armour is (pack 8).

Worst three: **Hoplite** (one swing in packs 5, 7, 10; abilities first ready t24–t39; Aspis never), **Knight** (12% share; dies in a won fight t59.6; melts in 20 ticks under chip; Clarity to full allies), **Bard** (378 a swing, a battery for the Cleric). The Hexer is close (Malison 280 a pulse, pack 6).

Best three: **Assassin** (Backstab crits and the Bleed cascade, pack 3), **Stormcaller** (Arc, Chain lightning on all four, Shock paying every time a foe moves, pack 11), **Valkyrie** (Gale spear's tempo, breaking Bulwark, Rally, the only third slot that fired and mattered, pack 12 t71.6). The Vampire's fantasy reads too, but what it produced was pack 10.

### 10. Enemies and bosses
Boss moves either one-shot or don't land: Crush (pack 4) and Tempest (pack 10) kill on every landing with eight or six ticks of warning and no counter in reach; Inferno (0.75 a fight) never landed in pack 7. Hailstorm is pitched right (1,340–1,915 to all every ~40 ticks, pack 12). Elites: the ogres feel like elites (slow, heavy, Stun); the Shieldman wastes 36 ticks and deals 708 an appearance; the ghouls' threat was their basics, not Raise (0.17 a fight). Minions mostly act before dying (Wolves 97%, Drones 92%, Imps 89%). Felt like nothing: Snakes (95 an appearance), lone Wisps and Drones (though seven killed a Knight), the Shieldman, the Bomb Bug, the Fire Giant at its knob. Regions read mainly through the boss: clubs, Burn, Taunt-and-snipe, Bulwark.

### 11. Drama
Close: pack 3 (tank down t59.6, win t62.2), pack 9 (healer down t43.1, win t59). A comeback attempt: pack 12 after t96. Decided in ten ticks: packs 2, 7, 11, and pack 10 at t23.5. Slogs: pack 10 (305 ticks), pack 4 (36 ticks of a solo), pack 1 (62 ticks never under half). One-shots both ways (items 3 and 13). Lost before tick 0 by carry-over: packs 2 and 6, arguably 4 and 12.

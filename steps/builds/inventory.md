# The twelve builds: an inventory for the director's read

For each build: what you see watching it, its routes, its key pieces by rung with a line each, its mythics, its formations, and the versions that pull toward it. Names are the cards' names; the lines are the cards in fewer words. Only a build's spine is listed (the pieces the designer data marks as its own, `PIECE_BUILDS`), not every common that leans its way.

**Before you read:** five kinds are first fills (axe, hammer, orb, trophy, cloak). Their rares and legendaries were written to a kind's profile, not designed from a build. Where a build's far end leans on them, the build's last line says so.

## Landslide: a few huge blows from behind stone

Watch for: Shield bars on the front, long quiet with small numbers, then one blow that takes a chunk off an elite. Fights run long. Spends MP on stone.
Routes: Geomancer · Knight · Hoplite / Geomancer · Paladin · Cleric / no earth: Knight (great weapon) · Hoplite · Bard.

- **Rung 1:** a slow weapon and a Shield source. Carapace charm (amulet: start behind a Shield), Roaring greataxe (start with Might), Warden's hammer (swings add Defense as damage), Keystone cuirass (plate: hit harder per Defense), Mason's hammer (each hit builds a Shield from Defense). - **Rung 2:** Millstone (amulet: basic attacks half as often, two and a quarter times as hard), War horn (the slowest hitter starts with many Might), Bastion plate (start Guarded), Stone totem (start behind a Shield from Defense), Giant's knucklebone (trophy: crits Guard you), Heartsplitter (great weapon: swings from max HP, not Attack), Rubble shield (kite: when your Shield breaks, the breaker is Stunned), Rootstone hammer (swings Sunder, from Defense). - **Rung 3:** Cornerstone maul (great weapon: plus half your Shield on every swing, and swinging never spends it), Worldbreaker (great weapon: half as often, triple, Stuns), Mountainfall (hammer: plus a fifth of max HP a swing), Rubble plate (a Shield break hits every foe for the most it held, and rebuilds a quarter), Spiked bulwark (kite: Thorns from Defense), Earthmother totem (allies' Shields a third larger; a break gives Might), Dragon's tooth (trophy: crits strike again past Defense). - **Mythic:** Mountain's crown (Geomancer amulet: each crit rebuilds its Shield by the damage dealt).
- **Versions:** Geomancer Boulder (Rockfall: double, slower, at the biggest foe), Faultline (Bedrock: crit chance doubled while shielded; a break makes the next hit a triple crit), Stone skin (Earthen shell on everyone, smaller); Knight Stone guard (Fortify the ally with the most Defense, plus a Shield), Crushing bash (double, from HP and Shield together, no Stun); Hoplite Shieldbearer (Phalanx: half as often, double, Defense adds); Bard Rousing chorus (Might on all, double on the slowest).
- First fills in its spine: the hammers and the trophies.

## What the shape says before you read

- **The spines are uneven.** Cold Snap has 31 pieces and 8 versions, Ambush 6 versions, Lockdown 41 pieces; the Toll has 97 pieces and 25 versions, Thousand Cuts 63 and 21.
- **Five builds lean on first-fill kinds at their far end:** the Hunt (axes, trophies), Landslide (hammers, trophies), Thousand Cuts (cloaks), Bonfire and Overflow (orbs).
- **Several pieces sit on three or four spines** (Hummingbird charm, Echo charm, Cataclysm staff, Hexweave mail, Bell of the hour, Oathbound shield, Orb of the second sun). That's the web working, or the same piece everywhere, depending on how it felt in play.

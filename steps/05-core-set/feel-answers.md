# Feel check: answers

Read as a fresh reader: the sheet, the five packs, and nothing else. Where I give enemy HP it is me adding up the damage lines until "is down"; the game never shows it.

## Pack 1

Knight, Cleric, Ranger on the Old Road. Lost at the bandit camp, fight 4 of 6.

**1. Retell.**
Three easy fights: bandits fell to arrows and bashes, the Knight blocked nearly every wolf bite for 0, and the snake pit was a slow poison drain that left the Cleric at 840 and the Ranger at 980 going into the camp. There an Archer crit the Ranger dead in the opening volley (1410, from 980 HP) and twenty seconds later another Archer crit killed the Cleric as it started a Prayer. The Knight then fought alone for ninety seconds, cycling Fortify, Protect and bashes: it killed the Shieldman, Archer A and the Bandit, sitting at 460 HP from t80 onward on nothing but shields. It died at t120 to Archer B, which had taken one hit and needed one more.

**2. Enemies.**
- Bandit: ordinary. 460 on the back line, 100 on the Knight, 450 on a crit. About 4000 HP.
- Wolf: six at a time, bites for 260, and the Knight blocks nearly all of them. About 2000 HP, so one or two hits each. Harmless with a Knight in front.
- Snake: bites for 260 and poisons on every bite, including fully blocked ones. The poison is 120 every two seconds for about ten seconds, and it re-applies faster than Purify clears it. About 2000 HP.
- Shieldman: a tank. Alternates Shield up (TAUNT) and Brace (GUARD). The Knight's hits dropped to 260 under Brace, the Ranger's venom arrow did 800 instead of 1280. Hits for 100. Took around 10,000 to kill.
- Archer: the killer in this pack. 500 on the Knight, 860 on the back line, crits of 1410. About 2000 HP, two Knight hits, so "falls fast" is true if anyone hits it.
- Ogre, Ogre Chief: never reached.

**3. Why lost, and next colour.**
Three things stacked. First, there is no recovery between fights: the party walked into the camp with the Cleric at 42% and the Ranger at 33%, both inside one crit of an Archer. Second, once alone the Knight spread its swings over all three foes (Archer B, Archer A, Bandit, Bandit, Archer A, Bandit, Bandit) instead of finishing the two Archers it had already wounded; one hit on Archer B at t99 instead of the Bandit and it wins. Third, the Knight's protection went to the wrong place at the wrong time: when the Cleric was at 220 HP at t20 the Knight put its Protect on itself, and the taunt it threw when the Ranger fell lasted t10 to t18, between two enemy volleys, so it caught one Shieldman poke and nothing else.

Next time I'd want someone whose job is to stop the one-shot on the back line, and area damage to kill the Archers before their second volley. I'm picking from the blurbs.
Colour: Hoplite, Cleric, Stormcaller

**4. Drama.**
Yes, the most of the five packs, in a grim way. The Ranger dropping to a single crit at t10 of the camp was a shock. The Knight's lone stand was real tension: it hovered at 460 HP for forty seconds behind shields that ran out exactly as volleys landed, and it died with the last enemy one hit from death. The Shield bash crit for 2835 that finally killed the Shieldman was a good beat. A loss on the last hit.

**5. Flat or unclear.**
- The snake pit is a wall of "takes 120 from POISON" lines. It is the fight that decided the run and there is no moment in it.
- The Cleric never tried to raise the Ranger. It had twenty seconds, attacked once, then started a Prayer and died. The blurb promises "the fallen raised". I can't tell if Raise was unavailable or just not chosen.
- Purify cleared one person once while three were poisoned, then never again. Felt like it wasn't trying.
- Every fight is silent until t6, then enemies act and the party follows at t8 or t10.
- The Knight's solo is 75 lines of Fortify / Protect / soak. The tension was there but the read was long.

## Pack 2

Same trio, same dungeon. Won.

**1. Retell.**
The first three fights went as in pack 1 but kinder: fewer crits on the back line, Protect went on the Ranger, and the party reached the camp at 63 / 76 / 66%. The Ranger's first venom arrow killed Archer A with poison ticks before its second shot, all three burned the Shieldman down, and the first Prayer of either run finally landed. At the bridge the Ogre's Flatten hit the Ranger for 1920 but a shield ate most of it. In the boss fight the Chief's Crush crit the Knight for 8400 and killed it from full HP with a 1500 shield up; the Cleric raised it, the Knight's next Shield bash stunned the Chief mid-windup and cancelled its second Crush, the third Crush landed for 5400 and left the Knight at 1100, and the Chief died to a Burn tick at t108.

**2. Enemies.**
- Bandit, Wolf, Snake, Shieldman: as in pack 1. Archer turned out to have exactly 2000 HP: 1400 plus five poison ticks killed it to the point.
- Ogre: slow. Its plain attack has a four-second windup ("begins attack" t16, lands t20) and hits the Knight for 500. Flatten has a four-second windup, does about 1900 and stuns. 16,000 to 17,000 HP.
- Ogre Chief: the Ogre writ large. Plain hits of 500 on the Knight, 860 on the Ranger. Crush has an eight-second windup and does 5400, or 8400 on a crit, more than the Knight's whole bar. A stun during the windup cancels it. Brings two Bandits. Around 60,000 HP by my rough adding.

**3.**
Won.
Colour: -

**4. Drama.**
Three real moments. The Knight evaporating at t38.5 to a Crush crit, full HP and shield up, was the biggest single swing in all five logs. The Cleric raising it eight seconds later and the fight carrying on felt earned. And "Ogre Chief is interrupted: Crush is lost" at t75.5 is the one time in five packs where something the party did answered something the enemy was doing. The boss dying to a Burn tick is a wet ending.

**5. Flat or unclear.**
- The boss fight is seventy seconds of the same loop (Venom, Fire, Rending, arrow, bash, Prayer) with poison and burn ticks every second or two. Outside the three Crushes nothing changes. The Chief's ordinary 500s are background noise next to the 8400, so it has effectively one move.
- I couldn't tell how close the boss was to dying at any point. I only know it took about 60,000 because I added it up afterwards.
- "Cleric is healed for 0 (2000 HP)" dozens of times under Prayer. Noise.
- Blocked snake bites still poison the Knight ("takes 0 BLOCKED" then "Knight: POISON" three times). Reads like a bug even if it is a rule.
- The bridge and the boss have the same escort and the same shape. The rehearsal is a good idea, but the boss adds nothing the Ogre didn't except size.

## Pack 3

Hoplite, Cleric, Stormcaller on the Old Road. Won.

**1. Retell.**
The Stormcaller shredded the toll gate in twenty seconds: arcing strikes and chain lightning put Shock on everyone and the bandits hurt themselves each time they acted. Then five of six wolves jumped the Stormcaller at once and left it at 100 HP, then 40; it survived the fight and died to the first snake bite of the next one. The Hoplite and Cleric ground through the snake pit alone under constant poison, the Cleric bottoming out at 20 HP before its own staff hit healed it, and raised the Stormcaller at t48. In the camp the Stormcaller killed Archer A and was killed by Archer B in the same second; the Hoplite's plain swing crit the Shieldman for 4429. The bridge was quick. In the boss fight Crush killed the Hoplite, then the Stormcaller, each raised in turn, and the Hoplite's 2940 swing finished the Chief four seconds before the third Crush would have landed.

**2. Enemies.**
- Bandit: about 4000 HP; hits the Stormcaller for 580.
- Wolf: as before, but with no Knight they go straight for the 2000-HP caster: five on one target in the first volley. "Hunts in packs" was true this time.
- Snake: poison as before; the Cleric spent the whole pit at or under 1000 HP from it. One took 3300 from a Hoplite swing and died, another needed 2310.
- Archer: still 2000 HP (crit 1260 + 800 killed it), still one-shots a weakened caster (980 on 500 HP).
- Shieldman: around 10,000 HP. Spear thrust did 210 to it against 930 to others; the Hoplite's plain swing did 4429.
- Ogre: Flatten 1800 plus stun on the Hoplite; about 17,000 HP; Shock on it reached 14 and it hurt itself for 900 to 1100 per action.
- Ogre Chief: Crush 5400 to 5880, one-shots either front-liner or the caster. Around 60,000 HP. Shock reached 20.8 and ticked for up to 1666 when it acted.

**3.**
Won.
Colour: -

**4. Drama.**
Plenty, almost too much. Stormcaller at 100 then 40 HP in the wolf fight; dying to the first bite of the next fight (grim rather than dramatic); the Cleric at 20 HP from a poison tick at t50 with a Raise half-cast; the Hoplite's two 4400-plus crits; and the finish, a 2940 swing with Crush four seconds out. The Stormcaller killing Archer B with a Shock tick after it was already dead was a nice touch. The run felt like a win against the odds. It also felt lucky.

**5. Flat or unclear.**
- The Hoplite "plants its great shield beside the most vulnerable ally to catch the killing blow". The Stormcaller took the killing blow three times (t6 snakes, t10 camp, t76 Crush) and the Hoplite caught none of them. Aspis went on the Hoplite itself in two fights. AEGIS did eat one hit each on the Cleric and the Stormcaller, but those were a 20-damage snake bite and a 100-damage bandit poke. The promise and the log don't match.
- The Hoplite's named ability, Spear thrust (690 to 1050), does a quarter of its plain swing (2940 to 3300, crits 4400 to 4700). The blurb's "slow, crushing blows" turn out to be the unnamed swings. Odd.
- "SHOCK 2.8", "SHOCK 5.6", "SHOCK 20.8": I worked out it stacks and the ticks scale with it, but the number itself means nothing to me. Same for "SUNDER 2.1" and "BURN 9.2".
- Nothing the party does reacts to "Ogre Chief begins Crush." The eight-second telegraph is right there and the auto-fight ignores it. Whether a stun lands in that window is luck of the cooldowns.
- The Stormcaller spent roughly 85 of 290 seconds dead. Fun class, paper body, and nothing in this trio keeps foes off it.

## Pack 4

Ranger, Cleric, Pyromancer on the Old Road. Lost at the snake pit, fight 3 of 6, at t6.

**1. Retell.**
Three bandits died fast to arrows and firebolts, though two of them put the Pyromancer at 490 and a crit put the Cleric at 1190. Six wolves then hit whoever they liked: the Cleric was at 160 HP six seconds in, the Pyromancer died at t18 with a Fireball half-cast, the Cleric died at t30 mid-Prayer, and the Ranger finished the last three wolves alone at 410 HP. The snake pit lasted two bites.

**2. Enemies.**
- Bandit: hits the 2000-HP casters for 580, crit 930. Three of them took both casters to half in the first exchange.
- Wolf: with nobody to block, six bites of 260 to 510 a round land on 2000-HP bodies. That is the whole story.
- Snake: 260 a bite, two bites, done.
- Everything else: unseen.

**3. Why lost, and next colour.**
No front line. Every enemy hit went straight to a 2000 or 3000 HP body and nothing blocked, guarded or taunted; the Cleric's 700 heals every ten seconds couldn't cover six wolves. Then, as in pack 1, no rest: a lone Ranger at 410 HP was sent into five enemies, which was never a fight. The run was over at t30 of the wolf pack. The Pyromancer's "ramping Burn" barely started: three casts in the whole run.

Keep the fire, add plate.
Colour: Knight, Cleric, Pyromancer

**4. Drama.**
The Cleric at 160 HP at t6 of the wolf fight was a jolt, and the Ranger's lone clean-up of three wolves was a small satisfying beat. The ending was the opposite of drama: a two-line fight and "Lost at t6." No comeback was possible, and I could see that from t18.

**5. Flat or unclear.**
- The game sends a party with two dead members into the next fight. Is that a rule? It felt broken rather than harsh.
- Fireball takes four seconds to cast and the Pyromancer died two seconds in. Watching the caster stand still and die mid-cast was flat.
- The dungeon says "clearable by the ungeared starting trio". This isn't that trio, fine, but nothing in the text hints that three casters is a death sentence; the Cleric's blurb says it "keeps the party standing through a whole dungeon".
- Two fights in and the run was decided. Shortest and least interesting pack.

## Pack 5

Knight, Bard, Vampire in the Crypt. Lost at the boss, fight 5 of 5.

**1. Retell.**
The Vampire did almost all the killing for five fights. Against skeletons the Bard's dagger did 0 and the Knight's sword did 140, so the bone yard took 45 seconds of Blood drains. The dark hall and curse room went fine; the Witch died to a Shield bash before it cursed anyone. The ghoul pit was a 92-second loop in which Ghoul B was killed five times and raised four, and the Knight, with nobody to heal it, went from 3450 to 1766 on 100-damage pokes. In the boss fight the Bard died in twenty seconds; the Reaper's Death bell put a ten-second DOOM on the Vampire (3500 damage, down to 500, then it drained back to full), then on the Knight twice (a 2330 shield saved it at 426 HP the first time; a 1330 shield didn't the second), and the lone Vampire took one more DOOM to 500 and one scythe hit.

**2. Enemies.**
- Skeleton: the heavy armor is real. Dagger 0, sword 140, Blood drain 1400. Hits 460. 3200 to 4300 HP.
- Ghost: the Vampire's Blood drain did 350 RESIST to it, and its own hits did 350 RESIST on the Vampire; the Knight's sword crit it for 1650 and a bash did 1750. Hits the Knight for 700. So magic passes through it and swords don't. About 6500 HP.
- Bat: 260 bites. One MISS against it in nine or so swings; "hard to hit" was one miss. Two hits to kill.
- Witch: hits 500, dead in 23 seconds, never cursed. I know nothing about its curse.
- Ghoul: 100 on the Knight, 460 on the Vampire. Took 8500 to 10,000 to drop the first time, which is a lot; raised ones dropped after about 3000. When one dies the others start an eleven-second Raise, and if two try the second fails. They did this five times in one fight.
- Reaper: 170 to 620 on the Knight, 530 on the Vampire, nothing frightening by itself. Death bell: four-second cast, then DOOM, and ten seconds later 3500 damage, shields count against it. Rang every 34 seconds. Took over 38,000 and was still standing; no idea how close it was.

**3. Why lost, and next colour.**
No healer and no curse lifter. The Knight's bar went down every fight and nothing brought it back; by the boss it was at 1766 with a Bard at 791 beside it. The Bard did nothing to skeletons for a whole fight and never healed; the Vampire heals only itself. DOOM is a flat 3500 on a timer, so a Knight under 3500 needs a full shield at the exact moment or it dies, and the second time the shield was 1330. Against a boss with more than 38,000 HP the lone Vampire drains roughly 350 a second and the DOOM clock wins. The Knight's taunt when the Bard fell (t20 to t28) again fell between enemy swings and caught one bat.

I'd want a healer who lifts curses and something that shelters from spells, and keep the Vampire since it carried.
Colour: Paladin, Cleric, Vampire

**4. Drama.**
Some. DOOM taking the Vampire to 500, "BLOODLUST" kicking in, then a 1260 drain-heal back to 2600 was a real comeback beat. The Knight surviving its first DOOM at 426 HP because Protect happened to give a 2330 shield that time was a close call. The ghoul pit was more exasperating than dramatic. The ending was a slow strangle, not a cliffhanger: once the Knight died I knew the Vampire would lose, and it took 36 more seconds of the same two lines.

**5. Flat or unclear.**
- "Bard attacks Skeleton A. Skeleton A takes 0 BLOCKED." four times in the bone yard, six in the run. The game knows the dagger can't hurt bone; the Bard keeps stabbing.
- What the Bard's songs did I mostly had to infer. War ballad's MIGHT took the Vampire's drains from 1400 to 2307, which is visible; Allegro's HASTE shifted the Knight's swings by a second or two; Lullaby's SLOW I could not see at all.
- "BLOODLUST" isn't in the Vampire's blurb and isn't explained. I guessed "low-HP rage, bigger drain heals".
- The Vampire's WEAKEN was doing real work (the Reaper hit the Knight for 170 weakened and 500 not; a weakened Ghoul hit 249 instead of 460) but only visible by comparing numbers across lines.
- The Witch's whole gimmick fizzled. The curse room was a nothing fight.
- The Reaper's ordinary hits are so weak that the whole fight is waiting for a bell. 148 seconds.

## Across all packs

**6. Build-up and difference.**

Old Road's shape is three bandits, six wolves, snakes with bandits, the camp, an ogre with bandits, the Chief with bandits. Enemy count goes down while single-enemy threat goes up, and the bridge is an honest rehearsal of the boss (slow windup, big stunning hit). That is the right shape. But for the starter trio the danger peak was not the boss: it was the snake pit's poison drain rolling into the camp's archer crits. One of two starter runs died there, and the other reached the boss by not getting crit. The boss itself was long rather than tense: one monstrous move every 30 to 40 seconds and a hundred lines of ticks in between. So it builds in theme and in damage per hit, but the danger has a hump in the middle and a long plateau at the end.

The Crypt felt genuinely different, and the difference was readable from the names alone: bone that blunts weak hits, a ghost that shrugs off the Vampire, ghouls that dig each other up, a reaper that rings a ten-second death bell. Old Road asks "do you have enough HP and a front line"; the Crypt asks "does your kit have an answer to this", and a dagger Bard, a 140-damage sword and nobody to lift DOOM were the wrong answers. It built up as attrition (the Knight's bar sinking fight after fight) rather than as escalating threat. The bell is a better boss mechanic than Crush because you can count down to it, but the party had no way to answer it either, and the fight was far too long for what happens in it.

Things that hurt both dungeons: no recovery between fights, so a run is really one long fight with scene changes; bosses whose HP is invisible and huge, so you can't feel whether you're close; and auto-combat that never reacts to an enemy's windup even when the log telegraphs it eight seconds ahead.

## Reading the logs themselves

- Status numbers are opaque: "SHOCK 2.8", "BURN 9.2", "SUNDER 5.2", "MIGHT 3.9". Shock stacks so I guessed intensity; Burn is always 9.2 and lasts sixteen seconds, so it is neither duration nor tick. A player would not know what the number is.
- Order: "Knight's SHIELD ends." comes before "Knight takes 8400, 1500 soaked by a shield." Likewise "Vampire's DOOM ends." then "Vampire takes 3500 from DOOM." The effect ends before the line that says it did something. Reads backwards.
- "Knight takes 500, 500 soaked by a shield (2420 HP, 1000 shield)": my first read was 500 to HP plus 500 to shield. It means 500 total, all to the shield. "takes" plus a number that never reached HP is misleading.
- "takes 0 BLOCKED" followed by "Knight: POISON" three times. If blocked bites poison, the log should say the bite landed. If they shouldn't, it's a bug.
- Repeated "Knight: POISON. Knight: POISON." when already poisoned, with the tick unchanged. Noise, or hidden stacking I can't see.
- "is healed for 0 (2000 HP)" under Prayer, dozens of times.
- Heals have no source. "Cleric is healed for 460" at t10 is the Cleric's staff hit; "Knight is healed for 200" at t36 is REGEN; "Vampire is healed for 420" is drain. I sorted them by rhythm and adjacency, not by the text.
- Shield sizes: 1500 most times, then 2300, 1590, 2330. I eventually worked out a new Protect adds to whatever shield is left (800 + 1500 = 2300). The log never says so.
- "Bat A: MISS." reads as the bat missing. It was the Knight missing the bat.
- "Cleric takes CRIT 0 AEGIS": "CRIT 0" is odd. "AEGIS absorbs a crit" would be clearer.
- "Ogre begins attack" is the only plain attack with a windup line. Fine once understood, but it reads like a missing ability name.
- "Hoplite uses Paired guard on Hoplite." then both Hoplite and Cleric get GUARD. The target line is incomplete.
- Indented lines sometimes belong to the previous action and sometimes don't: "t36 Knight attacks Ogre. / Ogre takes 1090. / Ranger's STUN ends." makes the stun look caused by the hit.
- The Stormcaller's Arc strike and its SHOCK application are split into two timestamped blocks, so the status looks like a separate event.
- "(Neutral)" after the party list is never explained.
- Every fight is silent until t6, then everyone acts within a few seconds. If there is a reason, the log doesn't give it.
- Party HP carrying over and dead members staying dead between fights is never stated; I inferred it from the opening HP lines. Pack 4's "Cleric 0/2000 HP, Pyromancer 0/2000 HP" header is where I realised.
- "Ghoul C's Raise fails." with no reason. In context (Ghoul A got there first) it's guessable.
- GUARD knocks about 240 off every hit (wolf 260 to 20, bandit 460 to 220, archer 500 to 260), so it makes small foes harmless and barely dents an Archer. Readable only by comparing lines.
- No sense of enemy health at all, not even "looks hurt". By design, but with 40,000 to 60,000 HP bosses it makes the long fights shapeless.
- "Knight: TAUNT." appears with no cause line when an ally falls. The class blurb explains it; the log doesn't.
- Cast times vary from half a second to eight with no cue beyond the gap between "begins" and "uses". Knowing a Crush windup is eight seconds would change how the Crush lines read.

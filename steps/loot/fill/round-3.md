# The fill, round 3: the lead's rulings on the fresh read

Ruled on `fill/fresh-read-items.md` (one reader, every entry, 50 findings) and on `fill/fresh-read-engines.md` (one reader, each engine's parts together). Applied by hand to the shares, to `fill/shared.md` and to the spine. A ruling is about loops, overlaps, broken rules, wrong wearers or unclear cards, never strength.

## For every share

- **A card gives a percent as the sheet shows it.** Crit chance is a percent on a card: "+50% Crit chance, up to 80%", "crit 80% of the time", "+20% Crit chance". A percent stat an effect gives is its percent (Bloodsworn mail's 20% Lifesteal). Thorns is an amount of damage, which the interface scales, so in the fill its card keeps the scale's word and the build prints the amount (Urchin plate, Spiked targe, Spinebark pole).
- **A share of HP is a share of max HP,** and the card says "max HP" (thirteen cards changed).
- **A hit that lands a second time is never called a strike.** Cards say "lands twice" or "a second landing" (Still hand, Tempest cloak, Dragon's tooth, as Hunting spear and Twinfall already do). "Extra strike" is Double strike's, and "strikes with its weapon" is a swing-sized hit out of turn.
- **A kill is "lands a killing blow",** and a swing it calls "comes at once" (Hunter's buckler, Headsman's axe).
- **"Damage that would knock it out" is any damage,** a status's included (Last bastion, Swan's grace). "A blow" is a hit.
- **"Every so often" is 22 ticks,** the short cooldown word. A card on any other beat prints its ticks (Everflame lantern's 3, Kindled robes' and The forge's 4, Burning heart's 10). Where a first beat comes early it's in the numbers, and on the card only where the player has to know (Clear-headed charm's "the first at once").
- **A piece's stack word is the piece's count,** through `own()`, whatever hit carries it. Curse-book's "a few" was twice Jolt spear's on the same kind of hit, so its card says "several".
- **A Mark doesn't take what a piece or a seat sends.** Round 2's ruling for pieces holds for a formation's rule too (The forge), and Wick's numbers now follow it.
- **A fallen foe's fire moves once, in one wording** on its four cards (Wick, Smouldering orb, Ember heart, Pyre line's Torch): "if something else in the party moves it too, it's split evenly between them."
- **A status that's moved or copied keeps its stacks and the time it has left.** That isn't applying it, so "nothing applies a status for less than its own length" doesn't reach it (Idol of the hungry dark, Scapegoat charm, the copies). A line for the vocabulary.
- **A piece on one step may take the stat its effect reads, or its road's lean,** in place of its kind's signature (Ragged axe's Attack, Reaver's axe's Crit damage). The kind's commons carry the signature plain.
- **A card doesn't carry a ruling's sentence.** Where a card said what another card couldn't do to it ("whatever else calls for it", "it carries no riders"), the card now says what happens and the numbers keep the rest (Overture coat, Second dawn, Downhill charm, The standard, Toll gate). "A rider" is the design's word and is off every card.
- **A formation's first sentence is its promise,** and stays: `builds.md` asks a formation for "a promise in plain words". The rule that a card doesn't say what a thing is for is for pieces.
- **"Its ability with the longest cooldown" says which it means on the card** where it's the slowest (Reveille, Frozen tome, as Floe charm does).

- **Oathkeeper** is blank for a wearer that doesn't Taunt, as its notes say. It stays, and goes on the director's list of pieces that do little for one wearer.

## The engines

The engine reader worked each of the twenty-eight from the fill's numbers: 25 meet, 3 don't. Nine of the 25 meet in one kind of fight or with one more part, and its file says which.

- **The riposte doesn't meet from its keystone alone.** The class that dodges (the Monk) builds no cover by hitting, and the class that builds cover by hitting (the Valkyrie) has no Evasion: in Duelist's leathers she dodges one blow in five. Hooded cloak, a Shield on every dodge, is what banks the cover. The spine's link says so now. `builds.md`'s "enough to see it: the dodge's piece on a Valkyrie" isn't true as built, and goes to the director.
- **The blood mage doesn't meet at a common's Lifesteal,** which the spine's link says is enough. At "the same share" a wide spell loses life on four foes at a tenth of Lifesteal, and gains on three at a fifth. The price's size is the director's (round 1 sent it), so nothing is changed: it's the report's first decision, with half the share as the other version.
- **The crescendo's price is small at an ordinary pace:** about one cast's worth in a fight, where the link says it empties a pool. Left as written for the director, with two fifths more a stack as the other version.
- **The shatter bell rings the wrong ability for two of the four classes `builds.md` names.** "Slowest" is Condemn for a Paladin, by two ticks, and the Aegis for a Hoplite; for nine more classes it's a buff, a heal or a revive. `builds.md` says "slowest" and names Smite and the shove, so the document disagrees with itself. Left as written for the director, with "its slowest ability that deals damage" as the other version.
- **The mirror:** what's sent back is seen, but a Guarded wall still takes most of a boss's great blow. The wall's own number is small only under an Aegis or a Shield. Nothing changes; the report says so.
- **Stormbrand's card gives its size,** as Thunderhead maul's and Lightning-glass's do.
- **Every start-of-fight effect's card opens "when each fight starts"** (Opening shot), so a player can tell what Overture coat and Second dawn make happen again.
- **Everflame lantern with Bellows wraps is over its line once the Monk is faster** (The forge's Heat, Tireless bell). It's left: it takes five parts and a lone boss, it grows by 1% to 3% a pulse, and a fire that size ends the fight. Both shares' *Loops* and the spine's list say so, and the lantern is in the director's sample.
- **Three listed loops are corrected:** Hunter's mark with Writ of judgement is closed by Writ's card; Floe charm with Meltwater orb on a Frostweaver rings Stillness, not Blizzard; The covenant with Bloodstone reliquary joins the casts whose price is gone.
- **The covenant's sum in the formations share was taken before Defense,** and is corrected: a Fireball is even on four foes and a Chain lightning never quite pays.

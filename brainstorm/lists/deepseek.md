0. Troubles enemies can give a party

1. Heavy Armor that shrugs off many small hits.
2. Evasion that dodges physical burst.
3. Elemental resistance or weakness that punishes the wrong damage type.
4. High single-target burst that deletes one unit early.
5. Healing that outpaces the party's damage.
6. Stacking buffs before a big hit.
7. Silence or stun that cancels wind-ups.
8. Damage-over-time that ignores Armor and resistance.
9. MP drain that starves abilities.
10. Targeting manipulation that bypasses the tank.
11. Barrier/shield that soaks the first burst.
12. On-hit or on-knockout reactions like thorns, enrage or reanimate.
13. Fight timers or enemy enrage that punish slow kills.
14. Cleanse or immunity that undoes the party's plan.
15. Companions/adds that add extra attackers.

1. Classes

1. Vanguard — armoured line-holder. Passive: gains Armor when hit. Uses Taunt and Barrier to absorb the first push. Needs: Taunt, Barrier, Thorns.
2. Duelist — evasive crit fencer. Passive: gains Evasion after dodging. Applies Bleed and Haste to win long exchanges. Needs: Evasion, Combo, Bleed, Haste.
3. Pyromancer — fire caster. Passive: magic crits apply Burn. Uses Burn and Vulnerable to punish resisted targets. Needs: Burn, Vulnerable, Focus.
4. Cleric — healer. Passive: excess healing becomes Barrier. Uses Regeneration and cleanses to keep the party standing. Needs: Regeneration, Barrier, Overheal.
5. Ranger — steady physical damage. Passive: first hit applies Mark. Uses Mark and Multistrike to focus a target down. Needs: Mark, Multistrike.
6. Paladin — defensive support. Passive: redirects part of damage taken by the lowest-HP ally to itself. Uses Barrier and Taunt. Needs: Barrier, Taunt, Overheal.
7. Warlock — drain caster. Passive: heals from status damage it deals. Uses Doom and Poison to grind foes down. Needs: Doom, Poison, Lifesteal.
8. Shaman — buffer. Passive: heals also give a short Haste. Uses Haste and Regeneration to speed up allies. Needs: Haste, Regeneration, Echo.
9. Monk — many-hit blunt fighter. Passive: builds Combo on consecutive basic attacks. Uses Stun to interrupt wind-ups. Needs: Combo, Stun, Multistrike.
10. Engineer — timer bomber. Passive: its timed statuses can be Detonated. Uses Burn and Vulnerable before popping them. Needs: Burn, Vulnerable, Detonate.

— staples end —

11. Blood Knight — self-harming bruiser. Passive: heals when Bleed deals damage. Uses Bleed and Enrage to trade HP for pressure. Needs: Bleed, Enrage, Lifesteal.
12. Chronomancer — time controller. Passive: extends buffs on itself. Uses Slow, Haste and Stun to warp the tick order. Needs: Slow, Haste, Stun.
13. Plaguebringer — stacking poisoner. Passive: Poison stacks spread on knockout. Uses Poison and Vulnerable to overwhelm cleanses. Needs: Poison, Vulnerable.
14. Sentinel — anti-evasion/anti-armor attacker. Passive: Penetration against Marked foes. Uses Sunder and Mark to open tanks. Needs: Sunder, Mark, Penetration.
15. Spellbreaker — anti-magic specialist. Passive: removes one buff when hit by magical damage. Uses Silence to stop casters. Needs: Silence.
16. Beastmaster — companion fighter. Passive: Companion attacks when the holder basic attacks. Uses Mark and Haste to focus fire. Needs: Companion, Mark.
17. Runekeeper — element shifter. Passive: basic attacks shift toward the foe's weakness. Uses Vulnerable and Echo to amplify elements. Needs: Vulnerable, Echo.
18. Bard — buff/debuff songs. Passive: randomly grants Haste or Regeneration each cycle. Uses Slow and Enrage to swing tempo. Needs: Haste, Slow, Enrage, Echo.
19. Necromancer — doom and returner. Passive: Reanimate once per fight. Uses Doom and Poison to finish weakened foes. Needs: Doom, Reanimate.
20. Alchemist — potion and vulnerability setter. Passive: cleanses itself at low HP. Uses Poison, Vulnerable and Barrier. Needs: Poison, Vulnerable, Barrier, Detonate.
21. Berserker — low-HP damage dealer. Passive: gains Enrage below half HP. Uses Bleed to keep pressure while risky. Needs: Enrage, Bleed.
22. Templar — anti-debuff tank. Passive: immune to Silence and Stun. Uses Barrier and Silence to protect the party. Needs: Barrier, Silence, Thorns.
23. Trapper — timer snare setter. Passive: Detonates Marked foes. Uses Mark, Stun and Burn to control the fight. Needs: Mark, Stun, Burn, Detonate.
24. Oracle — foresight support. Passive: Foresight warns before heavy hits. Uses Haste, Barrier and Mark to pre-empt trouble. Needs: Haste, Barrier, Mark, Foresight.
25. Mimic — status copier. Passive: Copy special stat lets it copy a status on hit. Uses Vulnerable to make copied damage stick. Needs: Vulnerable; Copy special stat.

2. Statuses a player would see

1. Taunt — buff: forces enemies to target the holder.
2. Barrier — buff: soaks a pool of damage before HP.
3. Regeneration — buff: heals over time.
4. Haste — buff: faster attacks and cooldowns.
5. Slow — debuff: slower attacks and cooldowns.

— staples end —

6. Burn — debuff: fire damage over time.
7. Poison — debuff: untyped damage over time; stacks.
8. Bleed — debuff: damage over time that worsens when hit.
9. Sunder — debuff: Armor down.
10. Mark — debuff: more likely targeted and takes more basic-attack damage.
11. Silence — debuff: cannot use abilities.
12. Stun — debuff: cannot act; cancels wind-up.
13. Enrage — buff: damage up, takes more damage.
14. Vulnerable — debuff: takes more damage from all sources.
15. Doom — debuff: after a timer, heavy damage or knockout.

3. Special stats

1. Evasion — chance to dodge physical hits.
2. Lifesteal — heals for a portion of damage dealt.
3. Thorns — deals damage back when hit.
4. Penetration — ignores part of Armor or resistance.
5. Execute — bonus damage to low-HP foes.
6. Multistrike — chance for basic attacks to hit again.

— staples end —

7. Echo — chance for an ability to trigger again at reduced effect.
8. Overheal — excess healing becomes Barrier.
9. Combo — basic attacks gain damage on consecutive hits.
10. Focus — magic damage rises while the unit avoids being hit.
11. Companion — adds a companion's extra attacks/actions.
12. Reanimate — once per fight, returns from knockout at low HP.
13. Detonate — timer/status effects can be popped for burst damage.
14. Copy — copies a status from a foe or ally.
15. Foresight — warns of the next heavy hit and gives a short defensive window.

4. Elements

1. Fire — aggressive, burning, burst.
2. Ice — slowing, brittle, control.
3. Lightning — fast, echoing, chain-like.
4. Earth — heavy, armoured, sundering.
5. Wind — evasive, hasty, light.
6. Light — healing, barriers, purification.
7. Dark — drain, doom, attrition.

# The fill: the lead's rulings

Oct 30, 2026. Seven agents wrote the fill (`fill/`), each from `fill-brief.md`, and each ended with its challenges, its engine needs and what it was least sure of. These are the lead's rulings on all of them, read together. Each agent applies its own share's rulings to its file; the global rulings apply to every share. Where a ruling and a spine line differ, the ruling wins, and the spine gets the change.

## Global rulings

- **G1. A status can be applied for less than its own length, never more.** The brief said never shorter, and that was the lead's mistake: the director's rule is about how a status scales, and Caravan applied a short Regen for months. "Short" is half the status's own length. Every "short Taunt", "short Haste" and "briefly" in the spine stands as written, at half length: the uncommons' Taunt and Haste at the start (10 ticks), Long march's Regen, The forge's Slow, Thorn hedge's Weaken, Stillness of mind's Taunt, Aftershock's Stun, Blossom. The Clarity and Regen that uncommons give at a fight's start are short too.
- **G2. Marked**, one definition: a debuff on a foe, 14 ticks, Refresh. The party's basic attacks pick a Marked foe; among several, by draw. Each unit keeps one Mark: a new Mark it applies moves its old one. Abilities follow their own targeting. Marked works inside the Taunt rule: while a foe Taunts, only basic attacks with Seek (Hunter's eye, a piece that gives it) follow the Mark past it.
- **G3. Heat:** +3% damage a stack, up to 15, kept all fight. Every share uses these.
- **G4. Fervor:** +5% damage a stack, up to 20, kept all fight, kept through a knockout.
- **G5. One swing shape per unit.** Phalanx, the Millstone, the Hummingbird charm, the Worldbreaker and the Hammer seats each reshape a unit's basic attacks. A unit takes one: its passive's first, then the first piece's in slot order (weapon, armor, offhand, amulet, then its seat). Each other shape on it gives Attack or Magic +1 instead (its weapon's stat). No more statues.
- **G6. Every uncommon carries an effect.** A special stat alone is a common's step. The armor share's and the offhand share's fixes stand.
- **G7. One uncommon effect per wearer group.** Where two kinds the same classes wear had the same uncommon effect from the spine's table:
  - Robes changes its Clarity-at-start and Shield-from-Magic uncommons; the staff keeps its own.
  - Mail and the lantern change their Ward-at-start uncommons; the rune sword's and mace's stay.
  - The totem's Guard below half becomes "the first ally to drop below half each fight is Guarded"; Plate's stays.
  - Haste at the start stays on the fist weapons and the amulet; Leather's and the bell's change.
  - The amulet's Taunt at the start becomes "Taunts the first time an ally drops below half, once a fight".
  - The sword's short Taunt at the start stays beside Mocking mail; only the Bramble knight can wear both.
- **G8. "Many" is past a rider's or a version's reach.** A single cast or hit lands at most "several"; "many" is for a passive build-up over a fight. Every "many" in a version or a piece's single application becomes "several".
- **G9. Double on a condition a party keeps up all fight is half again.** Double stays only where the condition comes a few times a fight.
- **G10. A revive comes back with every ability on full cooldown,** whoever revives (the rules' default), except where Einherjar or a piece says otherwise. Phoenix ember and the Vial of the last breath lose their Ambush tag.

## Engine additions, gathered

The spine listed 17. The fill adds these, grouped; the build brief takes them as one list.
- **Who and what an event names:** the unit that landed a killing blow; the applier of a status; the attacker and amount of a spent block; the MP cost just paid.
- **Conditions:** "has any of"; "hasn't acted this fight"; one unit's stat against another's; "the highest on its side"; a side's state as a reaction's or pulse's `if`; "an ally is knocked out"; "or" between an ability's conditions; counting a unit's buffs or debuffs, or the foes that meet a condition.
- **Targeting:** basic attacks aimed by a rule; picks by wind-up left and by basic-attack interval; a fallback that picks by something else; a reaction target that skips the event's other unit; seats as targets and as event sources.
- **Hits and strikes:** crits by condition; extra strikes sized and spread over several foes, still heard as basic attacks; a strike fired by a reaction, sized from the hit that fired it; an `area` tag; one swing shape per unit (G5).
- **Statuses:** a fallen unit's status acting on a timer; moving a status between units; a marker that's neither buff nor debuff; an HP floor for a blow or a while; a stat set to a value after other changes; a stat read live from another; heals from others blocked; MP past full, as HP.
- **Actions:** recasting the ability just cast; a cooldown on a reaction; one effect picked at random from a list; a mythic that changes its class's abilities by name.
- **The run layer:** passives that can be trained; an upgrade as two trainings.

That's about thirty-five additions on top of the spine's seventeen. Most are small extensions of what the engine already does; the build brief should cost them before building and cut any that serve a single piece, offering the piece a simpler shape instead.

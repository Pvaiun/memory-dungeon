## 2. Statuses a player would see

* **Poison** | Debuff | Deals un-resisted damage on a timer every few seconds.
* **Regen** | Buff | Restores a pool of HP on a timer.
* **Stun** | Debuff | Stops a unit from using all actions and cancels current wind-up.
* **Protect** | Buff | Raises Armor by a flat amount against physical hits.
* **Haste** | Buff | Increases Attack Speed and reduces ability cooldowns.

---

* **Vulnerability** | Debuff | Increases all damage taken by a percentage.
* **Shield** | Buff | Soaks a pool of damage before it reaches HP.
* **Silence** | Debuff | Stops a unit from using abilities only.
* **Momentum** | Buff | Adds stacks on landing a hit, boosting physical damage per stack, dropping on miss.
* **Soul Link** | Debuff | Reacts to taking damage by splitting a portion of it to another linked foe.
* **Chill** | Debuff | Lowers Attack Speed and extends action wind-up times.
* **Bloodthirst** | Buff | Reacts to landing a hit by restoring HP scaled from Attack.
* **Fragile** | Debuff | Lowers Armor, making heavy hits strip chunks of health.
* **Doom** | Debuff | Counts down on a timer and knocks out the unit when it expires.
* **Root** | Debuff | Stops a unit from dodging, setting Evasion to zero.

## 3. Special stats

* **Rage** | Builds when taking damage, increasing physical damage output and fueling berserk abilities.
* **Mana Flow** | Restores MP faster when actions are used consecutively without pausing.
* **Purity** | Decreases magic damage taken while above zero, dropping when hit by elements.
* **Weight** | Adds flat damage to blunt attacks based on how heavy the unit's gear is.
* **Combo Count** | Tracks consecutive successful basic attacks to trigger extra hits or effects.

---

* **Blood Debt** | Stores damage dealt to self to release as a burst of magical damage on an ability.
* **Shadow Depth** | Increases dark element damage and applies stacks of decay when attacking.
* **Precision Focus** | Increases critical strike chance the longer a unit goes without missing.
* **Armor Shred** | Permanently reduces target's Armor on each hit until the fight ends.
* **Soul Spark** | Generates charges on enemy knockouts to unleash instant magical strikes.
* **Momentum Velocity** | Increases basic attack frequency the more consecutive hits land.
* **Heat Buildup** | Increases fire damage over time while active, venting a burst when full.
* **Unyielding Resolve** | Prevents knockdown or lethal damage once per fight while above zero.
* **Curse Resonance** | Amplifies status duration on foes based on how many statuses they already hold.
* **Vitality Shield** | Converts excess healing received past max HP into a permanent damage-soaking pool.

## 4. Elements

* **Fire** | Scorching heat that lingers and breaks heavy armor.
* **Ice** | Freezing cold that slows down actions and wind-ups.
* **Lightning** | Sharp energy that strikes fast and bounces.
* **Holy** | Radiant light that purges corruption and heals allies.
* **Shadow** | Corrosive dark that drains life and feeds on decay.

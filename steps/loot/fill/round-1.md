# The fill, round 1: the lead's rulings

Work in progress: written as each share comes in, and sent to the agents once the whole fill has been read as one design. A ruling is about loops, overlaps, broken rules, wrong wearers or unclear cards, never strength.

## For every share

To go into `fill/shared.md` once round 1 is ruled.

- **"Every few ticks" is four ticks** (Regen's and Clarity's own beat), on every card that says it, unless the card states a beat of its own.
- **"The most that Shield held"** is the pool's highest point since the unit last had no Shield. Rubble plate, Second skin, Rampart and Shatterstone charm read it. A Shield rebuilt at half sends back half.
- **A Crescendo lands when a cast finishes,** so the first cast of an ability is plain.
- **"Gives itself" is a status laid at a moment:** Hidden, Taunt, Haste, Might, Guard, a Shield, Regen, Clarity, Aegis, and a counter a card gives. A state held only while something is true of the unit (Bloodlust below half, a draw while healthy) isn't a gift. Twin-knot and Twins share the first kind only.
- **Left-over damage is a hit on the foe it reaches.** It meets that foe's Evasion and Defense, and it counts as a hit taken (a wound pays, Thorns answer). It isn't boosted or crit again, and it carries no riders. Where one unit holds two pieces that carry the same left-over, it's split evenly between them, as a fallen foe's fire is.
- **A hit "of its basic attack's size"** (a strike with its weapon, an extra strike) takes whatever shape the unit's basic attacks have: Millstone's, Hummingbird charm's, a Hammer seat's.
- **"Drops below half" (or a third) is a crossing,** whatever took the unit there: a blow, a status, a drain or a price. A watcher of HP isn't a reaction to the paying. A unit that starts a fight under the line has dropped, and the piece fires as the fight starts. "Whenever" fires again each time the unit is back at the line or over it and crosses again; "the first time each fight" fires once. (Watch horn, Bear fetish, Holdfast, Warded mail, Feathered cloak, Mottled hide's and Portcullis's states aside, Shroud charm, Aegis mail.)
- **"Its ability with the longest cooldown"** is its slowest ability, by its whole cooldown, cooling or not. "The longest cooldown left" is the one with the most wait left. A card says which it means. The shatter bell's places (Icebreaker charm, Icefall's Breaker, Frozen tome) and Reveille mean the slowest.
- **"Nearest to ready"** is the least wait left among abilities still cooling (First light, Scrying orb, Dancer's cloak, Rubato).
- **A formation's rule for the whole party isn't an ally's gear or seat.** Tally coat and Second dawn don't make it happen again.
- **Carrying another unit's riders** (The standard, Mimic wand, Toll gate). The carrier takes each rider whole, reads its own stat for the stacks, and meets any condition itself (its own crits, first hits, counts). Toll gate's Collectors pass only what every swing adds. A rider the carrier's own swing already holds from the same source (a storm on the whole party, Crown of embers) isn't added twice.
- **Stacks from a seat are flat** (`FLAT_STACKS`), so a seat pays the same whoever sits in it.
- **"On the field" means on the foes.** Stormbrand and the Bolt seat count the Shock the foes hold; cards say "for each Shock the foes hold".
- **A strike a card aims at a named foe isn't a pick,** so a Taunt doesn't turn it: a strike at the attacker, at the foe that acted, at "that foe" (Duelist's leathers, Parrying spear, Answering thunder, Hushing idol). A rule that changes whom a unit's own swings or abilities pick is a pick, inside the Taunt rule unless it has Seek (Marginalia, Hoarfrost line's Breaker, a Mark).
- **"Nearly always" is four times in five,** a set chance on those hits that nothing raises (Ambush knife, Vulture's skull). A hit that always crits still does. A chance a card sets wins over Gambler's coin, as "always" does. The emptied quiver's link asks for a burst of three or four arrows, and at nine in ten it runs ten.
- **"Far more often" gives its number on the card.** Bloodscent knife and Spur's Opener are different sizes, so each says its own.
- **No card makes a crit certain for longer than one swing.** A card that raises Crit chance by a lot says "to four in five at most" (Bloodscent knife, The king's head). The emptied quiver's only brake is the crit that fails, and at a certain crit Quick draw never ends. Whether plain Crit chance should have a ceiling of its own is flagged for the director.
- **"Its hits", on a weapon,** means every hit its wearer lands: a swing, an extra strike or an ability's hit. The card says "its wearer's hits".
- **"Extra strike" is Double strike's second strike,** and what Flurry wraps chains from it. Arc's second hit, a leap, a ring and a volley are extra hits with their own names; each carries what its card says, and nothing that reads extra strikes reads them.
- **A hit that lands a second time** is the same damage again on the same foe, at once, with no new dodge or crit roll. It's a hit: a wound pays, Lifesteal drinks, counters count. It carries none of the first hit's statuses, and nothing repeats it. Where two cards would each repeat one hit or one cast, each repeats the first once: three in all.
- **A heal is HP an ability or a piece restores at once.** A Regen's pulses are the status's own: no card that reads its wearer's heals reads them, unless it names Regen.
- **Borrowed riders aren't passed on.** What a unit's swing carries by copying another's is part of its own swing and its own extra hits, and no third unit copies it from there.
- **The signature stat comes first unless it works against what the piece does.** Then the road's lean in the kind's header wins (Aggro on a chaser's spear).

- **"Pays for" an ability** means its cost was paid, in MP or in HP. A free cast isn't paid for.
- **"Carrying what the swing carries"** also carries what a status its wearer holds puts on its swings (a Thunderstorm). The spine's phrase gains it.
- **Someone stays in the light.** Hidden doesn't land on a unit when it would leave the foes no ally to pick; where several would hide at once, the last in seat order stays. It's a rule of Hidden's own, for the vocabulary, flagged for the director: gear and seats can now hide a whole party, and the game has no answer for that.
- **A start-of-fight status on every foe** reads its holder's Attack, at the card's word (Stormglass totem, Pitch lantern, Snowbound folio, the phial). As written in both offhands shares.
- **New target phrases,** for the vocabulary when it's built: the other ally with the least Defense; the ally with the highest Aggro; the ally with the slowest basic attack; the two foes with the highest Attack; the foe that last hit the healed ally; the ally with the lowest share of max HP, the payer aside; the foe with the highest current HP; the ally with the lowest share of max HP that can be healed; the ally with the least MP (the lowest share of max MP, the holder included); the other ally with the least max HP; the other foe with the most Chill. "The ally with the highest Aggro" reads Aggro as it stands, gear and seat counted, a Taunt not; a tie goes to the earlier seat.
## Amulets

- **Burning heart:** the agent's size stands (a few Burn each ten ticks, the slowest beat that never lets it go out). The spine's line changes from "every few ticks" to "every so often… so it never goes out".
- **Heart of winter:** the agent's reading stands. It copies Chill that lands on one foe; what already lands on every foe isn't copied. Its copies of cold a shatter passed on are damped as that cold is (code support 23).
- **Halved heart:** "at half it counts as below half its HP" stays on the card, and goes into the spine's line.
- **Twin-knot:** its boundary is the ruling above (a counter a card gives is shared; a state held while something is true isn't).
- **Second dawn:** by the letter. A formation's rule for the whole party isn't an ally's gear or seat, so it doesn't happen again.
- **Shatterstone charm:** a Shield worn through by a status sends nothing back. Stands.
- **Hunter's mark:** stands as written. The Mark sits where it sits, and a party with no reach can't follow it past a Taunt.
- **Names:** Icebreaker charm, Lodestone and Whetstone change (each clashes with another item). The rest keep the spine's name.

## Offhands, the first six kinds

- **Hoplon** counts what a Shield soaked and what an Aegis blocked of the blow: two numbers the game prints. What a Guard stopped is never shown, so Guard drops out of the card. The spine's line changes.
- **Shuttered lantern:** pending a look at the Cleric's wind-ups. If no Cleric ability winds up long enough to be covered, it moves to the orb.
- **Heart of the saint:** the agent's reading stands. Its holder's heals are abilities that heal directly; a Regen keeps its cost and its size.
- **Spiked targe:** Thorns at high stands. "High only on a legendary" is a rule for stat steps. Urchin plate (armor) should give the same level.
- **Sheltering kite:** "the other ally with the least Defense", as written. The spine's line gains the word.
- **Watch horn:** as the line says: an ally includes its holder.
- **War horn, Earthmother totem:** a fixed count of Might stands; a flat count says what it scales from by saying nothing does.
- **Everflame lantern:** its beat, against "every few ticks" at four: read its notes before ruling.
- **Names:** Spiked kite becomes Bossed kite (it gives no Thorns). Phalanx shield becomes Tower shield (Phalanx is the Hoplite's passive).
- **For the director:** `classes.md` gives the Pyromancer's ritualist a staff and a lantern, which can't be held together. Reveille reads as the Bard's Reprise does, at a different moment.

## Formations

- **Hoarfrost line's Breaker gains an aim:** while a foe is Stunned, its basic attacks go to that foe, inside the Taunt rule; its hits on a Stunned foe deal double. Without it the line's promise holds against a lone foe only. The spine's line changes. The held-foe pieces (Icebreaker, Still hand, Vulture's skull) stay as they are: a piece pays when it happens, and the seat is the formation's identity.
- **The standard, Toll gate:** the agent's reading of "carry" stands, with the one addition above (no rider twice from one source).
- **Spur's Opener, "crits far more often":** one number with Bloodscent knife (weapons-1), or two different phrases. Pending weapons-1.
- **Shared cup:** healing sent on goes to the ally with the lowest share of max HP that can be healed, so it isn't lost on an ally capped at half. The spine's line changes.
- **The covenant:** the floor at a tenth goes on the card and into the spine's line, as Sanguine vestments has it. Lifesteal high stands: the link is sized to it. "The same share" stays the line's rule, and its sums go to the director's sample.
- **Twins:** counters a card gives pass between Twins, as written.
- **Names:** The long wall becomes The palisade (one word from The last wall). Open wound becomes Bloodied ground (one word from the version Opening wound). The long winter keeps its name; the orb changes (offhands-2). Spur keeps its name.

## Offhands, the other seven kinds

- **Tireless bell** has no wearer who winds up long enough to use it. It stays a bell, and its rule widens: its holder's basic attacks never wait on its abilities, through their wind-ups and their recoveries. The spine's line changes.
- **Shuttered lantern** stays a lantern: a Cleric's Renew and Raise wind up, and a piece may be worth little to one wearer.
- **Bell of the hour:** every fifth basic attack stands. The count is the fill's to size, and the link asks for several times a fight. The spine's line and link change.
- **Marginalia and Curse-book** do nothing on a Bard. They stay; it goes to *Least sure*.
- **Hushing idol** goes at that foe whatever Taunts; **Marginalia** is inside the Taunt rule (the ruling above).
- **Bloodlust draught:** the card follows the definition: the HP it starts without isn't a price paid. The spine's line loses "(it pays)".
- **Annotated grimoire:** "pays for", in MP or in HP (the ruling above), so it works under an HP price.
- **Annotated score** fires when the other ally *pays for* an ability, not when it casts one. Two scores facing each other otherwise cast free without end. The spine's line changes.
- **The king's head:** every ally's crit chance is doubled for a while, to four in five at most. Without the ceiling a doubled coin makes Quick draw's crit certain, and its burst has no end. The spine's line changes.
- **Venom vial** at a half stands.
- **The deep well** loses its mana as armour tag: it can't be held with either keystone and gives no mana.
- **Heartsblood** counts what its holder's Lifesteal would heal, full or not. Stands.
- **Names:** Bloodletter's phial becomes Lancet phial; Idol of the deepening becomes Festering idol; Annotated score becomes Accompanist's score; Orb of the long winter becomes Meltwater orb.
- **Alike, for the director's list:** Ringing bell and Storm pike on a Monk; Snowbound folio and Grimoire of plagues in one kind.

## Weapons, the first six kinds

- **Storm pike** counts every hit its wearer lands, abilities included, and flashes on every fifth. At swings alone the count doesn't come round in a regular fight. The spine's line changes. It becomes Squall lance: a pike is a great weapon.
- **Reaver's axe changes.** Its sentence is the second half of Night's edge, and one Assassin can wear both. It becomes: when it lands a killing blow, its wearer gains many Might. The spine's line changes.
- **Rimed blade** adds several Chill, not a few, so it's seen on a Knight. The spine's line changes.
- **Sarissa's** extra hits carry nothing, as written. **Answering thunder** "strikes with its weapon", as written.
- **Mimic wand:** the agent's reading stands (the ruling above on borrowed riders).
- **Stormbrand** counts the Shock the foes hold. **Overkill axe** beside Downhill charm: the left-over is split evenly.
- **The spear's Hunt and Storm pieces** (Long spear, Hunting spear, Jolt spear, Squall lance) drop Aggro for their road's stats.
- **Quietus** gains the opening again's tag: it's a start-of-fight effect by the spine's definition, and Tally coat makes it strike every foe again at each kill.
- **Gravemark** does nothing in a party with no marker. It stays; it goes to *Least sure*.
- **Names:** Trophy spear becomes Victor's spear (a trophy is an offhand kind). Bloodletter keeps its name; the phial changes.

## Weapons, the other six kinds

- **Volley bow and Bellringer count to three,** not four: on a slow weapon the fourth swing misses half the regular fights. The spine's lines change. **Mender's mace** keeps "every so often".
- **Opening shot** gains the opening again's tag, as Quietus does.
- **Overrun** beside Downhill charm: the left-over is split evenly (the ruling for every share), not all to the weapon.
- **Saint's mace, Shepherd's mace:** a Regen isn't a heal (the ruling above), so they give a Bramble knight only their stats. They stay; it goes to *Least sure*.
- **Frostward blade's "a few"** stands: the fourth or fifth blow is within the link's "a few".
- **The riders' sizes** against the kits' own casts are the balance pass's.
- **Names:** Shepherd's mace becomes Anchorite's mace; Sunhammer becomes Noonday mace.

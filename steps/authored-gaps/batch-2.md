## 2. Cold Snap: cold without a wall (Road)

For casters and quick hitters there are Icefall's two frost seats, and otherwise plain Chill on a swing or at fight start (Fines). - Great: Basic attacks add Chill, from max HP.
- Fine: When hit: the attacker gains Chill.
- Fine: When it adds Chill to one foe, every other foe gains as much.
- Fine: Basic attacks and extra strikes add Chill.
- Great formation: Icefall: one seat's abilities add +50% Chill, one's basic attacks add a Chill, and any shatter readies the Breaker's first-slotted ability.

# Step 6 tuning trail

One line a round: what was wrong, what changed, what the simulation said. For later sessions; the director reads the report. Runs are first clears of a whole node (every fight of its run, HP and knockouts carried), with the simulation's best builds unless a line says otherwise. "win½" is the share of a node's sensible builds that win at least half their runs.

## Build

- The campaign as data: schema `campaign` block (nodes, needs, rewards) and a dungeon `foes` change; engine 0.7 applies `foes` at each fight start and to summons, through a `power` stat (+% damage on hits and reactions). `ruleset.lostCast: "cooldown"` for the core set (the worked example keeps "retry"). The validator checks the campaign: references, nothing paid twice, every need meetable with what's owned by then.
- `src/campaign.ts`: owned-at-node, rewards (a class brings its plain weapon), needs in words and checked against a party. Shared by the simulation and the interface.
- `src/sim/kits.ts` and `src/campaign-sim.ts` (`npm run campaign`): per node, every allowed party × owned formation × three kit styles (sampled to 360), screened on 6 seeds, the best 20 on 18 more, a 48-step climb, the best on 48 fresh seeds. About 2 minutes for all 20 nodes, 43,000 runs, on 4 cores.

## Rounds

1. **Baseline, first numbers.** 15 of 20 nodes won 100% by the best build, and half of all builds won everywhere from node 3 on: the director's "impossible to lose". Fire Mine 23% best (the Giant, as in the core-set report). The best kit everywhere: armor plus Heartstone, which doubled a caster's HP.
2. **Systemic numbers.** HP steps on gear and formations to half a point; Burn drops a tenth a pulse (not a quarter) at 20 a stack (not 50); Poison 160 for 12 ticks (not 90 for 10); Inferno 0.35 × Magic (not 0.5). Little change at the top (still 100% best on most nodes), more builds losing at the margin. As expected: the content was too weak for a geared party, which is what the per-node knob is for.
3. **Tuner, target "best build wins 85%".** Knob k: foes +k% damage, +k/2 % HP. Found k from −21 (Fire Mine) to +163 (Deep Crypt). Every node's best build at 81% to 96%, but win½ fell to 0% to 17% and the median build to 0% on every node from 3 on: one right answer per node, found by a climb no player runs. On a linear road that's a wall. Kept as a comparison; target changed.
4. **Tuner, target "a fifth of builds win half their runs, and the best wins 85%".** Knob split evenly (+k% HP and damage) so a hard node is longer and deadlier together. Every node at win½ 20% to 28%, best 75% to 100%. Knobs: Old Road −19, Road at Night −7, Wildfire +40, Crypt +103, Ossuary +55, Ogre Brothers +33, Fire Mine −41, Deep Freeze −24, Iron Wall 0, Ice Pass +80, Valhalla +85, Wild Hunt +40, Storm Tower +30, Knives Out +25, Long Road +78, Deep Crypt +83, Blood Moon +98, Judgement −15, Tower Top +33, Royal Court −21. 227,000 runs.
   - The Fire Giant's three nodes (Fire Mine, Deep Freeze, Judgement) need weaker foes than written; the whole run gets weaker to make its boss fair, so the fights before it go soft.
   - **Carry:** the previous node's best party, re-kitted, still wins most nodes (56% to 100% on 12 of 19). The needs make the player change one member; the other two tend to stay.
5. **Who wins: class and formation effects** (mean screening wins of builds with a class minus without, per node). Valkyrie +25 to +71 on every node; Cleric and Knight positive almost everywhere; Pyromancer, Ranger, Hexer, Bard and Monk negative almost everywhere (−10 to −30); the Bard in none of the 171 top-10 builds. Escort the best formation on most nodes (71 of 171 top builds), Scatter and Neutral negative.
6. **Class pass 1.** Gale spear 0.14 × Attack a hit (was 0.2), Rally 0.4 × Magic (was 0.6); War ballad many Might (was several); Burning fist 0.25 × Attack a hit (was 0.2); Burn 30 a stack (was 20: slower Burn was the same total but arrived after short fights ended, so the Pyromancer and Ranger lost by it); Escort slots lose their HP +1; Scatter's Decoy Armor −1 (was −2).
7. **The knob kills casters.** A class bench (Knight, Cleric and each class, full kits, reference fights at +100% foes) put the Pyromancer at 3 wins in 30 at the Ghoul Pit, its own showcase: two doubled Ghoul swings kill a 2,000-HP caster at tick 10. Knob changed: foes +k% HP but +k/2 % damage, so a hard node is mostly a longer one. Route fix: Veil (Aggro −2) came at node 18, so the Aggro-3 Pyromancer had nothing to hide behind for 16 nodes; now it comes with the Pyromancer, and Shadowsilk at node 5. Gale spear to 0.12 × Attack, Rally to 0.3 × Magic. On the bench the Pyromancer and Frostweaver then won their share; the Valkyrie still swept it.
8. **Class pass 2.** Fireball normal (0.35 × Magic, was light), Blizzard 0.3 (was 0.2), Gale spear 0.1. Pass 5 tuned the road: every node 20% to 46% viable (the ramp: 40% early, 20% late), best 85% to 100%. Normalised by how long each is owned, the Valkyrie is in about half of the top builds and the Knight and Cleric in about 44%; the Pyromancer 9%, Hexer 10%, Frostweaver 13%. Formations spread out (Escort 45 of 200 top builds, Vanguard 39, Caravan 37, Neutral 23, Scatter 21). 283 tests pass; four new ones for the engine changes and the helpers.
9. **Vertical progression measured** (`--power`): the starting trio, kitted from what's owned, clears an Old Road with foes at +14% (node 1), +75% (node 2), +140% (node 3), +218% (node 6), and then +218% to node 19: slot-filling is the whole climb, and it ends at node 6. Later gear is sidegrades, as the gear document intends.
10. **AI playtest** (an agent playing through `src/campaign-play.ts`, seeing only what a player sees): all 20 nodes cleared in 28 attempts, 17 first try; nothing after node 11 beat it. It used Knight, Valkyrie and one damage dealer in Vanguard from node 10 on, changing only what needs forced; the Monk was its best answer everywhere; it never wanted the Ranger, nor the Cleric once it had the Valkyrie; Blitz, Ambush, Tortoise, Last stand and Champion were never used. Nodes 6 and 10 were "hard and good". The share target counts builds a thinking player never picks, so a fifth of builds viable is far too easy for one.
11. **Fresh-reader feel check** (six packs, nodes 1, 6, 12, 19): Burn builds to 9 to 13 stacks and Poison matters, but Shock runs away on slow bosses (50 to 100 stacks, up to 8,000 from one action); Purify failed 16 of 16 times at node 6, starting on a 2-tick Stun that ended before it landed, and under lostCast a lost target cost a whole cooldown; boss fights at node 1 and 6 hinge on when the Knight's bash comes up; MP never shows in the logs (the interface shows it).
12. **The tightest direction, priced:** a variant where a won fight restores 30% of HP and MP (`ruleset.run.restore`, off in the core set). Tuned the same way: the Cleric falls from 86 to 54 appearances in the top-10 builds, the Hoplite rises from 40 to 52 and the Ranger from 34 to 38; the casters barely move. "Nothing restores between fights" is what makes the Cleric mandatory; it isn't what keeps the casters out.
13. **Round two from the playtest.** Target harder: the top 30% of builds win half on nodes 1 to 3, 12% on 4 to 9, 8% on 10 to 17, 6% on the last three. Shock capped at 25 stacks. A cast whose target is gone costs its recovery, not its cooldown (an interrupted one still loses its cooldown). Mercy heals three quarters of a Light hit (was half); Fire arrow a heavy hit (was normal); Burning fist back to 0.2 (the Monk was the playtester's answer to everything). Formations spread: Blitz at node 8, Ambush 11, Tortoise 13, Last stand 16, Champion 17 (they came at 17 to 19). The interface no longer says a summon is cast "on" a hero.
14. **Pass 6 at the round-two target:** after node 3, 7% to 25% of builds viable, best 75% to 100%. The knobs rose (the Crypt to +224, the Long Road to +157). Fixed a simulator bug on the way: a node's parties were sampled before its needs were applied, so Iron Wall (one legal party of 84) saw 2 builds; it now sees all 8.
15. **Second AI playtest** (fresh agent, the command line now showing foes' statuses, hit words and a status glossary): 20 of 20 cleared in 42 attempts (the first took 28), 10 first-try (was 17), never stuck. Hard: Valhalla 7 (three Ice Ghosts drain the tank before the Titan; answered by using the Valkyrie as a second healer), the Ogre Brothers 5 (the Knight runs out of MP; Prayer beads), the Long Road 5 (Heartstone on everyone, no Veils), Knives Out 3. First-try: nodes 1 to 3, 5, 8, 9, 12, 17, 19, 20. The Knight was in 18 of 20 winning parties (Shield bash answers every big wind-up), the Stormcaller in 8 of the last 9 (it carried the end), the Cleric in 13. Late rewards went unused, powerful pieces never worn. MP was invisible in the summary.
16. **Round three.** Shock 60 a stack (was 80). The last two nodes tighter (one in 25 builds viable on nodes 18 and 19, one in 33 on the finale, so it's the hardest run) and a larger sample late (600 builds). The command line shows MP left after each fight, and a split slime no longer reads as standing after a win.
17. **Pass 7** (nodes 10 to 20 at the round-three targets, 600 builds): best 90% to 100%, 4% to 10% viable.
18. **Third AI playtest** (fresh agent, MP now shown): 47 attempts, 19 of 20 cleared, **stuck on node 5, the Ossuary**, after 8. Its Haunted Hall was three Ghosts: every element resisted, so the Monk the node asks for spent its turns on a Fire Burning fist the Ghosts resisted; and three identical foes start every action together, so all three hit one unit on one beat (3,700 every 10 ticks), which only a Knight survives. The simulation had seen it: the node's best build won 75% on fresh seeds, under the 85% target, and nothing checked. Elsewhere: the Ice Pass 6 and the Storm Tower 5 were "hard but fair" (the Storm Tower's answer, Blitz with no tank against the Wisps, was its favourite moment); the finale was easier than node 19 again; default ability orders hide the Knight's Shield bash and the Valkyrie's Rally at the back; nothing says Wisps and Ghosts can't be blocked or dodged.
19. **Round four.** The Haunted Hall is two Ghosts and two Skeletons. Copies of the same enemy start a fight 1.5 ticks apart (`ruleset.stagger`): packs ripple, twin bosses no longer wind up in lockstep. The tuner now checks the best build on fresh seeds and eases the knob until it holds the target, so no node ships with its best build under 81%. Full re-tune (pass 8).
20. **Pass 8** (all nodes, round four): every node's best build 81% to 100% on fresh seeds.
21. **Fourth AI playtest:** 51 attempts, 19 of 20 cleared, **stuck on node 19, the Tower Top** (the King left at 16% on its best try). The Tower Top is the enemies document's lock (the Seek bench gets past the Soldiers' Taunt), tuned to one build in 25: a player who doesn't find that family is walled at the end of the road. The Ossuary fell first try this time. The Storm Tower took 8 (a cliff after twelve easy nodes; the Assassin, its key, arrives as its reward) and Judgement 7. New problems: a Vampire and two Bone Priests healing each other ran to the 5,000-tick safety stop and Ghouls raising each other ran 2,600 ticks; a hidden Bard stacked 123 Might on itself and the Storm King 38.
22. **Round five.** Nodes 10 to 19 at one build in eleven viable, the finale at one in twenty (no node narrower). The Tower Top's text says "Strike past them". An enrage (`ruleset.enrage`): past 300 ticks every unit, on both sides, deals 25% more damage, and 25% more again every 30 ticks, so no fight stalls; the fight says so once. Might capped at 25 stacks. Full re-tune (pass 9).

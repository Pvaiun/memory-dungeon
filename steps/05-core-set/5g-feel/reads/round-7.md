# Feel read, round 7

A cold read of `out/feel-r7/`: the glossary, twelve packs tick by tick, then the summary over 389 fights. No source, no other documents, no earlier reads.

## 1 to 4. Per pack

**Pack 1, Toll Gate (act 1 fight), won t57.** A build: basics until War ballad t5.7, Frost lance t7.5, Cure t9.3, then a rotation every 12 to 14 ticks. The Cleric's damage was 100% basic attacks (952 to 1,511 as Might rose 4.2 to 9.8, the one visible build). Chill peaked 6.3 and never shattered; bandits bit for 239 to 295 against 2,000-plus HP.

**Pack 2, Wolf Pack (act 1 fight, floor 8), lost t25.** Decided before t0: the Valkyrie arrived at 417/4,000 HP and died at t5.6 to 95-damage bites, against seven wolves at 6,360 HP. Burn 3.1 to 13.5 killed Wolf A alone (3,629, t15); Bleed 15.6 on two wolves nobody hit again did 0; the Monk cast Flow three times for two basics.

**Pack 3, Ogre Bridge (act 1 elite), won t76.** Abilities at t1 to t3, then safety: the Bramble knight (Thicket ×6) took every hit behind Earthen shell's 3,000 shield, and Flatten (t20.4, t64.6) was soaked whole. Bleed from Thorns was 32% of the party's damage (1,512 on a bandit, t31.4); the Vampire paid 800 HP to heal 800, twice; the knight never dropped below 4,436/6,000.

**Pack 4, Ogre Chief (act 1 boss), lost t87 with the boss at 3%.** The hasted Hexer cast nine abilities in 48 ticks: Sunder 20 and Weaken from t3, Burn to 28.6 and 49% of the party's damage. Crush left the Knight at 354 (t18.4), one-shot the Hexer (t52.4, 3,892 vs 2,000) and the Paladin (t86.8). Chill shattered at t36.4 (1,935 + Stun); Sanctuary ×2 put Ward on a physical boss; from t53 the Paladin plinked alone while Burn trickled 794 to 42.

**Pack 5, Imp and Hounds (act 2 fight), won t30.** A volley: Backstab CRIT 9,955 one-shot a hellhound (t10.1) and the imp (t29.2). The Pyromancer's basic (1,982 ×2) outhit its Firebolt+ (1,096); Fireball cost 1,000 MP for 1,491; Pyre (begun t25.5) never landed. Hellhounds bit for 110 plus Burn 1.

**Pack 6, Ghoul Pit (act 2 elite), lost t160.** Four ghouls at 32,800 HP (+310%) raising each other at 30%, eight times; the party dealt 178k (Smite+ 10,032 a cast, Bleed 22k, Shock 27k) and lost. Everyone was out of MP by t60 and the knight's Taunt lapsed t38 to 49. Knight down t86, Stormcaller t91, then 69 ticks of a lone Paladin Smiting raised ghouls.

**Pack 7, Fire Giant (act 2 boss), won t20.** Knob −61%: Barbed arrow+ at t1 put on Bleed 25.5 whose first tick (t3.1) did 3,057, 15% of the boss. Fire arrow+ into a fire boss healed it 3,675 (t10.3). Rending arrow 4,917 + Sunder 18.2, then Spear thrust CRIT 8,250 at t19.2; Inferno (begun t15.9) never landed.

**Pack 8, Court Wisps (act 3 fight), lost t31.** Arrived with the Monk at 18 MP (cast nothing all fight) and the Hoplite at 313 (Paired guard fizzled, t7). Eight foes; the Hoplite at Aggro 155 ate six 338-lightning zaps and died t20.9. Backstab+ CRIT 26,884 on a wisp with 86 HP left (t12.8); the Hoplite's one basic at t18.

**Pack 9, Tomb Lords (act 3 elite), won t29.** A volley at t1: Gale spear+ put Shock 30.9 on a mummy in one cast (a 2,099 tick at t5.8); Barbed arrow+ Bleed 17.3 ticked 2,074. Siphon and Soul jar (+500 MP to all per kill) kept everyone full, so the Knight's Clarity went to allies at 3,000/3,000. "Shield bash waits" under Stun read well; two 21,200-HP elites died in 19 and 28 ticks.

**Pack 10, Silent One (act 3 boss), lost t126.** The Vampire arrived at 48 MP, the Bramble knight at 138: Frenzy+, Blood gift+, Regrowth+ and a 5,670 Bramble lash+ never cast. **Pack 11, Wolf Pack (act 1 fight, floor 3), won t92.** The pack-1 party against six 5,760-HP wolves biting for 82 to 107. ## 5. Rhythm

With cooldowns carried in, nine of twelve fights opened with every ability landing by t3 (packs 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12); only packs 1 and 5 built. After that the middle breathes: each unit casts every 11 to 20 ticks between basics. Wind-ups pay off when they land big (Crush, Smite, Spear thrust, a shatter) and read as nothing when soaked (Flatten, pack 3) or when someone dies first (Pyre pack 5, Inferno pack 7, Curse pack 9). The dead stretches are lone-survivor tails: pack 4 t53 to 87, pack 6 t91 to 160, pack 12 t52 to 83, pack 10 t104 to 126. Tier does not set shape: a boss died at t20 (pack 7), a floor-3 fight ran 92 (pack 11), an elite 160 (pack 6); the summary's 10% to 90% bands overlap for every tier (act 1: fights 25 to 62, elites 32 to 78, bosses 47 to 123; act 3 bosses 7 to 218).

## 6. Abilities

A tempo of their own: Thicket 3.75 casts a fight, Stand fast 3.98, Gale spear 3.58, Burning fist 3.57, War ballad 3.74, Cure 3.4, Barbed arrow 3.3, Enfeeble 3.26, Blood drain 4.24. Once or never: Valhalla 0.04 (first t35.6; Einherjar has never been seen), Raise 0.17, Tremor 0.27, Pyre 0.5 (first t27.8), Spear thrust 0.54 (first t20), Unmake 0.63, Thunderstorm 0.71, Static charge 0.78, Sanctuary 0.96. A third slot with no head start and 1,000+ MP is a rumour in a 42-tick fight. Too big: Backstab crits (9,955, 26,884), Smite+ 10,032 five times (pack 6), Spear thrust CRIT 8,250, one-cast Shock 30.9 and Bleed 25.5. Too small for the cost: Sanctuary (1,000 MP for Ward, three casts, all against physical foes), Thunderstorm (1,000 for 2,579, pack 6), Fireball (1,000 for 1,491, pack 5), Blood gift (400 MP and 800 HP for 800). Tempo: the hasted Hexer casts every 3 to 6 ticks; the Hoplite swings 0 to 1 basics a fight (packs 7, 8).

## 7. Statuses

Burn: a decaying ramp, legible, 19% of the party's damage where applied; at 28.6 on a boss it was 49% (pack 4) and then a 36-tick trickle from 794 to 42; hound Burn on the party (55 a pulse) is noise. Bleed: 18%; bursts on the first tick (3,057 pack 7, 2,074 pack 9); a steady loop on the Bramble knight; nothing on a foe nobody hits again (pack 2). Shock: 11%; a line after every foe action, big then halving (2,720 then 1,360, pack 12); capped at 40 from two casts. Chill: 2%; the Frostweaver's lances peak at 6.3 and thaw (packs 1, 11), so the shatter only came from the Hexer's basics (packs 4, 12), where it was the best beat in the fight. Sunder: pinned at 20 from one Enfeeble; the Monk's and Stormcaller's 2.6 to 5.2 invisible. Might: the Bard's ramp (4.2 to 14.2, pack 11) is the one build-with-upkeep status that feels right; the Silent One caps at 15 by t59 and four Tollings do nothing. Taunt holds and its lapses hurt (pack 6 t38 to 49); Guard reads only through brackets. Weaken took Crush from 4,720 to about 3,700 and every one still killed. Stun pauses wind-ups and says so (pack 9 t14.8). Silence from Quiet was the fight (pack 10); from Garrote on ability-less minions, nothing. Regrowth's 520 a pulse mattered; Caravan's 165 to 200 didn't. Vanguard's shield goes in two or three hits; Earthen shell's 3,000 soaked Flatten whole. Ward never mattered; Aegis, Crust, Bulwark not seen. Clarity's MP pulses are legible but went to full-MP allies (pack 9). Bloodlust once (pack 10 t83.3). Lacerate: four Assassin crits (packs 5, 8), no Bleed line.

## 8. Mana

Punishing where it binds, absent where it doesn't, fatal at the tails. Casters with 650 to 1,000 MP spells starve inside a fight: the Paladin short 79% of its time (pack 6; Smite gaps of 24 to 44 ticks), the Stormcaller 85% (2,356 to 16 by t61), the Geomancer down to 146 (pack 3), the Ranger 1,115 to 209 (pack 7). The summary agrees: Bard 22%, Hoplite 18%, Geomancer and Stormcaller 17%, Paladin 16% short. The 200-MP skill users never notice: Knight leaves 76%, Ranger 70%, Valkyrie 69%; with Siphon and Soul jar the Valkyrie sat at 3,000/3,000 through pack 9. The killers are carried-in totals: a Monk at 18 MP cast nothing (pack 8); a Vampire at 48 and a Bramble knight at 138 were short 96% and 101% of their time (pack 10). Clarity, Refrain (+133 to +303 MP a Bard hit, pack 11) and Siphon (+112 to +348, pack 9) all showed and kept their recipients acting; no potion or tonic appeared in any pack. Mana was not healthy: it didn't bind the cheap classes, it shut down the expensive ones mid-fight, and whether a boss fight was playable was decided at the door.

## 9. Classes

Hexer: plays its description to the letter, 64% to 84% of its damage from statuses, every cast changes the boss, dies to Crush. Stormcaller: Shock on everything through Arc, 58% share (the summary's highest), starves and dies (27.3 KO per 100). Ranger: 50% share, the one class whose three slots all fire. Valkyrie: triple-hit Gale spear, Rally, Siphon; at Attack 10.5 it carried packs 9 and 10; Valhalla never. Bramble knight: 43% share, 69% statuses, the elite-killer by being hit, useless when starved. Paladin: Smite+ a nuke, Sanctuary a hole, starves. Monk: fine when fed, nothing when not. Assassin: Backstab crits that end fights. Vampire: Blood drain+ cheap and good, Blood gift nets zero, a passenger in packs 3 and 12. Pyromancer: 51% share, dies earliest (t24.6 mean), Pyre never. Geomancer: quiet and solid. Cleric: 99% basic-attack damage, in fights that didn't need its heals. Bard: a Might ramp and MP for others at 18% share. Hoplite: 0 to 1 basics a fight, its big move 0.54 times a fight, eats everything at Aggro 155. Knight: 10% share, dies the most (25.4 KO per 100). Frostweaver: its signature never happens, dies most of all (31.4). Worst three: Knight, Frostweaver, Hoplite. Best three: Hexer, Ranger, Bramble knight.

## 10. Enemies and bosses

The Ogre Chief acts six or seven times in 85 ticks, and five of its six Crushes in packs 4 and 12 killed; the summary gives it 1.7 party KOs a fight and 55% won. It is a lottery on who it picks. The Fire Giant casts Inferno in half its fights (0.5) and in pack 7 died before it landed. The Silent One's Quiet locks the party for half a fight and its Might caps by t59: legible, punishing, unplayable once silenced. Elites: the Ogre's Flatten was soaked whole (pack 3), the Mummies died in 19 and 28 ticks, the Ghouls at +310% are a 160-tick treadmill. Minions act before dying (97% to 100%, Wisps 82%) but wolves (82 to 124 a bite, 534 an appearance), hellhounds (219) and bandits are noise against 2,000 to 6,000 HP. The regions sound different (Burn on the party, Raise, Silence, Shock and drains); the minion damage doesn't.

## 11. Drama

One real finish: pack 4, lost with the boss at 3%, though its last 34 ticks were a lone Paladin. Decided in ten ticks: pack 2 (Valkyrie dead t5.6), pack 7 (boss at 70% at t3.1), pack 8 (18 MP). Long after decided: packs 4, 6, 10, 12, with 30- to 70-tick tails. Slogs: pack 6 (160), pack 11 (92 for floor 3), pack 3 (76 in no danger). One-shots everywhere: Crush ×5, Backstab ×3, Spear thrust, Smite+ ×5. No comeback in twelve packs.

## 12. The ranked list

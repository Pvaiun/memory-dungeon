# Colours and ways to win: a first cut

The agent's, made to be argued with, and not a ruling. It applies the director's intent (`intent.md`): the families of words are one thing, the ways a party wins are another, and the builds fused them.

## The ways to win

Each build's shape (`docs/gear-design/builds.md`, The rhythm space) with its keyword taken off. The nine come down to four. Carry and Attrition are the director's own words.

| Way to win | What it is | The builds that are it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carry | two serve one, who does the killing, in one blow or in salvos | Landslide, Barrage, Blood Pact |
| Attrition | all three pile something on the foes, and the pile does the killing | Bonfire, Cold Snap, the Storm, Thousand Cuts |
| Retaliation | the foes' own attacks do the killing, on one unit that takes them | the Toll |
| Rush | all three on one foe, and each kill pays for the next | the Hunt |

So four builds are one way to win in four colours, and three are another in three.

## The colours

A colour isn't its status and that status's payoffs: that's a way to win again. It's one flavour's own version of each job (something laid on the foes, a way to stay alive, a trigger), and one job it has no answer to. The hole is the reason to take a second colour.

| Colour | Lays on the foes | Lives by | Also holds | Its hole |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fire | Burn | nothing of its own | long wind-ups | staying alive |
| Frost | Chill, and the Stun it gives | foes that do less: Slow, Silence, Weaken | | killing fast |
| Storm | Shock | the dodge | speed: extra strikes, what a hit carries, a foe acting | one big foe that seldom acts |
| Stone | Sunder | taking it: Shield, Defense, Guard, max HP | Might, blows taken, Thorns | acting often |
| Blood | Bleed | Lifesteal | HP paid or held low | one big blow while it's low |
| Shadow | the Mark | Hidden | crits, kills, the fight's start, Focus | a long fight |
| Light | nothing | heals, Regen | what's given to an ally | killing |

- **No colour:** Swings, Casts, Attack, MP, Spread, and what's the player's (the first-slotted ability, what's worn, a passive).
- **The holes are a guess,** and the least sure part. Nothing in the game gives a family a hole today.
- **Seven is probably one too many.** Light is the one in doubt: giving may be something every colour does in its own way.

- **Few are about a status.** 16 name an affliction, and 27 name anything laid on the foes. The rest are about being struck, Shield, Defense, max HP, HP paid, heals and the fight's start.

## Every colour with every colour

Two grades, and only the second takes work.

- **Allowed:** nothing in the rules stops any two. Of 40 pairings of a way to live with a way to kill, 29 changed nothing (`four-lines.md`).
- **Designed:** a pair is a deck when pieces cross it (want one colour's word, give the other's) or a class holds both. The thin cells above are where authored gaps would write.

# Feel read, round 6

Twelve packs from 383 rogue fights at heat 0, read tick by tick, cold. Pack numbers and ticks are the evidence; the summary says how often each thing happens.

## Per pack

**1. Toll Gate (fight, act 1 f1; won t57).** Three 6,520-HP bandits against three one-ability starters: a small volley at t4–8, then fifty ticks of 295-point bites, nobody below 1,410 HP. The Cleric out-hit the Bard with its staff (32% to 16%); three of four Cures were 590-point top-offs (t9.3, t22.7, t49.7); Might banked to 9.8 on the Cleric because it acts least. A floor-1 fight of 57 ticks with zero risk.

**2. Wolf Pack (fight, act 1 f8; lost t29).** The party arrived at 449, 1,394 and 1,704 HP against seven 6,200-HP wolves; over before t0. Burn 13.5 cooked Wolf A dead by t15; Bleed 15.6 on Wolf F never fired (nobody hit it again); the Ranger died at t12 to six 74-point bites.

**3. Ogre Bridge (elite, act 1 f4; won t79).** The knight's HP sat at 5,194 from t3 to t76 while the bandits bled to death on its thorns (Bleed 37%). Flatten (t20.4, t64.6) went into shield and its Stuns fell inside a live Taunt. The Geomancer's CRITs dealt its normal 1,048 (t20.4, t50.1). Decided by t40, over at t79.

**4. The Packmaster (boss, act 1 f10; lost t141).** A gear passive killed the wolves by t39; then a hundred ticks in which the Hoplite swung four times in 114 ticks, cast Spear thrust once (t14, into a 1,354-HP wolf) and sat at 10–90 MP, while the Hexer re-applied a Sunder at 18 of its 20 cap since t3 six times. Crush: Aegis ate the first (t13.2), then one-shots of the Hexer (t81.5), the Hoplite (t114.4, CRIT 5,757 through Guard) and the Cleric (t140.6). Chill's ramp to 12 at t74 read beautifully and only delayed the Crush that killed the Hexer.

**5. Bone Pile (fight, act 2 f1; won t39).** Three 14,579-HP skeletons (+386%); Taunt held, two members took 0. Shock built to 25 and dealt 1,662–1,814 each time a skeleton acted; Smite+ did 6,174 at t16.4 because Brace's Guard switched Judgement on. Brace did nothing to lightning; Sanctuary's Ward (t29.4, 1,000 MP) met all-physical foes.

**6. Lava Flow (elite, act 2 f4; lost t65).** A Pyromancer in a lava mine, a Valkyrie at 386 MP. A fine opening (t3: Backstab CRIT 5,911, Burn 32.8), then at t18.5 the slime's remaining 16,071 HP became two 18,690-HP bodies and 51 Burn plus 11.7 Bleed vanished. The Pyromancer died at t22.9; Rally and Valhalla never affordable; t25–53 is 28 lines of Burn ticking 19–60. Unwinnable.

**7. The Giant's Wrath (boss, act 2 f10; won t30).** Knob −80%: a 10,449-HP boss. Imps dead at t4.6 and t6 (one never acted), the Giant stunned t7–15, Brand once (Burn 2: 110, 93, 79), Inferno begun t23.5 to land t35.9, the boss dead at t29.7 to a shatter (2,652 + Sunder 7.5). Renew+ went on an untouched Frostweaver (t11.6). A boss that never got to be one.

**8. The Watch (fight, act 3 f7; lost t50).** 145,000 HP of soldiers and drones; the Paladin arrived with 120 MP and cast nothing (7%), the Vampire with 113. The Monk did 81%: 17 actions in 50 ticks (Flow every 12 keeps 20-tick Haste permanent), Siphon pinned its MP at 3,000, Burn reached 52.7 and melted a 39,510-HP soldier. Thirteen Shield-up Taunts, three on one tick (t7.7–8.2).

**9. Tomb Lords (elite, act 3 f4; won t38).** Everything landed by t1.8 (Shock 30.9 from one Gale spear+). The Ranger did 55% with 4% of it basics: Bleed 2,074 a hit, Fire arrow+ 3,158, Rending arrow+ 4,628. The Witch died at t14.3 mid-Curse; two Shield slams stunned a Knight inside a live Taunt. An elite that touched one unit.

**10. The Silent One (boss, act 3 f10; won t125).** The best fight of the twelve. Tolling ×7 took the boss to Might 15 (hits 1,401 → 2,565, CRIT 4,304 at t48.6); Quiet ×5 silenced the party 64% of the time and ate 11 wound-up casts (Tremor never landed). The knight swung 974 ↔ 7,375 three times; its Taunt lapsed under Silence at t76.2 and the Paladin took 2,878 at t76.6; Burn finished the boss six ticks after the last swing. Ward cast twice against a physical boss; the Geomancer's CRITs equal to its normal hits (t14.7, t54.6, t88.1, t110.1).

**11. Cold Ghosts (fight, act 2 f2; lost t138).** Two casters against 75-resist ghosts: Thunderstorm 255 a target for 1,000 MP, the Hoplite's 2,133 every 22 ticks the only damage that counted. Guard did nothing to 847 dark (t5, t65.4); the Stormcaller at 0 MP from t33. The Geomancer alone from t90 to t138, four swings of 374. The longest regular fight in the set, 48 ticks of it pointless.

**12. The Ogre Chief (boss, act 1 f10; lost t79).** Three Crushes, three kills: the Bard at t16.4 (it had swung twice), the Hexer at t47.7, the Paladin at t78.6. Malison's Burn and Shock on all three foes is the Hexer at its best (46%, 81% statuses). Sanctuary (t21.7, Ward against a physical boss) left no MP to Absolve a Hexer at 329 HP from t24.7 to t47.7. The Bard: 2% of damage.

## Across all packs

### 5. Rhythm

The opening is a volley every time: the first abilities land by t3 in nine packs and by t7 in all twelve (pack 9: three by t1.8; pack 1 the slowest, t5.7). Then a lull to t12–16, then waves every 11–14 ticks driven by the short cooldowns (Thicket, Stand fast, Gale spear, Burning fist, Cure, Enfeeble: 3–6 casts a fight) while the long ones come once or never. Pile-ups: pack 5 t15.6–21.4 (six abilities), pack 10 whenever a Silence lifts (t30.7, t55.2, t80.1: three "begins" on one tick). Dead stretches are of two kinds: Burn tick lines between rare swings (pack 6 t25–45, pack 12 t50–64) and a lone survivor against a wall of HP (pack 11 t90–138, pack 4 t84–141).

Wind-ups read: "begins Crush (lands t13.2)" eight ticks ahead is the clearest line in the game and pays off six times in eight. Flatten paid into shields (pack 3); Inferno (pack 7), Curse (pack 9) and Tremor (pack 10) never landed. Tiers have no shapes: regular fights ran 29 to 138 ticks, elites 38 to 79, bosses 30 to 141. Summary: act 2 bosses average 34.5 ticks and are won 73%; act 2 elites 79.6 and 48%; act 3 elites 40%, bosses 100%. The elite is the boss and the boss is the breather.

### 6. Abilities

The tempo-makers: Thicket (3.7–4.1 casts a fight), Stand fast+ (6.3), Blood drain (4.1), Gale spear (3.8), Burning fist (3.4), Cure (3.3), Enfeeble (3.2), Frost lance (3.2). Once or never: Raise 0 casts in 35 fights; Valhalla 0.11 (first t66.9); Tremor 0.33; Unmake 0.31; Thunderstorm 0.53; Pyre 0.6; Spear thrust 0.71; Absolve 0.68 (first t23.2); Sanctuary 0.73; Blizzard's first cast at t25.4 in fights averaging 45. Third slots are decoration, and the auto-player can't afford them after the first two (pack 12).

Too big for the cost: Smite+ (650 MP, 6,174–6,851 plus Burn 26; packs 5, 10), Backstab (325 MP, CRIT 5,911 at t3, pack 6), Pressure point (4,238 + Stun for 325, pack 8). Too small: Enfeeble's 400 MP for a Sunder already capped (pack 4), Gale spear's 3 × 342 into 37,000 HP (pack 6), Thunderstorm's 255 a target (pack 11), Sanctuary whenever the foe hits physical. Tempo: the Hoplite swings every 22–28 ticks (pack 4) and reads as asleep; the hasted Monk with Siphon acts every three (pack 8); everyone else sits at one action per 8–12 ticks.

### 7. Statuses

**Burn**: the biggest ramp and the noisiest; peaks of 40–53 (packs 6, 8) melt 39,000-HP foes, Brand's Burn 2 (pack 7) is invisible, and the decay leaves 20–30 lines of 19–200 after every big cast (pack 6 t25–53). 224 fights, 19%. **Bleed**: well-shaped where hits follow (pack 3, 37%; pack 9, 2,074 a hit), nothing where they don't (pack 2, Wolf F). 20%, the biggest. **Shock**: the clearest, because it fires when the foe acts and the number arrives with its cause (pack 5: 1,814 as a skeleton swings). **Chill**: rare (95 fights, 4%) but its slow is visible (Inferno 8 → 12.4 ticks, pack 7) and its shatter an event (pack 4 t74, pack 7 t29.7). **Sunder**: 156 fights, 0 damage, 18 of its 20 cap from one cast (pack 4 t3), nothing against magic; a bracket, never a number. **Weaken**: a 25–30% cut that leaves Crush at 3,119–3,318, still a one-shot. **Might**: banks on whoever acts least (pack 1); Tolling's 15 doubles a boss's swings (pack 10). **Guard**: nothing against magic (pack 11; pack 5's Brace), about 25% against physical. **Taunt**: works for the party; on foes, Shield up is 300-MP noise (pack 8: 13 casts; pack 9 t14.3: an ability fired through two taunters). **Stun**: on a taunting wall opens nothing (packs 3, 9); on a boss it held one Crush (pack 4 t74). **Haste**: a 20-tick buff on a 12-tick cooldown is permanent (pack 8). **Shield / Aegis**: the real mitigation (Earthen shell soaked 11,062 in pack 10; Aegis ate a 3,552 Crush at pack 4 t13.2 and a 1,270 ordinary hit at pack 11 t64.7 after waiting 58 ticks). **Ward**: never met an elemental hit. **Regen** (590) and **Clarity** (+160) show at numbers a player notices. **Silence**: the only status that dictated a fight's rhythm (pack 10).

### 8. Mana

Short: the Stormcaller (pack 11: 0 MP at t33; summary 22% of its time, the worst), the Hoplite (pack 4: 77%), the Geomancer (packs 3, 10, 11: 35–79%), the Paladin after a Sanctuary (packs 8, 12), the Valkyrie arriving at 386 (pack 6). Never short: the Cleric (pack 4: 7,000 MP, regained more than it spent) and anyone wearing Siphon or beside a Soul jar: the Monk (pack 8: regained 4,641 of 2,975 spent), the Paladin (pack 10) and Valkyrie (pack 9) ended at cap. Mana changed what classes did by subtraction: the four short above became basic-attackers for 60–120 ticks. Arrival did more than spending: classes arrive at 61–84% MP (Bard 61%, Stormcaller 63%), and packs 6 and 8 were decided by it. Clarity, Refrain (69–220, packs 1, 12) and Siphon all show; only Siphon is big enough to feel.

### 9. Classes

Bramble knight: plays its hedge exactly (45% share, 72% statuses). Stormcaller: 60% share, the highest; Arc plus Shock reads (pack 5); starved and blunted by resist (pack 11). Ranger: 48%, basics 4%: three arrows, three wounds. Pyromancer: 51%, Burn 47%. Monk: with Siphon and permanent Haste a machine gun (pack 8). Paladin: Smite+ and Judgement read; Sanctuary wastes a third of its MP (packs 5, 10, 12). Assassin: plays its description (pack 6). Vampire: Lifesteal 1,117 a hit with Bloodlust flickering on and off inside one tick (pack 8 t9.7); Blood gift pays 800 HP to heal a shielded wall (pack 3 t3); 24 KOs per 100. Hexer: Malison is the curse fantasy (pack 12), Enfeeble a capped Sunder every 11 ticks (pack 4); dies most (26.3 per 100, mean t30). Valkyrie: good Shock, a 4,095 Rally+, a Valhalla at 0.11 a fight. Knight: 14%, a wall; fine, invisible. Frostweaver: the best-reading status, a Blizzard that first lands at t25. Cleric: 100% basics, 5,051 healing a fight mostly as top-offs, Raise never. Geomancer: 15%, blank crits in three packs, Tremor 0.33; Earthen shell is the best defensive spell in the game and all the Geomancer is. Hoplite: four swings in 114 ticks. Bard: 19%, 82% basics, 2% in pack 12.

Worst-feeling three: the Bard (a passenger with a fractional buff), the Hoplite (asleep), the Geomancer (blank crits, a shield and nothing else). Best-feeling three: the Ranger (every arrow visible, a tempo of its own), the Bramble knight (the fight happens on it), the Stormcaller (the clearest status in the game), with the caveat that the last two are dominant in the summary, not balanced.

### 10. Enemies and bosses

Bosses' moves come: Crush 2.61 a fight (every ~30 ticks), Tolling 5 and Quiet 3 (pack 10), Flatten ~1, Shield slam 1.2. When they land: Crush is a kill (six of eight), Flatten and Shield slam go into shields and stun a wall that doesn't care, Inferno 0.67 a fight and never seen landing. Elites: the Ogre and Mummies hit a wall and never threaten (packs 3, 9); the Lava Slime is the hardest foe in the set by arithmetic (pack 6). Minions act before dying (≥83%, most 97–100%), though Fire Imps (pack 7) and the Witch (pack 9) didn't. Regions read as resist tables (ghosts 75, slimes fire 50, skeletons Armor 8), and a party that can't be re-drafted pays for the mismatch with a 138-tick loss. Felt like nothing: Bandits (295 a hit), Fire Imps, Hellhounds (279 a fight), Shield up (4.6 casts a fight per Soldier).

### 11. Drama

Close calls: pack 10 throughout (997 HP at t76.6, 974 at t106.1, the Burn kill at t124.4). Comebacks: none; a party behind stays behind. One-shots: Crush ×6; two 821 hits on a 1,460-HP Pyromancer (pack 6 t22.9). Decided by t10: packs 2, 6, 8 (arrival), pack 7 (a 10,449-HP boss stunned at t7). Long after decided: pack 11 (48 ticks), pack 4 (57), pack 6 (42), pack 3 (39, safely), pack 12 (31).

### 12. The ranked list

What looked wrong, worst first:

1. **Fights lost before t0.** Packs 2 (HP 449/1,394/1,704), 6 (Valkyrie 386 MP), 8 (Paladin 120 MP, Vampire 113): three of twelve decided by what the party carried in. Summary: classes arrive at 61–84% MP, the Bard at 61%.
2. **Elites are the bosses; bosses are the breather.** Pack 6's slimes (~112,000 effective HP, +167%) against pack 7's boss (10,449 HP, −80%, dead at t30 before Inferno landed). Summary: act 2 elites 48% won at 80 ticks, act 2 bosses 73% at 34.5; act 3 elites 40%, bosses 100%.
3. **Crush is a kill, not a blow.** Six of eight: pack 4 t81.5, t114.4 (CRIT 5,757 through Guard), t140.6; pack 12 t16.4, t47.7, t78.6. Weakened it still lands 3,119–3,318. Summary: Ogre Chief 9,460 a fight, 2.61 Crushes, 1.6 KOs, 55% won.
4. **Fights that run long after they're decided.** Pack 11 t90–138 (one Geomancer at 374 a swing), pack 4 t84–141, pack 6 t23–65, pack 12 t47–79, pack 3 t40–79. Summary spans: act 3 elites 19–146 ticks, act 1 bosses 38–120.
5. **The slime split gains HP and drops every status.** Pack 6 t18.5: 16,071 HP left became two 18,690 bodies; Burn 51 and Bleed 11.7 gone. 11 appearances.
6. **Third-slot abilities never come.** Raise 0 casts in 35 fights; Valhalla 0.11 (first t66.9); Tremor 0.33 (pack 10: lost at t14.7, never retried); Unmake 0.31; Thunderstorm 0.53; Pyre 0.6; Spear thrust 0.71; Blizzard first at t25.4.
7. **The Geomancer's crits add nothing.** Pack 3 t20.4, t50.1 (CRIT 1,048 = 1,048); pack 10 t14.7, t54.6, t88.1, t110.1; pack 11 eight times (CRIT 374 = 374). Other classes' crits are ×1.5 (pack 1: the Bard's 736 against 474; pack 4 t55.2: the Cleric's 1,260 against 840). Its passive doubles crit chance. 15% share.
8. **The auto-player spends the Paladin's MP on Ward against physical foes.** Packs 5 t29.4, 10 t6 and t86.8, 12 t21.7 (then no Absolve for a Hexer at 329 HP for 23 ticks). Summary: Sanctuary 0.73 casts a fight at 1,000 MP. Also Renew+ on an untouched unit (pack 7 t11.6) and Cure as 400–600 top-offs (packs 1, 4).
9. **Guard and Sunder don't touch magic.** Pack 11: "847 dark (guarded)" equals 847 unguarded, 975 MP of Paired guard; pack 5: Brace's Guard did nothing to lightning and doubled the Paladin's hits. Sunder: 156 fights, 0 damage, 18 of its 20 cap from one cast (pack 4 t3), recast six times.
10. **Silence eats every long wind-up.** Pack 10: 11 casts lost, the party silenced 64% of 125 ticks, nothing with a 6- or 8-tick wind-up fits an 8-tick window. It reads, and it's too much.
11. **Burn's tail is noise and its peaks are all over.** Pack 6 t25–53 (28 lines of 19–60), pack 12 t50–64; peaks from 2 (pack 7: 110 a pulse) to 52.7 (pack 8: 2,607). Summary mean peak 20.1.
12. **Siphon and Soul jar turn mana off.** Pack 8's Monk regained 4,641 of 2,975 spent (17 actions in 50 ticks, Haste permanent); pack 10's Paladin and pack 9's Valkyrie ended at cap; the Stormcaller is short 22% of its time.
13. **The Bard is a passenger.** Pack 12: 2% of damage, dead at t17 having swung twice; pack 1: 16%, lowest. Summary: 19% share, 82% basics, arrives at 61% MP. Its Might banks on the slowest ally.
14. **The Hoplite is asleep.** Pack 4: four swings and nine actions in 114 ticks; pack 11: four swings in 90; Aegis waited 58 ticks for a 1,270 hit (t64.7). 74% basics, 22% share.
15. **Stun on a taunting wall, and foe-side Taunt, do nothing.** Packs 3 (t20.4, t64.6) and 9 (t14.8): Stuns inside a live Taunt; pack 8: 13 Shield ups, three on one tick; pack 9 t14.3: Gale spear+ through two taunters. Summary: Stun 64 fights, Taunt 60, Soldier 4.6 Shield ups a fight.

What looked right:

- **Shock** fires with its cause (pack 5 t20.9: 1,814 as the skeleton swings; pack 9 t34.8: 2,320), and was the only thing that hurt the ghosts in pack 11. 171 fights, 12%.
- **Bleed** on the hedge and the arrows (pack 3, 37%; pack 9, 2,074 a hit): the biggest status at 20%, visible every time it fires.
- **Chill's** slow and shatter (pack 7 t29.7: the Giant dies before Inferno; pack 4 t74: Crush waits). Rare, but every stack reads.
- **Boss moves on a cadence a player can learn**, each telegraphed 6–8 ticks: Crush every ~30, Tolling every ~13, Quiet every ~25 (packs 4, 10, 12).
- **Pack 10 as a whole**: a wall swinging 974 ↔ 7,375, a Taunt that lapses under Silence (t76.2) and costs the Paladin 2,878 at t76.6, a Burn that finishes the boss after the last swing.
- **Nukes that read**: Smite+ 6,174–6,851 after a six-tick wind-up (packs 5, 10), Backstab CRIT 5,911 at t3 (pack 6), Rending arrow+ 4,628 (pack 9), Pressure point 4,238 + Stun (pack 8).
- **Judgement against Brace** (pack 5 t16.4): a foe's buff turned into its liability, legible and funny.
- **Shields as mitigation**: Earthen shell soaked 11,062 in pack 10; Vanguard's 1,450 took the first hits in packs 7 and 9.
- **Minions act before they die** (≥83%, most 97–100%), and **no cast fizzled** anywhere (0.00 down the whole table).
- **Regen and Clarity** pulse at numbers a player notices (590 and 160; packs 7, 9, 10).

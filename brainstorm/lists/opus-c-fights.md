# Fights Brainstorm

## 0. Trouble

1. Plated foes whose Armor eats small hits.
2. Slippery foes that dodge most physical attacks.
3. Foes that resist an element hard, or are weak only to an unusual one.
4. A long wind-up that knocks out whoever it lands on.
5. Steady damage to the whole party that wins a long fight.
6. A healer that undoes your damage while the others hit you.
7. Swarms of weak foes that waste big single hits.
8. Assassins that always go for the lowest-HP unit.
9. A foe that Taunts the party onto itself so the others go untouched.
10. Foes that can't be targeted for stretches of the fight.
11. Stuns, Silences and MP drain that stop casters from casting.
12. A foe that grows stronger every few seconds, forcing a race.
13. Stacking Poison or Bleed that outpaces healing.
14. Thorns that punish fast, many-hit attackers.
15. Barriers and buffs that have to be stripped before damage gets through.

## 1. Classes

**Knight**: An armored wall that keeps the enemy looking at it.
It has heavy Armor and a high targeting stat. Its abilities Taunt every foe onto it or put a Barrier on itself. Passive: while foes are Taunted onto it, it takes a flat amount less from every hit.
*Statuses:* Taunted, Barrier. *Special stats:* Block.

**Warrior**: A soldier with a big weapon who wins the long fight.
Slow, heavy swings spill onto a second foe. Its abilities hit all foes or crack one foe's Armor wide open. Passive: every hit it lands adds a stack of Sundered.
*Statuses:* Sundered. *Special stats:* Splash.

**Cleric**: A temple healer who keeps everyone standing.
It heals the lowest-HP ally, lays Regen on the whole party or removes one debuff. Passive: its heals are much stronger on allies below half HP.
*Statuses:* Regen. *Special stats:* none.

**Mage**: A scholar who hurls the elements.
It casts big single-target bolts and slower blasts at all foes, and its element depends on which abilities it brings. Passive: each spell leaves the target slightly weaker to that element for a while, stacking.
*Statuses:* a stacking "weaker to this element" debuff. *Special stats:* Quickcast.

**Rogue**: A quick blade that's never where you swung.
It makes fast dagger hits and has high Evasion and Crit. Its abilities Poison the target or slip it into Veiled. Passive: after it dodges, its next hit is a guaranteed crit.
*Statuses:* Poison, Veiled, a "next hit crits" buff. *Special stats:* Crit Power, Double Strike.

**Ranger**: A hunter who picks the prey and doesn't miss.
It fires quick piercing shots. Its abilities mark a foe as Hunted or loose a volley at several random foes. Passive: it deals extra damage to anything Hunted.
*Statuses:* Hunted. *Special stats:* Accuracy.

**Monk**: A mountain ascetic who fights with fists and breath.
Fast blunt flurries knock foes out of their wind-ups, and an ability Stuns one foe. Passive: every hit it lands restores a little MP.
*Statuses:* Stun. *Special stats:* Double Strike, Interrupt.

**Paladin**: A holy knight who smites and shelters.
Its Light smites heal the lowest-HP ally for part of the damage, and it can put a Barrier on an ally. Passive: it steps in to take hits aimed at allies below a quarter HP.
*Statuses:* Barrier. *Special stats:* Cover.

**Bard**: A wandering musician whose songs carry the party.
Its songs give the whole party Haste or more damage for a while, and a sour note Silences a foe. Passive: whenever it finishes a song, every song it has on allies refreshes.
*Statuses:* Haste, Silence, a party "deals more damage" buff. *Special stats:* none.

**Berserker**: A northern warrior who fights harder the nearer death gets.
It makes big two-handed swings and has little Armor. Its abilities spend its own HP for extra damage. Passive: the lower its HP, the faster it attacks.
*Statuses:* an Attack Speed buff that grows as HP falls. *Special stats:* Lifesteal.

---

**Runesmith**: A Norse carver who writes power into weapons.
It carves runes on an ally's weapon. One turns their basic attacks to Fire or Frost, and one adds a piece of elemental damage to every hit. Passive: its runes last the whole fight.
*Statuses:* an "attacks deal this element" buff, an "extra elemental damage on hit" buff. *Special stats:* none.

**Oracle**: A Greek seer who saw the blow coming.
It puts Fated on the foe with the highest Attack, or gives one ally extra Evasion for a while. Passive: whenever a foe starts winding up an ability, that ability's target gets a small Barrier.
*Statuses:* Fated, Barrier, an Evasion buff. *Special stats:* none.

**Onmyoji**: A court diviner who binds with paper talismans.
Its seals Silence the foe with the highest Magic or Chill all foes, and its wards put a Barrier on an ally. Passive: nothing it puts on a foe can be removed.
*Statuses:* Silence, Chill, Barrier. *Special stats:* none.

**Hoplite**: A Greek spearman who's strongest in the shield wall.
It makes steady piercing thrusts. Its abilities raise its own Block or put a small Barrier on every ally. Passive: while it stands, every ally has extra Armor.
*Statuses:* Barrier, a party Armor buff that ends when the Hoplite falls. *Special stats:* Block.

**Valkyrie**: A chooser of the slain who isn't ready to choose yet.
Its spear strikes hit foes below half HP harder. It marks the lowest-HP ally so that if they fall, they rise again a moment later. Passive: every time any unit falls, it gains Attack for the rest of the fight.
*Statuses:* a "rises once after falling" buff, a stacking Attack buff. *Special stats:* Executioner.

**Ronin**: A masterless samurai who fights best alone.
It makes slow, heavy slashing draws that crit often. Passive: each time an ally falls, it gains a large stack of Attack and Crit chance.
*Statuses:* a stacking Attack-and-Crit buff. *Special stats:* Crit Power.

**Dragoon**: A lancer who leaps out of reach.
Its leap lands one huge piercing hit on the highest-HP foe, and it can also sweep all foes. Passive: it can't be targeted while winding up any ability.
*Statuses:* Veiled. *Special stats:* Armor Pierce.

**Spellbreaker**: A witch-hunter who unmakes magic.
It strips buffs and Barriers off foes and Silences the foe with the highest Magic. Its hits knock casters out of their wind-ups. Passive: it has high resistance to every element.
*Statuses:* Silence. *Special stats:* Interrupt.

**Warlock**: A pact-sworn caster who pays in blood.
It casts Shadow bolts. Its curses put Festering on the foe with the highest Magic and Bleed on a random foe. Passive: whenever it loses HP, it regains MP.
*Statuses:* Festering, Bleed. *Special stats:* Lifesteal.

**Miko**: A shrine maiden who purifies.
It removes debuffs from allies and strikes foes with Spirit. Passive: any ally it cleanses can't receive debuffs for a short while.
*Statuses:* a short "immune to debuffs" buff. *Special stats:* Tenacity.

**Gladiator**: An arena champion who wants every eye on it.
It has a high targeting stat and Thorns, and its abilities hit back harder the more it's been hit. Passive: each hit it takes gives it a stack of Attack that fades slowly.
*Statuses:* Thorns, a stacking Attack buff. *Special stats:* Counter, Spikes.

**Chronomancer**: A mage who bends the ticks.
It Hastes allies, Chills foes or Stuns one foe outright. Passive: while it stands, every ally's cooldowns run faster.
*Statuses:* Haste, Chill, Stun, a party cooldown buff that ends when it falls. *Special stats:* Head Start.

**Alchemist**: A tinkerer with a belt of flasks.
It throws flasks at random foes that Poison, Sunder or Chill them and splash onto a second foe. It drinks a tonic for Regen. Passive: its debuffs land with extra stacks.
*Statuses:* Poison, Sundered, Chill, Regen. *Special stats:* Splash.

**Martyr**: A pilgrim who gives itself away.
It pays its own HP to heal the lowest-HP ally, and it has a high targeting stat and little Armor. Passive: when it's knocked out, every ally is healed and gains a Barrier.
*Statuses:* Barrier. *Special stats:* none.

**Illusionist**: A trickster of mirrors and fog.
It wraps an ally in mirror images that each make one physical hit miss, and it can Veil an ally. Passive: a foe that misses one of its allies becomes Hunted.
*Statuses:* a stacking "mirror images" buff, Veiled, Hunted. *Special stats:* none.

## 2. Statuses

- **Poison** (debuff): loses HP on a timer; stacks build up and drain away one at a time.
- **Stun** (debuff): can't act at all, and whatever it was winding up is cancelled.
- **Silence** (debuff): can't use abilities, only basic attacks.
- **Haste** (buff): attacks faster, and its cooldowns run faster.
- **Regen** (buff): heals a little on a timer.
- **Barrier** (buff): soaks a pool of damage before HP.

---

- **Taunted** (debuff): must attack the unit that taunted it.
- **Bleed** (debuff): loses HP each time it finishes an action, so it hurts fast units most.
- **Chill** (debuff): attacks and cooldowns slow with each stack, which melt one at a time.
- **Sundered** (debuff): loses Armor with each stack.
- **Hunted** (debuff): more likely to be targeted, and takes extra damage from everyone.
- **Fated** (debuff): takes one heavy hit when it runs out, unless it's removed first.
- **Thorns** (buff): anyone who lands a physical hit on it takes damage back.
- **Festering** (debuff): loses part of every heal it receives as damage.
- **Petrified** (debuff): can't act at all, but takes far less damage.
- **Veiled** (buff): can't be targeted until it finishes its next action.

## 3. Special stats

- **Lifesteal**: heals for a share of the damage it deals.
- **Crit Power**: how much harder its crits hit.
- **Armor Pierce**: ignores this much of the target's Armor on each hit.
- **Accuracy**: cancels out this much of the target's Evasion.
- **Block**: chance to shrink a physical hit by a large share.
- **Counter**: chance to answer a physical hit with a basic attack.
- **Tenacity**: shortens the debuffs put on it.

---

- **Double Strike**: chance a basic attack hits twice.
- **Splash**: a share of each hit also lands on another random foe.
- **Interrupt**: chance on hit to cancel the target's wind-up.
- **Executioner**: extra damage against targets below half HP.
- **Cover**: chance to take a hit aimed at an ally below a quarter HP.
- **Quickcast**: chance an ability skips its wind-up.
- **Head Start**: its cooldowns start the fight this far along.
- **Spikes**: while above zero, it carries Thorns of that strength.

## 4. Elements

- **Fire**: wild and hungry; beasts, plants and frost-giants fear it.
- **Frost**: slow and patient; it tends to bring Chill, and northern giants laugh at it.
- **Storm**: sudden and scattered; quick strikes that jump between random foes and crit often.
- **Stone**: heavy and grinding; big slow hits, home of Sundered and Petrified.
- **Light**: clean and searing; it burns the undead and demons and travels with healing.
- **Shadow**: cold and draining; curses, life-stealing, the weapon of things that hate the living.
- **Spirit**: the stuff of souls and kami; the one thing that truly hurts ghosts and yokai.
- **Arcane**: raw magic with no nature of its own; almost nothing resists it, and nothing is weak to it.

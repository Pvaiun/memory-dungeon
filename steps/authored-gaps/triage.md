# Effect cull: Greats and Fines

Duplicates left out. Lines are changed where a note said how; the notes themselves are removed.

## Great (42)

- Healing past full becomes a Shield on it, Regen included.
- At fight start: the other ally with the least Attack gains the wearer's Attack, and the wearer keeps only 33%.

- Abilities use the Attack of the ally with the most Attack.
- Each cast gives the wearer a Crescendo (+20% damage, healing and stacks each, up to 10).
- On a dodge: the hit lands on another foe instead.
- At fight start: readies the slowest ability of the ally with the most Attack.
- Abilities scale from Defense in place of Attack.
- Every other ability it casts is free.
- While Hidden: hits deal ×2.
- When an ally pays HP, the other ally with the lowest HP% heals as much.
- On a dodge: strikes the attacker with its weapon.
- When it gives itself a status, the other ally with the lowest HP% gains it too.
- Once a fight: survives a knockout at 1 HP, Hidden.
- With no offhand: +12% Evasion, and basic attacks deal +25%.
- Basic attacks don't spend Might.
- Every Shield another ally would gain goes to it instead.
- Max HP and max MP trade places, after all other gear is counted.
- When a foe breaks its Shield: sends back 200% of the most it held.
- Never makes a basic attack. Defense ×2.
- Abilities deal +4% per Shock on the target.
- When a foe falls: every ally's start-of-fight effects happen again.
- At fight start: Stunned. After 10 ticks: every ability is ready, and Hasted.
- Extra strikes roll Double strike again, up to 2 more, each 20% weaker.
- Basic attacks deal extra damage equal to 50% of all Shield the party holds.
- Each heal it gives: the ally's next hit deals extra damage equal to 50% of the heal, adding up.
- +50% Crit chance. Hits that don't crit deal −50%.
- Basic attacks add Chill, from max HP.
- Abilities are free while it has Clarity.
- The part of its holder's heals that would go past full becomes a Shield on the healed ally.
- On a dodge: next basic attack comes at once and lands 2 times.
- Starts every fight at half its HP (it pays), and its Lifesteal is doubled.
- Basic attacks come 50% as often, deal ×3 and Stun their target.
- On a dodge: 3 Heat (5% faster each, up to 10). When hit: loses 50% of its Heat.
- Starts each fight at 50% HP and can't be healed above it. Counts as below 50%. Hits deal +50%.
- Basic attacks pay 5% of max HP and deal 4× what they paid as extra damage.
- Basic attacks deal +5% per Shock on the target.
- Each ability the wearer casts heals it for 3% of its max HP.
- Basic attacks come only when a foe acts: once ready, each waits for one, then strikes it at once.
- Hits deal +10% per different debuff on the target.

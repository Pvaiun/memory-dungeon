## Sorted

**Design (the substance; what they change is in `interview.md`):**
- Aggro dead beyond the first step; do enemies have it (they do: a stat, and Taunt by Shield up); want the share shown.
- Taunt and Stand fast extremely strong; healing mandatory; Spear thrust strong; the Hunter's passive terrible; Might spent on attack and everyone tanky, so Might gear is a minor bonus not a strategy; max shields maybe too low; magic damage high, basic attacks especially; the campfire's heal underwhelming. (Numbers, noted for the balance pass; not acted on here.)
- Rarity: no stats on most cool gear; no vertical increase, so "upgrade a gear" can't be used with confidence; power flat across legendaries; gear a situational trigger not an upgrade; the Vampire in Flurry wraps and Thrall's chalice the lowest damage dealer; uncommons with rules.
- Two-handers a curse: one trigger, the offhand lost; classes starting with one lose a choice; the Bard should start with the wand; Storm pike's on-hit stack odd on a two-hander.
- Bespoke words where a known status would do: Spite for Thornheart (why not Thorns), Dawnblade (why not Might), Thousandth cut (why not Sunder), Wallflower's trigger as a pattern, Fervor, "every so often".
- Conditions the player can't line up: the stunned payoff, the revive build (never a positive death; Last bastion didn't trigger returns), the double strike build, the burst-down build; the Undying formation's intent; the wall-well formation's intent.
- Casters: the elemental mages weak from a low action economy, binary (nuke or die).
- Classes: make them mechanic themes (Thornheart transforming a class; Stonewall feeding the Geomancer); mixed classes (the Geomancer's Attack); different versions of a class learning different skills; the Paladin's passive debuff version by default; Bloodlust without lifesteal hard to use; recruits at half HP.
- Loot fit: the Ranger sharing an offhand kind with the Bramble knight and Geomancer, so pieces feel coded for one or the other; the Monk never wanting the Cleric's or Stormcaller's offhand; tome against sealed against item a fun choice.
- The run: swapping not needed (the best run recruited nobody until the end of act II); a hard fight early is super easy; act I's enemies not spiky enough to need counters; the Rival Company enjoyed; Coup de grâce's reset fun.
- Damage types: each class needs a resist stat; not enough magic foes; can't tell a boss's type or a foe's Armor against its Magic resist.

**Interface and legibility (for the next interface session):**
- The sheet: two presses to open; can't see it from the learn screen; mana cut off with big numbers; basic attack damage not scaled in the sheet; Vanguard's shield amount and scaling; Spiked bulwark showing nothing until the fight; Absolve+ showing Absolve's number; the passive first in the upgrade panel; amounts and scaling missing on many items (Canopy staff, Spinebark pole, Venom vial, Idol of the hungry dark, Bell of the hour); stats in effect text on the wrong scale; a "dps" stat on weapons; how much Weaken, Might and the rest.
- The field: auto-attack bars empty then filling in a tick; MP gain yellow; no feedback for an unaffordable ability; the shield off the HP bar; mana on the field; the formation not visible in combat; "resist" on every magic hit; shatter pop-up missing; variable impact length missing; hit pause scaling less with speed; speeds 1.5×, 3×, 5×; status tick rate inconsistent; on-start effects before the first turn; sprites overlapping.
- Loot, map and camp: feedback when a slot is empty at a pick; after an elite see both choices at once; the fight preview confusing; event result text too fast; gold not shown at level-up; the revived ally seen after the boss, not at the next act; a confirmation on leaving training with opportunities left; the prisoner convoy with a full roster and no party screen; common formations orange; HP and MP inverted on the bench; cooldown proportional to slot (a question).
- Wording: Fervor "through a knockout"; Battle prayer "is dispelled"; Overgrowth "taunts itself twice over"; Bones of the returned "on cooldown"; Vigil "up 2 steps"; "half again", "two fifths"; Thunderstorm+ "if it deals none"; Funeral bell's Fervor.
- Settings: defaults, hide debug settings.

**To check in the build (possible bugs):** Silence and Stun not stopping a cast (the logs show nine holds and two interrupts in 111 fights; the Ogre's wound-up basic swing, which shrugged off Silence, was fixed Nov 2); Aegis never triggering (it was on the party in two fights); Last bastion not triggering returns; Absolve+'s number; both Blood drain versions removing Weaken; the elite event paying twice; the prisoner convoy with a full roster; overlapping sprites; recruits at half HP; Might on abilities scaling from HP or Armor; the 3.2-tick basic recovery.

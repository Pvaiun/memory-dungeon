# The fresh read: the items

One reader who hadn't seen the design, reading every entry in the eight shares as a player meeting it in an offer of three, and as a checker against the spine, `fill/shared.md` and the vocabulary. Nothing here judges strength. What a share's own *Challenges* or *Least sure* already says isn't repeated, unless the entry doesn't match its ruling.

Written a share at a time. The cross-fill sections are at the end.

## Weapons, the first six kinds (`weapons-1.md`)

Budgets all add up. No card names a build or a partner. Nine findings.

- **Still hand.** The card says "strikes twice" and "the second strike", on a fist weapon, where round 1 made "extra strike" mean Double strike's second strike. A player reads it as Double strike; the notes say it isn't one. Hunting spear says the same rule as "lands twice… the second landing". Fix: use Hunting spear's words.
- **Answering thunder.** The spine's line still says "strikes it with a basic attack, if it's free to". The entry says "strikes that foe with its weapon", which isn't a basic attack. The entry follows round 1; the line is stale.
- **Bloodscent knife.** One quantity in two units: "50 more Crit chance" and "four in five". Gear shows Crit chance in steps, so a player hasn't met "50". Fix: give the lift and the ceiling the same way.
- **Ambush knife.** "Whatever its Crit chance" reads as the foe's. Fix: "whatever its wearer's Crit chance". The card also doesn't say the chance is set and can't be raised, which the notes rest on.
- **Bloodletter.** The card says "each of its swings" where the rest of the share says "basic attacks", and the notes say an extra strike doesn't pay. Fix: "each of its basic attacks".
- **Squall lance.** "The flash's own hits aren't counted", but the card never calls anything a flash. Fix: "those hits aren't counted".
- **The Red Feast.** "Healed up to half its max HP" reads two ways: to the half mark, or by as much as half. The notes mean the first. Fix: "healed back to half its max HP, if it's below that".
- **Parrying spear.** It fires on a dodge, and only the Monk dodges by its class line. The Hoplite and the Valkyrie have no Evasion, and the piece's steps (Aggro, HP) bring none, so for two of three wearers the card is blank without another piece. The notes say so for the Hoplite only. An Evasion step would suit it. Oathkeeper is the same shape (only the Knight Taunts in its kit), and its notes say so.
- **Reaver's axe, Ragged axe.** Neither carries Lifesteal, the axe's signature. Round 1 lets a piece drop its signature only where the signature works against what the piece does, and here it doesn't. Either the ruling gains "or a lean kind's single step", or the two swap. Overkill axe gives its reason; these don't have one the ruling allows.

## Weapons, the other six kinds (`weapons-2.md`)

Budgets all add up, and every piece above common carries its signature. No card names a build or a partner. Four findings; the near pairs are under *Cards that read alike*.

- **Overrun.** The card says the left-over "carries into a random foe". "A random foe" is the vocabulary's phrase for a pick a Taunt decides and, after the kit change, a Mark takes. Round 2 rules that this pick does neither. So the card promises a player holding a Mark the opposite of what happens. The spine says "another foe". Fix: "into another foe, at random", or the vocabulary's "a second, random foe".
- **Executioner's longbow, Giantsbane, Martyr's mace.** Each says "its HP" ("above half its HP", "below 70% of its HP") where the first weapons share and the vocabulary say "max HP". Round 1 asked for the vocabulary's words on kept cards too. Martyr's mace also gives one line as a percentage and three as fractions. Fix: "max HP" throughout, and one way of counting on the mace.
- **Twinfall.** "A moment later" has no size on the card; the numbers say one tick. Round 1 took "for a moment" off the kept cards for the same reason. Fix: "a tick later".
- **Staff of echoes.** The card doesn't say whom the second cast is aimed at: the first cast's target, or a new pick by the ability's own phrase. It matters for a Cure (the lowest ally may have changed) and for a Firebolt that just killed its foe. Encore has the same gap in the kit. One clause settles it.

## Armor (`armor.md`)

Budgets and cell sizes all match the spine. No card names a build or a partner. Five findings. (Urchin plate beside Spiked targe is already on the director's list, by round 1.)

- **Urchin plate, Bloodsworn mail.** "It has Thorns, high" and "it has Lifesteal, high" put a scale word on the card. Round 1 ruled the size; this is about the word. The interface shows special stats as values, so a player can't tell how much (see *Words used two ways*).
- **Last bastion.** "A blow that would knock its wearer out": the share's header says a status, Thorns and damage sent back aren't blows. So a Burn pulse or a Doom knocks its wearer out through the piece, and a player reading the card expects to be saved from anything. Fix: say "a hit", or say "any damage" if that's meant.
- **Keystone cuirass, Urchin plate; Banked robe, Glimmer robe.** Blank for some wearers, and only Keystone cuirass's notes say so. The two plate uncommons need a Shield, which the Hoplite, the Paladin and the Bramble knight don't make: half of Plate's uncommons do nothing for three of its five wearers without another piece. Banked robe (stacks of Burn, Chill, Shock) and Glimmer robe (an ability that deals damage) do nothing for a Cleric or a Bard, by their kits. Not wrong for a rare; thin for uncommons that are meant to be general.
- **Overture coat.** "Each happens once a kill, whatever else calls for it" is a ruling's sentence on a card. A player meeting the coat alone can't tell what else would call for it. Fix: "…happen again, once for each kill".
- **Tithing leathers.** Still shares a name's root with Tithe wand, which a Bard can hold beside it, and the two tithe different things to different units. The first weapons share raised it and neither round ruled.

The share says "half its HP" on every card where the first weapons share says "half its max HP": see *Words used two ways*.

## Offhands, the first six kinds (`offhands-1.md`)

Budgets and cell sizes all match the spine. No card names a build or a partner. Eight findings.

- **Wick.** Its numbers say that with no other foe burning the fire "goes to a random foe… and that random pick follows a Mark". Round 2 rules the opposite for what a piece sends: a status sent to a random foe goes by draw and a Mark doesn't take it (Winter's chain was changed for this). The entry doesn't match its ruling.
- **Bossed kite, Comrade's targe, Charnel icon, Shield of the chosen.** Each card names the stat an amount reads and not its size: "deals extra damage (from its Defense)", "gains a Shield (from its holder's max HP)", "is healed (from its holder's Attack)". The sizes are in the numbers (a touch, a fifth, a light hit, a third). The weapons shares put the size on the card ("a Shield of a quarter of its wearer's Attack"). Fix: do the same here.
- **Briar totem.** It carries the name of the Bramble knight's passive, Briar, which does nearly the same thing, and a Bramble knight wears it. Round 1 renamed Tower shield because Phalanx is the Hoplite's passive. The same reason holds here.
- **Heart of the saint.** "The same share of its max HP" doesn't say as what; Flagellant's vestments finishes the sentence ("as they'd cost of its max MP"). A Cleric can wear both. The notes say there's then one price, but the two cards have different floors (never below 1 HP; won't cast below a tenth of its HP) and nothing says which holds.
- **Holy water.** Blank on a Paladin, one of its two wearers: Absolve is its only heal and already cleanses. Kept from today's, so it may be known.
- **Spiked targe.** Of its three wearers only the Bramble knight Guards itself in its base kit. The notes don't say so. Guard is common enough that this is small.
- **The share's *Loops*** still describes Bellows wraps at a tenth beside Everflame lantern ("the fire then grows without limit"). Round 2 made it a twentieth, and the first weapons share's *Loops* has the new sum (it nearly holds and doesn't grow). The section is stale.
- **Everflame lantern.** The spine's line still says "every few ticks", which round 1 made four. The card says three, as ruled. The line is stale.

"Every so often", "its hit fells a foe" and "Thorns, medium" are under *Words used two ways*; the four reaction riders are under *Cards that read alike*.

## Offhands, the other seven kinds (`offhands-2.md`)

Budgets and cell sizes all match the spine. No card names a build or a partner. Seven findings.

- **Crimson dregs.** "Its holder's hits on a bleeding foe heal it" reads as healing the foe. Fix: "heal its holder".
- **Venom vial.** The first sentence says each hit raises the Bleed by half of what's left; the second says its hits leave the wound as deep as they found it. A player reads the first as growth and the second as none. And the two agree only while Bleed closes a third a hit (`STATUS.bleed.drop`): move that number in balance and the card is wrong. Fix: say the one thing that happens ("its holder's hits put back what the wound closes"), sized by what closed and not by "half".
- **Bell of the hour.** "Casts its first-slotted ability off its cooldown" is the spine's phrase, and to a player it reads the other way: cast when it's off cooldown. Fix: say it on the card ("casts it at once, whatever its cooldown, with no wind-up; its cooldown stays where it was and its MP is paid").
- **The king's head.** "Doubled for a while" has no size on the card; the numbers say 20 ticks, and it's the piece's own length, so nothing else tells the player. Fix: "for 20 ticks".
- **Bloodlust draught.** It carries the name of the Vampire's passive, Bloodlust, doubles the same stat, and a Vampire wears it. The same case as Briar totem, and as the Phalanx shield round 1 renamed.
- **Orb of the rising sun.** "Nothing makes its abilities cost more for the Crescendo they hold" means something only beside Crescendo charm. Alone in an offer, the sentence points at a rule the player hasn't met. It's the nearest any card in the six gear shares comes to naming a partner.
- **Idol of the hungry dark.** It moves a buff "with its stacks and the time it has left", so its holder gets a Haste or a Guard for less than the status's own length. Kept from today's, and maybe meant: but "nothing applies a status for less than its own length" has no words for a status that's moved. One line in the rules would cover this, Scapegoat charm and the copies.

"A few" on Curse-book, "strikes twice" on Tempest cloak and Dragon's tooth, and "the blow" on Swan's grace are under *Words used two ways*.

## Amulets (`amulets.md`)

Budgets and the cell sizes match the spine. No card names a build or a partner. Five findings.

- **Lightning-glass.** "Its wearer's abilities deal 4% more for each Shock on their target. The Shock stays." Thunderhead maul is the same sentence with "hits" and 5%, and a Paladin, a Hoplite or a Geomancer wears both. An ability's hit is a hit, so the maul's card contains the glass's. Both shares list the pair under *Least sure* and neither round ruled it. It's the plainest case in the fill of gate 1's "two pieces one unit could wear together never read the same".
- **Ember heart.** "Until then the wearer holds it, shown as a number, and it doesn't burn down." The card doesn't say whether the held fire is Burn on the wearer. If it is, it hurts the wearer, lights Burning heart and is passed on by Carrier's coat; if it isn't, "holds" is the wrong word, since it's the cards' word for having a status. One clause settles it.
- **Burning heart.** The card gives two beats and a designer's reason in one line: "Every so often (every 10 ticks, so the fire never goes out)". "Every so often" is the 22-tick word everywhere else. Fix: "Every 10 ticks, its wearer sets itself alight: a few Burn on itself."
- **Downhill charm.** Its card carries the whole ruling on left-over damage, "riders" included, a design word no gear card but this one uses. Overkill axe and Overrun keep the same ruling in their numbers and say it in a line. One treatment for the three; the axe's is the one a player can read.
- **Second dawn.** "Whatever else calls for it, and nothing repeats it" is Overture coat's sentence again, with the same trouble: alone in an offer it points at a card the player may not have.

Tinder charm, Twin-knot, Halved heart and Carapace charm are as hard to read or as thin as their own *Least sure* says; nothing to add.

## Formations (`formations.md`)

The counts match the spine (13, 12, 10), and every card does what its line says. Six findings. The seat names that mean a different rule in each formation are already on the director's list.

- **The forge.** Its notes say that with nothing burning, "the foe with the most Burn" is a random foe "and that pick follows a Mark". That's the same reading round 2 ruled out for what a piece sends (see Wick, above). Mimic wand says the same rule without it ("whatever statuses the basic attacks of… add to their target"). The standard is a common, a road's first step, and it's the longest common card in the fill. - **Bloodied ground.** "Each hit it lands on a bleeding foe heals it" reads as healing the foe, as Crimson dregs does. Fix: "heals the Blade".
- **Thorn hedge.** The Anchor has "Thorns", and the next seat but one is called Thorn and sends damage back that isn't Thorns: it's "sent back", so Briar, Spite totem and anything else that reads Thorns don't read it. The card gives a player no way to know that. A different seat name would do.
- **The wheel.** "Crits more", "regains MP in fights" and "heals on it are stronger" have no size, and the notes say the first is one "the player won't see" (3.5 points). Kept word for word by its line, so it may be meant; it's the one formation whose seats a player can't size.

It's also the nearest the fill comes to saying what a thing is for, which `docs/rules.md` forbids for pieces. Raised once, in case the rule is meant to reach formations.

"A few" on a seat, "every few ticks" on The forge and "20 points more Crit chance" on Spur are under *Words used two ways*.

## Words used two ways

- **"Its HP" and "its max HP".** 17 cards say "half its HP" or "a third of its HP"; 56 say "max HP". Nearly all the 17 are in armor (8), the second weapons share (3) and the second offhands share (3). Round 1 asked for the vocabulary's words in full.
- **"Strike".** Round 1 made "extra strike" Double strike's second strike, and the spine made "strikes with its weapon" a swing-sized hit out of turn that carries the swing. Three cards use the word for a third thing, a hit that lands a second time and carries nothing: Still hand ("strikes twice… the second strike"), Tempest cloak (the same), Dragon's tooth ("strikes again"). Round 2 ruled what the last two do, not what they say. Hunting spear, Twinfall and The mountain say "lands twice" or "lands a second time". Fix: those words on the other three, and in the spine's lines for Still hand and Tempest cloak.
- **"Blow".** A hit, in "a killing blow" (round 2) and in armor's header. Any damage, a status's included, on Swan's grace ("the blow that would knock its holder out"). Last bastion uses the same sentence and doesn't say which.
- **A kill.** "Lands a killing blow" on most cards; "when its holder's hit fells a foe" on Hunter's buckler, which round 2 makes the same thing. "Comes at once" (Second thrust, Harvest scythe) and "readies its next basic attack at once" (Headsman's axe) are also one rule.
- **"Every so often".** The short cooldown word, 22 ticks, by `loot.md`. On the cards it's five things: a beat counted from the fight's start (Mender's mace); a beat whose first comes halfway (Comrade's targe, Warding bell, Idol of the hungry dark, The wheel); a beat whose first comes at once (Clear-headed charm); a wait between two firings (Second skin: "this can happen every so often"); and 10 ticks (Burning heart). No card gives the 22. Beside it, "every few ticks" is four on The forge, where Kindled robes, the same rule on a robe, says "every 4 ticks".
- **"A few".** Today's scale already has two sizes: a passive's few, and an ability's, which is double. The fill adds a flat 2 on a seat, half the ability's count (Hail), three fifths (Frost fist) and half again the passive's (Frost armor). So "a few" is 2 to 6 stacks at one Attack, and the card can't tell a player which. One pair is worth aligning now: Curse-book's "a few" on an ability's hit is the ability's count, and Jolt spear's and Ragged axe's "a few" on an ability's hit is the plain one.
- **"High" and "medium" on a card.** "It has Thorns, high" (Urchin plate, Spiked targe), "Thorns, medium" (Spinebark pole), "Lifesteal, high" (Bloodsworn mail). It's the seats' convention, which has a table. On gear the stats show as values, and other cards give a figure: "adds 15 to its Thorns" (Spite totem), "Lifesteal +2%" (Tallymail), "Evasion by 35" (Stillness of mind).
- **Crit chance has four ways of being counted:** steps on the stat line (+1), "50 more Crit chance" (Bloodscent knife), "20 points more Crit chance" (Spur), and fractions ("four in five", "about half", "doubled"). One way on the cards.
- **"The longest cooldown" and "the longest cooldown left"** are one word apart and mean different abilities (the slowest; the most wait left). Floe charm and Icefall's Breaker explain theirs on the card. Reveille and Frozen tome don't.
- **"A random foe"** on Overrun isn't the vocabulary's (see the second weapons share).
- **"Holder" and "wearer".** Both offhands shares say "holder"; weapons, armor and amulets say "wearer". Harmless, and one word would be tidier.
- **"A moment".** One tick on Twinfall ("a moment later"); no time at all on Evensong staff ("cast in a moment, with no wind-up").
- **"The volley".** Volley bow's arrows at every other foe; Barbed volley, an ability; and on Open season's card, every ally's next basic attack at once. A Ranger with the bow in that formation has both.
- **Four afflictions and five.** Malison, Wildmagic wand and Carrier's coat count Burn, Chill, Shock, Bleed and Sunder. Tinder charm, Festering idol and Grimoire of plagues list the first four. The spine's lines say four, so it's the design; a player who learns the five from Malison will expect Sunder on the others.
- **Names.** None is used twice. These are close enough to mislead:
  - *A wearer's passive on a piece it wears:* Briar totem (the Bramble knight's Briar), Bloodlust draught (the Vampire's Bloodlust).
  - *One unit can wear both:* Rubble plate and Rubble shield; Lamprey claws and Lamprey mail; Tithe wand and Tithing leathers; Challenger's token and Challenger's plate.
  - *One kind:* Orb of the deep and The deep well.
  - *A version and a piece of its class:* Plague and Grimoire of plagues.
  - *Three of one word:* Thunderhead maul, Thunderhead totem and Storm front's Thunderhead seat. The versions share lists the three saints on one Cleric and the three pyres.

## The fifteen least sure, a few of the best among them

Eleven doubts, then four I'd show as the fill at its best. The doubts are about what a player reads and whether the entry holds together, not strength.

1. **Venom vial** (vial, rare). The card says a raise and then says it comes to nothing, and its sum holds only at today's value of Bleed's close.
2. **The standard** (formation, common). A road's first step in three colours, on the longest common card in the fill, in a word ("rider") no player has been given.
3. **Huntmaster's quiver** (the Ranger's mythic). The biggest resize in the fill, and its card contradicts the ruling on Barbed volley.
4. **Lightning-glass** (amulet, rare). One word and one percent from Thunderhead maul, on three classes that wear both.
5. **Overrun** (great weapon, rare). "A random foe" promises a Mark will steer it, and round 2 rules it won't. `builds.md` wanted it aimed at the weakest.
6. **Heart of the saint** (relic, legendary). Its sentence stops short of Flagellant's, the two have different floors on one Cleric, and its own share doubts it ever holds a Paladin low.
7. **Bell of the hour** (bell, rare). An engine's keystone whose one sentence reads the wrong way round to a player.
8. **Parrying spear** (spear, rare). A keystone that's blank for two of its three wearers without another piece, with no Evasion in its own steps.
9. **Ember heart** (amulet, rare). Whether its wearer is on fire while it "holds" the fire decides three other cards.
10. **Last bastion** (plate, legendary). "A blow" may or may not cover a Burn pulse, and the wearer it saves ends the fight at 1 HP, which carries.
11. **Urchin plate** (plate, uncommon). An uncommon that's blank for three of five wearers, sized by a word and not a figure, and already on the director's list beside Spiked targe.

The best, as cards a player understands at once and that change what a unit is for:

12. **Sarissa** (spear, legendary). One sentence, a picture, and steps that are exactly what it needs.
13. **Orb of the deep** (orb, rare). "Damage that would reach its holder's HP comes out of its MP instead, point for point." Nothing to explain, and it turns a stat into another stat's job.
14. **High pyre** (the Pyromancer's Pyre B). A version that changes when a spell fires, by a number the player can read on the foe.
15. **Matador's cape** (cloak, uncommon). "Each time its holder dodges a hit, it Taunts." A first step that's seen the first time it fires and gives a class a job it didn't have.

## What I couldn't check

- **Whether a size is seen.** I read the numbers each entry gives and didn't work them against a fight. Where an entry says "seen", I took its sum.
- **The interface.** Several findings rest on what a player is shown (stats as values, a stack word or its count, how Crit chance is printed). I read `docs/` for that and not `ui/`.
- **The engines.** Whether two halves meet is the engine reader's.
- **Today's cards.** Where an entry says "kept: today's card", I didn't compare it against `content/core-set/`, beyond one search for the word "touch".
- **Budgets and counts** I checked by hand and then with `npx tsx steps/loot/fill-check.ts`, which only reads: no step is off its budget and no name is used twice. It also reads this file and the brief as shares and reports them for having no end heading; that's the check's, not a fault in the fill.

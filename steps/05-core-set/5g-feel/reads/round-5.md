# Feel read, round 5

A cold read of twelve packs from `out/feel-r5/` (336 rogue fights at heat 0 behind them), tick by tick. Packs and ticks are the evidence; the summary says how often.

## Per pack

**Pack 1: Toll Gate (fight, act 1), won t51.** Three bandits at 6,080. Anthem went to the Cleric at t5, t17.8 and t30.8, and its hasted basics at Might 7.8 to 15 (1,479 to 1,915) made the healer the top damage dealer: 49%, all basics. Three Frost lances built Chill 6.2, 10.5, 12; the shatter at t40.3 (2,280) killed Bandit C. Cure ×5 mostly overhealed (548 into a 274 hole, t9). Nobody fell below 962/2,000. Safe and flat; decided from the first Anthem.

**Pack 2: Wolf Pack (fight, act 1), lost t10.** Not a fight. The party arrived with the Vampire at 0 HP, the Stormcaller at 65, the Paladin at 491, against seven wolves at 5,840. Smite began t4.8 (6-tick wind-up); the Paladin died t7.9 to eight bites of 81 before it landed. One Chain lightning at t9, then the Stormcaller died t9.4. Two wolves never acted. Decided in the previous room.

**Pack 3: Ogre Bridge (elite, act 1), won t45.** Stand fast at t4: every hit for 45 ticks landed on the Knight. The Ogre's basic swing has a 6-tick wind-up and reads as a slow club; Flatten at t23.1 hit 1,155 and stunned the Knight 8 ticks. Bleed was 55% of the Ranger's damage; three decaying Bleed lines after a Gale spear (t30.7: 554, 369, 246) read perfectly. The Knight did 5% and ended at 874/6,000, the only bar that moved. An elite that felt like a regular fight with a Stun in it.

**Pack 4: The Chief's Return (boss, act 1), lost t71.** The Hoplite entered with 200 of 2,000 MP: Aspis (Aegis, the Crush counter) and Spear thrust never castable. Backstab crit 6,672 on the Ogre at t7.5, the best single hit of the twelve. Then Crush three times, three deaths: t13.9 Bramble knight CRIT 7,361 "(guarded)" from 4,173; t44.1 Assassin 4,732 from 3,000; t70.3 Hoplite 4,142 from 2,161. The 8-tick wind-up reads and nothing answers it. From t48 to t70 the Hoplite stood alone and swung once (t51.9).

**Pack 5: Cold Ghosts (fight, act 2), won t45.** Resist-75 ghosts against three casters. Firebolt 507, Fireball 297 to 446, Pyre 1,699 for 1,200 MP and 8 ticks (t32.3), while untyped Burn pulsed up to 2,022 every two ticks: Burn was 68% of the party's damage. Ghost hits of 979 took half a caster; the Hexer died t19.6. The Paladin was Hidden 24 ticks, then "takes 0 dark IMMUNE" (t28.7, t40.7) from two 30% resists: untouched all fight. Weaken read (979 to 770); Sunder 10.4 and Chill 2.8 showed nothing.

**Pack 6: Golem Hall (elite, act 2), lost t52.** Two golems at 68,460 (+389%). The Geomancer entered at 396 HP and died t3.7; the Frostweaver, full at 2,500, was one-shot at t6.1 (2,563). The Monk soloed t6 to t52: Burning fist and Flow every 12 ticks, Double strike, Sunder at the cap by t19, Burn ticking 700 to 1,400, dodging four of six swings. 38,397 damage took one golem to 45%. Crust 6 against Sunder 20 cancelled with nothing to see. The Monk looked wonderful; the fight was absurd.

**Pack 7: The Reaper (boss, act 2), won t48.** The best fight. Bats dead by t6.1 having dealt 318. Reap at t9.7 hit all three for 1,321 to 1,701; Death bell at t17.7 put Doom on the Knight, paying 3,900 at t27.7 (5,115 to 1,215). Fire arrow's Burn 27.6 did 12k; Shock on the boss reached 30.9, 2,469 an action. Final Reap t47.4: Ranger 1,701 twice to 98/3,500, and the boss died the same tick. Soul jar gave +500 MP to all per kill, so MP was never a thought. Every boss move came and mattered without killing.

**Pack 8: Ghoul Feast (fight, act 3), lost t102.** Four ghouls at 33,120 (+314%). The Vampire (140 MP) and Bramble knight (145 MP) could not afford Blood gift+, Frenzy+, Regrowth+ or Bramble lash all fight. Thicket+ held every ghoul on the Bramble knight for 54 ticks at 608 a hit; each hit returned 600 Thorns and Bleed, 98% of its 39,827. Blood drain+ (100 MP, 3,000 to 4,800) was 81% of the Vampire. Ghoul D fell t59.8; three ghouls began Raise, one landed (30% back), two "fail (no target)". Valhalla at t73 revived the Bramble knight at 600 HP, dead by t78.9. Bloodlust lit at t99, two ticks before the Vampire died.

**Pack 9: Honour Guard (elite, act 3), won t104.** Four Soldiers at 34,830 hitting 828. Frost lance+ carries 12 Chill, the cap, so every cast is a shatter: 2,557 plus 6,630 plus Stun, eight times (t6.4 to t101.3), 61% of the party's damage. The Knight's Static coil amulet did 25,574 (30%) with no action by anyone. The Knight dipped once to 1,720 (t70.8) and was at 4,475 two ticks later. Stand fast+ every 9 ticks (10 casts) was its rhythm; Clarity went to the Knight itself 7 of 10 times. Fortify on units never hit (t10.2, t93.2). The Soldiers' 18 Shield up Taunts showed nothing. Never in doubt.

**Pack 10: The Silent One (boss, act 3), won t141.** 180,180 HP. Quiet silenced all three for 16 ticks four times: 64 of 141 ticks on basics only, and the first lost casts I saw (t107.6). Shield bash's Stun held Quiet three times ("Quiet waits"), the one counterplay that read. Tolling ×7 took its Might to 10; its swing went 1,624 to 1,752. The Knight never fell below 1,401; Rally+ healed 3,000 to 4,095 every 18 ticks. Shock pinned at 40 for 3,200 an action. The Knight's basic: 39. Siphon printed "+148 → 3,000" about twenty times. Decided by t40, over at t141.

**Pack 11: The Packmaster (boss, act 1), lost t105.** Hoplite at 230 MP, Stormcaller at 25 MP and 800 HP. Wolves dead by t28. Crush t13.2: 3,160 "(weakened, guarded)" took the Hoplite 3,567 to 407; t47.7 and t79.4 killed the Stormcaller and Hoplite. Weaken plus Guard did show here (3,160 against pack 4's 7,361). Enfeeble ×7 kept Sunder at 20 and the Hoplite's basic was 1,942 before and after. The Hexer's basics built Chill 2.8 to 12 over 30 ticks and shattered t53.3 for 3,240 plus Stun: a slow build that paid. Then the Hexer alone t79 to t104, 498 a swing against 15k HP, 4,500 MP unspent.

**Pack 12: Ghoul Pit (elite, act 2), lost t67.** Four ghouls at 34,239 hitting 1,174. The Bard entered at 2,500/4,500, buffed itself, took two hits to 152 by t5.5, died t16.8 with Thunder chord begun and never landed. Malison put Burn and Shock 11.2 on all four at once; Enfeeble put Sunder 20 (its cap) in one cast. The Hexer died t30 and its statuses ticked 35 more ticks: 53% of the party's damage from a dead unit. The Monk soloed t30 to t66, dodging 11 of 14, killed Ghoul C at t45.5 (raised by t50), and cast its last Flow at 10 MP.

## Across all packs

### 5. Rhythm
Openings are a volley: head starts put every slot-one and slot-two ability on the field by t3 to t7 (pack 7 killed both bats by t6; pack 12 fired every ability by t12). The middle is cooldown loops; the dead stretches are the tails of lost fights (pack 4 t48 to t70, one swing; pack 11 t79 to t104) and silences (pack 10 t84 to t100). Wind-ups that read: Crush's 8 ticks (too well, since nothing answers it), the Ogre's club, Death bell's ten-tick Doom. The 1-tick wind-ups are just a line, and Smite's 6 never landed in pack 2. Tier shapes don't hold: at normal knobs regular and elite fights were both 45 to 51 ticks (packs 1, 3, 5); at act 3 knobs a regular fight was 102 (pack 8) and an elite 104 (pack 9). Summary: act 2 bosses average 39 ticks against act 2 elites at 70; act 3 regular fights 58, band 26 to 98.

### 6. Abilities
Firing enough to matter: Stand fast (4 to 10 a fight), Gale spear (3.6 to 8), Barbed arrow (3 to 7), Burning fist and Flow (3 to 5), Enfeeble (2.5 to 7), Cure (3 to 6), Blood drain (4 to 8). Once or never: Valhalla (0.3 a fight; pack 8 t73 bought six ticks), Raise+ (never), Pyre (0.6, first t26), Tremor (0.5, first t31), Spear thrust (0.69, first t24.5), Unmake (0.17), Stillness (first t37). Third slots start uncharged on the longest cooldowns, and a 45-tick fight ends before they come round twice. Too big for their price: Frost lance+ (300 MP, 13 ticks, an instant 6,630 shatter and Stun), Blood drain+ (100 MP for 3,000 to 4,800). Too small: Pyre and Fireball against anything resistant; the Knight's 39-point basic. Tempo: the Monk acts every two to four ticks; the Hoplite every twenty (three basics in 70 ticks, pack 4), which reads as absence when alone; the Knight's lines are a re-Taunt metronome.

### 7. Statuses
- **Burn**: the game's biggest damage source (23% share over 177 fights; 68%, 56%, 55% in packs 5, 6, 12). A legible ramp with visible decay. Untyped, so it ignores resist 75 and 25: the only thing that works on ghosts and bosses.
- **Bleed**: the most legible status, a line per hit taken, decaying in threes after multi-hits (pack 3 t30.7). Shape right.
- **Shock**: Gale spear's triple hit puts 22 to 31 on in one cast; cap 40 reached (pack 10); 2,400 to 3,200 a boss action. The Static coil's 25,574 (pack 9) was an amulet, not a kit.
- **Chill**: the Hexer's build to 12 over 30 ticks (pack 11) is the right shape. Frost lance+'s 12 in one cast (pack 9) is a nuke pinned at the cap. Under 6 stacks it shows nothing (packs 5, 8).
- **Sunder**: invisible. 138 fights, no moment where a number visibly changed (pack 11: seven Enfeebles, 1,942 before and after; pack 6: 20 Sunder against 6 Crust, nothing).
- **Might**: reads when big (pack 1's Cleric; pack 8's War banner 7). Might 1 from the Siren shell is a noise line. The Silent One's 10 stacks moved its swing 8%.
- **Taunt**: the shape of packs 3, 8, 9, 10. Works. On foes (Soldiers' Shield up, 18 casts) it shows nothing.
- **Guard**: about 20% off ghoul hits (608 vs 743, pack 8), invisible against Crush (7,361 "guarded"). **Weaken**: reads every time (979 to 770; Crush to 3,160). **Stun**: reads and matters (Flatten, shatters, Shield bash holding Quiet).
- **Silence**: a long lock (64 of 141 ticks, pack 10) that barely dented output because statuses kept ticking.
- **Regen, Clarity, Shield**: quiet and right; Vanguard's 1,200 soak reads at every start. **Doom**: the best status here, a countdown that paid. **Aegis**: never appeared (two Hoplites without MP). **Hidden** plus two dark resists read IMMUNE (pack 5), which looks wrong. **Crust** (23 applications) and **Bloodlust** (two ticks before death) showed nothing.

### 8. Mana
Who ran short: whoever carried MP in from the previous room. Hoplite 200 and 230 (packs 4, 11), Stormcaller 25 and 395 (packs 11, 2), Vampire 140, Bramble knight 145 (pack 8), Monk 310 (pack 12): six of twelve packs had a member unable to afford its second or third ability all fight, and twice that was the Crush counter. Who never spent: the Valkyrie pinned at 3,000 by Siphon and Soul jar (packs 7, 10), the Cleric at 4,700 of 5,000 (pack 1), the Hexer at 4,500 while its allies had none (pack 11). Mana changed what a class did only by removal. Clarity showed as +120/+160 pulses, Refrain as +150 into a full bar, Siphon as a flood. Summary: Stormcaller short 17% of its time and regains 336 of 921 spent; Ranger and Cleric never short.

### 9. Classes
Valkyrie (32 to 48%; spear, Shock, big basics, heals, revives) and Ranger (50 to 60% in boss fights, 46 to 61% from statuses) play their descriptions and dominate. Knight is a wall: 5 to 8% damage, 16 to 23k taken, basics 39 to 461. Cleric overheals and was the top damage dealer by someone else's buff. Monk is a stream of lines and soloed two lost fights. Pyromancer is Burn (76% statuses); its spells read tiny. Frostweaver is the shatter. Hexer is statuses (89 to 96%), dies first (summary: 38 KOs per 100, the worst), and either detonates after death (pack 12) or spends seven Enfeebles on nothing visible (pack 11). Paladin: Judgement never fired; Hidden and IMMUNE left it untouched (pack 5). Hoplite: three or four swings a fight, Aspis never affordable. Assassin: one spectacular Backstab, then dead to Crush. Bramble knight: a hedge that hurts by being hit, with a 158 to 599 basic. Vampire: one affordable move (Blood drain+, 81%), Bloodlust too late. Stormcaller: 54% on five basics (pack 11), the shortest of MP. Geomancer: dead at t3.7, unseen.

Worst-feeling: **Bard** (pack 12: self-buff, dead t16.8; pack 1: 14% itself, its buff made the healer the DPS), **Hoplite** (packs 4, 11: a swing every 20 ticks, the Crush counter never cast), **Hexer** (passenger when others kill, invisible Sunder, first to die). Best-feeling: **Monk** (packs 6, 12), **Ranger** (packs 3, 7, 10), **Valkyrie** (packs 7, 8, 10); the Assassin's 6,672 Backstab (pack 4 t7.5) is the best single moment.

### 10. Enemies and bosses
Ogre Chief: Crush 2.42 a fight, five kills in six landings (packs 4, 11), the sixth left 407 HP; act 1 bosses won 49%. Reaper: a move every ten ticks, each mattered, none killed; the model. Silent One: a lock for 45% of the fight and no one near death. Ogre: Flatten 1.12 a fight, 1,155 plus Stun, an elite move that reads. Ice Golem: swings that one-shot casters, Crust invisible. Soldiers: 828 a hit and 18 Taunts that do nothing. Ghouls: Raise 0.3 a fight, three begin at once and two fail. Minions act before dying (97 to 100%) and deal 420 to 660 an appearance: bats dealt 318 and died by t6 (pack 7). Regions read through gimmicks (resist 75, Raise, Doom, Quiet) more than numbers; the Ice Pass is the casters' nightmare, the Crypt is where the drama is.

### 11. Drama
Close calls: Ranger at 98/3,500 as the Reaper fell (pack 7 t47.4), Knight at 874 (pack 3). Comebacks: none; a down member stayed down, except Valhalla's six-tick zombie (pack 8). One-shots: Crush ×5, golem swings on two casters (pack 6), the Bard in two hits (pack 12 t5.5). Decided in the first ten ticks: packs 2, 6, 12. Long after decided: pack 4 t44 to t71, pack 11 t79 to t105, pack 10 t40 to t141. Slogs: packs 8, 9, 10 (102, 104, 141 ticks).

## 12. The ranked list

What looked wrong, worst first:

1. **Fights arrive pre-lost.** Six of twelve packs started with a member at 0 to 800 HP or 25 to 310 MP from the previous room (packs 2, 4, 6, 8, 11, 12). Pack 2 lost at t10 with the Vampire at 0 HP at the bell; pack 6's Geomancer died t3.7 from 396 HP; packs 4 and 11's Hoplites never cast Aspis against Crush for want of 400 MP. The summary's 2 KOs a fight at act 1 bosses and 1.7 at act 2 elites carry this.
2. **Crush is a kill every time.** Pack 4 t13.9 (7,361 crit, guarded, from 4,173), t44.1 (4,732 from 3,000), t70.3 (4,142 from 2,161); pack 11 t47.7, t79.4. Five deaths in six landings; Guard shows nothing. Ogre Chief 43 appearances, 2.42 Crushes a fight, act 1 bosses 49% won.
3. **Basic swings one-shot casters at the knobs.** Pack 6 t6.1: 2,563 on a full 2,500 Frostweaver; pack 12 t5.5: two hits of 1,174 took the Bard to 152; pack 5: 979 is half a caster. HP-2,000 classes die in two swings at +100% damage.
4. **The HP knob turns late fights into slogs.** Pack 8 (a regular fight) 102 ticks, pack 9 104, pack 10 141. Summary: act 3 regular fights 58 ticks (26 to 98); act 2 bosses 39 against act 2 elites 70: the tiers invert.
5. **Frost lance+ is an instant shatter.** 12 Chill, the cap, in one cast: 6,630 plus Stun eight times in pack 9, 61% of the party's damage, for 300 MP every 13 ticks. Enfeeble+ pins Sunder at 20 the same way (pack 12 t7).
6. **Sunder is invisible.** 138 fights, zero legible moments; pack 11's seven Enfeebles with a 1,942 basic before and after; pack 6's 20 Sunder against 6 Crust.
7. **Untyped Burn makes resistance meaningless and spells pointless.** Pack 5: Firebolt 507, Fireball 297, Pyre 1,699 for 1,200 MP, Burn pulses to 2,022; Burn 68%. Summary: Burn the largest share in the game, 23% over 177 fights.
8. **Third slots fire once or never.** Valhalla 0.3 a fight (pack 8 t73: a 600-HP revive dead by t78.9), Raise+ never, Pyre 0.6, Tremor 0.5, Spear thrust 0.69, Unmake 0.17.
9. **Fights run long after they're decided.** Pack 4 t44 to t70 (one swing), pack 11 t79 to t104 (498 a swing at 15k HP), pack 10 t40 to t141 (nobody under 1,401).
10. **Gear passives swamp kits.** Static coil 25,574, 30% of pack 9; Siphon pinning the Valkyrie at 3,000 MP (pack 10, about twenty "+148 → 3,000" lines); Soul jar +500 MP to all per kill (packs 7, 9); Siren shell's Might 1 a noise line every basic.
11. **Enemy Taunt shows nothing, Ghoul Raise looks absurd.** Soldiers' Shield up 18 casts in pack 9 (3.8 a fight, their only move); three ghouls begin Raise together, two "fail (no target)" (pack 8 t59.8, pack 12 t46).
12. **The Silent One is a lock, not a threat.** 64 of 141 ticks silenced, seven Tollings that moved its swing from 1,624 to 1,752, Knight never under 1,401 (pack 10).
13. **Hidden plus two dark resists reads IMMUNE** (pack 5 t28.7, t40.7): a Paladin untouched for 45 ticks.
14. **Walls swinging for nothing.** Knight basics of 39 (pack 10), Bramble knight 158 to 215 (pack 8): a line a player stops reading.
15. **Small stacks show nothing.** Chill 2 to 3 from Frozen heart (pack 8, 17 applications), Chill 2.8 (pack 5), Crust (pack 6, 23 applications).

What looked right:

- The Reaper (pack 7): Reap at t9.7 across all three, Doom paying 3,900 ten ticks after its bell, the boss down at t47.4 with the Ranger at 98 HP. Every move came, every move mattered, none killed.
- Bleed and Burn read tick by tick and pay in proportion (pack 3 t30.7; pack 12's Burn carrying on 35 ticks after the Hexer died).
- Taunt shapes a fight visibly (packs 3, 8, 9, 10): the wall's bar is the one that moves.
- Weaken reads in the numbers (979 to 770; Crush 7,361 in pack 4 against 3,160 weakened in pack 11).
- Shield bash's Stun holding Quiet (pack 10 t39.3, t74.5, t130.7): counterplay you can see.
- The Monk's tempo: a line every two ticks, soloing packs 6 and 12.
- The Hexer's slow Chill build to a 3,240 shatter (pack 11 t53.3).
- Backstab's 6,672 crit and Phalanx's 4,139 (pack 4): the big hitters hit big.
- Vanguard's Shield soaking the first 1,200 (packs 3, 7, 9, 10) and Flatten's 1,155 plus Stun (pack 3 t23.1): openings and elite moves that read without killing.
- Act 1 fights at normal knobs run 45 to 51 ticks with everything landing (packs 1, 3, 5).

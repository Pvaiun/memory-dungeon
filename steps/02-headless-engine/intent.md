# Step 2 intent

Gathered in the step's session, Oct 15, 2026. The brief's questions about fight logs were withdrawn after the director pointed out that they don't shape the engine. These items come from the conversation that followed.

- [lean] Fight logs are mostly for the AI, to measure how combat turns out, and maybe for debugging. The director doesn't expect to read them: they're dense.
- [idea] Players may eventually see combat logs. That would be a separate feature with its own UI, showing a share of what the engine records.
- [firm] The rule language's building blocks (effects, triggers, flags and the rest) are placeholders. The director hasn't had input on these concepts.
- [lean] Perk picks are the only choice in a run that the player makes and that can change the outcome, as the game is planned now.
- [lean] Don't make perk picks the only input the engine allows. If other inputs turn out to matter to the experience, it should be possible to add them later, elsewhere in a run.
- [firm] The director wants input at the start of designing the battle system, not to review it once it's built. (Added Oct 16, after the first design draft built the battle system ahead of that input.)
- [idea] Simulate every perk choice. With only 3 or 4 picks per run and 3 perks per pick, there may be few enough paths to try them all. That would also show how much the picks change the outcome.
- [lean] It will be very hard to tell which perks are the right picks without a human involved.

## Left to the designer

- How a run moves forward through its fights and perk picks.
- The random-number generator.
- How much of the schema's rule language to build now. The director asked what this means, and it was explained in the session; the building blocks being placeholders bears on it.
- The fight log's format.

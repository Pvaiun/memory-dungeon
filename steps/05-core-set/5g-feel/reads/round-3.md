# Feel read, round 3

A cold read of the twelve packs in `out/feel-r3/`, with the glossary and the summary (316 fights). Ticks are the packs'.

## Per pack

**1. Toll Gate (fight, won t46).** A grind: three bandits at 5,160 fall at t35, t42.7, t45.3; nobody dropped below 1,175/2,000. The Cleric was the top damage dealer (47%) with light basics carrying the Bard's Might (1,915 at t42.1); its Cures were half overheal (t9.5). All three Anthems went to the Cleric. The Frostweaver's Chill (6.2, t12.9) showed only through Brittle (903 vs 598). Flat; Last rites never mattered.

**2. Bandit Horde (fight, lost t39).** Lost before t0: the Valkyrie arrived dead, the other two at 30%, against five bandits at 6,200. The Monk did 86%: one Burning fist (t9.6) put Burn 11.8 on Bandit A, which did 3,153 over 16 ticks, more than the fists. The Paladin swung twice and cast nothing (Smite ready t19, dead t18.3); Judgement and a "judgement" amulet faced foes with no buffs. An execution.

**3. Twin Ogres (elite, won t71).** The best fight here. Ogres wind up 6 ticks to swing, which reads heavy; the Hexer (arrived 1,313/2,000) died to the first two (t15–16). Both Flattens landed at t26 (1,294 + Stun on the Ranger), "Fire arrow waits", and t26–35 is dead air with only Clarity lines on a Ranger at full MP. The Ranger's Bleed (1,248 a hit taken) and Burn 18.4 (1,011 fading to 324) were 62% of its damage; the Vampire finished at 200 MP. A close call at t38 (Ranger 573/3,000), a kill by a Bleed tick at t55.2.

**4. The Packmaster (boss, lost t115).** Titled Packmaster, fielding the Ogre Chief. The great moment: Enfeeble's Weaken landed (t9.4) inside Crush's 8-tick wind-up and the Hoplite took 2,475 (t14.4) instead of about 3,900, a kill. Then the Stormcaller, arrived at 800 HP and 255 MP, died to a wolf (t23.7) having cast nothing; Crush one-shot the Hexer through Weaken and Guard (2,748, t51.5); and the Hoplite fought alone for 63 ticks, swinging every ~24, with 13- and 18-tick gaps of nothing (t64–77, t92–110). Chill from the Hoplite's armour held the boss to nine actions in 115 ticks; no line says why. Aegis (t31.2) was never spent.

**5. Bat Roost (fight, won t46).** A wall that plays itself: Thicket+ every 11 ticks, every bite 219, every bite answered by 300 Thorns and Bleed stacks (2.1 → 16.8; 2,016 a hit at t46). The knight never went under 4,787/6,500 and got a 3,250 shield it didn't need (t28.9). Bramble lash missed all three casts (t14.8, t29.9, t44.8): 450 MP for zero. At HP +269% the fight still ended on time with nobody threatened.

**6. Golem Hall (elite, lost t106).** Two golems at 54,600 with no abilities; the party dealt 70,000 and lost. Eight buff lines at t0 before a swing, then 36 Regen lines. The Knight took 13,390 of 17,757, died to a crit (t55.9), was raised at 550 HP "ready" (t58.4), taunted at once (t59.4) and died again (t68.1): 1,200 MP for ten ticks. Garrote's Silence on foes with no abilities (×3) did nothing. Crust cut hits by two thirds (Knight 602 at t6, 210 at t46.3) with no bracket to say so. Backstab crit 4,224 (t20.1) was the highlight.

**7. The Reaper (boss, won t123).** Bats dead by t15, then 108 ticks of one loop: Thicket, lash, Anthem+, drain, Bard swing. Three Death bells (t13, t58.5, t103.5) each paid 3,500 ten ticks later on a 6,500 knight who was full again within ten. The Reaper's swing did 560 weakened-and-guarded while Shock 40 (one Thunder chord+ gave 35.4 at t27.1; the second pinned 40) cost it 3,200 each action (t60.6): it hurt itself five times harder than it hurt the party. Chill 12 from the Bard's weapon held all fight. Never in doubt, 43 ticks over length.

**8. Sparks (fight, won t21).** A volley: Blitz Haste, three wisps under 40% at t3, Wisp D dead without acting (t6.9). Zaps of 588 lightning through Armor 4 put the Paladin at 921/3,375 by t16.5, the only tension. Pressure point 3,975 one-shot the last wisp (t20.1). The Paladin cast nothing again (Smite+ would have landed t21.4).

**9. Honour Guard (elite, lost t38).** Four Soldiers at 38,340 (153,000 HP) against a party arriving with MP 100 and 145. One Burning fist+ (t8) put Burn 38.2 on Soldier C: 13,413 damage, 31% of the party's, most of it after the Monk died (t8.2, one crit at 1,113 HP). All four Soldiers Taunted within two ticks (t11.9–14.1): four Taunts cancel. Smite+ began t14.7, the Paladin died t18.1; third pack, zero casts. The Vampire alone from t18, Bloodlust visible (heals 1,382 and 1,140 at t29–31), zapped 855 at a time to death.

**10. The Silent One (boss, lost t249).** Three times a boss's length. The Bramble knight (arrived 2,817/6,000, 135 MP) taunted at t5 and was dead at t19.2. Quiet every ~27 ticks for 16 of Silence: silenced 60% of the fight; Valhalla was lost to it (t71.8, 1,200 MP) and so was the Rally that would have saved the Valkyrie (t126.4). Then the Vampire alone for 121 ticks at 355 a basic, taking the boss from 62% to 51%, kept alive by Bloodlust drains (1,861 → 6,000 at t227.2), with ten-tick gaps of nothing (t101–111, t235–245). Enrage at t201 and a 4,806 crit (t247) ended it. Decided at t19.

**11. The Ogre Chief (boss, won t79).** The Pyromancer's fight: Burn 13 → 22, topped up every 14 ticks, 700–1,200 every 2 ticks, 61% of the party's damage; the boss died to a Burn tick. Crush one-shot the Geomancer from full through Armor 8 (3,793, t17.3; Earthen shell ready t23) and the Hoplite from 2,681 (4,232, t47.6). The Hoplite swung once in 47 ticks (t13). The Pyromancer survived solo on two misses (t35, t67) and an Aegis (t51). Thrilling to watch, decided by dice.

**12. The Reaper's Court (boss, lost t81).** DOOM on 2,000-HP units is a death sentence with a countdown: the Cleric (bell t12.2, dead t22.2) and the Pyromancer (t70 → t80) died to 3,500 at near-full HP; the Hoplite was left at 121 (t51.1). Burn 18 → 27.3 was 51% of the party's damage; Pyre 5,919 landed at t44. The Hoplite died with Aegis up to a 650 guarded hit (t72): the "big blow" threshold is invisible. Regen at t0 was 30 lines of noise.

## Across all packs

**5. Rhythm.** Regular fights breathe (packs 1 and 5 at t46; summary means 42–51); pack 8's 21 ticks is a volley. Openings are a build in packs 1, 3, 5 (basics to t8, then abilities) and a buff dump in packs 6 and 9 (eight status lines at t0). Elites and bosses share one shape, minions dead by t15 then a sponge, and both run 70–130 ticks with a tail after the decision (pack 4: 63; pack 10: 230). Dead stretches come from Stun plus a foe wind-up (pack 3 t26–35), a lone slow swinger (pack 4) or Silence (pack 10); pile-ups from multi-hit abilities and Refrain (pack 6 t7.7: nine lines). Foe wind-ups pay off: Crush's 8, Death bell's 6 then DOOM's 10, the Ogres' 6-tick swing are beats you watch. Party wind-ups mostly don't: "begins X (lands t+1)" has nothing behind it, and the real ones (Smite 8, Pyre 8) are where casters die mid-cast (pack 9 t14.7–18.1).

**6. Abilities.** Short, cheap skills carry: Blood drain (3.45 casts a fight), Anthem (3.2), Thicket (2.9), Gale spear (2.8), Burning fist (2.7), Barbed arrow (2.4). The long ones barely exist: Valhalla 0.21 a fight (first t66), Raise 0.14 (t67), Unmake 0.6 (t62), Tremor 0.7, Thunderstorm 0.29, Fire arrow 0.73 (t36), Pyre 0.58 (t44), Spear thrust 1.09 (t40); in a 42-tick fight they never come. Too big: one Thunder chord+ pins Shock at 40 (pack 7); one Burning fist+ worth 13,413 (pack 9); Crush and DOOM against 2,000–3,000 HP (packs 11, 12). Too small for the cost: Fireball, 800 MP for 637 + Burn 7 on a boss (pack 11 t34); Aspis (item 12 below). Tempos that read: the Bramble knight's 11-tick Thicket, the Pyromancer's 14-tick Firebolt, the Hoplite's 24–48-tick swing. The Paladin has none.

**7. Statuses.** Burn: the biggest damage source (21% where applied), a ramp with upkeep that reads perfectly on a boss (pack 11) and a nuke from one cast (38.2, pack 9). Bleed: legible and fair, big hits on a bleeding target (pack 5: 2,016), 17%. Shock: good on minions (419 a wolf action, pack 4), pinned at 40 from one cast on a boss (pack 7). Weaken: the best-feeling status, in brackets and decisive (pack 4). Sunder: visible as bigger numbers (971 vs 641, pack 3). Chill: invisible as a slow, yet it held two bosses to an action every 12 ticks (packs 4, 7); only Brittle makes it show. Taunt and Guard: the knights' rhythm; Guard is a wall (219 bites, pack 5). Stun: reads ("Fire arrow waits"), but a Stun at 1% boss HP (pack 3 t70.1) is nothing. Silence: a lock on the party (pack 10), nothing on foes (pack 6). DOOM: the best boss status, a visible countdown, but a fixed 3,500 against 2,000 HP. Clarity: often on full allies (pack 3) or the caster itself (pack 6 t43.1). Aegis: blocked 1,346 and 0 (packs 11, 12) by a rule nobody can see. Haste: never visibly changed anything. Crust: 50 applications, no bracket.

**8. Mana.** The Vampire ran dry in four of six packs (3: 200; 7: 540; 9: 10; 10: 5) while handing Clarity to a Ranger at 3,000/3,000 (pack 3). The Pyromancer went 2,730 → 250 in 65 ticks and stopped casting (pack 12). Never spent: the Paladin (0 MP in three packs), the Geomancer (pack 11), the Cleric (300 of 3,620, pack 12). The summary's "short of MP" (7% at most) hides this: carry-over empties MP between fights, so fights start dry rather than running dry. Refrain was the one mana loop that showed (pack 7, +315 a swing). Mana changed what a class did only for the Vampire and the Pyromancer.

**9. Classes.** Pyromancer: dominant (53% share in the summary, 82–90% in packs 11–12, two thirds from Burn); it sets the boss on fire and everyone else watches. Bramble knight: its description to the letter (67% from Thorns and Bleed), a self-playing wall when healthy (pack 5), dead in two swings when it arrives hurt (pack 10). Vampire: drain, heal, Bloodlust, the fullest kit here, starved of MP. Ranger: 62% from statuses (pack 3), reads like a ranger. Monk: one cast decides a fight (pack 9). Valkyrie: Shock and Rally; Valhalla once in five fights. Bard: a buff machine; its damage is Shock's cap. Hexer: 3–10% damage, dies most (39 KOs per 100, the highest), but its Weaken was the best play in the set (pack 4). Knight: 9–14% share; Taunt, die, revive, Taunt, die (pack 6). Hoplite: one swing a fight (pack 11), Crush bait, 34 KOs per 100. Paladin: zero casts in three packs; Judgement meets no buffs. Cleric: one heal then DOOM (pack 12). Geomancer: one-shot before its shell (pack 11). Stormcaller: 58% share when alive, dead at t23 on average, earliest of all. Frostweaver: Chill only through Brittle. Assassin: big Backstabs, Silence and Vanish wasted. Worst three by feel: Paladin (never casts), Hoplite (never swings, dies to the one move it is there for), Knight (taunts itself to death). Best three: Ranger, Vampire, Bramble knight, each with a loop a player can see and name; the Pyromancer is the strongest, not the best-feeling.

**10. Enemies and bosses.** Minions act before dying (summary 99–100%; wisps 71%) and matter only when the party arrives hurt (a wolf killed the Stormcaller, pack 4 t23.7); in boss fights they are gone by t15 (packs 4, 7, 12). Elites: the Ogres are an elite (6-tick swings, Flatten + Stun); the Ice Golems are a 109,000-HP wall with no move; the Soldiers are four 38,000-HP Taunters whose Taunts cancel. Boss moves come 2–3 times a fight (Crush 2.25, Death bell 2.5; Quiet 9) and each either kills a member outright or is healed through (pack 7). Boss basics are nothing on a wall (196–650) and 40% of a caster. Regions: the Tower Top's lightning ignores armour, which a player won't know (Paladin Armor 5, 855 a zap, pack 9); the Ice Pass has a mechanic nobody can see; the Crypt's bats and skeletons are nothing.

**11. Drama.** Close calls: the Ranger at 573/3,000 then Blood gift (pack 3 t38–49); the Pyromancer at 806 solo for 30 ticks (pack 11); the Paladin at 921 (pack 8). Comeback: one drain from 1,861 to 6,000 (pack 10 t227.2), then an enraged 4,806 crit. One-shots: Crush ×3 (packs 4, 11), DOOM ×2 (pack 12), a 915 swing on the Hexer (pack 3 t16), a crit on the Monk (pack 9 t8.2). Decided before t10: packs 2 and 9 at arrival, pack 10 at t19. Long after decided: pack 10 by 230 ticks, pack 4 by 63, pack 12's last ten ticks a countdown nobody could answer. Bosses KO 1.9–2 members a fight: a boss fight going well is one member short by the middle.

## 12. Ranked

What looked wrong, worst first:

1. **Fights decided by arrival state, not play.** The party starts dead, at 30%, or with 100 MP (packs 2, 9, 10, 4). Act 2–3 boss wins 20% and 0%, 2 KOs a boss fight: the norm.
2. **Boss signature moves one-shot.** Crush 3,793 through Armor 8 from full (pack 11 t17.3), 2,748 through Weaken + Guard (pack 4 t51.5); DOOM 3,500 kills every 2,000-HP unit it lands on (pack 12 t22.2, t80). At 2.25 Crushes and 2.5 bells a fight, that is two deaths a boss fight.
3. **Slogs after the decision.** 230 ticks (pack 10), 63 solo (pack 4), with 10–18-tick stretches of nothing (pack 4 t92–110; pack 10 t235–245). Boss 10–90%: 7–249 ticks.
4. **HP knobs turn elites into walls.** 109,000 (pack 6), 153,000 (pack 9), 191,000 (pack 10) against parties dealing 40–90,000. Act 2 elites: 50% won.
5. **The Paladin never casts.** Zero in three packs; Smite's 8-tick wind-up outlives it (pack 9 t14.7–18.1). Summary: Smite 1.2 a fight at t28; Absolve 0.86 at t37.
6. **One cast pins a status at its ceiling.** Shock 35.4 → 40 (pack 7 t27, t60), Burn 38.2 worth 13,413 (pack 9 t8), Might 15 (pack 7). Burn (21%) and Bleed (17%) are the damage the game is really about.
7. **The Hoplite swings once** (pack 11 t13; twice in 72 ticks, pack 12) and is the boss's favourite target: 34 KOs per 100.
8. **Boss pace set by invisible Chill.** Nine actions in 115 ticks (pack 4), ten in 123 (pack 7); nothing says the boss is slow or why.
9. **Clarity and Blood gift to the wrong unit.** Eight pulses on a Ranger at full MP (pack 3) while the Vampire ends at 200; the Knight's Clarity on itself (pack 6 t43.1). The Vampire is dry in 4 of its 6 packs.
10. **Revive, then Taunt, then die.** The raised Knight at 550 HP taunts at t59.4 and dies at t68.1 (pack 6); Valhalla 0.21 casts a fight, Raise 0.14.
11. **Status no-ops.** Four Soldiers Taunting at once (pack 9 t11.9–14.1); Silence on golems with no abilities (pack 6, ×3); Stun on a boss at 1% (pack 3 t70.1).
12. **Aegis is a riddle.** Spent on 1,346 (pack 11), on 0 (pack 12 t53), not on the 650 that killed its holder (pack 12 t72), never in pack 4.
13. **Bramble lash missed all three casts** against bats (pack 5): 450 MP for nothing.
14. **The boss hurts itself more than the party.** The Reaper's 560 swing costs it 3,200 Shock (pack 7 t60.6), five times a fight.
15. **Log noise.** Regen 30–36 lines (packs 6, 12), four lines a Bard swing (pack 7), nine a Gale spear (pack 6 t7.7), 1-tick "begins" lines that mean nothing.

What looked right:

- Weaken landing inside Crush's wind-up and taking it from a kill to 2,475 (pack 4 t9.4 → t14.4): the one moment that reads as play, and the brackets make it legible.
- DOOM as a countdown and the healers answering it (pack 7: bell, 3,500, Blood gift+, Chorus+, full in ten ticks).
- Burn's ramp with upkeep on a boss (pack 11); Bleed's big hits on a bleeding target (packs 3, 5); Bloodlust showing when it matters (pack 9 t29, pack 10 t227.2).
- "Fire arrow waits" (pack 3 t25.9) and "Valhalla is lost" (pack 10 t71.8): Stun and Silence read in one line.
- The Ogres' 6-tick swings and Flatten (pack 3): an elite that feels like one.
- Regular fights on length (packs 1 and 5 at t46; means 42–51), the rhythm of Thicket and Firebolt, Refrain's visible MP (pack 7), Einherjar's "ready" (pack 6 t58.4), and Enrage ending a stall (pack 10 t201): right that it exists, wrong that it was needed.

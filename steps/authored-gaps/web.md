# The web: what's kept, what's placed, and the count

The effects the web holds so far: the director's Greats (`triage.md`, `triage-formations.md`), the seats they starred, and what their answers to batches 1 to 4 added, with the count of what every effect is set off by, reads and changes. No piece is written here, and nothing is sized or slotted.

## Where the Greats sit

By what each is about. Each Great is listed once.

- **The HP bar** (paid, held low, healed): an ally's price heals another · the half-HP start with Lifesteal ×2 · held at 50% for +50% · basic attacks paid in HP · a heal on every cast · crits heal the lowest ally · a heal becomes the healed ally's next hit.
- **The Shield:** overheal becomes a Shield (on itself; on whoever its holder heals) · every other ally's Shields go to it · a broken Shield sends back ×2 · basic attacks add half the party's Shield.
- **The MP bar and the cast:** damage hits MP in place of HP · max HP and max MP trade places · every other ability free · abilities free under Clarity · Crescendo · the top-Attack ally's slowest ability readied at the start · Stunned for 10 ticks, then everything ready and Hasted.
- **The swing:** never swings, Defense ×2 · 50% as often, ×3 and a Stun · only when a foe acts · doesn't spend Might · Double strike rolls again · no offhand.
- **The dodge:** the hit lands on another foe · strikes the attacker · the next swing at once, 2 times · Heat, lost when it loses HP.
- **A stat read from somewhere else:** abilities from the top ally's Attack · Attack lent to the weakest ally · abilities from Defense · Chill from max HP.
- **The fight's moments:** the party's opening again when a foe falls · a knockout survived at 1 HP, Hidden.
- **Paid by a state:** ×2 while Hidden · more per Shock on the target (abilities; basic attacks) · more per different debuff · +50% Crit chance with every other hit halved · a status given to itself shared with the lowest ally.

**Cut from the Greats by the director:** the opening again on its wearer's own kill, for its wearer alone. It was the party's one made smaller, with the same partners, and worn together the two doubled the opening, which batch 1 cut. If the opening gets a second way to repeat, it fires on something other than a foe falling, and it's the director's to write.

**What the Greats aren't.** Three of the 41 name a status road's status (Chill once, Shock twice). None names Burn, Bleed, Sunder, the Mark, Taunt, Aggro, Guard or Thorns. Nearly all either move something to a place it doesn't normally go, or change how a unit acts at a price.

## The formations

From `triage-formations.md`: 7 Great, 19 Fine, and the seats the director starred.

- **Great:** Icefall (two lay frost, and any shatter readies the Breaker's first-slotted ability) · The cathedral (a first-slotted ability with no cooldown, fed mana by two) · The standard (two carry what the Lead's swing carries) · Glass and stone (one deals and takes ×2) · Twins (what a Twin gives itself, the other gains) · Bannerman (one never acts, and the others gain its Attack and Defense) · The shepherd (one takes the hits meant for the lowest ally).
- **Starred seats in Fine formations:** the Warden (a hit on the Anchor heals it from the Warden's max HP) · the Peak (hits land twice while every ally holds a Shield) · the Torch (a fallen foe's Burn passes to the foe with the most HP) · the Conductor and the Well (casts and blows taken feed the Soloist's mana) · the Lifeline (takes half of what the Anchor takes).
- **What they share with the gear Greats:** one unit's stats, statuses, hits or mana becoming another's. Twins is Twin-knot's rule on two seats; Bannerman sits with the pieces that stop a unit acting; the Shepherd and the Lifeline move blows the way the gear Greats move Shield.

## Added by batch 1

The director's answers, placed by what they do, with their replies folded in (`batch-1-answers.md` has their words). Words only: nothing is sized or slotted yet.

- **Mana from the foes' debuffs:** a little MP regen for each stack of any debuff on the foes. Pays most where stacks pile up fast. Ties casting to every status road.
- **The pile:** damage statuses the wearer lays (Burn, Bleed, Shock, and Chill at the director's word) go to the foe holding the most of that status, unless that foe is at its cap. Wide appliers become one foe's pile. The agent reads it as the wearer's own applications.
- **Shock from casting:** every tick of a wind-up lays Shock on every foe. Long casts become the Storm's source; it pulls against Cast speed.
- **Shock from wide abilities:** any ability that lands on more than one unit, allies included, lays Shock on every foe.
- **A dodge heals:** a share of the blow it would have been. With overheal as a Shield, a dodger builds a Shield by dodging.
- **Crits set off a hit's triggers 4 times:** the statuses it carries, Double strike's roll, anything worded "on a basic attack" or "on a hit". Never the hit's own damage, so no Lifesteal. It reaches cooldown and mana effects too.
- **More max HP as the fight starts.** Current HP stays where it is and the fight's end cuts it back, so nothing carries out. It's the exception to the content test that makes such a status heal into its room.
- **Might as the fight starts** (a Fine, the director's favourite opener): stacked each time a foe falls by the piece that repeats the opening.
- **Blur for the whole party as the fight starts.** A Blur's miss is a dodge, so the dodge Greats work on units with no Evasion.
- **Defense for wearing no armor** (an amulet). With "no offhand", a family: paid for an empty slot.
- **A Mark on the foe with the least max HP as the fight starts:** a plain part that brings the first kill early.
- **A formation where two seats swing the third:** the third takes no action of its own and basic attacks whenever either of the others does. Ruled by the director: its swings happen even on a unit that "never makes a basic attack".
- **A formation with one Shield:** one seat holds triple the Shield and draws more; the others can't hold any.

**Cut by the director:** start-of-fight effects happening 2 times (the opening keeps one multiplier: again when a foe falls, for the whole party); statuses that deal ×2 and drain faster (replaced by the pile).

**Threads the director settled:**
- **Shock has two patterns:** keep the charge high and read it, or make the foes act and spend it.
- **Shield has two patterns:** one unit holding all of it, or everyone holding some (the Peak).
- **The unit that doesn't act is there to benefit the others.** Nothing is asked to make it strong itself.
- **Cold Snap is about reaching the shatter.** Nothing pays for a foe left half-frozen.
- **No thread for debuffs held on purpose.**
- **The Toll has its payoff** (the Shield that sends back ×2).
- **The player steers who dies only through a Mark or an ability's own aim,** so nothing asks for a reward by who died beyond a Marked foe falling.
- **The plain openers come in** because the piece that repeats the opening needs them.

**Found by placing them** (nobody wrote these down):
- Party Blur, the opening again on a kill, and any "on a dodge" Great: every kill re-Blurs the party, and each Blur spent sets off a dodge.
- Crits ×4 with Chill from max HP: a wall's crits freeze.
- The two-seats-swing-the-third formation with the ×3 swing that Stuns, or with "never makes a basic attack, Defense ×2": the price costs nothing in that seat.
- The one-Shield formation with the Shield that sends back ×2 and the piece that takes every ally's Shield.
- The pile with Shock from casting: a long cast puts the whole field's charge on one foe.

## Added by batch 2

Placed by what they do, with the director's replies folded in (`batch-2-answers.md` has their words and each read). Words only: nothing is sized or slotted.

- **Extra strikes set off what a basic attack sets off:** the statuses the swing carries and anything worded "on a basic attack". The Fine "Extra strikes carry the swing's effects", a little wider, brought in because Thousand Cuts needs it. It doesn't roll Double strike again by itself: the Great does, to its own limit.
- **Chill from statuses laid on its own side:** each status its wearer lays on itself or an ally adds Chill to the foe with the most Chill. Read as its wearer's own applications, once for each ally a buff lands on, and to a random foe when none holds Chill. Cold Snap's way in without a wall.
- **Cooldowns from an ally's cast:** when another ally casts, a fixed amount comes off its own cooldowns, split evenly over the abilities it has slotted. What the two who serve a caster get for themselves, and the one thing that pays for slotting fewer.
- **Might when a foe is Stunned:** any Stun that lands, a shatter's included, whoever caused it.
- **A Stun it lands leaves Slow behind:** its wearer's own Stuns. On a boss both count toward its resistance to control, so the party's Stuns run out twice as fast.
- **HP into MP when a fight is won:** its wearer gives up half the HP it has left and gains that share of its max MP, as far as its MP has room.
- **Burn from the first-slotted ability:** on every target, allies included, at the director's word: what goes first is the player's choice, and a heal or buff slotted first burns whoever it lands on. The first thing in the web that lays Burn. The pile moves only Burn laid on foes.
- **Mana while Hidden:** MP regen for as long as a unit is Hidden. Weak until a party can hide a caster.
- **Attack for HP missing,** counted in points, not as a share, so a unit with a lot of HP gains most.
- **A formation where one pays, one raises and one gains:** the first seat pays a share of its max HP for every basic attack and ability; the second raises the first when it falls; the third gains Attack for every share of HP the party is missing. Ruled by the director, as written: the price can kill the first seat, the second raises it every time, and a fallen ally counts in full for the third ("a super risky strategy. I think we should pay it off"). It's the one thing in the game that wants a fall, an exception to the loot's rule that nothing does. After its first fall the first seat comes back, acts once and falls again while the second stands, so the third stays near its peak; the risk sits on the second seat, whose fall leaves the first down for good, and a knockout carries. The third seat's Attack is sized to pay for that.

**Left out, and the director told why:** Chill turned into Bleed (two roads' statuses on one card, which the builds ruling barred then and allows now: it comes back if the director asks for it, and Chill is on watch); a Shield when a foe is Stunned (Shield is overused); Might while Hidden (the unit's own damage, on top of ×2 while Hidden; the director agreed).

**Dropped by the director:** a formation whose seats gain Evasion, Defense and max HP when a foe falls; and their first formation for HP held low, where two seats lost HP every tick (HP carries, so a party couldn't wear it for long).

**Threads the director settled:**
- **The Storm's two patterns, in their words:** one holds the foes slow or still so the charge reaches its cap and is read; the other stands under their attacks while the charge spends itself. Nothing makes the foes act sooner: they act by themselves, and faster foes hurt the party first.
- **The swing and the cast need no link.** Every unit does both, so a link is good on every unit and a thought for none.
- **Nothing asks a player to slot fewer abilities.** One ability doesn't mean fewer casts. The cooldowns piece is the one thing that tilts that way.
- **Chill is a damage status** for the pile.
- **Thousand Cuts' piece is about its extra strikes.** Asked twice, nothing Great names Bleed: it comes from the kits and the plain pieces.
- **A kill needs no formation.** Felling foes is every party's aim, so a formation about it can only reward it or make it easier. The Great that repeats the opening already makes a formation with start-of-fight seats do something on each kill.

**Found by placing them:**
- The pile with Chill: a wearer's wide Chill gathers on the coldest foe, so foes shatter one after another.
- Chill from statuses with overheal as a Shield: every heal past full is a Shield laid, so a healer freezes foes by healing.
- Chill from statuses with Might when a foe is Stunned, on one unit: a shatter Stuns, the Stun gives Might, the Might lays more Chill.
- Cooldowns from an ally's cast, on two units: each readies the other, and only mana stops them. Beside the Conductor: its casts feed the Soloist's mana, and the Soloist's casts ready it. In the Cathedral, the Voices are readied with no mana to cast.
- Burn from the first-slotted ability in the Soloist's seat: Burn on every cast. With the pile, a wide first ability's Burn lands on one foe; with mana from the foes' debuffs, the fire helps pay for itself.
- Attack for HP missing with more max HP as the fight starts: current HP stays put, so the gap is missing HP and becomes Attack at once, and again on each kill when the opening repeats. With the knockout survived at 1 HP: Hidden, with nearly all of the bonus.
- HP into MP with damage that hits MP in place of HP: the mana is the body, so the trade costs little. It also sends its wearer into the next fight hurt, which Attack for HP missing reads.
- Burn from the first-slotted ability with Chill from statuses: Burn on an ally is a status laid on its own side, so a first-slotted buff burns an ally and chills a foe.
- The paying formation with the Great that heals an ally when another pays HP: the first seat heals the party by acting.
- Mana while Hidden with the knockout survived at 1 HP: a second wind, once a fight, out of reach.

## Gaps seen, not yet asked

Thin spots seen while placing answers: candidates for a later batch, and only one of the places cards come from (`report.md`, Next). Each has to pass the check in `report.md` (ruling 9) before it goes out.

- **Guard on gear.** Hidden from Guard has the Knight's Fortify and two versions for partners, and doesn't work on a unit that Taunts with its Guard (Taunt ends Hidden), which is how most walls gain it. Guard is a staple, so it's the plain parts' to carry, not a card; write some when the plain parts are written, for units that don't Taunt.
- **Something for Bleed and Sunder to be laid by.** Each has one piece that names it, and both pieces read or need the status, so the supply is still the kits. A second Sunder or Bleed piece needs a job first.
- **Haste** is named once, and three roads run on it. A payoff for being Hasted wasn't asked: Haste already pays by itself on every unit, so the card would have asked for what the game does.
- **The Knight and the Ranger,** when the web is fuller, at the director's offer.
- **The Shield and the cast,** **a formation for the dodge** and **a formation about heals:** each sits on an overused or watched mechanic, so none is asked until the web has grown.
- **Thorns, an Aegis, Taunt and Weaken** are named by nothing, and the kits supply all four. None has a job to ask from; the Bramble knight and the Hoplite's card (batch 4) is where the first two may turn up.
- **Mythics:** sixteen, one batch, from scratch, once the gear is placed. Upgrades stay as part of the kits, apart from the kit changes the director writes into an answer. Neither is ruled yet.

**Owed to the plain parts by batch 4:** Thorns on the plain Plate and Mail pieces, for the swing that scales from it · Guard on gear for units that don't Taunt (already owed).

**Not Chill and Burn as a shape for now** (the director offered one): Chill is on watch and has Icefall. A second two-status shape is welcome where neither status is on watch; Burn and Sunder have the least.

**Seen while writing batch 4:**
- **The opening Mark aims a loaded blow at the smallest foe.** It sits on the foe with the least max HP and takes the party's basic attacks, so a hammer's heavy swing goes there first. The damage that carries past a kill now answers it.
- **Four effects name the first-slotted ability** and stack on one cast. The card that asked for another slot was withdrawn; don't ask it again, and flag a fifth.

**Weighed for batch 4 and not asked,** with the reason, so they aren't weighed again without something new:
- **A longer wind-up for a bigger ability** (partners for the two wind-up effects). The plain answer was cut: today's loot has it (abilities wind up longer, deal more and add more stacks) and neither pile does. The wind-up effects work on any ability with a wind-up, and most on the long ones three classes hold.
- **Something for the two beside a struck ally** (the Toll's two who profit, on gear). The director has seen the shape three times as a formation and kept it only as Fine, cut the one that loaded a hammer from blows on an anvil, and withdrew the Toll's card in batch 1: the Toll has its payoff.
- **The weapon slot left empty,** after the offhand, the armor and the amulet. Coverage: a party lacks nothing without it. If the director writes it on the open card, it joins the family.
- **Something for a Stun or a Silence a boss has come to resist.** The resistance is what bounds the four effects a Stun or a Silence sets off, and it rests on stacks the player can't see.
- **Clarity from more hands.** It has partners enough: the Knight's, the Vampire's and the Frostweaver's base kits give it.
- **A formation that reads the party as a set** (what the three share or don't). Coverage, and its plain answer is a reward for matching gear, which is the first failure.
- **A formation about the early fight and the late one.** The director cut the two shapes that turned over time (seats that rotate, a party that surges and ebbs), and asked for no third counter.

# Gate 1: the loot spine

The spine is `spine.md` in this folder. Part 1 is its rules (the stock, new words and counters, the rules bent, the code support); Part 4 is the web. This page is what you need to judge it.

## Decisions for you

**1. The counts.** The spine's stock is 314 pieces against today's 272, plus the 12 plain weapons and 16 mythics as now:

| Slot | A kind holds (common · uncommon · rare · legendary) | Today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weapon, 12 kinds | 2 · 3 · 3 · 2 | 2 · 2 · 2 · 2 |
| Armor, 4 types | 3 · 4 · 4 · 3 | 3 · 3 · 3 · 3 |
| Offhand, 13 kinds | 2 · 2 · 2 or 3 · 2 (three rares on the kite shield, totem, tome) | 2 · 2 · 2 · 2 |
| Amulet | 6 · 8 · 8 · 9 | 6 · 6 · 6 · 6 |

Formations: 12 common, 14 rare, 11 legendary, and Neutral (today 11, 12, 9). Nine versions change, five mythics are rewritten, and no kit changes beyond the two you approved.

*Why:* an offer picks a slot, a class, a kind, then a piece, so the number of pieces in a kind doesn't change how often a road is offered its pieces; only the share of the kind's pieces that serve it does. More pieces buy variety (two runs reach a road by different pieces) at the price of each piece coming round less: a weapon piece a third less often than today, an armor rare a quarter less. The extra go where the roads are many (every weapon kind is worn on four to six roads, every armor type on six or seven) and on the amulet, the one kind everyone wears, which is where engines that cross classes put their keystones.

*The other way:* keep weapons at 2 · 2 · 2 · 2 and armor at 3 · 3 · 3 · 3 (about 260 pieces). Every piece is met a third more often, no road loses a step it has, and each kind serves fewer of its wearers' roads with a piece of their own. I'd keep the spine's: you asked for too much over too little, and the first steps that have to show in act I sit on weapons.

**2. What `builds.md` says that the spine shows doesn't hold, or holds only by an assumption.**
- **Malison's Bleed.** The approved change replaces its "one more stack of each"; it didn't say whether its few Bleed stay. The spine keeps them: that keeps Thousand Cuts' "four base kits open wounds" true and gives Malison something alone. Confirm or drop.
- **"Five engines can start in act I."** Four can (red rain, held charge, reaping, brink). The lead's colour needs a rare formation or a legendary amulet, which act I offers about one run in eight.
- **Some keystones are rarely offered,** even with two or three homes each. On their own roads' parties, by the end of act II: the frozen gate 18% from pieces (42% with the Frostweaver's version), the goad 21% (32%), the riposte 29%, the crescendo 36%. Every colour and the emptied quiver are 16% and 14% from pieces, and most runs that hold the Hexer or the Ranger have them anyway from their kits and versions. None of these is a design fault: each is an anchor's, a dancer's or one class's engine, as `builds.md` writes them. Say if any should be met more; the lever is putting it on a common seat or an amulet.

**3. Two facts about the game that the counts rest on, both outside the loot.**
- **The offer rarity doesn't rise by act.** `loot.md` says later acts roll more rares and legendaries; the config rolls every act's regular spoils on one table (35 / 32 / 27 / 6), and only the shop's legendaries and the formations' rarity rise. So act I offers as many rares as act II, and the spine puts first steps on rares as well as uncommons. Either `loot.md`'s line is rewritten to say so, or the offer roll gets a table by act (the balance pass's call). If it does, the first steps on rares become act II's.
- **Training offers every option, not three.** Your intent is three choices a training; the code offers everything. The spine counts a version as met once its class knows the ability. If training becomes three choices, the keystones held in versions (the reaping, the judge's drum, the frozen gate, the emptied quiver, every colour) are met a third to half as often, and they'd want a second piece or seat each.

**4. New words.** Heat becomes speed that builds while the field burns (Bonfire's far end is "feeds faster"); Charge is renamed Wrath, because "charge" is the Storm's word for Shock everywhere; Beat, Crescendo and Cascade are new; Surge, Fervor and Tempo are retired. Seven counters, as today. Twelve rules are new or bent, each on the items named in the spine: Marked on random picks and Malison's breadth (approved), a foe's action called early, a blow read as it was struck, damage carried past a kill, damage taken from MP, an ability cast by a count of swings, start-of-fight effects happening again, a status passed on by its holder, an ability brought back by the foes it hit, a gift copied, and the party's whole stone read by one blow.

## What the spine is

- **Every rare and legendary piece (160), every formation (37), the nine changed versions, the sixteen mythics, and the two kit changes,** each as one line: what it does, its kind and rarity, the roads and rungs it serves and how much to each, the engines it's part of, and what the player sees for a first step or an engine's part.
- **The uncommons by kind, named (86), with a rule for the commons** and each kind's stat leans by road.
- **The parts that recur written first,** then everything else built round them; **each engine's link** (what one part makes, what the other reads); and **the web checked:** every rung reached by more than one route, every keystone in two to five places, every door with pieces both sides want, eight exotics, nothing fenced off, every road's sign in a kit in act I.
- **Kept from today where it serves a road:** about half the rares and legendaries keep a name from today (83 of 160), many with a changed card or rarity; among those kept as they are: Hearth, Hoarfrost plate, Rubble plate, Thousandth cut, Flurry wraps, Quietus, Executioner's axe. Every item that served only Phoenix, Lockdown, Harvest, Overflow or Ambush is gone or rebuilt, apart from a small net (Lamp of vigil, Swan's grace, Oathbound shield and the revive versions), which `builds.md` keeps as protection for every build.

## What the numbers say, and how sure

From the offer roll and the offer volumes of 40 simulated runs (about 34 pieces offered an act, and four of them for a given party member):
- **Every road's parties are offered a build piece by the end of act II** most of the time: 71% to 96% by route, before versions. Cold Snap is lowest (72% to 73%), and it leans on its versions and on the Chill counts.
- **Act I offers a road's first step** to 37% to 84% of its parties, on top of the sign the kits already show. Cold Snap and Thousand Cuts are lowest (Thousand Cuts' step is mostly the faster weapon kind, which the count leaves out).
- **A run is offered about nine engines' keystones by act II** from pieces and seats, eleven with its kits and versions. It meets several and not all, which is what found synergies should be.
- *How sure:* the volumes are the simulated player's, whose path and roster differ from a person's; the count is right to within a few points, not exact. It's a check that the stock is met, not a measure of strength.

## Least sure

- **Marked on random picks** may make the Hunt too easy to fall into: with one Mark source, every random bolt, arrow and bash in the party follows it.
- **Foes called to act early** (the goad) speed them up, which a player may never choose.
- **Black ice** can take a cold field down in one tick; it may read as one burst, not a string.
- **Cards that may read alike** for a class that wears both kinds; the fill's shared index is where the rest get caught.

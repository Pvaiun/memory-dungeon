# Batch 2: the director's answers and the agent's read

Copied from the page's store on Nov 17, after the agent's reads went on. Each card: what was asked, the director's answer, and the agent's read. Three cards wait on the director's reply (Hidden and the two formations); their reads change when it comes. The open card wasn't answered.

## Thousand Cuts: a Bleed piece

**Asked:** Do you want a Bleed piece you'd call Great? It could be a new way to apply Bleed, or something that happens when a bleeding foe is hit. If Thousand Cuts doesn't need one, skip it.

**The director (answered):**

> Note: for near it 1, chill also counts as a damage status.
>
> Extra strikes apply trigger the same on hit effects as basic attacks.
>
> Or if you want a more specific bleed enabler for setups that don't have bleed yet:
>
> Convert any chill applied into bleed.

**The agent's read (Placed):** Placed: the first one. An extra strike sets off what a basic attack sets off: the statuses the swing carries, and anything worded "on a basic attack". It's the Fine you kept ("Extra strikes carry the swing's effects"), a little wider, and Thousand Cuts is what needs it. I'm reading it as not rolling Double strike again by itself: your Great does that, to its own limit. An extra strike that crits sets its triggers off 4 times, so I'll size the two together. Left out: Chill into Bleed. It puts two roads' statuses on one card, which your builds ruling rules out, and its wearer could never bring a foe to the shatter. Tell me if you want it anyway. Your note is taken: Chill counts for the pile. That does more than it looks. A wearer's wide Chill now gathers on the coldest foe, so the foes shatter one after another. Nothing in the web names Bleed still, and I won't ask a third time. Bleed comes from the kits and from the plain pieces, which are mine to write.

## Cold Snap: cold without a wall

**Asked:** I need a second way to get a foe to its Chill cap, for a party with no wall to lay Chill from max HP. An applier.

**The director (answered):**

> Applying a status effect to your own team adds many chill to the enemy with the most chill.

**The agent's read (Placed):** Placed. Each status its wearer lays on itself or an ally adds Chill to the foe with the most Chill. I'm assuming three things: it counts only its wearer's own applications, a buff on all three allies counts 3 times, and with no Chill on the field it goes to a random foe. It makes a support the frost's source. A Shield, a Regen, a Might or a Blur each brings the shatter nearer, with no wall. Two things placing it found. With overheal as a Shield, every heal past full is a Shield laid, so a healer freezes foes by healing. And with your Might on a Stun (card 5) on the same unit, it loops: a shatter Stuns, the Stun gives Might, and the Might lays more Chill. I'll size "many" against a buff on the whole party, so it may come out smaller than you wrote.

## Barrage: the two who serve

**Asked:** I need something a unit gets for itself when another ally casts: a payoff the two who feed a caster would wear.

**The director (answered):**

> When an ally casts, distribute 24 ticks of CDR spread across every slotted ability. - so that means (8 to 3, 12 to 2, or 24 to 1)

**The agent's read (Placed):** Placed. When another ally casts, its own cooldowns come down by a fixed amount, split evenly over the abilities it has slotted. I'm reading it as another ally's cast, never its own. It answers card 9 too, as you say: it's the one thing in the web that pays for slotting fewer. Two units wearing it feed each other. At 24 ticks a cast they'd keep each other always ready (a quick cooldown is 14 ticks, a long one 40), so I'll size it below that. What stops them then is mana, which is right for Barrage. In the Cathedral the Voices give their MP away, so there it readies abilities they can't pay for. Beside the Conductor it closes a loop: its casts feed the Soloist's mana, and the Soloist's casts ready it.

Tell me if you meant only one of the two. Left out: the Shield. The web has too much on Shield already: 8 effects, and 7 of them build one holder's pool.

## Between fights

**Asked:** I need a piece about what a unit carries from one fight into the next: its HP, its MP or its cooldowns. Either it reads what its wearer walked in with, or it does something when a fight is won. Not a plain refill: your rule is that no build lasts more fights than another.

**The director (answered):**

> When a fight ends, convert 50% of remaining HP% into MP%

**The agent's read (Placed):** Placed. When a fight is won, its wearer gives up half the HP it has left and gains that share of its mana: at 80% HP it drops to 40% and gains 40% of its max MP. I'm assuming it moves only what its MP has room for. It isn't a refill, since the HP is really gone, so it keeps your rule. What placing it found. With damage that hits MP in place of HP, the mana is its body, so the trade costs it little. With your Great that starts every fight at half HP, most of that price is already paid. And it arms your answer to card 11: the unit walks in hurt, so it walks in with more Attack.

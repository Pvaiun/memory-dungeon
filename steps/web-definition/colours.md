# Colours and ways to win: a first cut

The agent's, and not a ruling. It applies the director's idea (`intent.md`) to the game as built: the 16 classes and their identities, not the 108 effects. It's read from the kits' wording, and nothing in it is measured.

## Where the agent pushed back

- **The builds are decks, not colours.** Pulled apart, the nine don't give nine colours. They give about seven colours and five ways to win, and each build is one colour winning one way. Barrage is a way to win with no colour, and Light is a colour with no build. The nine stay, as samples of what a party ends up as.
- **A colour needs something it can't do, and mixing has to cost.** Themes alone sort nothing, which was the fault of the abstract axes. The cost is in the game already: three units, one of each class, four slots each, and payoffs that want one word stacked. What a colour can't do has to be designed. The proposal: each colour has its own way to live, and one has none.
- **It's still a web.** Five colours make 10 pairs, seven make 21, nine make 36. Not every pair needs a game. A class that holds two colours says which pairs the core set stands behind.
- **The 108 effects aren't restarted yet.** They're written in the 39 threads. Once each thread has a colour, every effect falls in one colour, two (a door) or none, and the count says how many hold.

## The ways to win

Five: the seven clocks of `steps/authored-gaps/web.md`, with two pairs merged. They're shapes a party takes, never shown to the player.

| Way to win | What's watched | Colours that reach it in today's kits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strike first | the opening, then each kill making the next easier | Shadow |
| Build and break | a quiet build-up, then one blow or one burst | Stone, Fire, Frost |
| Wear down | something that grows on the foes while the party outlasts them | Fire, Frost, Storm, Blood |
| Make them pay | the foes' own attacks and actions doing the killing | Stone, Storm, Blood |
| Feed one | two serving one unit, which does the killing | Fire, Blood, with Light serving |

## What it shows already

- **Fire and Frost reach the same two ways to win,** as the director saw. They differ in how they live: Fire can't, and Frost lives by holding the foes still. That makes them partners, which the builds kept apart.
- **Shadow reaches one way to win, and Strike first is reached by one colour.** Both are thin.
- **Light has no way to win of its own.** It's a colour, or it's what every party borrows.

## Least sure

- **Seven or six.** Shadow and Blood share the Assassin and the Ranger and could be one colour.
- **Storm's own way to live.** Its classes live by a Shield from hits, by Evasion and by Shock's cut, three different things.
- **Sunder, Might and crits sit in several colours.** Each needs a colour that holds it first.
- **The ways to win differ less than Magic's.** So far only a fight's length and where the foes' hits land were measured to change what a piece is worth (`four-lines.md`).
- **Whether a player ever sees a colour.** Today nothing names a build to the player.

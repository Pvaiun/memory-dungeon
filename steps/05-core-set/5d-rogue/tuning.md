# Step 5d tuning trail

One line a round. The simulated player is `src/rogue-ai.ts` (rules of thumb, no hidden information); `npm run rogue` plays whole runs with it (`src/rogue-sim.ts`), `npm run rogue:calibrate` sets each fight's own knob (`src/rogue-calibrate.ts`). Win rates are the simulated player's at heat 0, leaders Knight, Cleric and Ranger in turn. With 600 runs a rate is good to about ±2 points; 800, ±1.7.

**Targets, set before tuning:** the simulated player wins about a third of its runs (a careful human should do better, and heat is the way up); bosses end more runs than anything else; a regular fight wears the party down rather than ends the run; elites pay for their risk; every decision kind (route, draft, spoils, campfire, shop) is worth points of win rate when made well rather than at random.

1. **First build** (120 runs, first-guess knobs): 37% won. The Fire Mine loses 82% of the runs that enter it (the Crypt 13%).
2. **The trace** (300 runs): 28%. Attrition is weak: 77% of fights start with the party above 80% HP, a won fight costs 10 points of party HP, and deaths cluster in a few fights (the Fire Giant's three fights kill 58% to 86% of parties that meet them, the Powder Room 48%).
3. **Every fight its own knob** (`npm run rogue:calibrate`): snapshots of real parties before each fight, then each fight's knob bisected until it costs its tier's share of HP against those parties made whole (easy 12/14/16, regular 18/20/22, elite 32/34/36 by act) and a boss wins its share (12/20/28%). 100 fights in 50 s. Bomb Bugs can't be eased enough (three explode for a wipe, as their document says): no fight has more than two, and packs that explode never grow. Result: 13%, half the runs dead in act I. Measuring at full MP overrated the casters, who reach a boss short of mana.
4. **Regular fights measured with MP as it is, bosses against parties as they arrive:** 15%; regular fights and elites kill as many as bosses.
5. **Cheaper fights** (easy 10/11/12, regular 15/16/17, elite 28/30/32): 19%. Elites in acts II and III kill 9% to 15% of the parties that walk in above 80% HP.
6. **Classes, unbiased** (1,200 runs, the draft at random; then each class as leader with random companions, 240 runs each): as leader, the Vampire wins 37%, the Monk 20%, the Knight 13%, the Bramble knight and Valkyrie 12%, the Paladin 11%, the Pyromancer and Stormcaller 10%, the Geomancer 9%, the Ranger 8%, the Cleric, Hoplite and Assassin 7%, the Bard and Hexer 5%, the Frostweaver 4%. The weak ones die in act I (the Bard reaches act II in 32% of runs, the Vampire 84%).
7. **The starting ability** (each alternative, 240 runs): only the Vampire moves (Wither 19%, Frenzy 38%, Blood drain 37%). No alternative lifts a weak class by more than 2 points: their trouble is the class, not the start. **The Vampire starts with Wither.**
8. **Measured class values in the simulated player's draft, elites 25/26/27:** 32%. Bosses cause 56% of deaths, elites 19%, regular fights 26%.
9. **Each decision, priced** (600 runs each, that decision at random): route 19% (−13), spoils 11% (−21), campfire 10% (−22), shop 28% (−4), events 27% (−5), draft 15% (−17), which ability to learn 32% (0); no gear beyond the plain weapon, 0%. Avoiding elites 34% (+2), seeking them all 28%.
10. **What a reward is worth** (gifts at the start, 600 runs each): three legendaries +4, three rares +12, 150 gold +0. The legendaries are sidegrades by the core set's rule, and the player hoarded gold (spent 160 of 450). **A legendary piece carries a rare's two affixes; the player buys anything worth a gear step, best value for the gold first.** 38%; three legendaries +15, three rares +13, 150 gold +6; avoiding elites still +3.
11. **Elites 22/23/24:** 30% (re-calibration measures fights against the now stronger parties, so the targets, not party power, set the difficulty). Elites break even.
12. **An elite's trophy:** its legendary piece is won outright, on top of the pick (Slay the Spire's relic), and calibration on 300 runs with 60 parties a fight. 800 runs each: taking elites when healthy 36%, avoiding them 34%, seeking them all 35%.

# Feel read, round 0

A cold read of twelve rogue fights, tick by tick, checked against the summary of 376 fights. No numbers are proposed.

## Per pack

**Pack 1, Toll Gate (fight, won t56).** Three bandits poke for 268; nobody drops below half. Anthem (t8.7, t25.4, t42.1) makes the Cleric the carry: its staff basics go 1,120 → 2,078 → 2,464, 50% of the damage. Frost lance pins Chill at 12 in one cast (t12.9). The Bard hits for 378 (12%); Cure overheals every time. A healer out-damaging everyone on floor 1 reads wrong; the fight is pleasant and risk-free.

**Pack 2, Night Wolves (fight, lost t31).** The party arrives at 39%, 44% and 30% HP against seven 5,600-HP wolves. Chain lightning (t9.2) puts 25–29 Shock on four of them and each pays 3,023–3,600 the moment it acts (t10.2, t13.7, t14): Shock is 43% of all damage, the Stormcaller 81%. The Valkyrie casts once and dies at t18.2 before Rally is ready. Lost on arrival; the wolves were numerous, never dangerous.

**Pack 3, Twin Ogres (elite, won t67).** Ogres telegraph everything ("begins attack, lands t11.2"). The Knight taunts (t8.7, t25.4), so both Flattens land on it inside a tick (t26.2, t27.1), 1,326 each: dead from 2,120, stunned for the last tick of its life. The Assassin's Backstab crits (2,964 at t20.1 and t48.2) and Bleed (780 → 519 → 346 a hit) are the fight; the Paladin's first Smite lands at t29.1. From t35, one Ogre at 76% against three unthreatened units: 32 ticks of grind.

**Pack 4, The Packmaster (boss, lost t90).** The opening is the Bramble knight at its best: Thicket at t9, four wolves hit it, take 300 thorns and Bleed, and are dead by t25. Then Crush: t25.1 kills the knight (4,857 vs 2,048, Regrowth two ticks from landing), t57.4 the Geomancer (5,023 through a 1,500 shield), t89.7 the Vampire (5,598). Three Crushes, three kills; from t57 the Vampire pokes a 33,660-HP boss for 502 a swing while waiting to die.

**Pack 5, Snow Pack (fight, won t46).** The best-shaped fight. The Knight taunts and holds six snow wolves at 223 a bite (171 after Fortify, t26.4); nobody else takes a point. Firebolt+ puts Burn at the 40 cap in one cast (t9) and Wolf D dies to a 2,000 pulse at t9.6; Fireball on four at t34 is a visible turn, and Burn's decay finishes three wolves by t46. Pyre (ten ticks, 1,200 MP) lands at t46 for 8,444 on a wolf at 15%. The Monk's Sunder on Armor-0 wolves and Flow's Haste show nothing; twelve lines say "healed 0 by Lifesteal".

**Pack 6, Lich Lair (elite, lost t62).** The Monk takes a 2,143 crit and a 1,220 bolt and is dead at t6.1 with no cast. The Mummy (38,800 HP) taunts three times, so the 7,760-HP Lich is never hit. The Cleric starts Renew at t23.7 and dies at t26.3 before it lands. The Hoplite's Aegis (t27.9) eats Soulfire whole at t47.3, a wind-up paying off perfectly in a fight long lost. 9,915 damage to a sponge in 62 ticks.

**Pack 7, The Reaper (boss, won t215).** 102,382 HP, and a Bard whose Frostfang pins Chill 12 on the boss from t15 to the end: the Reaper swings ten times in 215 ticks. Death bell → Doom reads beautifully (ten ticks, then 3,500 flat): the first takes the Bramble knight 4,300 → 800 (t26.7), the second kills it (t71.4). The Vampire opens with 20 MP; Refrain's +255 a Bard swing buys it 13 Blood drains, which grow to 3,960 under Might and heal up to 2,376 in Bloodlust. From t71 nothing changes for 144 ticks: Anthem, basic, drain, Chorus, Doom, repeat.

**Pack 8, The Watch (fight, lost t46).** Every foe bolts for 633. The Frostweaver arrives at 942 HP and is dead at t6.4 with no cast; one Chain lightning (t9.2) makes Shock 60% of the party's damage, and the Stormcaller dies at t16.3. The Cleric then stands alone 30 ticks against two 33,930-HP soldiers, curing itself and pulsing Regen for 40, until Last rites triggers at 301 HP (t43.5), two ticks before death. Raise+ was ready at t60.

**Pack 9, Lich Pair (elite, won t28).** The fastest fight and the one where a build visibly works: thorns, a shocking amulet and Briar stacking on Barbed arrow+ to Bleed 56 on the Mummy. The Mummy then bleeds 6,762 on one hit (t11.4), 4,508, 3,005, 2,696: a fifth of an elite per poke. The knight fell to 1,308 at t20.9 and Rally (2,250, t24.4) answered: a real close call. Rending arrow+ for 7,867 ends it; Soulfire began at t22.2 and never landed. An elite at +254% HP gone in 28 ticks.

**Pack 10, The Silent One (boss, lost t136).** The Paladin, the party's strongest unit, enters at 0 HP and never acts. The boss has 217,800 HP; the two who can act deal 33,230 in 136 ticks. The Hexer's one moment is Unmake at t54.6 ("Quiet is lost"); it dies three ticks later to a 2,411 swing. Sunder 20 from one Enfeeble+ is the one place Sunder read: the Vampire's basic went 119 → 451 (t7 → t18) and back when it lapsed (t121). Lifesteal pulled the Vampire from 233 to 1,988 in one tick (t40 → t41), a comeback inside a fight over before it began.

**Pack 11, War Camp (elite, won t83).** A 3,592 crit basic and one Firebolt kill the Shieldman elite at t12.9. An Archer and the Ogre then take the 1,230-HP Pyromancer apart by t16.7. Flatten (t26.8) stuns the Geomancer mid-cast and "Earthen shell waits" eight ticks, which reads. The Hoplite swings three times all fight (t13, t39, t82) for 3,100, 4,851, 4,851: slow and heavy, as it should. From t40 one Ogre and one Archer poke shields for 43 ticks.

**Pack 12, Ogre Bridge (elite, lost t106).** The Paladin begins Smite at t20.1 (ten ticks), is Flattened at t27.9 (1,820, Stun, "Smite waits") and killed by a bandit's 282 at t29 with Smite still waiting. The Assassin one-shots both bandits with 5,879 crits (t20.1, t48.2) and does 231 a swing between. The Frostweaver has Magic 1.5: Blizzard for 216. The Ogre, at 90% when the Paladin fell, is at 40% when it Flattens the Frostweaver for 2,430 at t105.3. Seventy-seven ticks of two glass units chipping a tree.

## Across all packs

**5. Rhythm.** Every fight opens the same way: three basics by t5, the slot-one ability at t8–t10, and in five of twelve packs the decisive thing has happened inside the first 17 ticks (packs 2, 6, 8, 10, 11). The opening is a volley, never a build. Fights breathe only when a tank holds (packs 3, 5, 7, 9); otherwise the middle is attrition on 2,000-HP units. Pile-ups: two Flattens inside a tick (pack 3), four wolves paying Shock in four ticks (pack 2). Foes' wind-ups read every time. The party's are one tick and invisible, except the ten-tick ones, which die or arrive late (packs 4, 5, 6, 12). Regular, elite and boss are one shape at different knobs: act 1 fights 49 ticks, elites 53, bosses 69, then act 3 bosses 133 with a spread of 18 to 415. An elite in 28 ticks (pack 9) and a regular fight in 56 (pack 1) is backwards.

**6. Abilities.** The ones that matter fire by t10 and every 14–18 ticks after: Firebolt, Chain lightning, Anthem, Thicket, Barbed arrow, Blood drain (5 casts a fight, the most used), Backstab (a burst every 25 with 231–535 swings between). The third slots mostly don't exist: Raise 0.03 casts a fight, first at t65; Valhalla 0.14; Unmake 0.20; Tremor 0.40; Pyre 0.48 at t41; Sanctuary 0.50; Thunderstorm 0.56, dealing 776 a cast against Chain lightning's 2,113. Too big for their cost: Firebolt (300 MP, a 2,000-a-pulse Burn), Chain lightning (one cast, 60% of a fight). Too small: Frost lance and Blizzard on a Magic-1.5 Frostweaver (216–434), the Vampire's basics on bosses (119–261). A tempo of their own: the Hoplite, the Assassin, the Bramble knight's 11–15 tick loop. Most others are a cooldown timer.

**7. Statuses.** The damage statuses are bursts, not ramps: one application lands at or near the cap and decays (Burn 40, Shock 25–30, Bleed 50–56, Chill 12, Sunder 20, Might 15, each from one cast or a swing or two: packs 1, 2, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11). Shock is a nuke (3,000–3,600 each time a 5,600-HP wolf acts); Bleed on a stacked target is absurd (6,762 on one hit). The ramp exists only where basics build it: the Hexer's Chill 3 → 12 over 48 ticks (pack 10), Burning fist 13 → 23 → 33 → 40 (pack 5). Decay is legible everywhere (Burn 2,000 → 1,700, Bleed 780 → 519 → 346) and feels right. Invisible to a player: Sunder on Armor-0 foes (wolves and bandits are two thirds of foe appearances), Weaken (a 13–38% shave), Haste (never legible), Clarity (always on a full unit), Guard (223 → 171). Locks that read: Taunt, Stun holding a cast, Silence interrupting. Chill 12 on a boss is a lock too strong (pack 7).

**8. Mana.** Only the Vampire is ever hungry, and it changes what it does: 360 → 10 (pack 4), 20 at the start (pack 7), 0 (pack 10); it casts Blood drain the moment 150 MP accrues and nothing else (summary: 35% left, the lowest). The Stormcaller had one cast in it in pack 2; the Pyromancer spent 2,600 of 3,345 in pack 5, one Pyre from empty. Everyone else never thinks about it (Cleric 85% left, Hexer 93%, Ranger 94%, Assassin 91%); Siphon refills units already full (pack 11). Refrain showed clearly: pack 7's Vampire is a Bard-funded class. Clarity never showed. Stillness was never cast.

**9. Classes.** Plays its description: Bramble knight (65% of its damage from Thorns and Bleed), Assassin, Hoplite, Pyromancer (Burn 60%), Stormcaller (Shock 55%), Ranger (Bleed 42%), Vampire, and the Bard when it has Attack (pack 7), not when it hasn't (pack 1). Doesn't: Cleric (all its damage is basics; its healing is overheal or too slow for one-shots; Raise never), Hexer (0% ability damage, debuffs nobody sees, Unmake its one moment), Knight (10% share; holds in pack 5; its Taunt collects every signature move), Paladin (Judgement never triggered in any pack; 0 casts in packs 10 and 12), Geomancer (20% share), Frostweaver (Brittle doubles a small number). Glass dies before it plays: Stormcaller down at t21 mean, Ranger t22, Pyromancer and Hexer t31. Dominant: the Vampire (34% share, last standing in packs 4, 7, 10) and whichever caster carries a capped status (Stormcaller 60%, Pyromancer 50%, Ranger 49%). Worst-feeling three: Hexer (invisible), Paladin (absent, or dying in its own wind-up), Cleric (busy, pointless). Best-feeling three: Bramble knight, Vampire, Pyromancer; the Hoplite's swing and the Assassin's burst close behind.

**10. Enemies and bosses.** Boss moves come often (Crush 1.85 a fight, Death bell 3.5, Quiet 5.25) and every one matters too much: nothing sits between a one-shot and a 500 basic (packs 4, 7, 12); Doom's 3,500 is more than a Bard's whole HP. Elite is a knob, not a foe: the Shieldman dies at t12.9 (pack 11), the Mummy at t27 (pack 9) or not at all (pack 6). Elite Taunt makes the minion untouchable (pack 6: the Lich took 0). Most foe moves are promised, not delivered (Soulfire 0.33 an appearance, Flatten 0.68). Minions do act before dying (96–100%, Drones 83%). Snakes deal 91 an appearance. Regions differ in damage type and the Crypt's casters hit much harder (1,220–2,143), not in shape.

**11. Drama.** Close calls that paid: pack 9 (knight at 1,308, Rally), pack 10 (Vampire 233 → 1,988). Comebacks that changed a result: none. Kills in one or two blows: ten across twelve packs. Decided by t10: packs 6, 8, 10 and effectively 2. Decided long before the end: packs 4 (33 ticks), 6 (36), 7 (144), 8 (30), 10 (79), 11 (43), 12 (77). A player would feel the first fifteen ticks and then watch the clock.

## 12. The ranked list

What looked wrong, worst first:

1. **Fights lost before tick 1.** Pack 10 (a Paladin at 0 HP into the final boss), pack 2 (609/2,000 and 1,576/4,000 into seven wolves), pack 8 (942 HP, dead at t6.4). Summary: act 3 bosses won 17%, 2.2 KOs a boss fight.
2. **Long dead stretches after the decision.** Pack 7 t71–t215, pack 10 t57–t136, pack 12 t29–t106, pack 6 t26–t62. Summary: act 3 bosses 18–415 ticks, act 2 elites to 147.
3. **Signature moves are one-shots with nothing between.** Crush ×3 = three kills (pack 4 t25.1, t57.4, t89.7); Doom 3,500 flat (pack 7 t71.4); Flatten 2,430 (pack 12 t105.3). Summary: bosses 1.5–2.2 KOs a fight.
4. **Statuses land at the cap in one cast.** Burn 40 from a Firebolt (pack 5 t9, pack 11 t11.5); Shock 25–30 from a Chain lightning (pack 2 t9.2, 3,600 a wolf action); Bleed 56 (pack 9 t11.4, 6,762 a hit); Chill 12 from one Frost lance (pack 1) or two basics (pack 7). Summary: Burn 28% and Shock 21% of party damage, mean peaks 28 and 21.
5. **Taunt makes the tank the one-shot's address.** Pack 3 t26.2/27.1, pack 4 t25.1, pack 7 t26.7 and t71.4. Summary: Bramble knight 33.8 KOs per 100 fights, the highest.
6. **Glass dies before it casts.** Pack 8 Frostweaver t6.4, pack 6 Monk t6.1, pack 11 Pyromancer t16.7. Summary: Stormcaller down at t21 mean, Ranger t22.
7. **Ten-tick wind-ups die or arrive at the end.** Smite (pack 12 t20.1 → dead t29), Pyre (pack 5 lands t46 of 46), Regrowth and Renew lost to death (packs 4, 6). Summary: third slots first cast at t37–t65; Raise 0.03 a fight.
8. **Elites are a knob, not a shape.** Shieldman dead t12.9 (pack 11), Mummy t27 (pack 9) or untouchable (pack 6). Summary: act 2 elites 28–147 ticks.
9. **Sunder, Weaken, Haste, Clarity show nothing.** Sunder on Armor-0 wolves (packs 2, 5); Haste never legible (pack 5, Flow ×3); Clarity on full units (packs 3, 5). Summary: Sunder applied in 143 fights; one visible moment in the packs (pack 10 t18).
10. **Log noise a player reads one beat at a time.** "healed 0 by Lifesteal" ×12 (pack 5), ×20+ (pack 7); "MP +150 → 6,000" (pack 6); thirty Burn ticks of 54–661 on a 217,800-HP boss (pack 10).
11. **Ability text doesn't match what lands.** Earthen shell "5,000 + 5,000" gives 1,500 + 2,250 (pack 4 t28.2; "8,000 + 8,000" in pack 11 t34.8); over-cap numbers on Anthem, Frost lance, Firebolt+.
12. **The Cleric as carry, the healer as nothing.** 50% of damage in pack 1; Regen 40 a pulse and Last rites two ticks before death in pack 8. Summary: 100% basics, 85% MP left, Raise 0.03.
13. **Elite Taunt protects the minion completely.** Pack 6: the Lich took 0 damage in 62 ticks behind three Shield ups.
14. **Chill locks a boss from a weapon affix.** Pack 7: Reaper at Chill 12 for 200 ticks, ten swings in 215.
15. **Foe moves promised and not cast.** Soulfire began t22.2, fight ended t28 (pack 9). Summary: Soulfire 0.33 an appearance, Flatten 0.68, Cheer 0.27.

What looked right: foes' wind-ups read every time and give the only real anticipation (Ogres, Crush, Death bell → Doom); Aegis eating Soulfire (pack 6 t47.3), Stun holding a cast (pack 11 t26.8) and Unmake interrupting Quiet (pack 10 t54.6) pay off visibly; the Knight holding six wolves while the Pyromancer burns them (pack 5) is the fight the game wants to be; the Bramble knight's loop (pack 4) and pack 9's thorns-and-Bleed build feel like kits working together; Refrain funding a 20-MP Vampire into 13 Blood drains (pack 7) is a support story a player would notice; Burn, Bleed and Might decay legibly; the Bloodlust comeback (pack 10 t41) is the one genuine swing; minions act before they die.

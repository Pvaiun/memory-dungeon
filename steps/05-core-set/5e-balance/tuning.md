# Step 5e tuning trail

One entry a round. The sheet is `npm run balance -- --trios 80 --seeds 2` (about four minutes on four cores; 160 parties × 20 campaign runs, 7 free-play runs and 100 rogue fights × 2 seeds, plus the gear and formation probes). Knobs are frozen at the values of Oct 27, 2026 throughout the fix rounds; win rates are context. A class's "win" is the win rate of parties with it minus parties without it, campaign / free play / rogue. Every number changed is in `content/core-set/scale.ts` or `classes.ts` with a comment naming this step.

0. **Baseline** (`sheet-before.md`, `findings.md`): 61 flags. The Vampire +37 / +45 / +18, the Bard −16 / −30 / −21, the Hexer −10 / −16 / −14, the Assassin −9 / −26 / −12; Caravan +20.5 against Neutral on the same parties, Escort +11.2, Blitz +8.1; bosses casting their signature move 0.5 to 0.9 times a fight in free play; a third of Wolves, Wisps and Drones killed before acting; Pyre, Static charge and Stillness never cast; MP regained 110% of MP spent over a campaign run.

1. **How often units act, and what they can afford** (all in `scale.ts`): the long cooldown 42 → 36; foes' medium and long cooldowns 48 → 40 and 70 → 60; the very long wind-up 8 → 6; the slotted head starts 20/12/6 → 16/12/8; MP regen 0.05 → 0.04 of the stat every 4 ticks; Static charge and Stillness 1,200 and 1,600 MP → 400 (`classes.ts`). Result: 53 flags. Every boss now casts its move at least 0.85 times a fight in free play (the Ogre Chief 0.85, the Fire Giant 1.35, the Ice Titan 1.32; were 0.5 to 0.95); Wolves, Skeletons and Hellhounds act before they die (Wisps 33%, Drones 27% and Mini Slimes 28% still don't: the Wisp has 1 HP by design, and the other two enter mid-fight on cooldown, which the sheet now excludes). MP regained fell to 97% of spent; the Cleric is short of MP 6% / 12% of its time, the Hexer 7% / 12%, the Stormcaller 4% / 7%, the Geomancer 19% / 21%, the Vampire 8% in the campaign (its 200 to 330 costs on a 2,000 pool at regen 2); the Bard and Paladin still 0%. Vanish and Garrote 0.28 and 0.37 casts a regular fight (were 0.18); Stillness 0.21 (0.04); Pyre and Static charge still 0.04 and 0.09, because the Pyromancer and Stormcaller are knocked out at ticks 13 and 14 on average, before a third slot comes round. The campaign's spread win rate fell from 23% to 18% at the frozen knobs (the casters cast less).

2. **What actions do:** Regen 12 pulses of 3% → 10 of 2.5% (36% → 25% of max HP); Blood drain 0.75 → 0.5 of max HP; Spear thrust a normal hit (0.75 of Attack) → 1.5 of Attack (Phalanx triples the swing, not the thrust); Volatile 0.25 → 0.18 of Magic. Result: 50 flags. Caravan +17 → +14.7 (Regen is still the whole of it: Vanguard, Champion, Tortoise and Scatter sit within +2.4 of Neutral); the Herald's horn left the flags (+8 → +5). The Vampire +32 / +45 / +18 → +25 / +40 / +16: Blood drain fell from 60% to 46% of its damage, but it now swings more (54% basic) and heals itself 4,350 a fight, up from 3,850: Lifesteal high at 30% (60% under Bloodlust) on 1.6× an average unit's damage is the sustain, not the one ability. The Hoplite −5.5 / −15 / −6 → −3.7 / −12 / −2.4 (Spear thrust 61% of its damage at about 2 swings). The Bomb Bug 4.2× → 3.4× the median minion's damage. The Bard −13 / −25 / −22, the Hexer −9 / −17 / −15, the Assassin −8 / −20 / −11: unmoved, as expected (nothing in this round touched them).

   **The sheet itself:** the pooled flags were weighted by runs, so the rogue's 32,000 single fights outvoted the campaign's 6,400 runs five to one. The pooled sheet now averages the three sets' rates (each mode counts once) and sums their counts; the flags above are re-read from it in round 3.

3. **Who's worth bringing, first pass** (flags re-read on the equal-weighted pooled sheet): Lifesteal high 30 → 20, medium 15 → 10; Thorns high 300 → 250; Regen 25% → 20% of max HP; Escort's Shield 0.25 → 0.15 of max HP; Gale spear 0.25 → 0.2 of Attack a hit; the Bard's songs 650 → 400 MP and Allegro on the ally with the highest Attack or Magic (was a random ally); the Hexer's curses 1,600 / 1,300 / 1,300 → 1,200 / 900 / 1,000 MP; Hidden 14 → 20 ticks; **the Bard and the Hexer at HP 3** (the documents' 2; a change to the stat table, listed for the director). Result: 56 flags. The Vampire +16 / +36 / +12 (heals itself 3,030 a fight, was 4,350), the Valkyrie +13 / +31 / +9 (the Gale spear cut did little: its worth is Rally, Valhalla and a 3/3/3 body), the Bard −10 / −21 / −15 (knocked out in 48 of 100 campaign fights, was 55), the Hexer −7 / −12 / −9 (was −9 / −17 / −15), the Assassin −6.5 / −20 / −10 (Hidden's length did nothing: Vanish's first cast is at tick 26), Caravan +13.7. **A pattern:** in free play (no knob, full gear, a six-fight run) every class's number is two to three times the campaign's and the rogue's, both ways; the free set at 46% won is the most sensitive of the three, the campaign at 15% at its frozen knobs the most compressed.

4. **The healers, the formations' step, the campfire in free play:** Lifesteal 20 / 10 → 15 / 8; Regen 20% → 16% (8 pulses of 2%); Shock 60 → 70 a stack; Cure 0.45 → 0.55 of Magic for 1,000 MP (was 1,280) and Rally 0.7 → 0.55 (the Cleric's heal was a third of a unit's HP a cast, the Valkyrie's half, for half the MP: the healer was the worse healer); Blizzard 1,300 → 1,000 MP; **the Assassin at HP 3**; **a formation slot's step one and a half gear steps** (the gear document's own fix: probed, the five formations with no effect sat within 3 points of Neutral while the effects were worth 7 to 14); **one campfire in every spine run** (the rules document says every run has one; free play, in the interface and the sheet, had none). Result: the Assassin −4 / −11 / −4, within its band; the Vampire +13 / +25 / +10; the Valkyrie +13 / +29 / +8; the Bard −11 / −24 / −14; the Hexer −7 / −17 / −9; Caravan +12, Escort +12.6 (its slots are all plain bonuses, so the bigger step made it stronger), Blitz +6.4; Vanguard, Champion and Tortoise +4 to +5 (were +2). The Hoplite and Paladin are judged as supports from here (Aggro 3: the document plants them beside the party, not in front of it).

5. **The formations' effects and the two healers again:** Caravan's Regen 10 ticks (the status's own is 16; a formation's effect may set its length, as the Hourglass sets its Haste's); no Armor on the Escort slots (5a took their HP); Cure 0.55 → 0.65 of Magic; Rally 0.55 → 0.45. Result: 54 flags. Caravan +9.5, Escort +8.8, Blitz +6.7; the Cleric heals 1,560 a fight, the Valkyrie 1,320 (they were level); the Vampire +11 / +25 / +9; the Valkyrie +10.5 / +25 / +7; the Monk +6 / +21 / +6; the Bard −11 / −24 / −15 and the Hexer −6.5 / −17.5 / −10, unmoved by three rounds of numbers on their kits and a point of HP: **no progress in three rounds, written down and moved on.** Both are what the sheet can't price: a buff or a curse on one unit, in a party of three where a body that deals or heals is worth more than the buff it would get. The report puts the design options to the director.

6. **A last pass before the re-tune:** Blood drain 0.5 → 0.4 of max HP; the Valkyrie's Gale spear, Rally and Valhalla 400 / 650 / 1,200 → 500 / 800 / 1,500 MP (a mid-tier caster that was short of MP 3% of its time); Caravan's Regen 10 → 8 ticks; Escort's Shield 0.15 → 0.12.
   Result (`sheet-after-fixes.md`, at the frozen knobs): 54 flags. In the two modes people play, every class but two is within ±10 points: the Vampire +9.5 / +8 (campaign / rogue; it heals itself 2,150 a fight, was 3,850), the Valkyrie +8 / +7, the Monk +6 / +6, the Stormcaller +6 / +8, the Bramble knight +5 / +8; the Assassin −3 / −3, the Hexer −6 / −10, the Bard −10 / −15. Free play still spreads two to three times wider (the Vampire +24, the Valkyrie +23, the Monk +20; the Bard −25, the Hexer −19, the Frostweaver −14). Caravan +8.1 and Escort +7.9 against Neutral, Blitz +6.3, the slot formations +2 to +4.2, Ambush −1.7, Last stand −4.2. Every boss casts its move at least 0.74 times a fight in free play (the Ogre Chief; the rest 1.0 to 1.4). Still outside their bands and left for the report: the Bard and Hexer; Pyre, Static charge, Stillness and the other third-slot abilities in boss fights (their casters die first); the Stormcaller at 1.77× an average unit's damage and 1.3 casts a regular fight (its MP binds, as 5c meant); the Wisp (1 HP) and the Bomb Bug (its blast) by design; the Paladin and Bard never short of MP. Tests, typecheck and the smoke trios pass. Next: the knobs.
